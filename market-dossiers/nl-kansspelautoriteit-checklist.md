# Netherlands operator verification checklist — KSA

**Document type:** market-level control checklist, not a casino review. **Score:** not applicable.

## Official regulator source
The Kansspelautoriteit publishes a register of licensees searchable by company, website, app or licence number. It explicitly advises users to check this register to verify whether a Dutch licence was issued.

**Official register:** https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/register-licensees
**Consumer lookup:** https://kanspelautoriteit.nl/kansspelwijzer/
**Checked:** 2026-10-25; repeat lookup for each exact brand/domain before publication.

## Mandatory per-brand checks
1. Search exact domain, app and legal operator in the KSA register.
2. Save the exact result, licence scope/status, legal name and timestamp.
3. If no matching Dutch authorisation is found, HOLD/EXCLUDE pending compliance review; do not infer eligibility from another country’s licence.
4. Review Dutch-language terms, bonus restrictions, payment policy, complaints, self-exclusion and player-protection controls.
5. Avoid publishing affiliate calls to action for an operator not confirmed eligible for the Netherlands.
6. Recheck before publication and schedule a periodic audit.

## SEO and UX rule
A country-specific page must provide meaningful Netherlands-specific evidence and must not be a thin translated or doorway page. Use NOINDEX until eligibility and editorial gates pass. Any score must reflect documented evidence, not brand recognition.

## Visual asset rule
Only use commissioned or rights-cleared media; store source, permission, attribution, date and usage scope. No image permissions are granted by the regulator register.
