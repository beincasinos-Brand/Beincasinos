# Official source index — target market checks

**Last checked:** 2026-10-25. This index records authoritative starting points, not a blanket approval for any brand. Search each exact domain, legal entity and product in the applicable source immediately before publishing.

| Market | Official source | Minimum required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Great Britain | https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/register | Exact operator, domain/product scope, current licence status and regulatory actions |
| Netherlands | https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/register-licensees | Exact company/domain/app and Dutch licence status |
| Netherlands consumer lookup | https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/kansspelwijzer/ | Confirm whether the specific provider is authorised |
| Spain | https://www.ordenacionjuego.es/en/operadores-juego/operadores-licencia/operadores | Exact legal entity, domains, licence categories and current status |
| Italy | https://www.adm.gov.it/portale/monopoli/giochi/gioco_distanza/gioco_dist_concessionari | Exact concessionaire, domain and concession details |
| Ontario | https://www.igamingontario.ca/en/player/regulated-igaming-market | Exact operator and gaming website listing |
| Sweden | https://www.spelinspektionen.se/ | Exact licensee, product/domain scope and licence dates |

## Editorial constraints
- An entry in one jurisdiction does not prove eligibility in another.
- A regulator register does not prove a bonus is current, withdrawals are fast, or customer support is good.
- Preserve URL, exact matching result, date checked and evidence capture for each claim.
- If the exact match is ambiguous or absent, HOLD for compliance review; do not invent a positive status.
- Never calculate a final score from incomplete critical evidence.
