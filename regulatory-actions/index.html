How to read this page

Regulators publish actions when an operator breached its licence conditions. A payment in lieu of a penalty usually means the operator accepted failings and agreed remedies. We show these facts in each review under red flags and they do not by themselves change a score, which follows the published method on how we score.

This tracker only lists actions we have verified against an official source. It is not complete, and absence of an entry is not a clean record. Check the regulator's own register before you play. See how to read casino regulatory actions.
