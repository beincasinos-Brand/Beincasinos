The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) public register maps **netbet.co.uk** and the associated casino, live, lottery, poker, sports and Vegas subdomains to **Netbet Enterprises Limited**, account **39170**. ## 2. Material regulatory action

On **5 November 2025**, the UKGC published a regulatory settlement following a review of Netbet Enterprises Limited's remote operating licence. The Commission identified failings in anti-money-laundering/counter-terrorist-financing controls and social-responsibility/customer-interaction controls related to online casino and betting activities. The settlement outcomes included Commission costs, a public statement, and a **£650,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty**.

The public statement also identifies concerns involving other marketing requirements and regulatory returns. Attribute findings to the licensee and the relevant review period; do not convert historic findings into unsupported claims about the present state of controls.
