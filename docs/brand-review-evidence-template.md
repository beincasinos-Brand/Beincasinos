## Rating discipline

- Never infer payout reliability from an operator's marketing statement.
- Distinguish stated processing estimates from observed tests.
- Do not use “safe”, “best”, “guaranteed” or similar absolute language.
- Do not score an item whose material inputs remain unknown; show “not verified” where appropriate.
- Disclose affiliate relationships and explain editorial independence.
- Do not let commercial terms determine an editorial score.
- Keep market-specific rules separate; a licence in one country does not establish authorisation elsewhere.
