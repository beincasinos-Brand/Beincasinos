# Brand review evidence template (internal; do not publish as-is)

**Purpose:** reusable evidence pack for a single casino brand and one clearly defined customer market. This is a research template, not a review, legal opinion, endorsement, or proof that a service is safe.

> **Hard rule:** never publish a brand page or count it toward the 100 verified reviews until every mandatory gate below is evidenced, dated, independently checked, and signed off. Candidate lists, search snippets, an active licence alone, or an affiliate offer do not count.

**No automatic aggregate score:** material safety, legal, or money-handling issues must be explained rather than hidden by an average. A high score cannot override a failed licence/domain gate.

## 7. Publication gates

Mark each item only after evidence is attached:

- [ ] Correct brand, exact product, exact legal entity, and market scope established.
- [ ] Relevant active regulator permission confirmed from an official register.
- [ ] Exact customer-facing domain confirmed or any unresolved point explicitly blocks publication.
- [ ] Current terms, bonus terms, payments/withdrawals, KYC, complaints/ADR reviewed.
- [ ] Privacy/cookies and safer-gambling controls reviewed.
- [ ] Exact-entity and related-entity enforcement history checked; attribution caveats recorded.
- [ ] Every material factual claim has a source and check date.
- [ ] No fabricated firsthand testing, invented payment times, or unverified bonus promises.
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure and editorial independence checked.
- [ ] Risk information and market-appropriate help resources checked.
- [ ] Independent second reviewer completed and disagreements resolved.
- [ ] Legal/editorial sign-off completed.
- [ ] Final URL, canonical, internal links, schema, sitemap inclusion, and indexability reviewed.
- [ ] Public page contains only approved review material; internal evidence notes and drafts are excluded from build output.
