## Critical jurisdiction distinction

The official Gemeinsame Glücksspielbehörde der Länder (GGL) whitelist is dated 2 October 2026 and lists permitted gambling providers and their approved product categories/domains: https://www.gluecksspiel-behoerde.de/de/fuer-spielende/uebersicht-erlaubter-anbieter-whitelist

The GGL explains that virtual slot-machine games are a distinct category. Online casino table games are separately authorised by individual state authorities, and the GGL itself does not issue those online-casino table-game permissions. Its FAQ states that, as of 26 May 2025, Schleswig-Holstein and Bavaria had granted a total of five online-casino-game concessions: https://www.gluecksspiel-behoerde.de/de/fuer-spielende/informationen-fuer-spielende-faqs/faq-was-sind-legale-online-casinos-in-deutschland

Therefore the candidates below are **virtual slots / online gambling candidates**, not automatically full-service online casino brands. Product scope and state territory must be checked before any casino review is planned.

## Research gates before a candidate counts as a verified brand

1. Re-open the current whitelist and capture the exact operator, product category, domain, territory, and permission dates.
2. Confirm the domain's current legal terms, legal entity, registration requirements, and exact game product (virtual slots versus online table games).
3. For any Schleswig-Holstein-only online casino-game offer, do not generalise permission to the rest of Germany.
4. Check current payment methods, fees, withdrawal processing and settlement, verification rules, bonus terms, privacy/contact, complaint route, and safer-gambling controls.
5. Check regulator enforcement and relevant illegal-offer information; preserve a dated source log.
6. Require independent second-editor review and BeInCasinos publisher/legal/affiliate/privacy/market-launch approval before publication.
