# Paid-listing pricing validation plan

**Status: internal only — not a rate card, offer, or permission to sell.** BeInCasinos has not established audience reach or demonstrated buyer demand. No price should be advertised and no placement should be sold until the launch, legal, privacy, and audience gates are complete.

## Why pricing is not fixed yet

A paid listing is worth only what a buyer can reasonably justify from the inventory, audience, market eligibility, and evidence available. The site currently has no verified production analytics or Search Console performance to support traffic, click, lead, conversion, or return-on-spend claims. Pricing now would be a hypothesis, not a validated market rate.

## Proposed inventory architecture

These are internal package concepts only. Final prices, duration, placement, renewal, tax, cancellation/refund, reporting, and link treatment require owner approval and legal review.

| Package concept | Possible inventory | Non-negotiable limits |
|---|---|---|
| Standard paid listing | Clearly labelled operator entry with a short factual description, official link, market scope, and last-reviewed date | No rating boost, licence endorsement, “safe” badge, or guaranteed traffic |
| Featured sponsored placement | A separate, visibly labelled sponsored position in a defined area for a defined term | Cannot override eligibility checks or masquerade as editorial ranking; inventory and position must be described precisely |
| Annual listing | A fixed-term standard placement with scheduled fact-check/update checkpoints | Renewal and cancellation terms must be explicit; annual payment does not buy favourable editorial treatment |

Do not create “verified”, “trusted”, “best”, or “top-ranked” paid tiers. A paid customer must pass the same publication and market checks as a non-paying profile.

## Validation workflow

1. **Establish evidence first.** Collect dated Search Console and analytics exports after launch, with geography, device, page/query context, consent configuration, and limitations documented. Do not count forecast impressions as actual audience.
2. **Define the sellable inventory.** Identify the exact page and placement, visible sponsorship label, term, outbound-link policy, update schedule, reporting (if any), and removal/correction procedure.
3. **Build a qualified research sample.** Start with 15–20 brands that have a verified official domain, legal entity, current market permission, a legitimate official business contact, and a plausible reason to test awareness. Do not count affiliate-program presence as proof of paid-listing budget.
4. **Ask neutral discovery questions only after outreach is approved.** Ask how the brand evaluates directories/media, which markets it can legally target, what evidence procurement requires, whether it buys fixed-term placements, and what would make a small pilot useful. Do not claim BeInCasinos already has traffic.
5. **Separate evidence types.** Record (a) no response, (b) response but no interest, (c) interested in a proposal, (d) requested proposal, (e) negotiation, (f) signed, and (g) paid. Only a direct, documented response can support buyer-interest status; only recorded payment and a signed agreement support “won”.
6. **Test willingness to pay transparently.** Once the audience and inventory are real, offer a clearly scoped pilot proposal to comparable prospects. Record quoted price, response, objections, market, term, and outcome. Do not invent urgency, exclusivity, audience size, or competing offers.
7. **Set prices after evidence.** Review actual delivery cost, legal/compliance workload, measured audience, buyer feedback, and comparable inventory. Approve a written rate card only after the owner signs off.
8. **Review quarterly.** If no qualified buyer sees a defensible value proposition, improve the resource and audience rather than selling an unsupported promise.

## Required buyer qualification record

- Brand, exact official domain, and legal entity
- Target market and product permission source
- Official decision-maker/contact source and date checked
- Commercial signal and source URL
- Buyer stage and date of last interaction
- What the prospect said, recorded accurately (no inferred intent)
- Inventory requested and reason
- Price quoted, currency, term, taxes, and conditions
- Objections and next step
- Agreement, invoice/payment evidence, sponsorship disclosure, privacy and market review status

## Stop conditions

Do not quote or accept a paid placement when the operator/domain/entity cannot be verified, market eligibility is uncertain, a placement would target prohibited online-casino offers, a privacy or disclosure gate is open, or the buyer expects payment to influence factual verification or editorial conclusions.
