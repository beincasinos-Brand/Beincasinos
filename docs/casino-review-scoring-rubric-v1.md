# BeInCasinos casino review scoring rubric v1

Status: internal editorial standard. Do not display a score for a brand until evidence and second-editor gates are complete.

## Principles
- Score the product for a specific market and date; never transfer a score between countries or legal entities.
- A licence is an eligibility gate, not a bonus score. Materially unresolved legality or identity means HOLD, not a low or high score.
- Every score must be reproducible from recorded evidence. Separate official facts, observed product behaviour, independent consumer evidence, and editorial judgement.
- Do not invent hands-on tests, withdrawal outcomes, customer ratings, bonus values, or game counts.
- Disclose affiliate and commercial relationships. Payment for a listing must never change a score.

## Weighted 100-point model

| Dimension | Weight | What is evaluated |
|---|---:|---|
| Licence, identity and market fit | 20 | Exact domain/operator/register match; current product permission; target-market eligibility |
| Terms and bonus transparency | 15 | Eligibility, wagering, max stake, expiry, game contribution and withdrawal restrictions |
| Payments and withdrawals | 15 | Published methods, limits, fees, verification and processing language |
| Player protection | 15 | Age controls, limits, self-exclusion, reality checks and help resources |
| Complaints and customer support | 10 | Contact routes, escalation, ADR and response expectations |
| Games and product coverage | 10 | Verifiable game categories, supplier info and catalogue usability |
| Usability and accessibility | 10 | Mobile navigation, readability, keyboard/accessibility checks and observations |
| Evidence freshness and disclosure | 5 | Dated primary sources, limitations, affiliate disclosure and correction route |

## Calculation
For each dimension assign 0–5 only when evidence exists: 0 critical deficiency; 1 very weak; 2 below expectations; 3 adequate/mixed; 4 strong; 5 excellent and well evidenced. Weighted points = (dimension rating / 5) × dimension weight. Sum for a total out of 100. If a dimension lacks evidence, mark Not assessed; do not silently score it as zero or five. Do not publish a total until all required dimensions are assessed.

## Mandatory gates override score
Set HOLD — not rated if consumer domain/operator mismatch, current authorisation or market legality is unconfirmed, casino product permission is unclear, material terms cannot be checked, consumer-protection claims are unsubstantiated, or evidence is stale/contradictory. A high score cannot override a mandatory gate.

For each dimension record source URL, source type, market, date checked in UTC, exact claim supported, test method if applicable, limitations, reviewer and second-editor status.

Never fabricate user-star ratings. If collecting user reviews, disclose method, sample size, date range, moderation and whether reviewers are verified. Keep user ratings separate from editorial scores.

Listing fees, affiliate commissions and partnerships are recorded separately from editorial scoring. Label paid placements clearly. Paid status must not change ranking, score, findings or drawbacks.
