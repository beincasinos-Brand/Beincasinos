# Great Britain research batch 17 — William Hill domain/operator mapping

**Checked:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** official identity and jurisdiction-split lead; not a consumer review or endorsement.

## Primary evidence

- UKGC register search: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/results?business-search=William+Hill
- WHG (International) Limited licence summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39225
- WHG (International) Limited domain record: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/39225
- William Hill current terms and conditions: https://www.williamhill.com/terms-and-conditions
- William Hill Support regulatory disclosure: https://help.williamhill.com/hc/en-us

The UKGC search result associates **www.williamhill.com** with **WHG (International) Limited**, account **39225**, trading as “william hill online.” The brand must not be mapped to **William Hill Organization Limited** (account 2752) simply because that legal name resembles the brand: its UKGC record shows no domain names recorded and its listed remote activities do not establish the consumer casino permission for this domain.

William Hill's own terms state a market split: for Great Britain, Northern Ireland, Jersey and Gibraltar the site is operated by WHG (International) Limited; for customers outside those territories (with separate Ireland treatment), it says the site is operated by **William Hill Global PLC**, a Malta-registered company, and cites MGA licence **MGA/CRP/121/2006-05**. This makes market-specific operator and eligibility checks essential. A GB licence does not establish eligibility in Morocco.
