## Enforcement context requiring fair, precise attribution

The UKGC's 17 December 2025 decision records a review following a compliance assessment of four Paddy Power Betfair licensees, including Betfair Casino Limited. The Commission said the four licensees failed to comply with remote customer-interaction social-responsibility code provisions (SRCP 3.4.3, paragraphs 1, 4, 7, 8 and 11) and announced a combined **£2,000,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty**. Do not attribute the entire combined payment to Betfair Casino Limited alone or describe the payment as a standalone fine for this entity.

The regulatory-action page shows licence number 039435-R-319329-007, while the current summary page shows 039435-R-319329-009. Record this discrepancy and verify the current register again immediately before publication; do not silently normalize the licence identifier.
