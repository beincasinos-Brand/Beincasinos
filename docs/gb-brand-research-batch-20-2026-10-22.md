The UKGC lists **Blue Star Planet Limited**, account **43173**, with **Casino Remote** and remote betting permissions recorded as Active; its trading-name register lists **10bet** as Active. 10bet's own licence disclosure says the UK site is operated by Blue Star Planet Limited and cites UKGC licence 43173. The precise current domain mapping should still be captured from the regulator's domain page before publication; do not infer the relationship from a marketing page alone.

## Enforcement context

The UKGC announced on **16 February 2023** that Blue Star Planet Limited, trading as 10Bet and operating 10bet.co.uk, would pay **£620,000** following an investigation that found social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. This is a dated finding about the named licensee and period examined, not proof of present-day behaviour or a rating for every product. Review the original decision and any later actions before publication.
