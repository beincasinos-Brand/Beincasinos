The domain register lists `betano.co.uk` as **White Label**, not as a plain “Active” domain. Preserve that exact register label; do not silently rewrite it. ## Regulatory record

The current UKGC sanctions page for account 39576 states that no regulatory actions have been recorded for this business. The Commission states that sanctions and settlements remain on its public register for three years from publication, so the current listing should not be interpreted as a guarantee that no historic action has ever existed. The historic £2m regulatory action announced in 2022 related to BV Gaming Limited trading as **BetVictor** and included fairness, social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. It is relevant operator-group context, but must not be presented as a separate Betano-specific finding without evidence tying the findings to Betano.

## Consumer evidence not yet independently verified

The operator's terms describe licence scope and identity/occupation checks, including possible document requests. The existence of those written terms does not verify the speed or fairness of real withdrawals or the actual user experience. This dossier has not independently tested a promotion, deposit, withdrawal, complaint, account restriction, safer-gambling tool or customer support interaction.

Before publication, capture dated copies of:
- Full terms, privacy notice, promotions and all offer-specific exclusions.
- Deposit and withdrawal methods, limits, fees, verification triggers and published timeframes.
- Complaint process and applicable ADR route.
- Safer-gambling controls, self-exclusion and account-limit usability.
- Independent customer feedback, clearly separated from verified facts.
