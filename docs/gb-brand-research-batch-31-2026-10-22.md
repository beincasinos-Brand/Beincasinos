## Regulatory history — important context
The UKGC published a settlement for Skill On Net Limited dated 23 May 2023. The regulator identified failings in anti-money-laundering and safer-gambling/customer-interaction processes during January 2021–December 2022. The published outcome included a £305,150 payment in lieu of a financial penalty, including £105,650 divestment, an independent third-party audit requirement and other actions. Attribute this to Skill On Net Limited and the regulator's specified historical period; do not imply the amount was a PlayOJO-only penalty or that it describes current performance without further evidence.
