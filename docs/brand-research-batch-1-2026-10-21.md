## Publication gates still open for all five

- Exact consumer domain, legal operator, product and named market matched to a current primary regulator record.
- Current terms and conditions, bonus terms, payments/withdrawals, fees, limits and account restrictions recorded with source URLs and check dates.
- Complaints and escalation routes and safer-gambling controls verified from official sources.
- Current regulatory actions checked.
- Mobile/desktop/keyboard accessibility and core usability tested without account creation or gambling activity.
- All material claims attributed or independently evidenced; no invented ratings, payout-speed claims, safety claims or rankings.
- Publisher-wide legal, privacy, contact and editorial launch gates cleared.
- Commercial status recorded separately. A licence, product launch or supplier partnership is not proof that the brand wants to pay for a listing.

## Rate-limit and access-control rule

Research in small, sequential batches. Honour published limits and retry guidance; stop on 429/throttling or bot-protection responses. Do not rotate identities, proxy around limits, or otherwise evade controls. A blocked extraction remains an unresolved evidence item, never a reason to guess.
