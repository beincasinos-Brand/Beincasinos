# Corrected Tier-1 market scope — 25 October 2026

**Scope authority:** This note records the owner's latest target-market decision and supersedes older seven-market lists in earlier strategy notes. It does not establish that any gambling product is lawful, licensed, advertisable, or affiliate-eligible in a market.

## Current target set

The current 12-market audience research set is:

| Code | Market | Mandatory scope control |
|---|---|---|
| AU | Australia | Online-casino services covered by the Interactive Gambling Act are prohibited. Do not promote offshore online-casino offers as available to Australian readers. Any future content must be legally reviewed and must not facilitate prohibited services. |
| CA | Canada | Province- and product-specific checks. Do not infer nationwide online-casino authorisation from one province. Ontario requires its own operator/site and iGaming Ontario/AGCO checks. |
| GB | Great Britain | Verify the exact legal entity, domain, active UK Gambling Commission licence and relevant remote-casino permission; separately review advertising and affiliate rules. |
| US | United States | State- and product-specific only. Record each state, regulator, exact operator, domain and permitted product; never make a blanket nationwide legality claim. |
| CH | Switzerland | Verify current Swiss authorisation and exact domain against the competent official lists; a foreign licence alone is insufficient. |
| DE | Germany | Verify the exact entity, domain and product against the current GGL whitelist and any relevant state/product scope. Do not infer table/live-casino permission from virtual-slot or sports-betting permission. |
| NL | Netherlands | Match the exact operator, site and product to the current Ksa licence register; review local advertising and duty-of-care rules. |
| SE | Sweden | Verify the exact licensee, domain and licence category against Spelinspektionen; do not treat affiliated entities as interchangeable. |
| ES | Spain | Verify operator, domain and product against the current DGOJ register and applicable advertising rules. |
| IT | Italy | Verify the concessionaire, domain and product with the competent ADM sources; do not infer permission from a brand name alone. |
| FR | France | Restricted for online-casino promotions. Do not promote offshore online casinos as legal/available in France; distinguish product categories specifically authorised by ANJ. |
| BE | Belgium | Verify the exact operator, domain, licence class and current Belgian Gaming Commission status; check product and advertising restrictions separately. |

## Research and publication rules

1. This is an audience/research scope, not a list of approved markets for every gambling product.
2. Keep evidence and decisions separate for operator identity, domain, licence/product scope, audience availability, advertising permission, affiliate permission, bonus terms, payments, complaints, safer gambling, privacy, and image rights.
3. Use dated primary-source evidence. Record the source URL, date checked in UTC, exact entity/domain, product scope, reviewer and unresolved issues. Never fill unknown fields by inference.
4. A candidate, regulator listing, research dossier or draft page is not a completed consumer review. Do not invent firsthand testing, scores, ratings, bonus terms, payment performance, safety claims or commercial willingness to pay.
5. Keep unresolved candidates noindex and out of the public sitemap. Only publish after market-specific compliance review, editorial sign-off, image-use rights, accessibility/technical QA and release approval.
6. Do not merge old “ten roadmap markets” trackers into this 12-market scope without an explicit row-by-row reconciliation. Earlier tracker entries for Denmark (DK) and Schleswig-Holstein as a separate market are legacy planning records, not additions to this current target list. US state dossiers remain separate records under US.
7. Australia and France are included in the owner's audience-scope decision but remain restricted for online-casino promotion; inclusion must not be interpreted as permission to advertise prohibited/offshore offers.
