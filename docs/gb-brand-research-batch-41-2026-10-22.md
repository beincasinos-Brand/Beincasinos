# GB brand research batch 41 — Betty / Betty Gaming (Malta) Limited
**Checked:** 22 October 2026 (UTC)  
**Status:** internal evidence lead only; not publication approval.

## Identity and permitted scope
The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) public register lists Betty Gaming (Malta) Limited, account **100695**, with active Remote Casino and Linked Gambling Software permissions from 1 April 2026. The official domain register lists **betty.com** and **playbetty.co.uk** as active domains for this licensee. The live consumer site identifies Betty as managed by Betty Gaming (Malta) Limited and repeats account 100695. This is a strong initial operator/domain match for the Great Britain market, not a complete review or permission to promote the service in another jurisdiction.

## Consumer-facing offer claims observed
The live homepage advertises 50 free spins after a minimum first deposit of £20, states the free spins are valid for 48 hours and says no wagering is required on the offer. It also advertises instant withdrawals and says over 90% of withdrawals are processed immediately. These are operator marketing claims, not independently tested outcomes. The complete offer terms, eligibility restrictions, game list, minimum withdrawal conditions, verification delays and exceptions must be checked and archived before a review quotes them as settled terms.

## Safer gambling and support signals
The site describes deposit limits, net-deposit limits, timeouts and self-exclusion, and promotes live chat support. No account was created and no support interaction or safer-gambling control was tested. The exact complaints escalation route, ADR provider, support hours, customer-fund protection wording and privacy/data-controller terms remain to be verified from the current legal pages.
