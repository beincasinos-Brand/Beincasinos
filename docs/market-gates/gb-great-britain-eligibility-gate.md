# GB market gate — Great Britain

**Status:** internal research protocol, not legal advice.  
**Last protocol review:** 25 October 2026.  
**Public-market status:** not approved by this document alone.

## Authority and starting point

- **Primary authority:** UK Gambling Commission (UKGC)
- **Official starting URL:** https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses
- **Market-specific rule:** Verify the contracting operator, current remote casino permissions, trading name and domain against the UKGC register. Great Britain is distinct from Northern Ireland; a licence entry alone does not validate every affiliate claim or current bonus.

The official starting URL is a research entry point, not evidence that any particular casino brand is licensed. For each brand, navigate to the regulator's current register, authorised-domain list, enforcement/warning list and relevant legislation or guidance. Save the exact record URL and the date checked.

## Mandatory brand-level gate

For every proposed brand/domain, record:

1. Exact consumer-facing domain and any redirect/canonical domain.
2. Contracting legal entity and registered company details from current terms.
3. Regulator record URL, licence/registration number, status and permitted product.
4. Official regulator evidence that links the exact domain/trading name to that entity, where available.
5. Geographic restrictions and any exclusions in the operator's terms.
6. Enforcement actions, public warnings, suspensions or domain blocks relevant to the entity/domain.
7. Current bonus terms, if any: eligibility, wagering, expiry, max bet, game contribution, withdrawal caps and excluded payment methods.
8. Deposit/withdrawal rules, verification requirements, complaint escalation and safer-gambling controls.
9. Asset rights, affiliate relationship, commercial disclosure and local advertising compliance.
10. Research date (UTC), source snapshot/notes, reviewer and second-editor sign-off.

## Decision rule

No GB-eligible claim until the exact operator and relevant remote casino activity are confirmed in the current UKGC register, the domain relationship is evidenced, and UK advertising/consumer-protection rules are reviewed.

Decision values: PASS only with current primary-source evidence; HOLD when evidence is incomplete or conflicting; EXCLUDE when the market/product is not permitted or the brand fails the required gate. Never convert missing evidence into a positive assumption.

## Review and score rule

- No public rating while identity, market permission or material consumer terms are unresolved.
- Do not claim hands-on testing unless it was actually performed and recorded.
- A licence in another market does not transfer to Great Britain.
- Separate online casino from sports betting, poker, bingo, lotteries and land-based gambling.
- Keep affiliate compensation separate from editorial scoring and disclose commercial relationships.
- If market eligibility fails, do not create a country-targeted casino review page or market-specific bonus claim.
