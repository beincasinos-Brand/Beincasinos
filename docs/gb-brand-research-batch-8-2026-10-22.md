## 2. Editorial warning: competitor rankings are not verification evidence

Search-result pages published in October 2026 market casino rankings and claims about hands-on testing, game counts, payout speed, and bonus terms. These can inform SERP intent and page-structure analysis, but rankings, affiliate copy and unsupported “tested” language are not primary evidence for BeInCasinos.

For every candidate, independently check:
- exact official domain, consumer brand, legal operator and active UKGC remote-casino permission;
- current terms and conditions, including bonus eligibility, wagering, expiry, withdrawal limits, fees and verification;
- payment methods and published processing times without promising a user's actual payout time;
- support, complaints/ADR route, self-exclusion and safer-gambling controls;
- dated primary-source citations and a second editorial check.

Do not copy competitors' scores, wording, test claims or commercial rankings. No review should claim real-money deposits, withdrawal timing, support contact, or hands-on testing unless the team actually performed and recorded it.

## 3. Regulatory scope and current-law checks

A UKGC permission only establishes the relevant Great Britain permission and product scope; it does not establish legal availability in Morocco or any other country. Before publishing or promoting a page for another market, independently review that market's licensing and advertising/affiliate rules.

Do not repeat competitor snippets about a new bonus or wagering rule as legal fact without locating the relevant current UKGC rule or statutory source and checking its effective date and scope.
