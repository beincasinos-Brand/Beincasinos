## Required check for every GB candidate

1. Search the [official UKGC public register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses) by legal operator, trading name, and exact domain.
2. Match active remote Casino permission—not just sports betting, bingo, software, or a different operator's licence.
3. Review the register's regulatory-action information and relevant published statements.
4. Confirm the exact domain and brand relationship using an official operator source.
5. Verify the claimed launch date from a dated primary source; a licence start date is not a launch date.
6. Find a public, official commercial contact route; do not guess email addresses.
7. Keep willingness to pay as **Unconfirmed** until a real response or explicit published paid-media offer supports a different status.

The UKGC says its register includes activity, domain names, trading names, licence status, and regulatory actions; it also notes that declared domain/trading-name data is provided by licensees and may not always be accurate. See [UKGC register guidance](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses).
