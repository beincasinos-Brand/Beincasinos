## Evidence standard
The UKGC register is the authoritative starting point for account identity, licence activity and declared domains, but the Commission explicitly warns that trading names and domains are supplied by licensees and may be inaccurate. A register/index match therefore does not prove a domain is live, that it currently offers casino games, or that its legal terms identify the same operator. Before approval, inspect the live site and dated terms, map the contracting entity and relevant active remote-casino activity, check domain status, deduplicate brand/domain/operator relationships, and collect dated product evidence. See the [UKGC business register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/) and its [remote casino operating licence explanation](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/licences-and-fees/remote-casino-operating-licence).

## Deduplication and approval rules
1. One consumer brand counts once even if it has multiple domains.
2. One operator can operate multiple distinct brands; operator count is not brand count.
3. A domain in a register or third-party directory is a lead, not proof of a live, independently reviewable casino.
4. Do not write first-person play/testing claims, bonus figures, payout speed, payment availability, ratings or scores without dated, reproducible evidence.
5. Do not publish or mark a review approved until legal identity, licence scope, live casino product, terms, payments/withdrawals, KYC, customer support, responsible-gambling controls, regulatory history and editorial disclosure are documented in a brand-specific evidence dossier.
6. No production branch, Cloudflare Pages deployment, DNS, or production configuration changes are part of this research batch.
