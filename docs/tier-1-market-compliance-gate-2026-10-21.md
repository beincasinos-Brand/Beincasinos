# Tier-1 market compliance gate — affiliate and paid-listing model

**Status:** Internal pre-launch control, researched 21 October 2026. Not legal advice. This is a screening matrix, not a legal opinion or a complete statement of law. A regulator page is a starting point; check current legislation, licences, court decisions, commencement dates, ad formats, and the exact product before publishing or selling inventory.

## Release rule

Do not launch a market or place a paid/affiliate link merely because a brand has a licence somewhere. Every exact operator + domain + product + audience country must pass current primary-source checks and a documented legal/editorial review. If a rule, licence, product scope, or commercial payment model is unclear, hold the market/product and do not send traffic.

## Publisher, privacy and disclosure controls

- Treat the FTC's clear-and-conspicuous affiliate disclosure guidance as a minimum US-oriented baseline, not as a substitute for stricter local requirements. Example wording for review: “BeInCasinos may earn a commission if you use certain links. Commercial relationships do not determine our review scores or conclusions.” Place disclosures where a reader sees them before or with the recommendation/link, and localise wording as needed.
- Before collecting personal information, enabling analytics, using advertising pixels, processing contact forms, or sharing data with vendors, map the actual data flows and obtain jurisdiction-specific privacy advice. The privacy notice must describe what is really enabled, not hypothetical services.
- For a publisher operating from Morocco, obtain advice on applicable Moroccan Law 09-08 and CNDP formalities, including any required declarations/authorisations and international-transfer analysis. Do not assume a foreign host or foreign audience removes Moroccan obligations.
- Keep sponsored placements visibly labelled. Payment must not influence editorial eligibility, licence status, score, safety language or organic ranking.
- Do not describe research leads as approved operators, buyers, partners or paying customers. Do not claim traffic, impressions, conversion or revenue until supported by dated first-party evidence.
- Store a dated regulator record, exact URL/domain, product scope, market decision, reviewer, sources, and re-check date for every public operator profile and every commercial placement.

## Evidence record template

For each operator/product/market, record:

- Brand, legal entity, exact domain and product category
- Regulator/register URL, date checked and relevant licence/permission
- Official evidence that the domain/product is within scope
- Market advertising and affiliate restrictions checked
- Commission model (fixed fee / CPA / revenue share / hybrid) and specific legal approval
- Required age, audience, disclosure, targeting and responsible-gambling controls
- Privacy/data flow assessment and reviewer
- Decision: PASS / HOLD / BLOCK / RECHECK, with reason and next review date
