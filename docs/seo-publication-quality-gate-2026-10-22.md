# SEO and publication quality gate — 22 October 2026

Internal checklist for the BeInCasinos editorial pipeline. This document is not a public-facing page and does not approve any candidate.

## Candidate status must be evidence-based

For every candidate, keep separate fields for:
- consumer-facing brand and exact official domain;
- legal entity responsible for the domain;
- primary regulator record and current permission for the exact product and market;
- current terms and key restrictions;
- payment methods and published processing conditions;
- support and complaints route;
- safer-use and account-control features;
- dated source URLs, reviewer, second-check status, unresolved questions and final disposition.

Use HOLD whenever identity, permission, product scope or a material claim is uncertain. Do not treat a company group, supplier, regulator-index listing, competitor ranking, or affiliate listing as a verified consumer brand.

## Before a page can be published

- Verify every factual claim against an appropriate primary source.
- Separate current facts from historic regulatory events.
- Include a checked-on date and direct citations.
- Disclose commercial relationships clearly.
- Include contact/corrections and relevant safer-use information.
- Avoid copied ranking language, fabricated scores, unperformed testing claims, unsupported payout claims, and templated near-duplicate pages.
- Have a second editor independently check the evidence and scope.
- Confirm that the domain is technically reachable and canonical, and that legal, privacy, advertising and market eligibility checks have passed.

## SEO implementation checklist

- One useful page per distinct user intent; no mass-generated doorway pages.
- Clear title, H1, concise description, canonical URL, logical internal links and breadcrumb structure.
- Valid sitemap contains only canonical, indexable, approved URLs; no internal research notes.
- Robots directives, canonical tags, redirects and HTTP status agree.
- Structured data reflects visible content and does not fabricate ratings or review experience.
- Mobile layout, performance, accessibility, consent/privacy and broken-link checks are complete.
- Measure Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, position, indexed pages, query coverage and country coverage; annotate releases and compare over time.

## Delivery guardrails

Do not run unnecessary CI or deploy workflows when the repository's included Actions quota is close to exhaustion. Do not claim a route, deployment or indexing result has been verified without a fresh successful check. Do not expose credentials in chat or source control; resolve authentication failures through the platform's secret manager.

## Target accounting

Maintain separate counts for (1) candidates discovered, (2) brands identity-verified, (3) candidates fully reviewed, (4) reviews passing second editorial review, and (5) pages published and technically checked. Never collapse these into one inflated total. A traffic ambition is not a forecast or guarantee.
