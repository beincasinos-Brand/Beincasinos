## Required completion checklist per brand

1. Confirm live consumer domain, legal entity in terms/footer, and exact regulator domain/trading-name match.
2. Read current regulatory actions and enforcement outcomes; record date and impact neutrally.
3. Capture current jurisdiction-specific eligibility and product permissions.
4. Document current terms, bonus wagering conditions if referenced, payment methods, withdrawal stages/limits, fees, complaints route, privacy/contact and safer-gambling tools.
5. Separate observed facts from untested claims; do not invent deposits, withdrawals, support chats, or gameplay.
6. Independent second-editor review; only then may the row leave HOLD.
