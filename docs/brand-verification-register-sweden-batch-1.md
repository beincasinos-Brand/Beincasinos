## Legal/product scope

Spelinspektionen states that a licence for *commercial online gambling* (Chapter 7 of the Swedish Gambling Act) may cover casino games, online bingo and computer-simulated gaming machines. A separate betting licence covers wagering. A betting licence alone is not proof of casino permission. Use the regulator's active licence and URL records, then confirm the specific casino product and domain before treating any brand as eligible. The regulator also states that operators targeting Sweden without a Swedish licence are unlawful, and licensed operators must provide links to Spelpaus, deposit limits and self-tests.
