## Mandatory review gates

- The register entry establishes a licence category, not a complete review or proof that every game, bonus, payment method or promotion is currently available.
- Recheck the live licence register for the exact domain, licensee, validity period and commercial-online scope immediately before publication.
- Capture current terms/version, bonus terms if discussed, payment/withdrawal rules, KYC, privacy/cookies, safer-gambling tools, complaints, enforcement history and reproducible user-experience observations.
- Check shared ownership and corporate group relationships; do not imply brands are independent if they share a group.
- No unverified bonus, payout-speed, game-count, ranking or hands-on-testing claims.
- Require independent second-editor review and legal/editorial sign-off. Keep each candidate HOLD until every hard gate is complete.
