## Source-backed findings

### 1. Operator identity and market scope

The operator's terms identify Grand Casino Baden AG as the operator of jackpots.ch and give its Swiss address and FGB supervision/licence number. The same terms state that the offer is for the Swiss market and that account holders must be of legal age and have domicile or habitual residence in Switzerland, subject to the terms' detailed eligibility conditions.

The FGB's official online-gambling guidance says that a licence extension alone is not the full story: individual games also require FGB permits. Therefore, the profile must not imply that the platform's general licence number proves every game or feature has been independently checked in this audit.

### 2. Terms and withdrawals — material details to communicate fairly

The operator's GTC state that payouts may only be made to a Swiss bank or postal account in the customer's own name; payouts to credit cards or Revolut accounts are excluded. The operator says it may request identification or other details for a payout and may refuse a transaction where fraud is suspected or the transaction violates law. Its provisional-account terms state that identity documentation is required within 30 days and that restrictions apply before the account is fully opened.

The separate payments page says payouts go to a Swiss bank account in the customer's full name, says payments are free of charge, and gives an estimated 3–4 working days until a payment is released.

**Unresolved wording discrepancy:** the GTC mention a Swiss bank *or postal* account, while the payments page says Swiss bank account. The 3–4 working days is an operator-published estimate for release, not an independently measured end-to-end settlement time. Resolve the destination wording and confirm the current version/date of both pages before publishing any summary.

### 3. Safer-gambling controls and support

The GTC describe account-level daily net-loss limits, options for additional daily/weekly/monthly deposit or loss limits, a self-test, temporary game breaks, voluntary exclusion, and legally required exclusion in specified circumstances. The responsible-gaming page links to information about advice centres and exclusion tools.

This audit only reviewed published text; it did not test the controls inside a logged-in account, validate the response time of support, or verify every linked counselling contact. Before publication, check that the relevant links work and describe only controls that are currently available to the intended user.
