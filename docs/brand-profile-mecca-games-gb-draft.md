The official terms page shows an effective date of **16 December 2025**. The current welcome offer advertises a £20 bonus and 50 free spins after a new player selects the offer and makes a first deposit of at least £10. The published terms state:
- the offer is for new customers aged 18+ registering for the first time;
- Paysafecard deposits do not qualify;
- the £20 bonus is restricted to selected games;
- free spins and their winnings are playable on Kong 3 Even Bigger Bonus only;
- claiming the offer excludes eligibility for other Mecca Games welcome offers.

The homepage advertises 10x wagering and a £50 maximum win for the bonus and free-spin elements. Capture the full offer terms to verify wagering bases, expiry, maximum conversion and withdrawal restrictions before publication.
