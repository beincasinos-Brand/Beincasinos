## 2. Material regulatory history — operator-level, not a product test

On **20 November 2025**, the UKGC announced that Videoslots Limited would pay **£650,000**, receive a warning and complete an independent third-party audit after findings relating to anti-money-laundering controls and customer interaction/social responsibility. The regulator described shortcomings in the operator's monitoring of potential harm, the application of deposit limits, and AML/customer-due-diligence processes. Attribute these findings to the licensee and the regulator's review period; do not imply that every current control remains unchanged or that this dossier has independently tested remediation.

The UKGC also announced a **£2 million settlement on 15 June 2023** concerning earlier social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures at Videoslots Limited. These are material operator-level facts for a fair review and must not be omitted simply because the 2025 action is newer. Neither action alone proves the present state of every Mr Vegas product or current user outcome.
