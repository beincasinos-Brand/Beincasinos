## Editorial taxonomy

A full casino research site may cover these distinct verticals, with separate legal and evidence checks:

1. Online casino table games: roulette, blackjack, baccarat, craps and variants.
2. Online slots and video slots.
3. Live-dealer casino games.
4. Online poker and casino poker variants.
5. Bingo and online lottery products (only where relevant; do not automatically label them casino).
6. Land-based casinos and destination guides.
7. Sports betting and horse-race betting (separate vertical, not casino).
8. Sweepstakes/social casino and free-play products (label clearly; distinguish redeemable prizes, virtual currency and real-money gambling).
9. Gambling platforms, software studios and game catalogues (B2B/product coverage, not casino operator reviews).
10. Player protection, self-exclusion, licensing explainers, complaints and scam warnings.

Do not merge verticals into a single “casino licence” assumption. Every review must state what product was checked, where it is available, and the legal entity and jurisdiction.

## Candidate-counting rule for the 100 target

The original arithmetic of 10 markets × 10 brands is a target, not permission to manufacture legal casino operators. A market may count a brand review only if the exact product is lawful for the named audience and all verification gates are complete. In markets where online casino games are prohibited or only a narrower vertical is authorised, either:
- publish a clearly labelled legal/consumer-safety guide, or
- redefine that market's ten pieces as mixed-format coverage (e.g. licensed land-based venues, poker, betting, lottery or social casino) and count them as articles, **not** as ten verified online-casino brands.

Do not claim 100 “verified online casinos” if the project actually contains mixed verticals. Use separate KPIs: (A) verified online-casino operators, (B) other regulated gambling brands/products, (C) guides/venues, and (D) social/sweepstakes casino products.

## Mandatory brand-review evidence

For every real-money operator/product: current official regulator record, exact legal entity and domain, product permissions, current terms, bonus conditions, deposits/withdrawals, privacy/cookies, responsible-gambling tools, support/complaints, recent enforcement, permitted reproducible UX observations, affiliate disclosure, independent second reviewer and legal/editorial sign-off. Mark missing evidence HOLD; never invent payment or gameplay tests.
