## 1. Identity, domain and licence

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) register lists Blue Star Planet Limited under account 43173. Its licence summary lists active remote Casino, General Betting Standard — Real Event, and General Betting Standard — Virtual Event activities. The register lists the trading name **10bet** as active and `www.10bet.co.uk` as an active domain. These records support a GB-specific identity match; they do not establish permission in other jurisdictions.

## 2. Regulatory history and fair context

On 16 February 2023, the UKGC announced that Blue Star Planet Limited, trading as 10Bet, would pay **£620,000** following an investigation into social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. This is material evidence and must be disclosed clearly in any future review; it should not be omitted merely because it is historical.

Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/blue-star-planet-faces-gbp620-000-regulatory-action

The current UKGC register's sanctions page for account 43173 displays “No regulatory actions have been recorded for this business.” This is not consistent with the still-accessible 2023 announcement. The public register notes that actions remain on it for three years from publication, so the 2023 action may have aged out of the sanctions listing. Do **not** interpret the current sanctions page alone as proof that no historic action occurred.

Related primary source on individual management accountability:
- UKGC warning to former/individual management licence holders in relation to Blue Star Planet: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/221
- https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/222

These individual decisions should be described as individual sanctions and not mislabelled as new corporate penalties.
