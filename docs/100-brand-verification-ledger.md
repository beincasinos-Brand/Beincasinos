## Counting rules

- A brand is counted only once for a defined market and consumer domain; cross-market editions may be separate reviews only when the legal entity, terms, payments or game permissions materially differ and the editorial page clearly identifies the market.
- A regulator-listed candidate, research register entry, draft, or unreviewed public page is not an approved review.
- Multiple domains or sub-brands under one licensee must be deduplicated and justified.
- No brand is approved solely because a regulator listing exists. Casino-specific game scope and live consumer terms must also be checked.
- The second reviewer must be independent of the original evidence compilation.
- Keep research docs out of public deployment artifacts.
