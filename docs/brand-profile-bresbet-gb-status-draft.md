## Summary

BresBet is not eligible for a positive current-operator review based on the evidence currently available. The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) said on 28 August 2026 that it suspended BresBet Ltd's operating licence following enquiries into suspected social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failings. Its update says BresBet surrendered its operating licence on 4 September 2026. On 30 September, the Commission reported being aware that some BresBet customers were having difficulty withdrawing funds and said it would update the notice when more information became available. As of this research snapshot, the official notice does not provide a final resolution for BresBet customers.

This is a dated regulatory/consumer-status record, not a finding that every customer is owed money or that any individual claim is proven. The priority is to direct affected customers to current official guidance, not to present the brand as an active licensed casino.
