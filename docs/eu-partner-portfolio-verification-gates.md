## Mandatory approval checklist — every brand

- [ ] Publisher confirms the exact canonical domain and the intended country/territory in writing.
- [ ] Exact domain, legal operator, regulator, licence/permit number, and casino product permission match a current **primary regulator** source.
- [ ] Current terms, bonus terms, deposit/withdrawal conditions, payment agents, KYC triggers, fees/limits and account-closure rules reviewed and dated.
- [ ] Complaints route, ADR eligibility, customer support, privacy/data controller and safer-gambling/self-exclusion details checked.
- [ ] Relevant regulatory actions checked against the correct legal entity and described with dates and context.
- [ ] Any hands-on testing is genuinely performed and documented; otherwise label testing as not performed and do not imply personal experience.
- [ ] Commercial relationship and paid/affiliate links disclosed prominently; commercial status does not change findings or score.
- [ ] Original analysis and limitations written for that country; no copied competitor phrasing or unsupported claims.
- [ ] Independent second-editor review completed; all sources and evidence dates saved.
- [ ] Only after all gates pass: create a public profile, add it to the public sitemap, and include a clear corrections/contact route.

## Release controls

Do not deploy or request mass indexing as part of this research pass. The project constraint is to finish the verification set before one planned deployment. Keep internal evidence files excluded from the public build and verify that with the existing static-site checks when a combined release is eventually prepared. A passing build does not establish legal permission, publication readiness, or Google indexing.
