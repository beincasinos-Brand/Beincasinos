## Australia — critical market restriction

Australia must not be treated as a normal online-casino acquisition market. The ACMA's official guidance says online wagering services must be on its licensed interactive gambling providers register, but Australia's Interactive Gambling Act restricts online casino-style services to Australian customers. Do not build a consumer-facing list that implies offshore online casino brands are legal for Australian users. If pursuing Australia, separately research legally permitted gambling categories and obtain legal review first.

Official starting points:
- [ACMA — Check if a gambling operator is legal](https://www.acma.gov.au/check-if-gambling-operator-legal)
- [ACMA — Online gambling](https://www.acma.gov.au/online-gambling)

## Research rules for all markets

1. Keep the market, product type, exact domain, legal operator, regulator, licence scope, and date checked as separate fields.
2. Distinguish newly launched brands from a relaunch, rebrand, product expansion, supplier partnership, or newly granted licence.
3. A brand is not “approved” merely because it appears in press coverage or a commercial directory.
4. Record commercial status as **Unknown** until the operator provides a direct response, official rate card, media kit, or other reliable evidence.
5. No paid listing may override legal eligibility, consumer protection, responsible-gambling review, or editorial judgment.
6. Re-check every regulatory entry immediately before publishing and schedule periodic re-verification.
