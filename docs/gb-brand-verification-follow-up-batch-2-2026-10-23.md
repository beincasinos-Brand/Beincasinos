## Evidence boundary

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) public register shows licence-holder and product-licence information. Its own notice says trading names and domains are supplied by businesses and are not guaranteed accurate. An active Remote Casino licence is only an initial market-screening signal; it does not establish a complete review, current consumer terms, payment/withdrawal performance, or legality in any other country.

## Required work before any publication approval

For every row, record dated evidence for: exact official casino URL; current terms and legal contracting entity; deposit/payment methods and withdrawal rules/limits/timing/fees; bonus terms only if covered; complaint route and escalation; safer-gambling tools and restrictions; privacy/contact information; regulator enforcement history and outcomes; market-specific promotion rules; and a second-editor review of the draft and cited claims.

**Counting rule:** this batch adds six regulator-researched candidates, not six completed reviews. They remain on HOLD and do not increase the publication-approved count. Do not infer that an operator is available or authorised outside Great Britain. No outreach, affiliate activation, public listing, or endorsement is authorised by this research note.

## Source limitations

UKGC register notice: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/ — trading names and domain names are provided by the gambling business and the Commission cannot guarantee their accuracy. Cross-check the current consumer site, terms and licence holder before finalising each record.
