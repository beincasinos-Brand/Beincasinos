## 2. Regulatory history — material risk gate

The UKGC published an enforcement notice dated 22 October 2025 stating that Platinum Gaming Limited would pay a **£10,000,000 financial penalty**, receive a warning and undergo a third-party audit following AML and social-responsibility failings. The Commission described examples involving failure to identify and respond to markers of harm and deficiencies in AML risk assessment and due diligence. The regulator also said the operator cooperated.

- Primary notice: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/gbp10m-fine-for-online-operator-platinum-gaming-limited
- Account-specific actions register: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/actions/45322

The account-specific register records the action and its conditions. The UKGC news notice also says Platinum Gaming had faced enforcement in 2023, when it was fined £2.9m. Retrieve and review the original 2023 notice before describing its scope or comparing the two cases.

**Editorial rule:** report the regulator's findings and stated remediation/audit requirements in context. Do not infer that a licence makes a product risk-free, or that historical enforcement alone proves current performance.
