# Batch 17 — Australia market eligibility gate

Date checked: 2026-10-25
Status: COMPLIANCE GATE — DO NOT PUBLISH AU-ELIGIBLE ONLINE CASINO REVIEWS OR PROMOTIONS.

## Official rule
The Australian Communications and Media Authority says online casinos are a prohibited online gambling service under the Interactive Gambling Act 2001; prohibited services must not be advertised in Australia. See https://www.acma.gov.au/interactivegambling.

ACMA's current investigations page lists providers it found to be supplying prohibited interactive gambling services to Australian customers. The published list includes SG Casino, Legiano, Casea and Casinia among numerous others. See https://www.acma.gov.au/investigations-online-gambling-providers. This is a regulator enforcement finding and should be described precisely; do not turn it into a broad claim about a brand's status in other jurisdictions.

## Required BeInCasinos handling
1. Do not create Australian-facing online-casino bonus rankings, affiliate CTAs, or claims that offshore casino sites are legal for Australian players.
2. Mark any AU casino-targeted row as MARKET INELIGIBLE / HOLD; do not assign a score as if it were eligible.
3. If AU remains a target geography, require legal review to define whether an informational legal explainer can serve the market without promoting prohibited services. Keep it separate from casino acquisition pages.
4. Check current ACMA sources at publication time; enforcement lists and laws can change.
5. Do not infer that online sports wagering and online casino services have the same eligibility rules. ACMA maintains a register for licensed interactive wagering providers; online casinos remain a separate prohibited category under the cited guidance.
