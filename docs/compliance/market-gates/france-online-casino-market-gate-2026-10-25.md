# Batch 31 — France online-gambling market eligibility gate

Checked: 2026-10-25. Internal compliance research only. This is a market/product gate, not a brand review or legal opinion.

## Decision for BeInCasinos

**Do not publish or promote online-casino acquisition/review pages aimed at users in France.** The French regulator ANJ says online casino games are not authorised in France. The French government’s consumer guidance, updated 17 June 2026, likewise identifies online sports betting, horse-race betting and poker as the authorised online categories, with lottery games under the FDJ monopoly. An overseas licence is not a substitute for French authorisation.

## Primary sources

- ANJ — legal and illegal gambling offers: https://www.anj.fr/foire-aux-questions/offre-de-jeu-legale-et-illegale
- ANJ — authorised online gambling categories: https://anj.fr/foire-aux-questions/offre-de-jeu-legale-et-illegale/quels-sont-les-jeux-dargent-et-paris-sportifs
- French Ministry of Economy — online gambling rules, published 2026-06-17: https://www.economie.gouv.fr/particuliers/mes-droits-conso/eviter-les-arnaques/comment-sont-controles-les-jeux-et-paris-en-ligne
- French Senate record, government response published 2026-01-29, reiterating online casino games are prohibited: https://www.senat.fr/questions/base/2025/qSEQ250404232.html

## Scope and release gates

1. Block French-market online-casino bonus pages, rankings, sign-up CTAs, affiliate links and schema claiming French casino availability.
2. Do not infer that a .fr domain, French-language page, EU/EEA licence or operator presence in another country makes an online casino lawful in France.
3. If the business later considers French-language content, route it to legal review first and limit any potential scope to a category that is actually authorised in France (for example, ANJ-approved poker, sports betting or horse-race betting, where relevant). That would be a separate product and editorial scope, not a workaround for casino restrictions.
4. Do not create a French casino candidate list as if those sites were authorised. The lawful-category operator list must be sourced from the current ANJ register and checked by exact domain, operator and product.
5. Add a France exclusion to market eligibility checks and automated release review; re-check official sources before any future change because legislation can change.
6. Record the date and source for any later compliance decision. Require qualified legal review before publication.
