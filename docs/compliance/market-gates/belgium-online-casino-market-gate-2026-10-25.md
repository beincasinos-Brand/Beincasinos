## Primary regulator sources
- Belgian Gaming Commission: https://www.gamingcommission.be/en
- Official licensed operators information: https://www.gamingcommission.be/en/players
- Belgian legal framework and online gambling information: https://www.gamingcommission.be/en/online-gambling

Belgian online gambling is subject to national licensing and domain-specific controls. A foreign licence (for example, MGA, UKGC, or a licence from another EU state) does not by itself establish authorisation to offer online gambling to Belgian residents. A brand should not be presented as legal for Belgium unless its exact operator and website are matched to current Belgian Gaming Commission records and the permitted licence category.
