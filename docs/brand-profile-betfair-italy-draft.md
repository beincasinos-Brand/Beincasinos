## Executive finding

ADM's official register lists Betfair Italia S.r.l. and `www.betfair.it` under concession 16028. Betfair's own Italy regulatory disclosure says its Italian licence covers Exchange, Sportsbook and Casino. These sources support an initial market/identity finding, but the current casino product, terms and customer experience still need to be verified. The review remains HOLD.

### Withdrawals — operator-stated information, not independently tested

Betfair's payment FAQ says card withdrawals require that the card and same payment method have previously been used for at least one deposit. It describes card credits as arriving within 2–5 working days. This is operator-published guidance, not a verified test result; the currently available methods and timing must be rechecked after login/account eligibility is considered.

The operator says service costs are free and that it does not charge withdrawal commissions on the account service page. Third-party and banking terms should still be checked before stating that a transaction is entirely fee-free.

Sources:
- Payment methods and card withdrawal timing: https://www.betfair.it/aboutUs/metodipagamento/
- Terms / service costs: https://www.betfair.it/aboutUs/terminiecondizioni/notainformativa/

### Responsible gambling

Betfair's responsible-gambling page says temporary self-exclusion starts at seven days, takes effect immediately and cannot be revoked before the selected period ends. It also describes indefinite self-exclusion and a process for any later reactivation. Verify the complete current limits and national self-exclusion mechanics before summarising these features publicly.

Source: https://www.betfair.it/aboutUs/giocoresponsabile/
