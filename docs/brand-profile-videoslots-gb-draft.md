## 2. Material regulatory history

On **30 October 2025**, the UKGC imposed a **£650,000 financial penalty**, a warning, and an additional licence condition following findings concerning AML/CTF controls and customer interaction during October 2023–February 2024. The Commission said the operator cooperated and took immediate steps to remedy the identified issues. The decision required a third-party audit of the implementation of AML and safer-gambling policies, procedures, and controls.

The Commission's published examples included:
- monthly deposit-limit logic that did not include the customer's initial deposit, allowing losses to exceed the apparent monthly limit;
- insufficient interaction with customers showing indicators of potential gambling harm;
- weaknesses in AML risk scoring, source-of-funds escalation, record keeping, and monitoring of complex deposit/withdrawal activity.

Editorial treatment: describe the regulator's findings accurately and in context. Do not imply that these historical findings prove current controls are ineffective; independently verify the current remediation and any audit outcome before drawing a present-day conclusion.
