## 2. Regulatory settlement — 20 August 2026

The UKGC concluded its review with a **£609,104 payment in lieu of a financial penalty**, Commission costs and a public statement. The regulator identified AML/CTF and social-responsibility failures, including failures relating to remote customer interaction and financial-vulnerability checks.

The regulator's published examples included:
- a manual process that allowed customers aged 18–24 to exceed deposit limits set for that potentially vulnerable group;
- risk indicators such as high deposits, short high-velocity sessions, rising stakes, betting volume and turnover not always being captured and escalated promptly;
- a customer placing approximately 4,800 bets in one day and 7,000 the following day without being flagged at the time;
- a customer staking more than £215,000 in a day after a large win before the activity was identified by a report the following day;
- financial vulnerability checks not being completed for every customer who met the relevant threshold;
- source-of-funds and suspicious-activity reporting controls that were not sufficiently effective.

The UKGC said QuinnBet recognised the issues and took immediate action, including strengthening AML policies and improving how it identifies and responds to harm indicators. This is the regulator's account of remediation, not independent verification of every current control.
