# BeInCasinos target geographies — authoritative correction

**Confirmed by the project owner on 23 October 2026. This scope supersedes any older ten-market plan or tracker note.**

The only target geographies are:

- Australia (AU)
- Canada (CA)
- Great Britain (GB)
- United States (US)
- Switzerland (CH)
- Germany (DE)
- Netherlands (NL)
- Sweden (SE)
- Spain (ES)
- Italy (IT)
- France (FR)
- Belgium (BE)

## Out of scope

Ghana, Colombia, Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, South Africa, Nigeria, Denmark, and all other unlisted markets. Off-scope evidence may be retained in the internal archive for audit only. It must not be counted toward the target or turned into public target-market pages.

## Review and legal gates

- The active registry currently contains 162 market-specific candidate/research records. These are not 130 distinct global operators and are not 100 completed reviews. Publication-approved reviews remain 0/100 until all evidence and independent editorial/legal gates pass.
- Australia and France generally prohibit online casino services. Do not promote offshore online casinos as locally legal or invent casino candidates to fill a quota. Research legal scope and publish only legally appropriate explanatory content if approved.
- Canada requires province/territory-specific checks. Ontario evidence does not establish authorisation across Canada.
- The United States requires state/tribal-specific checks. Never imply nationwide online-casino authorisation from a single state or tribal record.
- For every other target geography, verify the exact active local permission for online casino games, contracting entity, domain, terms, payments, withdrawals, complaints/ADR, privacy, safer-gambling tools, accessibility and enforcement history.
- All candidate rows remain HOLD until all mandatory gates and independent second-review are complete. Never invent testing, scores, licensing, payment experience or approval.
