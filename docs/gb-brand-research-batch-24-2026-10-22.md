- Do not infer that a domain marked Active is currently accepting registrations or that a domain marked Inactive has no operator anywhere.
- Recheck the official register before publication; the Commission says domain and trading-name details are supplied by the business and may not be guaranteed accurate.
- Verify target-market legal terms, bonuses/wagering, payments/withdrawals, complaints/ADR, safer-gambling controls and support separately for each brand.
