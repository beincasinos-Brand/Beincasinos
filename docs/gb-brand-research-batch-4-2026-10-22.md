## Quality controls

1. A business-register search result is a lead, not a final licence verdict. Check licence activity, status, exact domain, trading name and legal entity together.
2. The UK Gambling Commission regulates Great Britain; do not extend its permission to Morocco, Northern Ireland, or other markets.
3. The register itself warns that domain and trading-name details are supplied by licensees and may not be guaranteed accurate. Independently verify against the operator's current official site.
4. Review regulatory actions, customer terms, withdrawal conditions, complaints and support routes, safer-gambling tools, privacy/tracking disclosures and commercial conflicts before writing a consumer-facing assessment.
5. Keep unresolved or contradictory records on HOLD. Do not infer hands-on testing, customer outcomes, bonus value, safety or willingness to pay.
6. Deduplicate by consumer brand and exact domain; multiple related companies or licences do not count as multiple brands.
