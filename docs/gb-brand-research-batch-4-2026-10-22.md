## 1. William Hill — resolve the active online operator, not just the brand name

Primary sources:
- UK Gambling Commission search results: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/results?business-search=William+Hill
- WHG (International) Limited, account 39225: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39225
- William Hill Organization Limited, account 2752: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/2752
- Official William Hill support/legal disclosure: https://help.williamhill.com/hc/en-gb/sections/17353063788317-Overview

The UKGC search currently returns two different legal entities:
- WHG (International) Limited, trading as “william hill online”, account 39225, with www.williamhill.com as a matching domain.
- William Hill Organization Limited, trading as “william hill”, account 2752, with no matching domain shown in the result.

The official William Hill support disclosure says that for customers in Great Britain, the website is operated by WHG (International) Limited and identifies UKGC account 39225. For customers outside Great Britain and specified other territories, it names William Hill Global PLC and a Malta licence. This is a material jurisdiction split: never use the UKGC account as evidence that the same product is authorised for users in other countries.

The current result for account 2752 shows several active non-remote licences and a Gambling Software Remote licence, but the displayed table does not show a Casino Remote licence. Do not use this account alone as proof of active online-casino permission. Confirm the exact remote casino permission and exact domain against account 39225 and the consumer-facing legal disclosure before review.

Required follow-up: inspect account 39225 licence summary and domain detail, current GB casino terms, payment/withdrawal conditions, complaints, support, safer-gambling tools, and relevant regulatory actions. Status: HOLD pending complete exact-product evidence.

## 2. L & L Europe Limited — recent UKGC settlement and withdrawal-related failures

Primary source: UK Gambling Commission announcement dated 21 October 2026: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/l-and-l-europe-pays-gbp900-000-for-failures-including-withdrawal-delays

The Commission says L & L Europe Limited will pay £900,000 as part of a regulatory settlement. It operates seven websites, including allbritishcasino.com, casinocasino.com and hypercasino.com. The Commission described:
- unfair terms that allowed only one withdrawal request at a time until the prior withdrawal was authorised;
- additional information being requested at withdrawal after sampled manual reviews, where the Commission said the information could reasonably have been requested earlier;
- anti-money-laundering control weaknesses; and
- safer-gambling alerts and interaction-effectiveness shortcomings.

This is an official, recent regulatory finding relevant to any candidate brand proven to be operated by L & L Europe Limited. It must not be attributed to an unrelated brand without confirming the exact legal entity and domain. Do not imply the settlement proves every current term is unchanged, nor that every site had identical defects.

Editorial handling:
