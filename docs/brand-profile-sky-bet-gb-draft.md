## 1. Current operator mapping

Sky Bet's current licensing page separates operator responsibility by product:
- **SBG Sports Limited**, UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) account **67370**: sports betting / virtual racing.
- **Bonne Terre Gaming Limited**, UKGC account **65519**: casino, bingo and poker products.

Official register sources:
- SBG Sports Limited: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/67370
- Bonne Terre Gaming Limited: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/65519
- Sky Bet licensing and regulation: https://support.skybet.com/app/answers/detail/licensing-and-regulation/

The older Bonne Terre Limited account **38718** has its remote licences shown as surrendered from **31 October 2025**. Do not reuse older copy that describes account 38718 as the current active operator. Verify the exact product terms and contracting entity on the live GB site at publication time.

## 2. Regulatory history

The UKGC published a 2022 action against Bonne Terre Limited, including a **£1.17 million fine** related to promotional emails sent to self-excluded customers and people who had opted out of marketing. Review the original regulator notice and scope before publication:
- Register entry for account 38718: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/38718
- UKGC search for Bonne Terre Limited: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/search/?q=Bonne%20Terre%20Limited

This historical action should be attributed to the entity and period named by the regulator. Do not automatically attribute it to SBG Sports Limited or Bonne Terre Gaming Limited without supporting evidence. A licence is not a quality or safety endorsement.
