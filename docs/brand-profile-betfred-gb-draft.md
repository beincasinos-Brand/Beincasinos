## 2. Regulatory history — important publication gate

### 30 June 2026 — £900,000 settlement

The UKGC says Petfre (Gibraltar) Limited agreed to a £900,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty after a compliance assessment found weaknesses in remote customer-interaction controls. The published findings included insufficient automated identification of harm indicators, failure to ensure immediate action when strong indicators appeared, and a seven-day gap before some flagged customers could be reviewed again. In one cited instance, a customer lost £17,900 in 24 hours without an additional interaction.

- Primary notice: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/petfre-gibraltar-limited-to-pay-gbp900-000-for-regulatory-failures
- Account-specific action: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/330

The Commission also stated that the operator implemented interim controls and later provided an action plan and steps intended to meet requirements. Include that context without minimising the findings.

### 20 May 2025 — £240,000 technical-standards penalty

The UKGC's register records a £240,000 penalty for a breach of technical standards. The Commission noted that the licensee identified and proactively reported the breach, took immediate remedial action and cooperated.

- Primary notice: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/286

### 28 September 2022 — historical £2.9m penalty

The UKGC announced that Petfre (Gibraltar) Limited, trading as Betfred, was fined £2.9m in 2022 for social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. Retrieve the full original notice and review its precise scope before publication.

- UKGC enforcement index: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/enforcement-action
