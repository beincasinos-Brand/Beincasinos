# Initial source triage — partner-sourced brand leads

**Checked:** 25 October 2026  
**Purpose:** record what the first web-source pass did and did not establish. This is not legal advice or a licence verification certificate. Search-result snippets are leads; every important fact must be confirmed on the live primary source and, for licensing, the regulator's own register.

## Priority finding: do not treat brand name as a unique legal identity

The first pass surfaced multiple domains and legal-entity statements associated with similar names. The exact customer domain, contractual operator, licence record and target market must all match before any lead can be called verified.

## Kokobet — HOLD, domain/operator verification required

**Primary-source leads**
- `https://kokkobet.com/terms-conditions/` — the page identifies the service as `kokkobet.com` and states that it is operated by `3-102-897762 LTDA / Luckywayz Limited B.V.`; it claims Curaçao Gaming Control Board licence `OGL/2024/1497/0884` and Costa Rica registration.
- `https://kokkobet.com/payment-policy/` — operator and licence claims repeated in the site's payment policy.
- `https://kokkobet.com/privacy-policy/` — privacy-policy identity claims repeated.
- Similar-looking domain surfaced separately: `https://kokobett.net/en/terms-conditions/`. Do not assume this is the same operator or an authorised mirror.

**What this establishes:** the `kokkobet.com` site's own terms make the stated operator/licence claims.  
**What it does not establish:** that the regulator independently confirms the current licence, that every target market is permitted, that a similar domain is the same business, or that payment/gameplay claims are independently tested.

**Next gate:** open the relevant regulator's live record and certificate, match the exact domain and legal entity, check regulatory actions and market exclusions, and retain a dated copy. No rating or bonus should be published before that.

## Casinia — HOLD, domain and jurisdiction conflict to resolve

**Primary-source leads**
- `https://casinia.com/en/` — consumer-facing brand site; the page promotes casino and sportsbook services and contains standard age/responsible-gambling messaging.
- `https://casiniaz.com/terms` — a different domain whose terms describe `casiniaz.com` as an authorised platform for Casinia, name `Stellar Ltd.` and state an Anjouan Gaming Board licence reference `ALSI-202511077-FI2`, while the terms snippet specifies a permitted AU jurisdiction.
- `https://casinia-casino-australia.com/license-information/` — another domain stating an operator identity for a Casinia Australia service.

**Risk / interpretation:** these are not interchangeable URLs. The conflicting domains, legal disclosures and jurisdiction wording require exact domain/operator mapping and independent regulator confirmation. Do not infer that a self-published Anjouan licence claim establishes authorisation in Australia or any other target market. The target-market list includes AU, but that alone does not make a product eligible there.

**Next gate:** confirm the actual official customer domain and contractual operator for each intended market, check the relevant regulator's own position and local law, document restricted territories, and exclude any market that does not pass. No Australian availability claim should be made on the basis of the search snippets.

## Legiano — HOLD, multiple market-specific domains/entities surfaced

**Next gate:** define one exact market/domain pair per dossier, then independently verify that pair against the relevant regulator, official terms, restricted-territory rules and current legal footer. Until matched, the lead remains on HOLD.

## Editorial decisions from this triage

- The three leads above remain **HOLD**; no public score, recommendation, bonus amount or market-eligibility statement is approved.
- A brand name or operator's own licence statement is not a substitute for the regulator's current record.
