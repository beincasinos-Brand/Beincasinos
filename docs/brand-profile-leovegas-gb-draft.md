The UKGC register lists LeoVegas Gaming PLC (account 39198) with active remote Casino and Bingo licences, plus active remote real-event and virtual-event betting permissions. The domain register lists leovegas.co.uk as Active, alongside betmgm.co.uk, betuk.com, pinkcasino.co.uk and 21.co.uk. The same register currently marks leovegas.com as Inactive. This dossier therefore concerns the GB domain and does not infer current UK authorisation from the .com domain.

The UKGC's actions page currently states that no regulatory actions have been recorded against the business in that register view. That must be read alongside the historical public enforcement announcement below; it is not appropriate to omit known historic enforcement simply because the current actions page has no entry.

## 2. Regulatory history — 3 August 2022

The UK Gambling Commission announced that LeoVegas would pay a £1.32 million penalty for social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. The Commission also announced an official warning and an audit to check effective implementation of the operator's AML and social-responsibility policies, procedures and controls.

The regulator described social-responsibility weaknesses including spend triggers for safer-gambling team review being set significantly higher than average customer spend without an explanation of why that was appropriate. The regulator's published account should be consulted directly for the full findings and context before a public review quotes or paraphrases specific examples.

This is historical regulatory context. It should neither be presented as proof of present-day performance nor omitted from an assessment of the operator's record. A live licence is not, by itself, a consumer-quality score.
