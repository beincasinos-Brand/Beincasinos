# GB brand research batch 35 — Midnite / dual UKGC licensee records
**Checked:** 2026-10-22 (UTC)  
**Purpose:** internal evidence log. Not a completed consumer review or publication approval.

## Executive finding
Midnite is a real consumer-facing betting and casino brand at https://www.midnite.com/. The current UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) register shows **two licensees** associated with the Midnite trading name/domain: Dribble Media Limited (account 42647) and Midnite Limited (account 101839). The official site's current footer and help-centre terms name different operator entities in different places. This is a material identity/contracting-entity issue and must be resolved before publishing a definitive operator attribution or a consumer review.

## Official register evidence
1. **Midnite Limited — account 101839**
   - Licence summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/101839
   - Current summary lists Active Bingo Remote, Casino Remote, General Betting Standard — Real Event Remote, General Betting Standard — Virtual Event Remote, and Pool Betting Remote from 11 August 2026.
   - Domain record: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/101839 lists `www.midnite.com` as Active.
   - Trading-name record: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/trading-names/101839 must be rechecked and archived before publication.
2. **Dribble Media Limited — account 42647**
   - Search result: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/results?business-search=Midnite shows the trading name `midnite` and domain `midnite.com` associated with Dribble Media Limited, as well as Midnite Limited.
   - Licence summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/42647

## Consumer-facing official disclosures
- Official casino page: https://www.midnite.com/casino currently states Midnite Limited operates for customers in Great Britain under UKGC account 101839, and Midnite Europe Limited operates for customers in Ireland under Irish licence details.
- Official About page: https://www.midnite.com/about currently repeats the Midnite Limited / 101839 statement for Great Britain.
- Official terms: https://help.midnite.com/en/articles/2686627-terms-conditions states in section 2.1 that “Midnite” means Midnite Limited for UK customers and Midnite Europe Limited for Irish customers. The material summary and eligibility section state that customers must be 18+ and resident in the UK, Republic of Ireland, or another permitted jurisdiction listed by the operator.
- A third-party investigation dated 3 September 2026 noted a conflict between the older Dribble Media Limited licence record and newer Midnite Limited record, and reported that the terms page had a licence-number inconsistency. This is a secondary lead only; resolve using the live primary records and the precise version of terms in force before making any public claim: https://www.clintonandco.co.uk/operators/midnite
- Midnite's own article says `midnite.com` is its only official site: https://www.midnite.com/blog/how-to-spot-the-real-midnite. Treat this as the operator's statement, not independent verification of the legal transfer.

## Material questions / risks

- Which legal entity is the contracting party for each current customer/product, and which entity handles balances, complaints and data-controller responsibilities?
- Does the live site, account-specific contract, UKGC record and any customer-transfer notice consistently identify the same operator?
- Do the latest terms clearly state the correct account number and complaint/ADR route?
- Does the consumer page explain any operator change to existing customers and the treatment of account balances/data?
- Great Britain permission does not establish eligibility in Morocco or other jurisdictions.
