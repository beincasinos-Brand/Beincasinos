This sequence matters: do not publish the September suspension as though it remains in force. Recheck the live licence summary and latest decision immediately before publication. ## 2. Regulatory facts

The Commission stated that:
- the suspension applied to the combined remote operating licence for the online business;
- the review followed anti-money-laundering concerns;
- the operator was expected to treat consumers fairly and keep them informed during the suspension;
- the suspension did not prevent customers from accessing accounts and withdrawing funds; and
- the suspension was lifted on 13 October 2026 following significant action taken by the operator.

Do not infer a financial penalty where the cited notice does not state one. Do not convert the AML concern into claims about a specific customer's funds or outcomes.
