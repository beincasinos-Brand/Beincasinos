# Operator prospect qualification scorecard

**Internal working tool — 21 October 2026. Not a public endorsement, legal opinion, rate card, or evidence that any operator wants to pay.**

## Purpose

The existing 100-name list is a research backlog, not 100 qualified prospects. Use this scorecard to decide which names deserve more research and which, if any, may later be approached about a clearly labelled listing. Do not score a brand as commercially qualified merely because it is licensed, new, visible in search results, or has a press contact. The separate Tier 1 master tracker now contains 103 brand-name research rows plus two market-category restriction rows; that larger count reflects research leads, not additional qualified buyers. The latest three UKGC-backed entries (Silkbet, BigBangBet, and RoyalOl / Speadboom) remain Hold because the consumer product, terms and full editorial evidence are incomplete.

## Gate 0 — market and product legality (mandatory pass/fail)

Before considering a brand for a market-specific public page or commercial promotion, record:

- Exact consumer-facing domain and legal operating entity.
- Target jurisdiction and exact product vertical.
- Current primary-source regulator evidence for that entity, domain and product where required.
- Relevant advertising, affiliate, referral, and paid-placement restrictions.
- Current operator terms and market availability.
- Source URLs, UTC check date, reviewer, and unresolved questions.

If any mandatory item is unresolved, set **Market gate = HOLD**. Do not compensate for a failed legal/eligibility gate with points, a fee, or a partner's assurance. France and Australia require especially careful product-specific treatment: do not promote offshore online-casino offers as authorised for either market. US assessment must be state-by-state and product-by-product. Switzerland, Germany, Great Britain and the Netherlands each require their own current official source checks.

## Gate 1 — evidence quality (mandatory pass/fail)

Set **Evidence gate = PASS** only when the record has a source-backed identity/domain match, current market-specific permission evidence, current terms, a review of material regulatory history, and an identified official contact route. An official register entry is a starting point, not a complete quality review. Record white-label relationships and do not infer independent brand ownership.

If the gate is not passed, status stays **Research / Hold** and the lead cannot be represented as verified or recommended.

## Gate 2 — research-priority score (0–10)

Use this only to order internal research. It is **not** a casino quality score, legal approval, ranking, or willingness-to-pay estimate.

| Factor | 0 points | 1 point | 2 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary-source identity evidence | Name-only lead | Partial official evidence | Exact entity/domain/product evidence found |
| Market fit | No lawful, defined scope identified | Scope may be viable but needs checks | A specific lawful scope is supported by current primary sources |
| Information value | Little useful verifiable information | Some current official terms or records | Clear consumer-relevant information can be documented |
| Research readiness | No reliable contact/source path | Contact or source path incomplete | Official source and appropriate business contact route verified |
| Distinctive editorial value | Duplicates existing coverage | Some useful differentiator | Material market/product or evidence gap worth explaining |

**Interpretation:** 8–10 = research first only if Gates 0 and 1 pass; 5–7 = continue targeted research; 0–4 = park unless new evidence appears. A high score does not establish commercial demand.

## Gate 3 — commercial qualification (keep separate from research score)

Commercial status begins at **Unconfirmed**. Change it only when direct, dated evidence supports the change.

- **Unconfirmed:** no direct response confirming interest.
- **Discovery eligible:** owner has separately approved a narrow research interview; the message clearly says it is research, not a sale, and is lawful for the recipient/channel.
- **Interest indicated:** an authorised representative directly asks for commercial details or confirms interest; preserve the source/date and exact scope.
- **Qualified opportunity:** decision-maker/contact, permitted market, desired format, budget or commercial process, reporting needs, programme restrictions, and next step are documented.
- **Contracted:** signed terms and any required compliance approval are recorded.
- **Live:** owner has approved publication and launch gates are cleared.

A contact form, press release, public affiliate page, new launch, or operator's presence in a regulator register is not evidence of willingness to pay. Do not send outreach until owner approval. No performance pitch may contain invented traffic, audience, rankings, conversion, revenue, or ROI.

## Commercial models to discuss only when approved

Potential models remain hypotheses, not current offers:

- Fixed fee for a visibly labelled directory listing.
- CPA where an operator's programme permits the traffic source and market.
- Revenue share under documented programme terms.
- Hybrid fixed fee plus performance commission.
- Clearly labelled sponsored content with editorial and legal review.

For each model, confirm eligible markets, attribution method, reporting access, payment terms, cancellation/removal rights, data handling, advertising disclosures, and whether the programme allows directory/review traffic. Never sell a rating, positive conclusion, guaranteed inclusion in a top-10 list, or undisclosed editorial influence. If the operator declines commercial terms, editorial eligibility must remain unchanged.

## Minimal evidence record

Copy one row per brand into the internal tracker:

| Field | Required value |
|---|---|
| Brand / domain | Exact name and URL |
| Legal operator | Exact entity or unresolved |
| Market / product | Specific jurisdiction and vertical |
| Regulator evidence | Primary-source URL + date checked |
| Terms / withdrawals | Official URLs + date checked |
| Regulatory history | Official source checked; findings or unresolved |
| Ownership / white-label | Confirmed, disclosed relationship, or unresolved |
| Advertising / affiliate gate | Pass / Hold / legal review needed |
| Research score | 0–10, with evidence notes |
| Commercial status | Unconfirmed unless direct evidence changes it |
| Contact route | Official business contact and source |
| Next action / owner | Specific check, responsible person, due date |
| Decision log | Dated reason for Pass / Hold / Park |

## Reporting honestly

Report funnel counts separately: names in backlog, identity checked, market gate passed, evidence gate passed, discovery conversations approved, direct interest confirmed, qualified opportunities, signed deals, and live listings. Never present all 100 backlog names as verified prospects or potential paying customers. Publish only evidence-backed, lawful, current information and disclose paid relationships clearly.
