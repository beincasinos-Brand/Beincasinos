## 1. Material licence and domain split

The UK Gambling Commission licence summary for **Mecca Bingo Limited (account 2396)** lists active remote and non-remote Bingo permissions. It does not list a remote casino permission on that account. Its domain record must be checked directly before attributing any product to this entity.

Separately, the UKGC record for **Rank Interactive (Gibraltar) Limited (account 57924)** lists active remote Casino, Bingo, Gambling Software, Real Event Betting and Virtual Event Betting permissions, and its domain record lists `meccabingo.com` as Active. The official Mecca Bingo privacy notice names Rank Interactive (Gibraltar) Limited as the service provider/data controller. The live site markets both bingo and slots.

This creates a product-specific mapping question: do not imply that Mecca Bingo Limited's bingo licence alone authorises casino products, and do not collapse the two legal entities into one operator.
