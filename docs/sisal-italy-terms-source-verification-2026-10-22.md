## Terms and account-opening claims to verify against the live site

The operator-hosted PDF indexed as “Termini e condizioni” identifies concession 16020 and says account opening requires the customer to be at least 18, have an Italian tax code, provide valid identity documentation, accept the account contract and privacy notice, and activate self-limitation tools. It also states one active account contract per customer with Sisal and a 15-day interval before opening a new Sisal account after closing a previous one.

Source:
- https://www.sisal.it/content/dam/new-dam/italy/canali/sisal-it/doc-pdf/doc-vari/doc-istituzionali/Sisal_termini_e_condizioni.pdf

These are operator-published contractual statements, not independent UX verification. Recheck the live terms link, PDF version/date and relevant clause numbers before any public use.

## Complaints and responsible-gambling evidence

Sisal's responsible-gambling FAQ discusses the independence of chance-based outcomes and warns against trying to recover previous losses through repeated play:
- https://www.sisal.it/gioco-responsabile/domande-e-risposte

The concession contract describes an account-related complaint route, a stated 72-hour operator response obligation and escalation to ADM where applicable. This is contractual text, not evidence that support actually responds within that period in practice:
- https://www.sisal.it/content/dam/new-dam/italy/canali/sisal-it/doc-pdf/doc-vari/doc-istituzionali/contratto-Sisalit.pdf
