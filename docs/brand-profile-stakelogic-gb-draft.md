## 2. Regulatory action — 25 June 2026

The UKGC announced that Stakelogic B.V. would pay **£122,835** following a finding that two of its games, **Super Stake** and **UltraStake**, breached the remote gambling and software technical standards by allowing a game cycle to complete in under the required minimum duration. The regulator's announcement says the minimum cycle duration is 2.5 seconds and that the games had a turbo-spin feature that allowed cycles to complete faster. The games were removed from the Great Britain market after the issue was identified.

This is a technical-standards finding about the named supplier and games. Do not imply that all Stakelogic titles, all supplier customers, or every current version of a game have the same defect. Verify the regulator's full notice and any later technical update before making a current availability claim.
