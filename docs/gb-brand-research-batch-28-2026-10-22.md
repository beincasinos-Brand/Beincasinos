## Regulator evidence
The UK Gambling Commission record lists Proper Bet Limited account 100990 with an **Active Casino Remote** licence from 22 May 2026, alongside active real-event and virtual-event remote betting permissions. This supports the licence status for Great Britain only; it is not evidence of permission in Morocco or any other country.

## Official offer terms observed on the homepage
The official homepage displays a new-customer promotion of 100 bonus spins at 10p each after £20 qualifying cash wagers on Big Bass Vegas 1000 using the stated promo code. It states that bonus-spin winnings become bonus funds with a 10x wagering requirement on those winnings, a £1 maximum stake per spin while wagering, a 7-day expiry/use window, and identity/age checks. These are homepage terms observed on the research date; capture and compare the linked full terms before any public review. Do not treat the offer as permanent.

The homepage states the offer is for new Proper Bet customers aged 18+ resident in Great Britain. This restriction must be made prominent if the brand is ever reviewed for a GB audience. Do not imply availability to Moroccan users.
