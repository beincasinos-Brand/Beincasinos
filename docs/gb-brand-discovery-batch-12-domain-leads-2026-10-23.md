## Deduplication and quality controls
- Count consumer brands, not licence holders or domains. One licensee can operate many sites; never count a legal entity or domain list as multiple brands without brand-level evidence.
- Treat UKGC domain status as regulatory-register evidence only, not a live HTTP check.
- Exclude inactive domains from the current active-domain shortlist. White-label entries require clear operator disclosure and an independent consumer-brand decision.
- Do not write rankings, ratings, bonus figures, payment times, RTP claims or “best” assertions without current source evidence and a reproducible review method.
- No candidate in this batch is marked verified, approved, or review-ready.
