## Scope rule

The regulator states that online casino includes roulette, blackjack, baccarat, online poker, bingo and gaming-machine games, and requires an online-casino licence. A betting licence alone is not sufficient. Use the regulator's current licence type and approved domain list, not a third-party affiliate page.
