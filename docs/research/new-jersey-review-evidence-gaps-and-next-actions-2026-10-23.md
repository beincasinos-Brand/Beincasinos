## Standard capture record

For each brand, save a dated record with:

- Exact market and product; regulator listing URL and date checked.
- Legal contracting party and any platform/host/licensee relationship, each labelled distinctly.
- Terms URL, effective/update date and archive/captured text.
- Offer terms: eligibility, qualifying deposit, bonus form, expiry, wagering, contribution, maximum bet, withdrawal cap and excluded games where applicable.
- Payments: accepted methods, deposit/withdrawal limits, fees, processing policy, verification and source-of-funds conditions.
- Responsible gambling: limits, time-out, self-exclusion, account closure and help/support routes.
- Complaints: casino-first procedure, regulator escalation and ADR if applicable.
- Evidence distinction: official policy vs observed test. Only log a test if performed; include date, method, steps and limitations.
- Named researcher, second checker, unresolved issue list, decision and next review date.

## Stop conditions

- Any unclear or inactive permission for the exact product/market.
- Contradictory or missing contracting entity.
- No current primary source for material bonus, payment, withdrawal or safer-gambling claims.
- Any suggestion that New Jersey authorization means authorization in another market.
- Any unperformed hands-on test described as performed.
- Any missing affiliate disclosure or unresolved editorial conflict.

**Publication rule:** Until these stop conditions are cleared and the evidence scorecard is complete, maintain internal HOLD status.
