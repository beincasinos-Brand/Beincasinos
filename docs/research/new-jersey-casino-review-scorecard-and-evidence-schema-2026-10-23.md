# New Jersey casino review scorecard and evidence schema

**Status:** Internal editorial standard, not a consumer-facing rating. Scope: use only for a casino product whose New Jersey eligibility has been verified against the current DGE list and current terms.

## Hard gates (pass/fail, not weighted points)

A candidate cannot receive a public score while any gate is unresolved:

- Exact New Jersey casino domain appears on the current DGE authorized-sites list.
- Current terms establish the casino contracting entity and the host/permit relationship is understood.
- Market access and age/geolocation restrictions are described correctly.
- Current bonus, game and payment rules are sourced or omitted.
- Safer-gambling controls, account closure, self-exclusion and complaint escalation are checked.
- No unsubstantiated claims about payout speed, customer-service tests, fairness, security, or player outcomes.
- Author/reviewer, checked date, affiliate disclosure, correction route and independent editor are assigned.
- Editorial and legal/compliance approval are recorded.

A failure means HOLD, not a lower score. DGE list: https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/internet-gaming-sites/

## Suggested rubric after all gates pass

Each dimension is rated 0–5 and must cite evidence. If a dimension was not tested or evidence is missing, mark N/A and do not normalize it away without disclosure.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Terms and offer transparency | 20% | Current bonus terms, wagering, expiry, max bet/cashout, eligibility and exclusions |
| Payments and withdrawal clarity | 20% | Published methods, limits, fees, verification and processing policy; separate observed tests |
| Safer gambling and account controls | 20% | Limits, time-out, self-exclusion, help resources, account closure/refund path |
| Licensing and operator transparency | 15% | DGE domain listing, current contracting entity, host/permit/platform mapping |
| Games and product information | 10% | Current catalogue, providers, game rules/RTP disclosures where available |
| Customer support and redress | 10% | Current support routes, response evidence if tested, complaints and DGE escalation |
| Accessibility and usability | 5% | Documented device/browser/accessibility checks; don't claim testing not performed |

Weighted score = sum of (dimension score / 5 × weight). Do not issue an overall score if a hard gate fails or if critical dimensions are N/A. The score reflects the stated methodology and evidence snapshot, not a guarantee of safety, winnings, fairness in every game, or future performance.

## Evidence record fields

Every factual claim should have a record containing: claim ID; brand and product; market; claim text; source URL; source type (regulator / current terms / first-party help / independent test / direct observation); source checked at UTC; quote or summary; scope and limitations; confidence (high / medium / low); reviewer; next recheck trigger; and publication status (hold / approved / expired).

## Rules for historic regulatory actions

- Attribute action to the exact licensee/operator named by the regulator, not casually to every brand in the corporate group.
- State date, amount and the regulator's actual finding; distinguish penalty, settlement, warning, and licence action.
- Explain market and product scope. A sportsbook action is not automatically a casino-product finding.
- Include regulator-stated remediation and mitigating context where relevant.
- Link directly to the primary regulator record; avoid sensational or unsupported conclusions.

## Refresh triggers

Recheck immediately after a regulator action, licence/domain/operator change, material terms/bonus/payment change, complaint escalation pattern, or broken source link. Otherwise set a documented periodic recheck date. A changed page should not receive a new last-checked date unless material content was actually checked.

## Responsible-gambling and dispute source

The NJ DGE says the customer must first complain to the casino and receive a response before submitting an internet dispute form: https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/internet-gaming-information/

## Final publication rule

A polished draft is not evidence of testing. If no real account, cashier, withdrawal or support test was performed, state that plainly and rely only on documented policy. Do not manufacture scores, reviews, testimonials or user experience.
