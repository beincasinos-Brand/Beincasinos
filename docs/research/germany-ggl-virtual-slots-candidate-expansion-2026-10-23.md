## Legal/product boundary

The official German Joint Gambling Authority (GGL) [Whitelist](https://www.gluecksspiel-behoerde.de/de/fuer-spielende/uebersicht-erlaubter-anbieter-whitelist) says it is the statutory list of authorised gambling organisers/intermediaries and was last updated **2 October 2026**. The list separates game categories, including **Virtuelle Automatenspiele** (virtual slots) and **Online-Casinospiele** (online casino games). A nationwide virtual-slots listing does not itself establish a permission for online table games or live casino. The existing MERKUR Spiel (DE-SH) record remains a separate Schleswig-Holstein-specific online-casino permission lead; do not generalise it nationwide.

This batch adds 13 domain/operator records from the GGL whitelist under the virtual-slot category. These may support market-specific virtual-slots research, not a claim that every brand offers an authorised table/live casino product. Each brand must be checked against its live legal notice and the exact product terms before it counts toward the site's target.

## Required checks before a review can be counted

1. Re-open the GGL whitelist on the actual publication date and preserve a dated record of the relevant operator, domain and game category.
2. Match the exact live German customer route, legal entity and permitted category.
3. Clearly label any review as virtual-slot-specific unless a distinct active online-casino permission is confirmed. Do not call a slots-only service a live/table casino.
4. Capture current German terms, bonus restrictions, payment/withdrawal methods and limits, verification, fees and processing language.
5. Verify complaints and escalation, safer-gambling limits, time-outs, self-exclusion and account closure.
6. Review privacy controller, cookie consent, accessibility and mobile usability.
7. Check GGL and relevant state-regulator enforcement history and operator/domain changes.
8. Complete date-stamped evidence and an independent second review. Do not claim any account, deposit, withdrawal or real-money UX test that did not happen.
