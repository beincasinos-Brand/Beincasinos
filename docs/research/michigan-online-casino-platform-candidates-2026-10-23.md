## Primary source and method

The Michigan Gaming Control Board (MGCB) publishes an official page titled [Authorized Online Gaming and Sports Betting Platform Providers in Michigan](https://www.michigan.gov/mgcb/internet-gaming-and-fantasy-contests/authorized-online-gaming-and-sports-betting-platform-providers-in-michigan). It maps Michigan casino operators/tribal partners to platform providers and identifies casino-game routes. The page states authorized activities for each casino operation. The candidates below were taken from the regulator's listed casino-games routes, not from affiliate rankings.

A platform-provider listing is a valid candidate lead, but it is **not** a completed operator review. The regulator page does not, by itself, resolve the exact customer-contracting entity, current terms, current offer conditions, payment performance, complaints, privacy, accessibility or current enforcement history. Recheck the live regulator page and customer terms before publication.
