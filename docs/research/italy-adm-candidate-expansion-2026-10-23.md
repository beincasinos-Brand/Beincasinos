# Italy — ADM-listed remote-gambling candidate expansion

**Checked:** 23 October 2026  
**Scope:** Italy only  
**Status:** Research leads, not approved casino reviews.

## Primary regulator evidence

The Italian Customs and Monopolies Agency (ADM) publishes an official register titled *Elenco dei concessionari autorizzati al gioco a distanza* (authorised remote-gambling concessionaires). The live register lists concession codes, concessionaires and domains. This is strong primary evidence that the named concessionaire/domain appears in the remote-gambling register, but **it is not by itself proof that every listed site currently offers online casino games**, that the product is live, or that its consumer terms have been reviewed.

Official source: https://www.adm.gov.it/portale/monopoli/giochi/gioco_distanza/gioco_dist_concessionari

## Mandatory validation before counting toward the 100

Each record is marked **research / HOLD**. Do not count it as an eligible casino brand until all of the following are confirmed from current primary evidence:

1. The concession remains current and the domain still belongs to the listed concessionaire.
2. The site currently offers the online-casino vertical (not just sports betting, lottery, poker or another remote product).
3. The precise contracting entity and applicable terms are confirmed on the live Italian route.
4. Current bonus rules, deposits/withdrawals, fees and limits, complaint/ADR process, safer-gambling tools, privacy/cookies and accessibility are reviewed.
5. Relevant ADM enforcement or other material regulatory actions are checked.
6. A second reviewer verifies all claims and dates.

These are research leads, not recommendations or endorsements. No account, payment or real-money gameplay testing is claimed.
