## Primary-source findings

1. The UK Gambling Commission's licence summary for Recro Limited, account 61549, lists Casino Remote as active from 21 December 2023 to current: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/61549
2. The UKGC domain page for account 61549 lists casumo.com as active: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/61549
3. The UKGC licence summary for Casumo Services Limited, account 39265, lists Casino Remote as surrendered on 3 November 2023: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39265
4. The former licensee's domain page lists casumo.com inactive, and its trading-name page lists Casumo inactive:
   - https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/39265
   - https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/trading-names/39265
5. In a published FOI response, the UKGC states that Casumo does not have a licence in its own right and that the Casumo website in question is run by licensed operator Recro Limited: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/about-us/freedomofinformation/print/casumo-services-ltd

## Interpretation

The evidence supports the current operator mapping to Recro Limited, not the former Casumo Services Limited record. Do not present the brand name as the legal licensee. The regulator warns that domain and trading-name fields are supplied by the gambling business, so cross-check the live site's current terms and footer before publication.

The former and current operator records must not be combined when summarising regulatory history. A “no actions recorded” page for one entity is not proof that the brand or a predecessor has no historic actions. Any historical action must be attributed to the correct legal entity and date.
