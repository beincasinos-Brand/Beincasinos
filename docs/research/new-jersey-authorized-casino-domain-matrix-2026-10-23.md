## Interpretation and controls

- DGE grouping is evidence of the listed domain's approved New Jersey internet-gaming context; it does not by itself prove every detail of a brand's player contract or commercial ownership.
- Do not infer approval in another US state, Great Britain, or any other country from New Jersey evidence.
- Do not publish bonus amounts, wagering requirements, game counts, withdrawal speed, fees, app quality, RTP or testing claims unless current primary evidence supports them.
- A regulator-listed domain does not equal hands-on testing. Clearly label desk research and do not imply a deposit, withdrawal or support interaction was tested if it was not.
- Keep every brand on HOLD until the required primary documents and independent editorial/compliance gates are complete.
- This matrix is deliberately limited to brands with current internal evidence notes; it is not a complete inventory of all domains on the DGE page and must not be used as a consumer-facing “best casinos” ranking.
