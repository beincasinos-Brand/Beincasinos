## Verification gates

Every added row is marked research/HOLD. Before it can count as an eligible online-casino review, verify:
1. Current operator licence, exact domain and active online-casino vertical (not merely sports betting, poker or other game types).
2. Exact contracting entity and current Spain-specific customer terms.
3. Current welcome offer and full restrictions, or omit promotional claims.
4. Deposits, withdrawals, fees, limits and identity checks.
5. Complaint route and any independent dispute resolution.
6. Safer-gambling tools, self-exclusion and account closure.
7. Privacy controller, cookies/consent and accessibility.
8. Relevant DGOJ enforcement history.
9. Date-stamped evidence and independent second review.

These are research leads only; no account, deposit, withdrawal or real-money play testing is claimed.
