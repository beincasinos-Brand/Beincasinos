## Remaining gates before any of these can be published

1. Re-open the regulator record and confirm it is current on the publication date.
2. Match the exact legal contracting entity, domain, product category and permitted territory.
3. Archive dated terms, bonus rules and material restrictions.
4. Capture deposit/withdrawal rails, fees, limits, verification requirements and realistic timing statements from primary evidence.
5. Verify complaints and ADR, responsible-gambling tools, local self-exclusion, privacy controller/cookies and accessibility.
6. Review enforcement and ownership/domain changes.
7. Conduct and record lawful, reproducible UX checks; do not imply deposits or withdrawals were personally tested unless actually done.
8. Require independent second review and final editorial/legal sign-off.
