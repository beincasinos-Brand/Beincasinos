## Regulatory actions — keep the two events separate

### 20 May 2025 — £240,000 financial penalty
The UKGC action page records a £240,000 penalty for a technical standards breach (licence condition 2.3.1). The Commission notes that Petfre identified and proactively reported the breach, took immediate remedial action and cooperated with the investigation.
Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/286

### 30 June 2026 — £900,000 settlement
The UKGC records a separate £900,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty after a compliance assessment conducted in May–June 2024 found social-responsibility/customer-interaction control failures. The regulator's public summary describes shortcomings in automated harm indicators and prompt interaction, including a case where a customer lost £17,900 within 24 hours without an additional interaction. The regulator also says the licensee delivered an action plan and significant steps to assure it that its current operating model meets requirements.
Primary sources:
- https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/330
- https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/petfre-gibraltar-limited-to-pay-gbp900-000-for-regulatory-failures
