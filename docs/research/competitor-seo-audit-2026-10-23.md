# BeInCasinos — competitor SEO and editorial audit

**Date checked:** 23 October 2026  
**Status:** Internal strategic research; competitor observations are based on publicly visible methodology pages and should not be mistaken for a full technical crawl or verified traffic data.

## Executive finding

The strongest visible differentiators in the sampled casino-review competitors are not simply word count. They disclose how licence eligibility is checked, what the reviewer actually tested, who signs off the review, how scoring works, how commercial relationships are separated from editorial judgement, and when facts were last refreshed. BeInCasinos should compete on evidence quality, country-specific eligibility, transparent review methodology, and fast maintenance—not on publishing 100 thin brand pages.

## What appears to matter most

1. **Licence-first eligibility.** Verify the exact domain, product, contracting entity, licence status and market before listing or recommending a casino. Global licence badges are not a substitute for country-specific authorisation.
2. **Evidence transparency.** Distinguish first-party policy claims, regulator records, independent observation and hands-on tests. Never say “we tested withdrawals” unless an actual documented test exists.
3. **Scoring consistency.** Publish weights and a reproducible rubric. Keep commercial relationships outside the score calculation; disclose affiliate links near relevant links and in a global policy.
4. **Named accountability.** Every review needs an author/reviewer, a second-editor or compliance sign-off, an “as checked” date and correction contact/process.
5. **Terms and payments detail.** Explain eligibility, wagering, expiry, game exclusions, max cashout, withdrawal limits, fees, verification and dispute escalation rather than repeating marketing headlines.
6. **Responsible gambling as a substantive section.** Cover local self-exclusion systems, deposit limits, time-outs, account closure and help resources. Do not use a high score as encouragement to gamble.
7. **Freshness and re-review triggers.** Recheck when a licence, domain, bonus, withdrawal clause, regulatory decision or complaint pattern changes. Show what changed.
8. **Useful market-specific pages.** Country-specific pages should not imply an operator is available everywhere. Where the legal position is uncertain, hold or exclude it instead of targeting a misleading keyword.
9. **Avoid templated thin pages.** Each brand page should add specific, source-backed information about its operator, terms, cashier, game mix, safer-gambling tools and unresolved caveats. A unique logo and swapped brand name are not unique value.
10. **Structured internal links.** Build useful relationships among brand reviews, licence explainers, bonus-term explainers, payment guides, safer-gambling resources and complaint/ADR guidance. Use breadcrumb and appropriate structured data only when page content meets the relevant requirements.

## Proposed BeInCasinos review framework

### Gate 0 — market and licence
- Exact target country and product are named.
- Current regulator record checked and archived.
- Domain and trading name mapped to the contracting legal entity.
- Licence scope covers the product and jurisdiction.
- Ineligible or unresolved brands are excluded from recommendation and kept out of public “best” rankings.

### Gate 1 — evidence collection
- Current customer terms and dated offer terms captured.
- Deposit/withdrawal methods, limits, fees and verification conditions captured.
- Safer-gambling, local self-exclusion, complaint and ADR routes captured.
- Relevant regulator decisions read at source and attributed to the correct licensee.
- Screenshots/notes record what was actually tested and when; no real-money tests unless authorised, lawful, safe and approved by policy.

### Gate 2 — scoring (proposed starting rubric; pilot and calibrate before public use)
- Licensing, ownership and regulatory transparency: 25%
- Safer-gambling and customer protection: 20%
- Terms and bonus transparency: 15%
- Payments/withdrawal policy clarity: 15%
- Games/product information and software transparency: 10%
- Support and complaints/ADR transparency: 10%
- Usability/accessibility and mobile experience: 5%

**Evidence cap:** Any untested dimension is marked “not tested” and must not receive a fabricated empirical score. Licence failure is a hard gate, not something a bonus score can compensate for. Do not use a total score until the rubric has been tested on a representative pilot set and approved.

### Gate 3 — editorial and commercial independence
- Named author and second reviewer.
- Commercial/affiliate disclosure.
- No paid placement or operator veto over a score.
- Conflicts recorded; corrections and material changes logged.
- Publication decision approved by editorial/compliance owner.

### Gate 4 — publish and maintain
- Visible “last checked” and “last updated” dates are distinct where appropriate.
- Internal research files stay out of public navigation and sitemaps.
- Public pages use canonical URLs, useful title/meta descriptions, descriptive headings, crawlable internal links and only accurate structured data.
- Sitemap contains canonical, indexable, useful pages only; no drafts, search pages, thin tag archives or hold-status pages.
- Monitor indexation, canonical selection, crawl errors, impressions, clicks and query/page pairs; do not treat “request indexing” as a guarantee.
- Re-review on material terms/licence/regulatory changes and on a documented schedule.

## SEO work plan

### Priority 1 — protect trust and index quality
- Resolve domain canonicalisation and redirects before scaling content.
- Audit robots.txt, XML sitemap, canonicals, noindex rules, status codes, internal links, metadata, structured data and mobile rendering.
- Keep internal research drafts and unapproved candidates outside the public sitemap.
- Add author/reviewer and methodology pages before claiming “expert tested”.

### Priority 2 — build topic clusters with genuine utility
- Core: country-specific casino review index; methodology; licensing guide.
- Support: bonus wagering explained; withdrawal verification/limits; complaint and ADR guide; self-exclusion/deposit limits; game-provider transparency.
- Brand pages: only where exact local licence/domain evidence and meaningful unique analysis exist.
- Comparison pages: explain the criteria and country scope; do not create near-duplicate pages for every synonym.

### Priority 3 — measure and iterate
- Use Search Console query/page data, analytics and a keyword tool to validate the 1,000-row seed backlog.
- No search volume, CPC, difficulty or ranking claims are invented in the accompanying CSV.
- Prioritise queries by intent, relevance, SERP opportunity, legal eligibility and ability to produce a better answer.
- Consolidate overlapping pages and add internal links based on actual query data.

## Limitations and next research actions

This is a desk audit of public methodology pages, not a full competitor crawl. Next, collect a controlled sample of 10–20 actual review URLs from each priority competitor; compare title/H1, page depth, review freshness, author, rubric, affiliate disclosure, schema, indexability, internal links and page speed. Record evidence and date. Do not copy competitor wording or proprietary assets.

**Decision:** Adopt transparent methodology, evidence provenance, jurisdiction gates and re-review controls as BeInCasinos differentiators. Keep all candidate brand pages on HOLD until their market/operator checks and source packs are complete.
