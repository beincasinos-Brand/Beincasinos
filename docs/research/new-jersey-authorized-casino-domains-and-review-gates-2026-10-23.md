# New Jersey authorized casino domains — evidence register and editorial gates

**Checked:** 23 October 2026  
**Status:** Internal research aid; not a legal opinion or approval to publish individual reviews.

## Source of truth

The New Jersey Division of Gaming Enforcement (DGE) states that internet-gaming approvals are issued on a rolling basis and that a site absent from its authorized-sites list is **not approved** to offer internet gaming in New Jersey. The live DGE list is the first stop for the NJ domain/host mapping; it does not alone establish the precise contracting party, payment performance, bonus terms, or authorization in any other market.

Official list: https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/internet-gaming-sites/

## Required checks for every candidate

1. Capture the exact casino URL and test canonical/redirect behavior without bypassing location or age restrictions.
2. Save current terms, privacy policy, bonus/game rules, payment/withdrawal rules, account closure and complaint escalation with date checked.
3. Match legal operator, host casino, permit holder and platform provider to the wording in the current consumer terms. Do not infer one entity from a parent-company brand.
4. Verify regulator status for the exact product and market immediately before publication.
5. Separate written withdrawal policy from observed payout experience. If no real test was performed, say so.
6. Document safer-gambling tools, self-exclusion, limits, complaint process and regulator escalation using current sources.
7. Require an independent editor and compliance review; include affiliate disclosures and no paid score/rank changes.
8. Keep incomplete packs internal and out of public navigation/sitemaps. Do not count them as completed reviews.

## Player-protection and dispute notes

The DGE's Internet Gaming Information page says a player must first complain to the casino and receive a response before filing an internet dispute form with the Division: https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/internet-gaming-information/

The DGE's responsible-gaming guidance describes requirements such as persistent access to player-protection information, visible support resources, account closure/refund pathways and self-imposed deposit, spend and time limits: https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/responsible-gaming-main/

These regulatory requirements are useful criteria for a market-specific review; they are not evidence that a particular brand's current interface was independently tested.

## Editorial decision

**Do not publish from this register alone.** Use it to choose candidates and then complete a source pack for each brand. The official DGE list supports a New Jersey domain/host match only, not a universal safety rating, bonus recommendation, payout-speed claim, or authorization in another jurisdiction.
