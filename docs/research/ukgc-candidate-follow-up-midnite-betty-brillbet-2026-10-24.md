# UKGC candidate follow-up — Midnite, Betty and Brillbet

**Checked:** 24 October 2026  
**Status:** internal research only; all candidates remain HOLD. No consumer review or approval is claimed.

## Source and scope rule

The UK Gambling Commission's [Public Register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/page/public-register) is the primary source for GB licence status and regulatory actions. Its register states that domain names and trading names are supplied by licensees and may not be guaranteed accurate. A licence entry alone does not establish that a specific live product is available, that its customer contract names the same entity, or that the product merits a positive review.

Then capture dated terms, bonuses, payments/withdrawals, complaints/ADR, safer-gambling controls, privacy/cookies, accessibility, enforcement and image-rights evidence; arrange independent review.
