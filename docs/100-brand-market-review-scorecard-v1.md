# BeInCasinos casino review scorecard v1

**Status:** editorial standard; not a licence opinion or a claim that any brand has been tested. Apply only after collecting dated evidence for the exact brand and market.

## Scoring rule

Score each category from 0 to 5, then multiply by its weight. Overall score is out of 100. If a category lacks evidence, mark it **Not assessed** and hold the overall score; do not silently assign a neutral score. A low score can be published only when its evidence and rationale are shown. Never use a numeric rating as decoration.

| Category | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Legal clarity and market eligibility | 20% | Current official regulator record, exact domain/operator match, product scope, market restrictions |
| Terms and bonus transparency | 15% | Current terms, wagering rules, expiry, max bet, excluded games, withdrawal restrictions |
| Payments and withdrawal transparency | 15% | Official deposit/withdrawal pages, supported methods, limits, fees, stated processing times |
| Player protection | 15% | Age gate, self-exclusion, time/deposit/loss limits, reality checks, support and complaint/ADR route |
| Games and product information | 10% | Verifiable categories/providers and market-specific catalogue evidence |
| Platform and accessibility | 10% | Direct browser/device checks documented with date, accessibility basics and performance observations |
| Customer support transparency | 5% | Contact channels, hours and independently repeatable support test if claimed |
| Ownership and accountability | 5% | Legal entity, privacy notice, complaints escalation and corporate disclosures |
| Editorial evidence quality | 5% | Dated primary sources, cross-checks, unresolved issues and independent editor sign-off |

## Anchors for 0–5

- **0 — Material failure:** reliable evidence of a serious shortcoming or required proof absent after a documented attempt. Explain which one; do not confuse “not found” with proof of misconduct.
- **1 — Very weak:** important gaps or major friction demonstrated by evidence.
- **2 — Below standard:** material limitations, accurately described.
- **3 — Meets baseline:** clear evidence of basic expectations.
- **4 — Strong:** evidence supports above-baseline performance.
- **5 — Excellent:** unusually clear, complete and independently corroborated evidence.
- **Not assessed:** evidence insufficient; overall score remains unpublished.

## Non-negotiable gates

1. Any unresolved legal-entity/domain mismatch, missing market permission, or unclear product scope means **HOLD**.
2. A bonus amount, wagering requirement, game count, payout speed, support response time or user rating cannot be guessed.
3. Do not call an offer “best” without a defined comparison set, dates and consistent criteria.
4. Never present affiliate commission, listing payment or commercial status as a quality signal.
5. Disclose affiliate relationships next to relevant commercial links. Paid placement must be labelled clearly and cannot buy an editorial score.
6. Hands-on test claims require a dated test log: device/browser, account state, steps, observed result and limitations.
7. A brand's overall score is market-specific. Do not copy scores between GB, CH, DE, NL, SE, ES, IT, FR, BE, AU, CA or US states.

## Required score explanation

For every assessed category record: score, evidence URL, access/check date, concise rationale, known limitation, reviewer initials. An independent editor must challenge high-impact claims and any score of 4 or 5 before approval.
