## Executive finding

Dabble's UK-facing site advertises a sportsbook and an in-app casino. The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) public register records Dabble Sports UK Ltd (account 65677), the active trading name “dabble”, active domain entries for `dabble.co.uk` and `dabble.uk`, and an active remote casino licence plus remote real-event and virtual-event betting permissions. The UK-facing casino page states that Dabble Sports UK Ltd is licensed by the UKGC. This is sufficient for a provisional identity/licence match, subject to rechecking the live legal terms and exact domain before publication. It is **not** enough to approve a consumer recommendation or a scored review.

## Official-site observations (not independently tested)

- The site promotes a new-customer offer of £10 in free bets after a qualifying £10+ single real-money bet within seven days of registration; it says free bets are credited within 24 hours after settlement and expire after seven days. The full promotional terms must be captured and checked before any offer assessment.
- The official casino page describes access through the Dabble app and refers to slots, live dealer tables and an in-house crash-style game. These are operator marketing statements, not independent quality findings.
- The page says game RTP is displayed and references deposit, loss and time limits. Availability and operation of these controls have not been usability-tested in a real-money account and must not be described as independently verified.
