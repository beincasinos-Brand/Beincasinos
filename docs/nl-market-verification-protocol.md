# Netherlands (NL) market verification protocol

**Checked:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** internal research protocol only; no Dutch brand is approved for public review by this document.

## Primary official sources

1. Kansspelautoriteit (Ksa), Kansspelwijzer — https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/kansspelwijzer  
   The regulator describes this as the overview of operators licensed to offer gambling in the Netherlands. The interactive register must be searched by operator, website, app or licence number. A search-engine snippet, third-party list, application, supplier approval or another country's licence is not a substitute.
2. Ksa, Q&A on online gambling licences — https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/vraag-antwoord-vergunning-online-kansspelen  
   The Ksa says only a licence granted under Article 31a of the Dutch Betting and Gaming Act (Wet op de kansspelen; KOA licence) authorises online gambling in the Netherlands. It also explains that one licence holder may operate multiple brands/domains; brand count must therefore not be mistaken for distinct licensed operators.
3. Ksa, information for players — https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/voor-spelers  
   Directs consumers to check the app, website or casino in the Kansspelwijzer and provides official safer-play / CRUKS context.
4. Ksa, licence-holder register overview (English) — https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/register-licensees  
   Additional regulator guidance on checking a company, website, app or licence number.

## Required evidence for each NL candidate

- Exact public-facing brand and domain as shown by the live Kansspelwijzer; capture the regulator entry and date checked.
- Exact legal licence holder and licence scope; check whether the brand/domain is actually linked to that holder rather than inferred from corporate names or marketing similarity.
- Current official terms for bonuses, wagering, deposits, withdrawals, limits, fees, identity checks, complaints and account closure.
- Netherlands-specific product eligibility and restrictions, checked against current Ksa sources and operator terms.
- Current responsible-gambling controls, local support and CRUKS information, accurately described.
- Ksa enforcement and public decisions searched for the exact licence holder and any connected trading name.
- Commercial relationship and affiliate disclosure recorded separately from editorial assessment.
- Source links, access date, evidence excerpts, unresolved conflicts and independent second review.

## Decision rules

- **HOLD** if the live official register does not clearly match the domain, brand and legal entity.
- **REJECT for NL coverage** if current official evidence does not establish the required Dutch permission for the product being discussed. Do not infer permission from a UKGC, Malta, Curaçao, Swiss or other foreign register.
- Do not claim a licence guarantees fair treatment, fast withdrawals, a good customer experience or suitability.
- Do not publish brand scores, rankings, bonuses, ratings, player testimonials or hands-on testing claims without evidence and an actual documented review.
- Dutch-market pages require their own legal, advertising and affiliate review before release; this protocol is not legal advice.
