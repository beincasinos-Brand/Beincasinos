## Material regulatory history — must be disclosed accurately

On **30 June 2026**, the UKGC published a settlement requiring Petfre (Gibraltar) Limited to make a **£900,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty**, plus Commission costs and a public statement, after a review found deficiencies in remote customer-interaction/social-responsibility controls. The Commission described failures to promptly re-interact with customers showing strong harm indicators, including a case in which a customer lost £17,900 within 24 hours without an additional interaction. The Commission also said the licensee had implemented interim mitigating controls and delivered an action plan. Present both the findings and the stated remedial steps; do not imply that a historical settlement alone proves the current product is unsafe, nor omit it from a review.

The register also shows a separate **20 May 2025** £240,000 financial penalty for a technical-standards breach following a key-event report. Keep this action separate from the 2026 social-responsibility settlement; do not merge dates or rationales.
