# BeInCasinos — casino review production standard

**Status:** Internal editorial specification; not a published review and not legal advice.  
**Applies to:** Each of the 100 target brand reviews.  
**Publication gate:** Keep a profile unpublished until the evidence checklist is complete and an independent reviewer signs it off.

## Required article structure

1. **Title and review metadata**
   - Exact brand and exact product (online casino, not sportsbook unless explicitly in scope).
   - Target jurisdiction and access date; review author and independent checker.
   - “Last checked” date, evidence confidence, and clear note if no account testing occurred.
2. **At-a-glance decision**
   - Jurisdiction-specific availability and eligibility, based on the regulator’s current record.
   - Who operates the service, licence/authorisation type, and official regulator link.
   - No “best”, “safe”, “licensed” or ranking claims without defined, sourced criteria.
3. **Licence and legal scope**
   - Match brand + exact domain + legal entity + casino permission.
   - Link directly to the official register entry, not merely a regulator homepage.
   - State exclusions and uncertainty plainly. Never generalise a state/province licence nationally.
4. **Games and providers**
   - Only name games/providers observed on the official site and record the access date.
   - Distinguish catalogue claims from independently tested availability.
5. **Bonus and promotion terms**
   - Quote the exact offer date, eligibility, wagering requirements, game weighting, max bet, expiry, withdrawal caps and restrictions.
   - If any term cannot be confirmed from current official terms, mark “not verified”; do not infer.
6. **Deposits, withdrawals and fees**
   - Separate listed payment methods from tested transactions.
   - Record min/max amounts, fees, processing windows, identity checks and source URLs.
   - Never claim a payout speed from marketing copy as a personal test result.
7. **Account, support and safer gambling**
   - Document account/KYC requirements, support channels/hours, deposit/time limits, self-exclusion, reality checks and help resources.
   - Do not imply protections work in practice unless directly tested and documented.
8. **Complaints and enforcement**
   - Search the relevant regulator’s enforcement and public notices; state date range and limitations.
   - Separate confirmed regulator action from allegations, user reports and unresolved complaints.
9. **Pros, cons and who should avoid it**
   - Tie every material point to observed evidence. No generic filler, fabricated user experiences, or invented scores.
10. **Verdict, sources and disclosure**
   - Explain the evidence-based conclusion and what remains unknown.
   - Provide dated primary-source citations and a corrections/contact route.
   - Disclose affiliate relationships near the recommendation and explain whether compensation affects placement.

## Minimum evidence package per brand

- [ ] Official domain resolved and identity matched.
- [ ] Exact legal operator verified.
- [ ] Current, jurisdiction-specific casino authorisation verified in the official register.
- [ ] Official terms, bonus terms, payments/withdrawals and safer-gambling pages archived or cited with access dates.
- [ ] Enforcement/complaints search recorded, including “no result found” and the search scope.
- [ ] Screenshots or notes support every first-hand claim; no fabricated testing.
- [ ] Brand image asset has a recorded source page and reuse permission/licence, or is replaced with a clearly labelled original editorial illustration.
- [ ] Reviewer checks factual accuracy, jurisdiction scope, affiliate disclosure and responsible-gambling language.
- [ ] Independent second-person sign-off and publication status recorded.

## Evidence and scoring rules

- **A:** Current official primary evidence directly supports the claim.
- **B:** Strong primary evidence exists but a material detail remains incomplete.
- **C:** Discovery lead / secondary evidence only; not publication-ready.
- **HOLD:** Any unresolved operator, domain, authorisation, key-term or source gap.
- **EXCLUDE:** Product/jurisdiction is outside the editorial scope or evidence contradicts eligibility.
- **APPROVED:** All mandatory checks complete and independently signed off. Only this status may appear as a published review.

Do not convert the number of tracker rows into a number of approved reviews. Research leads, evidence notes and draft copy are not completed reviews.
