## Findings

- UKGC public-register account **65519** lists **Bonne Terre Gaming Limited** with active remote casino and bingo permissions. The regulator's domain subregister lists `www.skyvegas.com` and `m.skyvegas.com` as active; its trading-name register lists `sky vegas` as active.
- Sky Vegas's own licensing and regulation help page says casino games, bingo and poker are carried out by Bonne Terre Gaming Limited, licensed by the UK Gambling Commission for UK and Isle of Man customers. Sports betting is separately carried out by SBG Sports Limited. Do not attribute the sportsbook licence/entity to the casino product.
- First-party general terms section 2.1–2.3 defines “we/our/us” for Sky Vegas and the related games products as Bonne Terre Gaming Limited and gives UKGC licence number 065519. The terms also say general terms take precedence over product game rules if there is a conflict.
- The regulator also shows an older, distinct entity **Bonne Terre Limited** (account 38718) with its remote permissions surrendered effective 31 October 2025 and inactive Sky trading names. This old record must not be mistaken for the active account 65519.
- First-party support material on customer funds says funds exceeding active customer balances are held in a specific bank account separate from the company's other operating accounts and describes the UKGC protection rating as “Medium”; it also explicitly says there is no absolute guarantee that all funds will be repaid. If this is used in a review, preserve the caveat and verify the current exact wording.
- A third-party page surfaced a welcome-offer summary last checked 14 October 2026, but this internal evidence pack does **not** treat it as authoritative or recommend repeating the offer. A specific promotion needs its official Sky Vegas offer page, eligibility, wagering/staking, expiry and game restrictions captured at review time.
