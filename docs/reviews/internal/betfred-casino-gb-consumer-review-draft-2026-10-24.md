## Material regulatory context

### 30 June 2026 — £900,000 settlement

The UKGC reported a settlement requiring Petfre (Gibraltar) Limited to pay **£900,000** after an investigation into social-responsibility controls. The regulator described weaknesses in identifying harm indicators, ensuring timely intervention when strong indicators appeared, and a process that could prevent a customer flagged for a safer-gambling review from being flagged for another review for seven days. The regulator cited one example in which a customer lost £17,900 within 24 hours without an additional interaction.

The regulator also stated that the licensee swiftly implemented interim mitigating controls and subsequently delivered an action plan and took steps to assure the Commission that its current operating model met requirements. The review must report both the findings and the stated remediation; do not imply current failures persist without newer evidence, and do not treat regulator statements as a substitute for testing the present product.

### 20 May 2025 — £240,000 technical-standards penalty

The UKGC record says Petfre was fined **£240,000** for a breach of Remote Technical Standards. The regulator's October 2025 summary described affected online slot games that failed to display the consumer's net position and games that celebrated losses as wins. The regulator stated the licensee identified and reported the breach, took immediate remedial action and decommissioned the affected games.
