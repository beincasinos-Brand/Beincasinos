## Regulatory record: material context

The regulator's record contains three financial penalties relevant to 888 UK Limited:

- **£23,000 — 19 August 2024:** a breach of the licence condition on credit-card use. The Commission recorded that the operator self-identified and reported the breach, took immediate remedial action, and cooperated.
- **£33,075 — 7 August 2025:** a failure involving the identification of customers across accounts held with group companies. The Commission also recorded self-reporting, remediation and cooperation.
- **£86,843 — 18 November 2025:** a breach of Social Responsibility Code Provision 3.5.3 relating to remote self-exclusion. The Commission described the failure as human error and noted that it was self-reported and remediated. It also stated that controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling were not affected.

These are material findings and should be disclosed clearly, with dates and context. It would be inaccurate to describe the operator as having a clean regulatory record; it would also be inaccurate to imply that the self-exclusion incident allowed excluded customers to gamble when the Commission's published account says the relevant access controls were not affected. The review must distinguish documented historic findings from any claim about current practices.

Primary source: [UK Gambling Commission — 888 UK Limited regulatory actions](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/actions/39028).
