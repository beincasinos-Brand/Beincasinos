## Decision summary

The official Gambling Commission register confirms that **Ladbrokes Betting & Gaming Limited** (account **1611**) has an active remote Casino licence, reference **001611-R-319348-018**, shown as active from 1 November 2014 to current. A separate licensee, **LC International Limited** (account **54743**), also has an active remote Casino licence **054743-R-330863-015** and lists **ladbrokes.com** as an active domain. The register search associates the Ladbrokes trading name with both entities. This is a material operator/domain ambiguity, so do not infer that one entity or one domain alone governs the exact GB consumer casino journey without additional first-party evidence.

The official register's domain-names page for account 1611 rendered the entity heading but did not expose the domain table in the research extraction. The Ladbrokes first-party homepage and attempted terms, responsible-gambling, and privacy URLs returned HTTP 403 in this research environment. These are access limitations, not proof that pages do not exist.

**Do not publish a scored review or label this brand “verified” yet.** This evidence pack is internal research, not a consumer-facing review and not a substitute for editorial/legal approval.
