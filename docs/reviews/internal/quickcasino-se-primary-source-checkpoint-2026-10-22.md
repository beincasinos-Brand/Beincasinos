## Primary-source finding

The Swedish Gambling Authority (Spelinspektionen) has a domain-specific licence-register page for `quickcasino.se`. The page identifies **Swedix Limited** as the licence holder and lists “Kommersiellt vadhållning” and “Kommersiellt online” as valid from **15 January 2026 through 14 January 2031**.

Official source: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/licensurl/?url=quickcasino.se

The regulator explains that its licence register lists operators with valid licences/permits and includes website addresses for online-gambling and betting licences: https://www2.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/

## What this does and does not establish

- It is direct regulator evidence that the exact domain `quickcasino.se` is listed against Swedix Limited and that the displayed online licence period is current on the research date.
- The register label “Kommersiellt online” alone should not be treated as a complete product-by-product assessment. Confirm the exact authorised product scope and any conditions from the regulator record before describing this as a casino-specific approval.
- A licence listing is not a quality endorsement and does not establish that the operator complies with every obligation in practice.
- No account was opened, no deposits or withdrawals were made, and no game, promotion or customer-support journey was tested.
