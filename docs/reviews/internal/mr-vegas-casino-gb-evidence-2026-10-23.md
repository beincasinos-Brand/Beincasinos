## Historical regulatory actions — material context

The UKGC announced a **£2 million settlement on 15 June 2023** after social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures at Videoslots Limited. The Commission stated that the operator ran, among other sites, `mrvegas.com`. The described issues included delayed reviews of customers displaying risk behaviours and delayed AML/source-of-funds action.

On **20 November 2025**, the UKGC announced a further **£650,000 financial penalty**, warning and licence condition after findings relating to the period October 2023 to February 2024. The announcement explicitly named Videoslots Limited's `mrvegas.com` alongside its other websites. The findings included ineffective monthly deposit-limit calculations in tested cases, inadequate customer interactions, and gaps in AML monitoring involving digital prepaid vouchers and withdrawal patterns. The regulator said the operator cooperated and took steps to remedy the issues, and required an independent third-party audit.
