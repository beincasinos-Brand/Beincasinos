# Betway Spain — DGOJ operator and casino-permission evidence (internal)

**Research date:** 23 October 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** Spain only  
**Status:** HOLD — not publication-approved  
**Hands-on testing:** None. No account, payments, gameplay, withdrawal, KYC or support tests were performed.

## Regulator and exact domain
The Spanish Directorate General for the Regulation of Gambling (DGOJ) profile identifies **BETWAY SPAIN, S.A.** (commercial brand Betway) and lists **https://www.betway.es** as its domain. Its current profile lists general licences for “Otros juegos” and betting, plus singular permissions for counterparty sports betting, other counterparty bets, horse-racing bets, **Black Jack, roulette and gaming machines**.
- DGOJ profile (English): https://www.ordenacionjuego.es/en/operadores-juego/operadores-licencia/operadores/betway-spain-plc
- DGOJ profile (Spanish): https://www.ordenacionjuego.es/operadores-juego/operadores-licencia/operadores/betway-spain-sa

This is Spain-specific evidence only. Do not conflate Spanish authorisation with permissions in other markets or assume that a list of licences proves that every game is available to every user.

## Recent/important technical and licence decisions
The DGOJ profile lists a **3 September 2025** decision for second technical-system homologation associated with the general betting licence and singular gaming-machine licence. The profile also records:
- 11 October 2024 extension of the general betting licence;
- 27 September 2024 extension of the gaming-machine licence;
- 5 April 2024 extension of the singular blackjack and roulette licences;
- 9 February 2023 homologation of the technical system for “Otros juegos”, roulette and blackjack.
The DGOJ's official 2025 technical homologation document is available through the authority's record: https://www.ordenacionjuego.es/en/cmis/document/alfresco/7f6455ee-1ba5-4223-92de-99a7906c99a8
