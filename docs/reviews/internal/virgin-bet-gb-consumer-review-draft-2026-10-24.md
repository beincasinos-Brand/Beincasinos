## Brand and operator identity

This draft covers Virgin Bet's Great Britain site, not Virgin Games or unrelated Virgin-branded products. Before publication, confirm the current legal operator displayed in Virgin Bet's live GB terms and match it to the UK Gambling Commission's current public register. A shared Virgin trademark does not mean Virgin Bet and Virgin Games have the same operator, licence or regulatory history.

Primary starting points:
- Official Virgin Bet site: https://www.virginbet.com/
- UKGC public register search: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register
- UKGC public register guidance: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register

**Identity gate:** This draft deliberately does not assert a licence account number or active status without a confirmed current account/domain match in the source record. Capture the legal operator name and account number from the live terms and public register before making a licensing claim or counting this brand as verified.

## Safer gambling and customer support — open checks

Before approval, verify and capture current information about deposit limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, GAMSTOP, reality checks, customer-interaction procedures, account closure and complaints escalation. Verify the currently named Alternative Dispute Resolution provider and escalation route. Policy text alone does not demonstrate that controls work in practice.

## Regulatory record — must be scoped to the correct legal entity

Once the legal operator is confirmed, inspect its UKGC business record, domain list, trading names and regulatory action history. If the operator is part of a wider group, distinguish entity-specific actions from group-level reporting and avoid attributing an action to Virgin Bet unless the regulator's record supports that link. Recheck the register immediately before sign-off.

Sources:
- UKGC business register: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register
- UKGC regulatory actions: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-actions
