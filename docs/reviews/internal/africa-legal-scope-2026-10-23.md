## South Africa: separate licensed sports betting from online casino

South African government consumer guidance published in June 2026 says online gambling is illegal except bets placed through online betting platforms of licensed operators, and states that online casino games and offshore platforms not licensed in South Africa operate unlawfully. It directs consumers to the National Gambling Board (NGB) verified operators portal and says operators should display a valid provincial licence.

- Government consumer guidance: https://www.vukuzenzele.gov.za/how-protect-yourself-illegal-online-gambling
- NGB verified operators portal: https://www.ngb.org.za/verified-operators/
- National Treasury sector risk assessment (2025): https://www.treasury.gov.za/comm_media/press/2025/Discussion%20Paper%20-%20National%20Gambling%20Tax.pdf
