## Relevant official enforcement history

The UKGC published a 23 March 2023 enforcement announcement covering **two separate operators**: 32Red Limited and Platinum Gaming Limited, then both part of Kindred Group. The Commission stated a combined £7.1 million penalty package; the stated amounts were £4,195,655 for 32Red Limited and £2,937,599 for Platinum Gaming Limited. It also described social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failings, including delayed identification of potential gambling harm, superficial customer interactions and weaknesses in financial/source-of-funds controls. Official announcement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/gambling-commission-fines-32red-and-platinum-gaming-gbp7-1m
