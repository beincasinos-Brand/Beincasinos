## BeInCasinos BE decision

1. Treat Belgium as a precise domain-and-licence market. A general international licence or the presence of a brand name is not enough.
2. Verify the exact current .be domain and its A+ licence record, then reconcile the consumer-facing brand and contracting entity against the current terms.
3. For Betano Casino, treat 31 December 2026 as a mandatory re-check point; do not assume renewal from the currently displayed expiry date.
4. Do not transfer a licence association from one brand/domain to another similar domain or to a foreign version of the same brand.
5. Keep all candidates HOLD / NOT SCORED until current terms, bonus, cashier/withdrawals, complaints/ADR, safer gambling, privacy, image rights, and independent editorial review are completed.
6. Do not add bonus amounts, scores, ratings, or claims of hands-on testing based on a licence table.
