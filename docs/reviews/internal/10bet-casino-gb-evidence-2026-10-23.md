## Regulatory history

On **16 February 2023**, the Gambling Commission announced that Blue Star Planet Limited, trading as 10Bet, would pay **£620,000** after an investigation identified social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. The Commission said the operator ran 10bet.co.uk and that the settlement amount would go to socially responsible causes. The finding must be attributed to the licensee, with the date and scope retained; it should not be represented as proof of the operator's current state or omitted from a balanced review.
