## Decision summary

The official UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) register identifies **Gamesys Operations Limited**, account **38905**, with an **Active Remote Casino licence 038905-R-319430-023**, shown from 1 November 2014 to current. The UKGC domain register lists `virgingames.com` as **White Label** under this licensee. This is a real, useful regulatory/domain link, but a White Label status is not a standalone finding that the brand is an independent licensee. Keep the legal licensee and consumer brand distinct in any review.

The official UKGC regulatory-actions page records a decision dated **22 November 2023** against Gamesys Operations Limited: a **£6,071,292 financial penalty**, a warning and an additional licence condition following findings relating to anti-money-laundering controls and customer interaction during November 2021–July 2022. The Commission stated the licensee cooperated and took corrective steps, and found no evidence of criminal monies in the specific customers it examined. Any eventual review must describe the scope and period precisely, not present the action as a current finding that every Virgin Games transaction is unsafe.

Virgin Games' first-party site links to current Terms, Privacy Policy, Cookies, Help/Contact Us and Responsible Gambling pages. The first-party site describes session reminders/spend limits and a GamProtect page. These pages have not yet been fully archived clause-by-clause in this pack. The current welcome offer, its complete conditions, payments/withdrawals, complaint/ADR details, and present legal-operator wording still need verification.

**Decision: HOLD.** Do not publish a scored review, make “best/safest” claims, or mark this brand verified until all required evidence and independent editorial/legal checks are complete.
