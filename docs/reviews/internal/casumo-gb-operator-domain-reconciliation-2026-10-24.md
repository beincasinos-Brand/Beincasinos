# Casumo — Great Britain operator and domain reconciliation

**Status:** HOLD — internal research only; not an approved review.  
**Research date:** 24 October 2026.  
**Scope:** Casumo-branded online casino in Great Britain.

## Key finding: distinguish the legacy brand record from the current operator record

The UK Gambling Commission public register search for Casumo returns two records:

- **Casumo Services Limited**, account **39265**: the Casino Remote licence is shown as surrendered, with the licence ending 3 November 2023. Its register domain record shows `casumo.com` inactive.
- **Recro Limited**, account **61549**: Casino Remote licence **061549-R-336718-003** is shown active from 21 December 2023 to current, and `casumo.com` is shown as an active domain.

The Commission's published FOI response specifically says Casumo does not have a licence in its own right and that the Casumo website covered by that request is run by licensed operator Recro Limited. That statement is stronger than inferring the operator from a shared domain alone, but it should still be rechecked against the live GB customer terms before publication.

## Editorial guardrails

- Do not present Casumo Services Limited's surrendered licence as the current licence for the site.
- Do not infer that the brand is unlicensed merely because the older entity's licence is surrendered; the register and UKGC FOI response identify Recro Limited as the operator of the website covered by that response.
- The absence of recorded regulatory actions for Recro Limited in the register is not proof that the product has no risks or that every consumer-facing control has been tested.
- Do not invent current offers, payment methods, withdrawal speeds, game counts, ratings or personal testing.
- This research note does not establish that the current site is suitable for every player.
