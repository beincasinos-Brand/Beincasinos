## What this source proves — and what it does not
- It supports that the ESBK lists these ten platforms as Swiss online-casino platforms as of the source page published 17 March 2026.
- It does not prove a particular welcome bonus is current, that all advertised games are permitted, that a withdrawal will succeed on a stated timetable, or that the platform passed a consumer usability test.
- ESBK notes that each online game needs authorisation; therefore game catalogue and product-specific claims require their own evidence. Current authorised game lists are published separately at https://www.esbk.admin.ch/en/safe-and-transparent-gambling/.
- The unauthorised-platform blocked list was updated 25 August 2026; check the latest list again at publication time. A casino's absence from that list is not, by itself, proof of authorisation.

## Review checklist required for each platform
1. Capture current first-party legal terms and identify the customer-contracting entity.
2. Match the exact domain, concession extension and responsible casino against the official ESBK page.
3. Check whether the specific games discussed are individually authorised; avoid broad catalogue claims without evidence.
4. Capture the current bonus landing page and full promotion terms, including wagering, expiry, maximum conversion, eligible games, deposit method exclusions and withdrawal rules. If any term is unavailable, say NOT VERIFIED and omit the offer from the public card.
5. Document deposit methods separately from withdrawals; capture fees, minimums, limits, processing times, verification requirements and exceptions. Website help pages are not cashier tests.
6. Capture responsible-gambling tools, self-exclusion, limits, breaks and support escalation routes.
7. Review privacy/data controller, complaint handling, accessibility, affiliate disclosure and image/logo usage rights.
8. Complete real-device consumer testing, second-person fact check, scoring evidence sheet and editorial/legal approval.
