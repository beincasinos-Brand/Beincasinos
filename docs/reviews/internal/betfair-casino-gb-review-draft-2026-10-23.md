## Regulatory history — 17 December 2025

The UKGC says its licence review followed a compliance assessment in April and May 2024 of four remote licensees trading under the Betfair and Paddy Power names: PPB Entertainment Limited, PPB Counterparty Services Limited, Betfair Casino Limited and TSE Malta LP. The Commission found failures to comply with parts of Social Responsibility Code Provision 3.4.3 relating to remote customer interaction. It announced a total payment in lieu of financial penalty of £2,000,000 across the four licensees.
