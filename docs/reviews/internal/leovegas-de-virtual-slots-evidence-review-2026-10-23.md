## Executive assessment

The GGL whitelist (marked updated 2 October 2026) lists **LVSports Limited / leovegas.de** under **Virtuelle Automatenspiele** with an initial permission date of 27 February 2023. The live German site resolves to https://www.leovegas.de/de-de/ and markets a slot-focused online Spielothek. The first-party imprint identifies LVSPORTS Limited, C94854, and names the GGL as supervisory authority. The spelling/capitalisation difference between “LVSports Limited” in the whitelist and “LVSPORTS Limited” in the imprint appears consistent but should be confirmed against the current licence record and customer contract.

The homepage currently advertises “up to €100 real-money bonus + 100 free spins” and describes the 100 free spins as “without wagering requirements.” The homepage also says “terms and conditions apply.” The full offer terms were not captured in this pass, so eligibility, qualifying deposit, expiry, maximum bet, excluded games, wagering on the cash bonus, withdrawal restrictions and the exact meaning of “without wagering requirements” remain unverified. Do not rate the offer as valuable or fair from the headline alone.

The first-party responsible-gambling page documents several tools, including daily/weekly/monthly deposit limits, loss limits and wagering limits, player history, time alerts and self-exclusion. The privacy notice was updated 7 July 2026. The cookie policy shows a last update of 3 October 2023, so its currency and any intervening consent changes require review. No numerical score or recommendation is assigned.
