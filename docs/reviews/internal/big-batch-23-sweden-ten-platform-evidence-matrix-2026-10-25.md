## Evidence checklist for each individual review
1. Save the dated official regulator record: licensee, exact website, category, status and dates.
2. Match the website's legal notice and terms to that same legal entity; resolve aliases and group-company structures.
3. Capture current bonus terms in Swedish: wagering, maximum bet, eligible games, expiry, deposit threshold, excluded payments and withdrawal restrictions. If incomplete, omit the bonus claim.
4. Verify deposit and withdrawal methods, fees, limits, processing times, identity checks and complaints routes separately.
5. Verify responsible-gambling tools, limits, time-outs, self-exclusion and the Swedish Spelpaus system. Do not claim a tool was tested unless it was.
6. Check privacy/cookies, support, accessibility, mobile usability, image rights and affiliate disclosure.
7. Complete real consumer testing where feasible, independent second review and documented editorial/legal approval before scoring.
