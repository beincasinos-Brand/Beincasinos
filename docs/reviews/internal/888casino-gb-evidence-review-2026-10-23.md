## Regulatory history — material negative evidence

The UKGC's account-level regulatory-action page records these financial penalties for 888 UK Limited:

1. **19 August 2024 — £23,000:** breach of licence condition 6.1.2 concerning use of credit cards. The regulator notes the operator self-identified and reported the breach and took remedial action.
2. **7 August 2025 — £33,075:** failure relating to identification of individual customers across accounts within a group company. The regulator notes self-reporting, remedial action and cooperation.
3. **18 November 2025 — £86,843:** breach of Social Responsibility Code Provision 3.5.3 concerning remote self-exclusion. The regulator notes that the issue was due to human error, did not affect controls that prevent self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling, and that remedial action was taken.

**Total of these three recorded penalties: £142,918.** This is an arithmetic sum of the three UKGC amounts above, not a statement about all historic regulatory costs. The most recent self-exclusion finding is directly relevant to a safety-focused casino review and must remain prominent and neutrally worded. These findings do not by themselves prove that the same failure is occurring now; the current control state has not been independently tested.
