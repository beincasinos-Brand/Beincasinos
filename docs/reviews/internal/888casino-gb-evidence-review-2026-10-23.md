## Provisional summary

The operator's own licensing disclosure says that the Great Britain service is operated by 888 UK Limited. These are useful identity and permission checks, but they do not establish the quality of the current product or customer experience.

The regulatory record requires prominent, balanced treatment: the Commission records financial penalties of £23,000 (August 2024, credit-card-use licence condition), £33,075 (August 2025, customer-identification requirements involving accounts across group companies), and £86,843 (November 2025, remote self-exclusion reporting/code compliance). The Commission's 2025 self-exclusion decision says the breach was due to human error, the operator self-identified and reported it, remedial action was taken, and controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling were not affected. This nuance should be retained rather than presenting either a clean record or an exaggerated claim.
