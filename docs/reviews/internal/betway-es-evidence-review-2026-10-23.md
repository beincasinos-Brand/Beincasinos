# Betway — Spain internal evidence review

**Research date:** 23 October 2026  
**Market:** Spain only  
**Status:** **HOLD — internal evidence review, not publication-ready**  
**Method:** DGOJ individual operator profile and a first-party access attempt. No account, deposit, withdrawal, support contact or real-money play was tested.

## Provisional assessment

The Dirección General de Ordenación del Juego (DGOJ) individual profile identifies **BETWAY SPAIN, S.A.** and directly lists **https://www.betway.es** as its domain. The profile shows singular licences for **Black Jack, Ruleta and Máquinas de azar** (slots), as well as betting categories. It records a second technical-system homologation on **3 September 2025** for the general betting licence and slots, a slots licence extension dated **27 September 2024**, and blackjack/roulette extensions dated **5 April 2024**. The profile marks the operator as adhered to the DGOJ phishing-alert service and says it has advertising authorisation.

A direct fetch of the operator homepage timed out in this pass, so the current Spanish customer terms, offers, payments, complaints, responsible-gambling tools, privacy/cookies and accessibility could not be inspected. This is a regulator-backed Spain-specific casino-vertical lead, not a completed consumer review.

**No score or recommendation is assigned.** Keep the row HOLD until current first-party evidence is captured and independently reviewed.
