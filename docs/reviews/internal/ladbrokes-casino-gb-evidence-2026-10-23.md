## Historic enforcement — do not collapse separate actions

On **17 August 2022**, the UKGC announced a **£17 million** regulatory settlement for Entain Group: **£14 million** related to failures at the online business **LC International Limited**, which ran 13 websites including ladbrokes.com, coral.co.uk and foxybingo.com; **£3 million** related to the separate **Ladbrokes Betting & Gaming Limited** operation running land-based gambling premises. The Commission also required additional licence conditions and a third-party audit.

On **31 July 2019**, the UKGC announced a **£5.9 million** penalty package following historic social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failings at Ladbrokes Coral Group covering November 2014 to October 2017. The announcement described failures across Ladbrokes and Coral and continued issues after their merger.
