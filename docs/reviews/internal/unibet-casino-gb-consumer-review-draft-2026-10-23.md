## Editorial summary

Unibet's Great Britain casino product should be assessed as a distinct product under the UK terms, not as a proxy for the wider international brand or its sportsbook. The available first-party terms identify Platinum Gaming Limited as the contracting party and state UK Gambling Commission licence 45322 for customers in Great Britain. The official terms also describe identity, age, address, location, financial-circumstance and source-of-funds checks; they state that restrictions, including withdrawal restrictions, can apply while verification is incomplete. These are contractual statements, not evidence that a particular customer's withdrawal was delayed or paid.

The operator's safer-gambling page describes several controls, including reality checks, activity information, deposit/net-deposit limits, product blocks, Time Out and GAMSTOP self-exclusion. This is useful published information, but availability, discoverability and actual behaviour have not been independently tested in this research pass.

**Consumer takeaway:** the evidence is enough to frame the questions a GB casino customer should check, but not enough to recommend Unibet, assign a score, advertise a current welcome offer, or make a claim about payout speed. This draft must remain internal until the verification checklist is closed.

The operator's terms describe verification checks covering identity, age, address, location, financial circumstances and source of funds. They also state that an account or withdrawal can be restricted while requested checks remain incomplete.

This must be presented as a disclosed contractual condition—not as proof that all customers experience a hold, a typical processing time, or a measured withdrawal outcome. We have not opened an account, deposited funds, requested a withdrawal or tested KYC in this pass.

Before publication, record the current terms version/effective date and verify the casino-specific rules, payment-method limits, fees, processing and pending periods, verification triggers, dormant-account rules, and any material differences by payment method.
