## Provisional summary

iGaming Ontario's regulated-market directory (listing accurate as of 1 September 2026) lists NorthStar Bets under NorthStar Gaming (Ontario) Inc. and links to northstarbets.ca. The operator's current homepage identifies AGCO registration OPIG1226485 and states that play is restricted to registered users aged 19+ physically located in Ontario. This supports an Ontario-specific candidate entry only; it is not evidence of authorisation across Canada.

The public help centre gives specific payment and responsible-gambling information. Stated withdrawal minimum is C$20; the operator says it has no maximum on its side, while financial-institution limits can apply and withdrawals above C$10,000 may take longer. Its help article says a withdrawal approval usually takes a few hours, may take up to 24 hours, and additional safety checks can take up to 72 hours in some cases. These are operator-stated process times, not independently tested outcomes.

A material caution for review writers: several search-visible promotional pages contain old or expired offer dates, including an offer page whose terms run through 31 May 2026 and another with a 13 January 2026 end date. Do not quote either as a live offer. Capture a live offer page and current terms at publication time, or omit promotional claims.

## Safer-gambling tools

Operator help article updated 3 February 2026:
https://support.northstarbets.ca/hc/en-ca/articles/6264866830865-What-responsible-gambling-tools-are-available-on-the-website

The help article describes:
- Daily, weekly and monthly deposit limits.
- Daily, weekly and monthly loss limits.
- Time-out, during which deposits and play are disabled while account access and support chat remain available.
- Self-exclusion for 6 months, 1 year or 5 years; the article says login and new-account creation are blocked during the selected period.
- Time-out and self-exclusion cannot be removed before the selected period ends.
- Limit decreases take effect immediately; increases have a 24-hour cooling-off period, according to https://support.northstarbets.ca/hc/en-ca/articles/6265067799569-How-can-I-increase-decrease-my-limits.

These are operator-described tools. The review must not claim that the flows were personally tested.
