## Material regulatory action — decision dated 22 November 2023

The UKGC found that Gamesys Operations Limited breached anti-money-laundering requirements and failed parts of its customer-interaction obligations between November 2021 and July 2022. The regulator imposed a warning, additional licence condition and a financial penalty totalling **£6,071,292** (including a divestment amount of £165,042). The UKGC also required a third-party audit to assess whether AML and safer-gambling policies, procedures and controls were being implemented effectively.

The regulator described weaknesses including:
- Some deposit-limit systems failing to identify risks quickly enough.
- Delayed or inadequate interactions with customers showing potential harm indicators.
- Inadequate records of customer interactions and decision rationales.
- AML triggers and due diligence that did not always identify risk promptly.

The UKGC said the licensee cooperated, advised it had taken corrective steps, and that it found no evidence of criminal monies being deposited by the specific customers reviewed.
