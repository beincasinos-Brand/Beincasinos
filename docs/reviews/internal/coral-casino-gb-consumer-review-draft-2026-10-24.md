## What can currently be verified

The UK Gambling Commission's public register identifies **LC International Limited** (account 54743) as the operator associated with Coral's Great Britain domain. The register lists an active Remote Casino licence, number **054743-R-330863-015**, from 1 July 2019 to the current date, and the domain search for `coral.co.uk` returns LC International Limited. The regulator's trading-name register lists **coral** as active under the same account. These records support the identity/licence starting point, but do not establish product quality, payment performance, current offer value or the effectiveness of consumer protections.

## Regulatory record — scope and limitations

The UKGC page for LC International Limited's regulatory actions returned **“No regulatory actions have been recorded for this business”** when checked on 24 October 2026: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/actions/54743

This is a finding about the current public-register page for this legal entity, not proof that every Coral-related entity or historical trading arrangement has a clean record. The UKGC's own register notes that domain and trading-name data is provided by gambling businesses and is not guaranteed by the Commission. Before approval, check the latest full register and relevant group/entity history again, and preserve the access date.

## Account verification and customer due diligence

Coral's official Advanced Player Protection page describes identity/address verification and possible source-of-funds requests. It states that identity documents may be submitted through its online uploader, mobile apps or email, and says document review normally takes no longer than 24 hours. This is an operator statement, not an independently timed test or guarantee.

Source: https://www.coral.co.uk/en/p/safer-gambling/advanced-player-protection

The final review should capture the page and applicable terms again on the approval date, including any conditions that can trigger further checks and what happens to withdrawals while verification is outstanding.

## Safer-gambling controls — published descriptions, not usability-tested

Coral's official Gambling Controls page describes:
- Daily, weekly and monthly deposit limits, including a distinction between deposit limits and net-spend limits.
- Limit decreases taking effect immediately and increases requiring a 24-hour confirmation period.
- Deposit curfews and reality checks.
- Play breaks of 15 minutes to 3 hours, with play/deposits blocked during the break.
- Product-specific account closure, time-outs and self-exclusion.
- Self-exclusion options covering Coral, Entain Group labels, or UK-registered online gambling sites through GAMSTOP.

Source: https://www.coral.co.uk/en/p/safer-gambling/gambling-controls

These are descriptions published by the operator. The draft does not claim that the controls were tested in a customer account, that their interface is accessible, or that settings propagate across every product in practice. The official “Our Approach” page says it was last reviewed in May 2025, so currentness and page version must be rechecked before publication: https://www.coral.co.uk/en/p/responsible-gaming/our-approach

## Provisional verdict

They do not yet support a fair overall rating of bonus value, withdrawals, customer service, accessibility or practical consumer-protection performance. No score or recommendation is assigned.
