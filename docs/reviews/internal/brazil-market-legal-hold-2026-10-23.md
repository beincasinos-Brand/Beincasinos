# Brazil market legal-status alert — 2026-10-23

Status: high-priority internal compliance checkpoint. This is not legal advice and not a consumer review.

## Primary-source finding

Brazil's Ministry of Finance says Provisional Measure (Medida Provisória) No. 1.394/2026, dated 25 September 2026, prohibits operation, offering, intermediation and advertising of fixed-odds betting nationwide. The Ministry says it applies to sports betting and online games, including offers by foreign operators to people located in Brazil, and also applies to state and district concessions/authorizations. Its published timeline says websites and apps intended to offer fixed-odds bets should be unavailable from 6 October 2026.

- Ministry explainer, updated 7 October 2026: https://www.gov.br/fazenda/pt-br/composicao/orgaos/secretaria-de-premios-e-apostas/medida-provisoria-ndeg-1-394-2026-entenda-as-novas-regras-para-as-apostas-de-quota-fixa
- Ministry legislation page (lists MP 1.394/2026 and implementing Portaria SPA/MF 3.005 of 2 October 2026): https://www.gov.br/fazenda/pt-br/composicao/orgaos/secretaria-de-premios-e-apostas/apostas-de-quota-fixa/legislacao

- Chamber of Deputies legal record for MP 1.394/2026: https://www2.camara.leg.br/legin/fed/medpro/2026/medidaprovisoria-1394-25-setembro-2026-800020-norma-pe.html
- Congress tracking page: https://www.congressonacional.leg.br/pt_BR/materias/medidas-provisorias/-/mpv/175871

The Congress tracking page, checked on 23 October 2026, says the measure is awaiting installation of its mixed commission and the deliberation window runs to 23 November 2026. The Chamber's legal record states no express revocation is recorded. Do not describe the former authorization list as current operating permission; recheck the live legal status and any subsequent court or legislative action before publishing.
