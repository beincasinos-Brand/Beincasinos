## Operator, licence and domain evidence

The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) public register identifies **LeoVegas Gaming PLC**, account **39198**, with an active Remote Casino licence. The register lists `leovegas.co.uk` as Active. ## Regulatory history — material context

On **3 August 2022**, the UKGC announced that LeoVegas would pay a **£1.32 million penalty** after social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. The regulator described weaknesses in customer interaction, including spend triggers and cooling-off decisions that were not adequately justified, and insufficient action on indicators such as denied deposits, cancelled withdrawals, long sessions and late-night/early-morning play. It also described shortcomings in financial risk triggers and the assessment of customers' financial circumstances.
