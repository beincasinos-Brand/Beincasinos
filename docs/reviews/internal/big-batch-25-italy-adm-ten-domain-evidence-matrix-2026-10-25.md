## Evidence requirements per brand
1. Save a dated official ADM record with concession code, legal concessionaire and exact website.
2. Match site legal notice and terms to the registered concessionaire; document any group-company or brand/domain relationship.
3. Capture current Italian bonus terms with date checked: wagering, qualifying deposit, max bet, eligible games, expiry, excluded payment methods, max cashout and withdrawal restrictions. If any material condition is missing, omit bonus claims.
4. Verify deposit and withdrawal options separately, including limits, fees, processing time, identity checks and payment exclusions. Do not imply a withdrawal was tested unless a real authorised consumer test occurred.
5. Verify safer-gambling tools, account limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, privacy/cookies, customer support and complaints process.
6. Check current regulatory notices, mobile usability, accessibility and image rights; a public logo URL does not establish reuse rights.
7. Record affiliate disclosure, independent second review, and editorial/legal approval before any score or public page is released.

The approved rubric totals 100: licence/operator 20; payments/withdrawals 18; safer gambling 15; product/games 12; bonus terms 10; complaints/support 10; UX/accessibility 8; privacy transparency 4; evidence quality 3.
