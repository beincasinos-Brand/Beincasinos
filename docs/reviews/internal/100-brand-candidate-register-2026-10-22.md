## Rules for counting
- A brand counts as **approved** only after a market-specific review is complete, evidence is archived, a second reviewer signs off, and editorial/legal gates pass.
- A single global brand operating in multiple markets is one unique brand in this register, but each market-specific review requires its own licence, domain, terms, payment and player-protection checks.
- Do not publish this internal queue. Do not assign scores, “best” claims, bonuses or trust labels from this list.
- Candidate naming is a research starting point only. Remove brands that are unavailable, illegal, inactive, duplicate, or fail exact operator/domain/licence mapping.

## Status key
- **QUEUE** — candidate name only; no completed verification implied.
- **HOLD** — known research obstacle or identity/domain/licence uncertainty.
- **APPROVED REVIEW** — none yet; requires all mandatory evidence and independent sign-off.
