## Verification checklist before review approval
For each domain, capture dated evidence for:
1. The domain is still the platform currently linked by the ESBK and resolves to the relevant Swiss-facing site.
2. The current operator/legal entity, licence extension, and any product/game-specific approvals relevant to claims made in the review.
3. Swiss eligibility, language/currency, registration and identity checks, deposits, withdrawals, limits, fees and processing times from current terms.
4. Bonus terms, wagering requirements, excluded games, expiry, maximum conversion/cashout and withdrawal restrictions.
5. Responsible-gambling tools, self-exclusion and complaints/escalation routes.
6. First-hand testing scope. Do not claim deposits, KYC, gameplay, support contacts or withdrawals were tested unless a reviewer actually performed and recorded them.
7. Dated sources, screenshots, affiliate disclosure, review/update date, and independent second-person fact-check.

## Interpretation limits
An ESBK listing is evidence that the regulator identifies a platform as a Swiss online casino; it is not a quality score, endorsement, guarantee of payout speed, or proof that every game currently displayed has the required permit. ESBK notes that individual games require approval. Do not describe these platforms as “best”, “fastest paying” or “fully tested” without separate, documented evidence.
