# Casino review publication quality gate

**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Status:** Internal editorial standard; not a public review.

## Non-negotiable publication gates

1. **Legal identity and jurisdiction:** identify the actual contracting entity, active regulator permission, customer-facing domain and market. A global brand name alone is not evidence of local eligibility.
2. **Primary-source citations:** date the regulator record and first-party terms; quote or paraphrase only what the source supports. Separate current terms from historic regulatory decisions.
3. **Regulatory fairness:** state the legal entity, decision date, finding and scope. Do not transfer a group settlement to one brand/operator without evidence; do not imply historic failings are ongoing unless current evidence supports it.
4. **Payments:** document methods, limits, fees, verification and processing times from current first-party sources. Do not claim personal testing unless a dated test log exists.
5. **Bonuses:** cover a specific current offer only after checking the official terms, eligibility, wagering contribution, deadlines, max bet, excluded games and withdrawal restrictions. If not verified, omit the offer.
6. **Complaints and ADR:** identify the process and dispute-resolution body from the applicable product agreement, not a generic group or sportsbook page.
7. **Safer gambling:** provide accurate links and explain available tools neutrally. Do not frame gambling as income, a way to recover losses, or a solution to financial stress.
8. **Original value:** include useful comparison, material limitations, market-specific analysis and clear distinctions from competing options. Do not merely reword operator marketing.
9. **Transparency:** disclose affiliate relationships clearly, explain review methodology, date the last review, identify the reviewer/editor, and provide a corrections/contact route.
10. **Technical eligibility:** verify production status, canonical URL, indexability and sitemap inclusion only after the page is approved and actually deployed.

## Evidence states (must not be conflated)

- Candidate: name in the research list.
- Evidence started: one or more sources gathered.
- Identity verified: operator/licence/domain mapping is supported.
- Review drafted: editorial draft exists.
- Approved: independent editorial and compliance checks passed.
- Published: the production URL returns the expected live page.
- Indexed: verified using a current Search Console/indexing observation.

A file in `docs/reviews/internal/` is not a published review. A commit is not a deployment. Sitemap submission is not proof of indexing.

## Quality rubric

Score each category 0–2:
- 0 = absent or unsupported
- 1 = partial / requires correction
- 2 = complete, sourced and independently checked

Categories: legal identity; terms freshness; payment evidence; offer accuracy (if used); complaints/ADR; safer-gambling information; regulatory fairness; original comparative value; affiliate transparency; technical validation.

**Publication threshold:** 18/20 minimum, no zero in legal identity, regulatory fairness, safer-gambling information or transparency, plus human editorial/compliance sign-off. A numerical score never overrides a legal or factual blocker.

## Google guidance

Google's people-first guidance emphasizes original analysis, substantial value beyond source summaries, demonstrable trust, and avoiding mass-produced pages created primarily to capture search traffic. Google's review guidance encourages useful comparison, pros/cons grounded in original research, and decision-relevant analysis. Apply these principles; do not promise rankings or impressions.

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews

Use this checklist before a draft can move from internal evidence to independent review. No page is approved or published by this document alone.
