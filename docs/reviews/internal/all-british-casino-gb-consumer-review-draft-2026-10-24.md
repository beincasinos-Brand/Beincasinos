## Major regulatory settlement — 21 October 2026

The UKGC announced that L & L Europe Limited would pay **£900,000** following a review that found failures in fair terms and withdrawal handling, anti-money-laundering/counter-terrorist-financing controls, social-responsibility systems and technical standards.

The regulator reported that the operator's terms restricted customers to one withdrawal request at a time until the previous request had been authorised. It also found that manually reviewing every fifth or tenth withdrawal for some customers led to requests for extra information at withdrawal time, even where it could reasonably have been requested earlier. The UKGC said withdrawals were withheld for reasons other than regulatory or legal requirements. The regulator stated that the operator amended its process so withdrawals were no longer withheld except where required for regulatory or legal reasons.

Other findings included:
- AML risk controls relying heavily on fixed financial thresholds and sampling rather than customer risk profiles.
- Safer-gambling alerts, including time-spent and failed-deposit signals, being enabled on some websites but not the full suite.
- Lack of a demonstrated process to evaluate the overall effectiveness of customer interactions.
- Failures to act promptly on some indicators of harm and to apply financial limits effectively.
