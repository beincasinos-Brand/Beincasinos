## Executive decision

The GGL's official whitelist, marked updated 2 October 2026, lists **Betway Limited / betway.de** under **Virtuelle Automatenspiele** (virtual slot games), with initial permission date 2 October 2026. This is a recent, category-specific regulator lead. It does **not** establish a nationwide permit for online table games or live casino; the GGL whitelist separates virtual slots from Online-Casinospiele and other categories.

The first-party German homepage redirects to `https://betway.de/en-de/` and exposes a welcome promotion of up to €100 with an 18x playthrough requirement. However, the extracted promotion page is a **sports** welcome offer, not proven to be a casino/virtual-slots offer. It must not be described as a casino bonus. The terms, RG, imprint and cookie routes returned only limited shell/footer text during this pass; full current customer terms and detailed policies remain unverified.

**No score or recommendation is assigned.** Do not publish a general “online casino” review unless the exact product category and any separate state-specific permission are confirmed. A narrowly scoped virtual-slot review may proceed only after the full evidence gates below close.
