## Primary-source findings

1. The Swedish Gambling Authority's published notice dated 2 June 2025 says Snabbare Ltd held licences for commercial online gambling and online betting for the website `snabbare.com`. It reports a warning and a SEK 5.5 million sanction fee for deficiencies in anti-money-laundering controls, including insufficient information about the source of customers' funds and inability to adequately assess whether transactions were legitimate.
   - Source: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/nyhetsarkiv/snabbare-ltd-far-varning-och-sanktionsavgift-for-brister-i-arbetet-mot-penningtvatt/
   - Decision PDF: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/globalassets/dokument/ovriga-dokument/beslut/varning---anmarkning/snabbare-ltd---varning-och-sanktionsavgift-brister-i-arbetet-mot-penningtvatt-2025-05-28.pdf
2. A separate authority decision dated 8 October 2025 closed an information-duty supervisory matter without further action after the authority reported finding no deficiencies in the reviewed website information. The decision described the licence then in force as ending 31 December 2025.
   - Source PDF: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/globalassets/dokument/tillsynsbeslut/avskrivna/snabbare-ltd---beslut-om-avskrivning-informationsansvar-2025-10-08.pdf
3. A 2021 authority decision also imposed a warning and SEK 65 million sanction fee over bonus offers. This is historical context and must be dated accurately; it must not be presented as a new 2026 enforcement event.
   - Source PDF: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/globalassets/dokument/tillsynsbeslut/varning-anmarkning/snabbare-ltd---beslut-om-varning-och-sanktionsavgift-bonuserbjudanden-2021-02-15.pdf
