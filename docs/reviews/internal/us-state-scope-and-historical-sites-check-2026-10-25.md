## Findings

### Pennsylvania (PA)

PGCB's current Interactive Gaming Operators page provides a state-specific list of regulated sites and includes brands such as Bet365, BetMGM, BetRivers, Caesars, DraftKings, Fanatics, FanDuel, Hollywood Casino Online, Horseshoe, Monopoly Casino, PlayLive!, and others. This establishes that those named sites are listed by the regulator as PA interactive gaming operators at the time checked; it does not establish eligibility in another state, prove a particular bonus is current, or constitute a quality review.

The PGCB former-sites PDF explicitly records that:
- Jackpot City operated under the Betway branding through 9 September 2025, then operations rebranded to Spin Palace Casino.
- Betway operations in PA ended on 4 March 2025 under the listed arrangement.
- Unibet Interactive, Inc. operations ended on 21 June 2024.
- PointsBet operations were rebranded to Fanatics after 21 January 2024.

These are historical PA arrangements. They must not be converted into current PA authorisation claims for the former brand/domain, and the exact current authorised domain/skin must be checked on the live PGCB list. A former-site entry is not proof of current availability.

### New Jersey (NJ)

The DGE permit-holder list last updated 22 September 2026 identifies internet gaming permit holders and affiliated Atlantic City casino entities. A permit-holder list is not itself a complete current list of consumer-facing websites or casino skins. Use the regulator's approved Internet Gaming Sites resource to verify the exact live domain and product scope before assigning an NJ brand.

## BeInCasinos US publication rules

1. Treat each US state as a separate market record. Never label a brand simply “US licensed” based on NJ, PA, or MI evidence.
2. For each proposed casino page, capture the current state regulator's authorised domain/skin, permit or certificate holder, consumer-facing brand, and contracting entity.
3. Distinguish current sites from historical former-site entries and rebrands. If a name/domain is only present in a former-sites list, keep it HOLD until a current authorisation is independently confirmed.
4. A regulator listing establishes regulatory listing only. Bonus terms, payment conditions, complaint pathways, safer-gambling tools, image rights, UX testing, and scoring require separate evidence.
5. Do not publish state-specific recommendations or bonuses until the exact domain and product are confirmed on the current regulator source and all editorial gates are complete.

## Scope limitations

This is a targeted check of official PGCB and NJ regulator resources, not an exhaustive review of every US prospect. No consumer testing, bonus validation, or score was performed in this check. Re-check the live regulator pages before publication.
