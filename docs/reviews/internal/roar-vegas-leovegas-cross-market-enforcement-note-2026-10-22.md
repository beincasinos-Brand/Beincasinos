# Roar Vegas / LeoVegas — cross-market enforcement and Denmark transition risk note

**Checked:** 2026-10-22  
**Status:** INTERNAL RESEARCH — HOLD. This is not a completed consumer review or legal opinion.

## A. Denmark: apparent overlapping operator/domain records need reconciliation

The Danish Gambling Authority's current public search results surfaced both:
- **Roar Vegas Limited** — domain `leovegas.dk`, online casino and betting: https://spillemyndigheden.dk/en-us/licensed-gambling-operators/roar-vegas-limited
- **LeoVegas Gaming Plc** — domains `leovegas.com`, `leovegas.dk`, `expekt.dk`, online casino and betting: https://spillemyndigheden.dk/en-us/licensed-gambling-operators/leovegas-gaming-plc

The operator's transition FAQ says the Danish LeoVegas.dk operation moves to Roar Vegas Limited and that old accounts under LeoVegas Gaming Plc are to be closed: https://www.leovegas.dk/kampagner/faq-ny-licens-leovegas

**Editorial consequence:** Do not infer from an operator announcement alone that the old regulator record is already removed or that the two records grant concurrent authority for the same customer journey. Confirm the live regulator entries and effective dates with the authority or archived decision/record before publication. The consumer-facing domain can remain the same while legal operator and account terms change; date-stamp all claims and avoid mixing old and new terms.

## B. Sweden: regulator sanction against Roar Vegas Ltd

The Swedish Gambling Authority (Spelinspektionen) published a decision dated **24 March 2025** titled “Anmärkning och sanktionsavgift – brister i omsorgsplikten” against **Roar Vegas Ltd**. The decision states a warning/reprimand (“anmärkning”) and a sanction fee of **SEK 8,000,000**. The regulator's summary says that, in three of twelve examined customer cases, the company did not sufficiently help customers reduce gambling when there were reasons to intervene; the authority considered that effective measures should have been taken earlier.

Official regulator decision PDF:
https://www.spelinspektionen.se/globalassets/dokument/ovriga-dokument/beslut/varning---anmarkning/roar-vegas-ltd---anmarkning-och-sanktionsavgift-omsorgsplikten-2025-03-24.pdf

**Attribution and fairness:** This is a historical Swedish regulatory finding against Roar Vegas Ltd. It is not proof of current Danish non-compliance, not evidence about every customer, and not a finding against LeoVegas Gaming Plc by default. Any consumer-facing summary must identify the Swedish jurisdiction, decision date, legal entity, issue and sanction precisely, and check for appeal, later status or remediation before publication.
