# Casino review quality gates

Updated: 2026-10-22. Internal editorial checklist; not consumer-facing copy.

## Identity and market
- Confirm the exact country route, current customer terms, contracting entity, regulator, licence number/status, permitted product and active domain entry.
- A licence in one country does not prove permission in another. Do not infer current permission from a parent company, historic licence, redirect or search snippet.
- Attribute regulatory actions to the exact licensee/group and record dates. If identity, product scope or domain mapping is unclear, mark HOLD.

## Required consumer evidence
For each market-specific review, capture dated primary-source evidence for eligibility/KYC, deposits, withdrawals and fees, bonus eligibility and wagering restrictions, games/providers, support channels and tested response (only if actually tested), complaints/ADR, responsible-gambling controls, privacy/tracking, and affiliate/sponsorship disclosure.

## Evidence integrity
- Prefer current regulator records, live terms, official payment/help pages, official RG pages and documented direct tests.
- Record exact URL, access date, factual note and reviewer for material claims.
- Search snippets, affiliate listings and secondary operator mappings are leads, not proof.
- Never fabricate payout speed, support tests, player experiences, game counts, RTP findings or bonus values. Mark unknown information as Not verified.
- When sources conflict or a critical claim remains unverified, keep HOLD.

## Publication gate
A review can leave HOLD only after market/entity/licence/domain match; core consumer terms checked; regulatory history reviewed; unsupported claims removed; affiliate disclosure and methodology shown; market-appropriate responsible-gambling information added; an independent second review recorded; and technical checks passed for canonical, indexability, sitemap, internal links, mobile layout, structured data and placeholder text.

## States and reporting
Prospect = candidate only. Evidence captured = source leads stored, not a finished review. HOLD = a critical gate is open. Ready for second review = evidence complete. Approved = second reviewer signs off. Published = live URL checked after release. Report candidate count, evidence-captured rows, drafts, approved reviews, published pages and indexed URLs separately; never conflate these metrics.
