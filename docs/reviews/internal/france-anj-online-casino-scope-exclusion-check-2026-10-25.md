## Primary-source findings

Checked 25 October 2026 against France's Autorité nationale des jeux (ANJ):

- Legal categories FAQ: https://anj.fr/foire-aux-questions/offre-de-jeu-legale-et-illegale/quels-sont-les-jeux-dargent-et-paris-sportifs
- Official approved operators: https://www.anj.fr/offre-de-jeu-et-marche/operateurs-agrees
- Illegal offer and risk guidance: https://www.anj.fr/la-dangerosite-de-loffre-illegale-de-jeux-dargent-et-de-hasard
- Administrative blocking list (non-exhaustive): https://anj.fr/liste-noire-des-sites-de-jeux-dargent-et-de-hasard-interdits-en-france

ANJ says online casino games are not authorised in France. The legal online categories include authorised sports betting, horse-race betting, online poker, and lottery under the applicable monopoly; casino games in physical establishments are a separate regime. An international casino licence does not make an online casino offer legal in France. ANJ also cautions that its blocking list is non-exhaustive, so absence from that list is not proof of authorisation.

## Limitations

This is a product-category scope check, not an exhaustive operator-by-operator audit and not legal advice. Recheck current ANJ sources before publication. Do not infer that a particular operator's sports-betting or poker authorisation extends to online casino games.
