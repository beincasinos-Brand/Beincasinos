## What NorthStar Bets is — Ontario scope

The regulator directory reviewed for this evidence pass lists NorthStar Bets under NorthStar Gaming (Ontario) Inc. and links to northstarbets.ca. The operator's homepage identifies AGCO registration OPIG1226485 and says customers must be at least 19 and physically located in Ontario. These details support treating NorthStar Bets as an Ontario-specific candidate; they do not establish authorisation in other Canadian provinces or territories.

The legal entity, live domain, registration status and terms shown to an Ontario customer must be rechecked immediately before publication. A regulator-directory listing is an identity and permission checkpoint, not a complete assessment of the consumer experience.

## Payments and withdrawals: published estimates, not test results

The operator's help centre lists Visa, MasterCard, eCheck, iDebit, Interac and other instant-payment options for deposits, and Interac E-Transfer, Visa, PayPal and Instadebit among withdrawal methods. Availability can depend on the account and current cashier; reconfirm the options on the live Ontario route before publication.

The operator states a C$20 minimum withdrawal and says it does not set an overall maximum itself, although a financial institution may impose limits and withdrawals above C$10,000 may take longer. Its help content says approval usually takes a few hours, may take up to 24 hours, and additional safety checks can take up to 72 hours in some cases. These are operator-published estimates, not independently measured performance. Do not describe withdrawals as fast, reliable or tested on the basis of this material alone.

The evidence pack also notes that a deposit may be required before registering a withdrawal method and that MasterCard deposits cannot be withdrawn back to MasterCard. Fees, per-method limits, account verification rules and payment-method ownership requirements still need confirmation from current terms.

## Safer-gambling tools: described by the operator

The operator's help centre describes daily, weekly and monthly deposit and loss limits, time-outs, and self-exclusion periods of six months, one year or five years. It says time-outs disable deposits and play while leaving account access and support chat available; it also says time-outs and self-exclusion cannot be ended early. Limit decreases are described as immediate, while increases have a 24-hour cooling-off period.

These are summaries of operator help pages, not the result of a live usability test. Before publication, check the current Ontario product and document how clearly customers can find and activate each control. Do not make claims about the tools' effectiveness or ease of use without reproducible evidence.

## Bonuses: no offer verified for publication

No current welcome bonus is approved for this draft. The research pass found promotional pages with end dates in January and May 2026, and a longer-standing page whose wording does not prove that the offer remains active. Repeating a headline offer from these pages could mislead readers.

Only describe an offer if the exact current Ontario customer offer and full terms are captured at publication time. Required details include eligibility, wagering requirements, game contribution, excluded games, maximum bet, expiry, withdrawal restrictions, and offer-stacking rules. If these cannot be verified, omit the offer.

## Complaints, privacy, accessibility and enforcement

The current evidence pack has not captured the full complaints process and escalation route, the complete privacy-controller wording and cookie-consent behaviour, or a documented accessibility assessment. Those areas are **not assessed**; the presence of links in a help centre is not enough to score them.

The evidence pack also flags that relevant AGCO enforcement history has not yet been fully checked. This must be researched from primary regulator sources before the review can present a balanced view of regulatory history.

## Provisional verdict

**Insufficient verified evidence for a rating.** The research supports a preliminary Ontario identity and jurisdiction check and records operator-published payment and safer-gambling information. It does not establish actual withdrawal performance, current bonus value, complaint handling quality, accessibility, or current enforcement context.

A useful final review should prioritise exact Ontario terms, transparent caveats and consumer-relevant comparisons over promotional language. Until the outstanding checks and independent review are complete, this draft must remain internal and unrated.
