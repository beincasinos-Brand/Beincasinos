### Regulatory action that must be represented fairly

The UKGC published a settlement for Betfair Casino Limited dated **17 December 2025**, concerning social-responsibility/customer-interaction controls following a compliance assessment in April–May 2024. The regulator states that four licensees trading under Betfair and Paddy Power names had failures against specified remote customer-interaction requirements, with a combined payment in lieu of a financial penalty of **£2,000,000**.

Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/308

Any public review must describe the date, scope and outcome accurately; do not imply the historical action alone establishes present-day performance or current failure. Check later remediation/current policies before drawing a present-day conclusion.
