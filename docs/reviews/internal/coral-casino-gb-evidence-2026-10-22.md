## 2. Enforcement history — material finding

The Gambling Commission's 17 August 2022 announcement says Entain Group would pay **£14 million** for failures at its online business **LC International Limited**, which ran 13 websites including coral.co.uk, as part of a £17 million total across online and land-based businesses.

The announcement describes serious social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures, including delayed or missing safer-gambling interactions, cross-brand account opening after restrictions, and inadequate source-of-funds / customer-due-diligence checks.
