## Review sections

### 1. Operator, licence and availability
State the legal operator, exact domain, regulator, licence/product scope, local eligibility, age restrictions and evidence date. Link the regulator's official register. Do not generalise authorisation from another market.

### 2. Games and product
Describe verified categories and providers only when evidenced. Distinguish slots, live dealer, table games, poker, sportsbook and lottery; do not imply one product's approval covers another.

### 3. Welcome offer and promotions

If any material term is missing or ambiguous, do not present the headline amount as a fully verified offer.

### 4. Deposits and withdrawals
List only current, locally available methods. Capture minimum/maximum, fees, processing estimate, verification conditions, limits and any method-specific rules. Separate operator-stated processing times from actual test results.

### 5. Account verification and user experience
Document the tested device/browser, region, date, registration journey, accessibility issues and support interactions. Never claim “tested” without a dated test record.

### 6. Support, complaints and dispute resolution
Record support channels/hours as observed, official complaints route, ADR/escalation route where applicable and regulator complaint options. Do not infer responsiveness from a contact page.

### 7. Safer gambling
Verify deposit/time/loss limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, reality checks, account closure and links to local support. Avoid presenting tools as effective without describing their scope.

### 8. Privacy, security and accessibility
Summarise the actual privacy/cookie disclosures, data-controller identity, account security options and observed keyboard/mobile/accessibility findings. Do not claim certification without evidence.

### 9. Pros and cons
Each item must map to a cited source or recorded test observation. No generic filler.

### 10. Methodology and limitations
Explain the scoring rubric, evidence date, test scope, affiliate relationship, what was not tested, and that offers/availability may change. Publish a score only after independent review.
