## Historical enforcement — keep the procedural status accurate

The Swedish regulator's 17 December 2024 update states that the Supreme Administrative Court declined leave to appeal in cases involving four operators. ComeOn Sweden Ltd's sanction was set at **SEK 13 million** for prohibited bonus offers and failures relating to duty of care. The sanction is historical; do not describe it as a current penalty or imply that the decision automatically describes present-day player experience.

The regulator's case list also shows a separate ComeOn Sweden Ltd supervisory matter about information responsibility/duty of care closed without further action on 2 July 2025. Keep this distinct from the earlier finalised bonus case.
