## Regulatory context — 7 August 2025

The UKGC public register records a **£82,687 financial penalty** against WHG (International) Limited, decision dated 7 August 2025. The regulator said the company failed to comply with a customer-identification requirement covering customers who held accounts across the licensee and a group company. The record says the licensee self-identified and proactively reported the breach, took remedial action and cooperated with the Commission.
