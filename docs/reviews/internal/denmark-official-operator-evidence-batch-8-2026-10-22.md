## General legal-scope warning

The Danish Gambling Authority's published operator guidance states that a Danish licence can only be used to provide gambling in Denmark and cannot be treated as permission to provide gambling in other countries. Official guidance: https://www.spillemyndigheden.dk/uploads/2025-06/Guidelines%20for%20operators%20of%20betting%20and%20online%20casino%20version%209.0%202025.pdf

Therefore, a Denmark licence/domain record must not be presented as global approval or as a recommendation to users in another country. The target market for any eventual public review must be explicit.
