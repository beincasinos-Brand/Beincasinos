The regulator describes its register as covering actors with valid licences/permits and includes website addresses for online licensees: https://www2.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/

## Operator terms reviewed at a high level

The current operator terms page identifies the website operator as Scandix Limited, gives Swedish licence number **24Si1786** and the same licence end date, and states that Swedish residents only may open accounts. It also describes checks against Spelpaus, mandatory deposit-limit selection during registration, reality-check prompts, and self-exclusion tools. Source: https://betinia.se/sv/rules

This is a high-level source checkpoint, not a complete audit of all general, bonus, payments, privacy and complaints terms. Do not interpret terms stated by the operator as independently tested performance.
