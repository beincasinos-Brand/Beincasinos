## Regulatory history — relevant consumer-protection findings

On **4 April 2024**, the UKGC announced a total settlement of **£582,120** across two bet365 licensees following a compliance assessment conducted in March 2022. For **Hillside (UK Gaming) ENC**, the casino/bingo licensee, the amount was **£343,035** in lieu of a financial penalty. The regulator found shortcomings in anti-money-laundering processes and social-responsibility interactions during the relevant periods. It reported that customer interactions were often not tailored to the individual customer journey or risk of harm, and the operator could not effectively establish whether customers had read and understood the information or advice provided.
