## 3. Withdrawals and account funds: what the terms say

The same terms state, among other things:
- Account funds are normally paid to the player's Danish NemKonto, with an alternative Danish bank account belonging to the player subject to the stated conditions.
- Withdrawals may be made through the withdrawal function or customer centre, and the terms say withdrawal can occur at most once per day.
- The terms reserve the possibility of transaction fees, with current fees to be shown on the operator website.
- The text says winnings of DKK 1,500,000 or more are transferred only 3–4 weeks after the game/bet concludes.
- The terms describe circumstances in which withdrawals may be refused or amounts reversed, including suspected fraud or breaches of terms.

These are published contractual provisions, not measured payout speed or proof that any specific player experienced a delay. Before scoring, inspect the current live withdrawal page and casino-specific rules; do not use lottery-only or retail-prize payout terms as evidence for online casino withdrawals.
