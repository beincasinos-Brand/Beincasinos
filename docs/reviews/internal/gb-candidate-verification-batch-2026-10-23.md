## Reusable per-brand verification protocol
1. **Identity and jurisdiction:** save the current regulator record URL, access date, legal entity, permission types, status, and exact approved domain. A brand name or search result alone is insufficient.
2. **Terms snapshot:** capture first-party general terms, casino rules, promotion terms, withdrawal/payment policy, privacy notice, cookies notice and complaints policy with version/date where available.
3. **Offer accuracy:** no headline bonus, wagering requirement, expiry, eligible-game list or maximum-cashout claim without the complete applicable terms. If terms are dynamic, inaccessible or contradictory, mark UNKNOWN/HOLD.
4. **Payments:** distinguish written operator processing targets from observed performance. No deposits or withdrawals are claimed as tested unless a lawful, documented test was actually performed.
5. **Player protection:** record deposit limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, GAMSTOP, reality checks and support routes only after checking the current GB-specific information and user journey where accessible.
6. **Enforcement:** use primary regulator notices and match the legal entity, date, activity and scope. Do not attribute group-level, sportsbook-only or land-based actions to a casino brand without evidence.
7. **Privacy and complaints:** capture controller identity, contact/DPO details where stated, data rights, cookies/consent, complaints escalation and ADR information.
8. **Editorial QA:** document source citations, timestamps, unresolved uncertainty, affiliate disclosure, independent second-editor review and legal/editorial sign-off.
9. **Release safety:** internal evidence stays outside the public artifact. No score or ranking is published while any material gate is open.
