## What this refresh establishes

### Current licence window

The Dutch regulator, Kansspelautoriteit (Ksa), announced on 17 September 2026 that TOTO Online B.V. received a follow-on online-gambling licence covering October 2026 through September 2031. The announcement names TOTO Online B.V. as the operator for both TOTO and Winnitt and says all eight recipients met the requirements for the renewed licences.

- Official Ksa announcement: https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/eerste-vervolgvergunningen-voor-online-kansspelen-verleend
- Ksa permit-holder page: https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/toto-online-b-v
- First-party TOTO website and licence disclosure: https://www.toto.nl/

### Enforcement record — Wwft instruction

The Ksa's official page, published 24 December 2025, says TOTO Online B.V. received a formal instruction (*aanwijzing*) for deficiencies under the Dutch Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Act (Wwft). The described concerns include ongoing monitoring of customer relationships and transactions, documenting risk-classification decisions and the adequacy of source-of-funds investigations. The Ksa says some issues were resolved during the investigation, and that the instruction required the remaining violations to be ended within six months, followed by a recheck.

- Ksa announcement: https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/wwft-aanwijzing-voor-toto-online
- Ksa decision PDF: https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025-28-10_01.356.880_Aanwijzing%20Wwft_TOTO%20Online_sanctiebesluit.pdf

**Date reconciliation:** The Ksa announcement page gives 24 December 2025 as the publication date. The decision PDF itself is headed 28 October 2025. Keep those as separate dates unless the regulator's underlying decision metadata confirms a single operative decision date. Do not call this Wwft instruction a monetary fine: the cited announcement describes an *aanwijzing*, not a fine.

The later licence-renewal announcement says the Ksa considered definitively established violations from the preceding five years during renewal assessment. It does not explicitly say in the announcement that every requirement of this particular Wwft instruction has passed a follow-up recheck. A current recheck/compliance outcome remains a publication gap.

### Customer support and complaints

TOTO's first-party contact page states that telephone support is available seven days a week, 24 hours a day at **070-550 08 05**, and that chat is available daily from **09:00 to 23:30**. For unresolved complaints, it directs customers first to customer service and then to the Ombudsman.

- Contact and complaint escalation: https://www.toto.nl/klantenservice/contact
- Customer-service hub: https://www.toto.nl/klantenservice
