## OLG.ca
Primary sources:
- Official online casino page: https://www.olg.ca/en/online-casino.html
- iGaming Ontario operator directory: https://www.igamingontario.ca/en/operator/operators
- Official terms: https://www.olg.ca/en/terms-and-condition.html

The official OLG page confirms OLG.ca offers online casino games and says online casino play is for Ontario; it states a minimum age of 19 for online casino gaming and online sports betting in Ontario. The iGaming Ontario directory specifically distinguishes OLG.ca as legally operated under the conduct and management of the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG), rather than as one of the contracted private operators. This is important taxonomy: describe it as a provincial public operator, not as an ordinary private casino brand. Evidence is Ontario-specific and does not establish availability in other provinces.
