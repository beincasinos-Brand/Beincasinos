Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/45322

The UKGC domain record lists www.unibet.co.uk as Active for account 45322. It also lists touch.unibt.co.uk as Active. The regulator notes that domain and trading-name information is provided by the gambling business and may not be independently guaranteed.

Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/45322  
Register methodology: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses

## Material regulatory action

The UKGC published a sanction against Platinum Gaming Limited dated **22 September 2025**. The published outcome lists licence conditions, a financial penalty and a warning. The Commission found breaches relating to anti-money-laundering controls and customer interaction during **January 2023 to May 2024**. The stated outcome included a **£10,000,000 financial penalty**, a warning and an additional licence condition. The decision says the licensee cooperated with the Commission.

Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/291
