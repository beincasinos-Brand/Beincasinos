**Checked:** 2026-10-23

## OLG.ca — Ontario

The iGaming Ontario operator directory explicitly states that OLG.ca is operated legally under the conduct and management of the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG). iGaming Ontario's “What We Do” page distinguishes OLG.ca, managed by OLG as an Ontario government agency, from private internet-gaming operators in the iGO market.

- Official site: https://www.olg.ca/
- iGaming Ontario operator directory: https://www.igamingontario.ca/en/operator/operators
- iGaming Ontario explanation of OLG vs iGO: https://www.igamingontario.ca/en/player/what-we-do
- Ontario Public Appointments Secretariat agency profile: https://www.pas.gov.on.ca/Home/Agency/241

**Scope:** Ontario-specific. Do not infer authorization outside Ontario or treat OLG as a private operator contracted by iGO.

## Loto-Québec — Québec

Loto-Québec's official online-gaming page identifies lotoquebec.com as its online gaming site and describes casino games (slots, table games, live casino, virtual sports and keno), lottery products and sports betting. Its 27 August 2026 public statement reiterates that lotoquebec.com is the only legal casino and sports-betting site in Québec.

- Official consumer site: https://www.lotoquebec.com/
- Official online-gaming overview: https://societe.lotoquebec.com/en/offering/online-gaming
- Official 27 August 2026 statement: https://societe.lotoquebec.com/fr/medias-et-partenaires/communiques-de-presse/2026/aout/marches-prediction-2026-08-27

**Scope:** Québec-specific. Confirm current account residency/location requirements and product terms before making more granular claims.
