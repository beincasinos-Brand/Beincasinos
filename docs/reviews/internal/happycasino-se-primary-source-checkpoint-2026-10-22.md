## Primary-source finding

The Swedish Gambling Authority (Spelinspektionen) has a domain-specific licence-register page for `happycasino.se`. The page identifies **Forseti Services Limited** as the licence holder and displays “Kommersiellt online” as valid from **29 June 2022 through 28 June 2027**. It also displays a separate commercial betting licence period from 9 October 2024 through 28 June 2027.

Official domain-specific source: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/licensurl/?url=happycasino.se

The regulator describes its licence register as the place to check operators with valid licences/permits and lists online domains: https://www2.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/

## Interpretation limits

- This is direct regulator evidence that `happycasino.se` is listed against Forseti Services Limited and that the displayed online licence period has not expired as of the research date.
