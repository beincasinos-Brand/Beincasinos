## Regulatory history — online operator

1. **20 May 2025 — £240,000 financial penalty:** UKGC found a breach of licence condition 2.3.1 concerning technical standards. The regulator states Petfre identified and reported the issue, took immediate remedial action and cooperated.
2. **30 June 2026 — £900,000 settlement/payment in lieu of financial penalty:** a licence review following a compliance assessment found failings in remote customer-interaction social-responsibility controls. The regulator described insufficient identification of harm indicators, lack of immediate automated action where strong indicators were found, and a process that could delay a further review for seven days after an account was flagged. In one instance, a customer lost £17,900 within 24 hours without an additional interaction. The regulator says interim mitigating controls and an action plan were implemented.

The total of the two above online-operator actions is **£1,140,000** (£240,000 + £900,000). This arithmetic sum does not include actions against separate group entities and is not a complete history of every Betfred-branded business.
