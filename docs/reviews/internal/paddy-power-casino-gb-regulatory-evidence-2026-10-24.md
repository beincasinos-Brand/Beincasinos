## Regulatory action — group-level context with exact entities

On 17 December 2025, the UK Gambling Commission published a regulatory settlement concerning remote customer-interaction/social-responsibility failings identified after compliance assessments in April and May 2024. The decision named four licensees trading under Paddy Power and Betfair:
- PPB Entertainment Limited (licence 039426-R-319332-024)
- PPB Counterparty Services Limited (licence 039439-R-319330-011)
- Betfair Casino Limited (licence 039435-R-319329-007)
- TSE Malta LP (licence 039561-R-319411-007)

The Commission said the four licensees would make a total payment in lieu of a financial penalty of **£2,000,000**. The finding concerned remote customer-interaction requirements. Do not describe the full amount as a fine against Paddy Power alone, and do not attribute each finding to a specific casino account without reading the full decision and mapping it carefully.
