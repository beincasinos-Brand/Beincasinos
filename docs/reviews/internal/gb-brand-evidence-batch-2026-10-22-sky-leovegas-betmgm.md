## Sky Vegas — successor entity/domain reconciliation

UK Gambling Commission records show two similarly named licensees associated with Sky Vegas:
- **Bonne Terre Gaming Limited**, account **65519**, lists `www.skyvegas.com` and `m.skyvegas.com` as active domains; its trading-name record lists Sky Vegas and other Sky products as active.
- **Bonne Terre Limited**, account **38718**, lists the Sky domains as inactive and its Casino Remote licence ended by surrender on **31 October 2025**. Its Sky trading names are inactive.

Primary sources:
- Active-domain record: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/65519
- Active trading names: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/trading-names/65519
- Prior entity's licence summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/38718

- Sky Vegas search results: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/results?business-search=sky+vegas

**Editorial consequence:** do not cite the old Bonne Terre Limited licence as current permission. Match the current customer terms and exact Sky Vegas product to Bonne Terre Gaming Limited before publication.

## Shared limits and decision

The UKGC register states that domain and trading-name information is supplied by gambling businesses and may not be guaranteed accurate. A register record is only one evidence layer. Current terms and exact contracting entity, product-specific licence scope, consumer checks, regulatory-history review and independent sign-off remain required.

**All three brands remain HOLD.** No hands-on account, deposit, gameplay or withdrawal test is claimed as completed. No GitHub Actions or deployment was triggered for this research update.
