# Australia target-market legal exclusion check — 25 October 2026

Status: internal market-scope guardrail; not legal advice, not an exhaustive operator clearance, and not publication approval.

## Primary-source findings

Checked 25 October 2026 against the Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA):

- Interactive Gambling Act overview: https://www.acma.gov.au/interactivegambling
- ACMA legal-operator check / licensed-provider register: https://www.acma.gov.au/check-if-gambling-operator-legal
- ACMA investigations and prohibited-services list: https://www.acma.gov.au/investigations-online-gambling-providers
- Federal legislation: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A00851/latest

ACMA states that online casinos are among the online gambling services prohibited from being offered to people in Australia and that prohibited services must not be advertised in Australia. Its investigations page describes casino-style games such as blackjack, roulette, poker and slots played for money as prohibited interactive gambling services when the statutory conditions are met.

The ACMA investigations page currently names several brands relevant to BeInCasinos' existing prospect pool among services found to have provided prohibited interactive gambling services to customers physically present in Australia, including **Legiano**, **Casea**, **SG Casino**, and **Casinia**. This is an adverse regulatory-scope finding, not a statement about the brands' status in every other country. It must not be paraphrased as a global licence finding or as proof that a particular current domain remains accessible.
