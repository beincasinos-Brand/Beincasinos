## Regulatory history and fair attribution

### Historical action — 12 March 2020

The UKGC reported that Betway was to pay £11.6 million following social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failings involving seven high-spending customers. The regulator's statement described examples where source-of-funds checks and effective safer-gambling interactions were not carried out adequately, and said £5.8 million had flowed through the business that was found, or could reasonably be suspected to be, proceeds of crime, with most of that money to be divested and returned to victims.

This is a material historic regulatory finding and should not be omitted from an editorial review merely because the current account actions page now says no actions have been recorded. Equally, the historic decision alone does not establish that the same failures persist today. No independent audit of current controls was performed for this draft.
