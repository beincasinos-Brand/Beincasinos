# Roar Vegas Limited / LeoVegas.dk — Denmark licence transition evidence

**Research date:** 2026-10-22  
**Status:** INTERNAL EVIDENCE — HOLD; not an approved consumer review.  
**Method:** Public-source review only. No account, deposit, gameplay, support contact or withdrawal test was performed.

## 1. Current regulator record

The Danish Gambling Authority's public register lists **Roar Vegas Limited** with domain **leovegas.dk** and licence categories **Online casino** and **Betting**. The register entry identifies its address as LeoVegas, Level 7, The Plaza Business Centre, Bisazza Street, SLM 1640 Sliema, Malta, and marks it as not licensed in Greenland.

Official regulator source: https://spillemyndigheden.dk/en-us/licensed-gambling-operators/roar-vegas-limited

The register page and operator terms should be rechecked immediately before publication; this note records the public-source snapshot found on 22 October 2026.

## 2. Operator's own transition notice

Official operator FAQ: https://www.leovegas.dk/kampagner/faq-ny-licens-leovegas

The FAQ says the Danish LeoVegas.dk operation is moving from **LeoVegas Gaming Plc** to **Roar Vegas Limited** under a new Danish licence. It says accounts opened under the prior licence/entity will be closed and customers must create a new account to play under the new licence. It also warns that progress in promotions/free spins/free bets may not transfer and describes a one-of-two choice between a welcome bonus and birthday bonus. Exact offer availability and conditions must be checked on the live offer page rather than inferred from this transition FAQ.

The FAQ states licence number **26-626113**, issued **21 August 2026** and valid until **20 August 2031** for online casino and sports betting.

## 3. Current terms cross-check

Official terms: https://www.leovegas.dk/vilkaar

The current terms identify **Roar Vegas Limited** as operator, give its Malta registered-office address, and state that the Danish online casino and sports-betting permissions use licence number 26-626113, issued 21 August 2026 and valid until 20 August 2031. This aligns the domain/operator mapping across the operator's terms and the regulator record.

Important entity distinction: **LeoVegas Gaming Plc and Roar Vegas Limited are not interchangeable legal entities.** Historical records, account arrangements, complaints or enforcement findings must be attributed to the correct entity and relevant period. Do not count the licence transition as two distinct consumer brands merely because the operator entity changed.

## 4. What this does and does not prove

Supported by the sources above:
- The regulator record lists Roar Vegas Limited and leovegas.dk with online-casino and betting categories.
- The operator describes a change in legal operator/licence and says existing accounts under the previous entity will close.
- The operator's terms state a Danish licence number and validity dates.

Not established:

- Current bonus wagering requirements and exclusions;
- Actual support quality, complaints outcomes or player satisfaction;
- Game catalogue/RTP, mobile accessibility, privacy compliance in practice, or effectiveness of safer-gambling controls;
