# High-value SEO content priorities — 2026-10-23

Internal editorial plan for BeInCasinos. This is a prioritisation document, not a claim of search volume or ranking.

## Priority 1 — strengthen existing helpful pages
1. Review methodology: explain scoring dimensions, evidence freshness, market eligibility, what was tested, conflicts of interest and when a profile is withheld.
2. Casino licence checker/guide: explain how to verify legal entity, domain and product permission directly in each regulator register; include market-specific caveats.
3. Bonus and wagering explainer: show a transparent worked example, distinguish wagering on bonus vs deposit+bonus, game contribution, expiry, max cashout and excluded payment methods.
4. Withdrawal guide: separate verification holds, operator processing, payment-provider settlement and bank processing; never imply an operator estimate is a tested result.
5. Safer-gambling guide: explain deposit limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, national schemes, support resources and complaint escalation by jurisdiction.
6. Wagering calculator: make assumptions visible, label results as mathematical estimates, explain that actual terms can differ and avoid implying expected profit.
7. Terms checklist: help readers compare offer eligibility, wagering, expiry, game exclusions, withdrawal caps, KYC and account restrictions.

## Priority 2 — comparison pages with real decision value
- Compare operators only after each compared profile passes the same market-specific evidence gate.
- Display the checked date and source links for licence/domain, bonus terms, withdrawals, privacy, support and safer-gambling tools.
- Keep unknown values explicitly unknown; never fill a table with guessed values.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives such as “best”, “fastest payout” or “highest bonus”.
- Separate product categories and jurisdiction permissions. A sports-betting licence must not be presented as proof of online-casino permission.

## Priority 3 — long-tail informational intent
Build answers around questions that can be resolved accurately from primary sources:
- How to verify an online casino licence and domain
- How wagering requirements are calculated
- What “bonus funds” and “cash balance” mean
- Why withdrawals may require identity verification
- How to read a casino withdrawal policy
- How self-exclusion and deposit limits differ
- How to complain about an online gambling operator in a specific jurisdiction
- How game contribution rates affect wagering calculations

These are topic ideas only; no monthly search-volume figures are asserted.

## Market/legal constraints
- Australia: do not frame prohibited online casino services as legal consumer options.
- France: do not treat online casino games as a lawful licensed vertical.
- Canada: segment by province and current provincial authorisation.
- Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Netherlands and Sweden: verify the exact entity, domain, product permission and current regulator status before listing a candidate.
- Great Britain and Italy: use the relevant official register and market-specific terms, not generic international landing pages.
- Where legal eligibility cannot be established, publish a consumer-protection or legal-explainer page instead of an operator recommendation.

## Internal linking
- Link each approved review to its regulator-verification explanation, bonus/withdrawal methodology, safer-gambling resources and relevant market hub.
- Link guides back to the relevant methodology and tool where useful.
- Use concise, descriptive anchors; avoid repeated exact-match keyword stuffing.
- Add links only where they help readers complete a real task.

## Measurement and release
- Track Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by page/query after verified indexing.
- Use analytics to identify pages with impressions but weak CTR, and pages with visits but poor engagement; investigate before rewriting.
- Do not promise a traffic target or infer indexing from a successful HTTP response.
- Keep candidate lists, internal evidence packs and unapproved reviews out of public navigation and sitemap.
- This plan creates no public pages and does not claim a ranking, impression or traffic improvement.
