## Evidence required for scoring
1. Capture the live Ksa register entry on the day of review, including entity, website, product category and licence status.
2. Confirm site legal notice and current Dutch terms match the registered entity and domain.
3. Capture dated bonus terms, wagering, eligible games, expiry, max bet, excluded payment methods and withdrawal conditions; omit offer claims if terms are incomplete.
4. Verify deposits and withdrawals separately, including methods, limits, fees, processing times, verification and complaint route.
5. Verify Dutch safer-gambling controls, mandatory limits, self-exclusion/Centraal Register Uitsluiting Kansspelen (Cruks), privacy/cookies and support.
6. Confirm image rights, accessibility, mobile usability, affiliate disclosure, consumer testing and independent second review.

## Publication gate
The Ksa renewal announcement is a licence-status signal, not a quality rating and not proof that every product from a brand is authorised. No review in this batch is scored; no bonus, withdrawal test, image rights or consumer test is approved. All candidates remain HOLD / NOT SCORED until exact current domain/product evidence and the full review are complete. No merge/deploy performed.
