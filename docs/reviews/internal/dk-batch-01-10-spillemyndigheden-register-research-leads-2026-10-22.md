- Capture Danish terms, bonus rules, cashier methods/fees, limits, stated withdrawal times, KYC/verification, complaints and responsible-gambling pages.

- Clearly distinguish desk research from hands-on testing. No account opening, deposit, wagering, KYC, customer-support interaction or withdrawal test was performed for this intake.
- Require a second editorial review and documented approval before any public rating, ranking or recommendation.
