## Material regulatory context — 20 August 2026

The UKGC published a regulatory settlement requiring a payment in lieu of financial penalty of **£609,104**, plus Commission costs, following findings about anti-money-laundering/counter-terrorist-financing controls and social-responsibility controls. The public summary describes weaknesses in detecting and responding to harm indicators, deposit-limit handling for customers aged 18–24, financial-vulnerability checks and source-of-funds/customer-risk controls. It gives examples of very high betting activity and disproportionate spending not being flagged promptly.
