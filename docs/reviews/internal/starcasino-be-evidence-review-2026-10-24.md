## First-party terms inspection — 24 October 2026

The first-party terms page was readable at https://starcasino.be/termsandconditions. It identifies **Chaudfontaine Loisirs SA** as publisher and merchant of record, and lists Green Island NV / Green Island Belgium BV as platform providers. The terms state a minimum registration age of **21**, a default moving seven-day deposit limit of **€200**, no credit-card deposits, and a **€10 minimum withdrawal** subject to approved methods, email validation, identity checks and possible additional security checks. The complaint clause directs users to support@starcasino.be, states a response target of **30 business days**, and says a complaint must be received within **7 days** of the issue or the operator may decline to accept it. The terms also describe a playing-break option and point to the Belgian Gaming Commission for self-exclusion information.

**Editorial issue to assess:** the seven-day complaint filing condition is material and should be quoted accurately with the applicable legal context, not hidden or interpreted as a legal conclusion. No deposit/withdrawal or support test was performed. Source page did not expose a clear revision date during this capture; recheck before publication.
