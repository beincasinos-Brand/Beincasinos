# Evidence batch: Betano UK regulator identity check

Checked: 2026-10-22
Status: internal evidence only; publication remains HOLD.

## What the primary register supports

The UK Gambling Commission public register lists **betano** as an **Active** trading name of **BV Gaming Limited**, account **39576**. The UKGC business search result also lists `betano.co.uk` as a matching domain. This is a useful regulator-backed identity/domain lead for a Great Britain research record.

It does not, by itself, prove that every product offered under the Betano brand is covered, that the site is available to every UK visitor, or that any consumer-experience claim is true. The reviewer must inspect the current account summary and licence scope, live customer terms, bonuses and wagering conditions, payment/withdrawal rules, complaint/ADR route, support channels, privacy/cookies, safer-gambling tools, and relevant regulatory history.

Do not transfer this UK record to Betano Brazil or any other country route. Each market needs its own current regulator and contracting-entity match.
