## Regulatory history — attribution matters

On 17 December 2025, the Commission published a settlement involving four licensees trading under Betfair and Paddy Power names. It found failures against remote customer-interaction requirements. The Commission's summary describes failings in Paddy Power Betfair social-responsibility controls following a compliance assessment in April and May 2024.
