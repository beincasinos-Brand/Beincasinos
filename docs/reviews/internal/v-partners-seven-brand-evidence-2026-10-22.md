## Publication blockers for every brand

- Capture the final consumer URL from the network's own outbound link, follow redirects, and record canonical domain.
- Identify the exact contracting entity in current consumer terms and match it to the official regulator's live record.
- Verify the exact domain and relevant product permission for each target jurisdiction; a general group/network claim is insufficient.
- Save dated copies/URLs for bonus rules, wagering, withdrawal methods, limits, fees, processing times, KYC, complaints/ADR, privacy, account closure and safer-gambling tools.
- Review the operator's regulatory history and distinguish entity-level actions from brand-specific findings.
- Do not claim BeInCasinos has registered, deposited, tested a withdrawal, tested game fairness, or contacted support unless the team actually did it.
- Include affiliate/commercial disclosure, a transparent methodology and a second-editor sign-off.
- Do not assign numerical scores until each score component has evidence and a reproducible rubric.

## Release decision

These seven records expand the 100-brand research pipeline; they do not make the overall 100-brand target complete. Keep each brand on HOLD until the above gates pass. No public review page, ranking, “verified” badge, or production deployment is authorised by this evidence batch alone.
