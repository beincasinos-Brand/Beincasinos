## Terms, verification and withdrawals

The general terms say customers will usually be able to withdraw an available balance, subject to conditions such as cleared deposits, completed verification, legal or regulatory requirements, or specified investigations. The operator says it may ask for further identity, address or source-of-funds information and may restrict an account if it cannot complete checks.

This means withdrawal availability depends on the account and circumstances; it would be inaccurate to promise an unconditional or fixed payout time from the general terms alone. ## Complaints and dispute resolution

Betfred publishes a staged complaints process. It says customer services aims to respond within 24 hours after receiving the relevant details; an internal case-review manager aims to respond within 72 hours; and complex matters may take up to eight weeks. Its preferred independent ADR provider is IBAS, although the procedure says another approved provider may be considered if IBAS is unavailable.

These are the operator's published process and target times, not a guarantee that an individual dispute will be resolved within a particular period. Customers should keep relevant dates, transaction records and the complaint reference.

Source: https://www.betfred.com/complaints

## Regulatory history that readers should see

The UKGC's public record for Petfre (Gibraltar) Limited includes these material decisions:

- **20 May 2025 — £240,000 financial penalty:** the Commission found a technical-standards breach following a key event reported by the operator. The Commission noted the operator identified and proactively reported the breach, took remedial action and cooperated.
- **30 June 2026 — £900,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty:** a licence review following a compliance assessment found shortcomings in remote customer-interaction controls. The Commission described weaknesses in identifying harm indicators and ensuring prompt action when strong indicators were present.

The decisions concern different issues and dates. A full public review should link the underlying notices, preserve the Commission's stated scope and avoid suggesting that remedial action or current control performance has been independently verified by this review.
