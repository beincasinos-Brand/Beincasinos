# Market-by-market 100-brand evidence gap audit
Date: 2026-10-23
Status: internal planning and QA only; no candidate in this file is approved for publication.

## Critical counting rule
The project goal is 100 distinct, evidence-backed, publication-approved brand reviews, not 100 names. Market variants of the same global brand do not become distinct global brands merely by repeating a name in several countries. Every profile must have a valid, product-specific legal basis for the target jurisdiction. Where online casino is prohibited or unavailable, do not fill a quota with an ineligible casino operator.

## Per-brand approval record (mandatory)
For every candidate, preserve:
- unique brand identity and duplicate-resolution rationale;
- target market, product vertical, canonical domain and date/time checked;
- regulator record, legal entity, permission, status and direct source;
- first-party terms, offer terms, payments/withdrawal policy, privacy/cookies, safer-gambling and complaint/ADR sources;
- dated facts, exact source quotations only where necessary, and a clear distinction between operator claims and tested observations;
- unresolved questions and explicit HOLD/APPROVED decision;
- independent second-editor and legal/editorial sign-off;
- proof the public artifact contains only approved content.
