## Legal market boundary

The French Autorité Nationale des Jeux (ANJ) current approved-operators list identifies `betclic.fr` for sports betting, horse betting and games of circle (poker), and `winamax.fr` for sports betting and poker. The ANJ states that online casino games, including online slot-machine games, are not authorised in France. A French sports-betting or poker approval is not a French online-casino licence.

This means neither brand should be presented as a French online casino on the strength of the ANJ list. To keep either in a casino-review portfolio, the research must first identify another specific jurisdiction where that casino product is legally offered and verify that market's regulator, domain, contracting entity and customer terms. If no suitable market is evidenced, exclude the brand from casino-review publication rather than stretching a sports/poker approval into casino authorisation.
