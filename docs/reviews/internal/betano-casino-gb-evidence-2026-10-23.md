## What current primary-source evidence supports

### Legal operator and permission
The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) licence summary for **BV Gaming Limited**, account **39576**, lists active remote casino, bingo, gambling software and remote betting permissions:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39576/

The UKGC trading-name register lists **betano** as an active trading name under the same account:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/trading-names/39576

The operator's current public website, https://www.betano.co.uk/, identifies the service as operated by BV Gaming Limited trading as Betano and states UKGC account number 39576. Its terms page likewise identifies a Great Britain remote operating licence and says it covers casino operation:
https://www.betano.co.uk/en-gb/pages/terms-conditions

### Important domain-register nuance
The UKGC's domain page for account 39576 lists **betano.co.uk as “White Label”**, rather than “Active”:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/39576

The regulator explains that domain and trading-name information is supplied by licensees and may not be guaranteed accurate:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses

This means the legal entity and product permission have useful support, but the exact consumer-domain relationship should be confirmed and documented rather than simplified into “active domain.” Recheck the footer, contractual terms, domain register and any relevant white-label arrangement before publication.

### Regulatory-action register
At the time of the search, the UKGC regulatory-actions page for BV Gaming Limited account 39576 stated that no regulatory actions had been recorded for this business:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/actions/39576

This is a scoped register result, not proof that there have never been complaints, incidents, actions against other entities, or actions in other jurisdictions. Reopen the current record immediately before publication and avoid broader claims than the record supports.
