2. **7 August 2025 — £33,075 financial penalty.** UKGC found a breach of SRCP 3.9.1 paragraph 3 concerning identification of individual customers across accounts held with group companies. The decision says the failure was due to human error, did not affect controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling, and that the operator self-identified and took remedial action. Decision: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/299
