# Brand identity reconciliation note — Jackpot City (Great Britain)

**Status:** HOLD — internal QA note, not a consumer review.  
**Date checked:** 24 October 2026.

## Why this record is blocked

The UK Gambling Commission register's record for **Betway Limited (account 39372)** lists **jackpotcity.co.uk** and **jackpotcitycasino.co.uk** as Active domains and lists **jackpot city** as an Active trading name.

Sources:
- Licence summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39372
- Domain list: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/39372
- Trading names: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/trading-names/39372
- Register search result for Jackpot City: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/results?business-search=Jackpot+city&uc=true

A search also surfaced a different-looking site, **jackpot-citycasino-uk.com**, whose own terms claim it is operated by Betway Limited/account 39372, while its separate licence-information page claims a different operator, **Bayton Ltd**, and a different UKGC account/licence number. Those claims conflict with each other and do not match the domain names shown in the UKGC account 39372 domain list. Treat this as an identity mismatch requiring direct regulator reconciliation; do not treat that domain as an official licensed Jackpot City destination based on the search result alone.

Potentially conflicting site surfaced during search:

- https://jackpot-citycasino-uk.com/license-information/

This note does **not** conclude that the site is fraudulent or illegal. It records an unresolved discrepancy between self-published claims and the official register. Do not reproduce the conflicting site's licence number, operator claims, bonus terms, privacy wording, logos or screenshots as verified facts.
