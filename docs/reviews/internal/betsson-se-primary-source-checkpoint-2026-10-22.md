## Primary-source observation

The Swedish Gambling Authority's licence-register entry for `betsson.com` lists the domain and the licence holders **Betsson Nordic Ltd** and **Spin Nordic Ltd**. The entry displays commercial online gambling permission for Spin Nordic Ltd from **24 August 2026 to 23 August 2031**, and commercial betting permissions as separate entries. Source: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/licensurl/?url=betsson.com

The regulator describes its licence register as a list of actors with valid licences or permits and says it includes website addresses for online and betting licensees: https://www2.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/

## What this does and does not establish

- It is primary-source evidence that the regulator's register associates `betsson.com` with the named licence holders and lists an active commercial-online licence entry for Spin Nordic Ltd.
- It does **not**, by itself, settle which entity is the current contracting party for every Swedish customer journey or how the older Betsson Nordic Ltd entry should be treated after the transition.
- Do not infer product quality, payout speed, bonus value, or customer-service performance from a licence listing.
