**Research date:** 23 October 2026  
**Market:** Sweden only  
**Status:** **HOLD — internal evidence review, not publication-ready**  
**Method:** Spelinspektionen official licence/enforcement records and a first-party access attempt. No account, deposit, withdrawal, support contact or real-money play was tested.

## Provisional assessment

Spelinspektionen's official notice dated **2 June 2025** states that **Betsson Nordic Ltd** holds a licence for commercial online gambling and betting on **betsson.com/sv**. This is a regulator-backed Sweden-specific licence/domain lead.

The regulator issued Betsson Nordic Ltd a warning and **SEK 6.5 million** sanction fee on **28 May 2025** for deficiencies in anti-money-laundering controls, including insufficient customer due diligence and insufficient information about the source of customer funds. The administrative court rejected the operator's appeal on **2 July 2026**, finding serious AML-rule violations; the regulator's decision page states that the operator appealed to the Administrative Court of Appeal. That appeal status must be checked again before publication.

A direct first-party fetch of betsson.com/sv returned a **403 “Not Available”** page with text saying the site was unavailable in the request's country due to online-gambling laws. This research environment's location is not evidence of what a Swedish resident sees, so the response must not be treated as proof that the Swedish service is unavailable. It does mean the current consumer site and terms were not inspected in this pass.

**No score or recommendation is assigned.** Current Swedish customer terms, casino bonus terms, payments/withdrawals, complaints, safer-gambling controls, privacy/cookies, accessibility, enforcement appeal outcome and independent second review remain open.
