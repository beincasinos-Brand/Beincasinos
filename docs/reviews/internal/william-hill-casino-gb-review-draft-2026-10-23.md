## Regulatory history

On 7 August 2025 the UKGC imposed a financial penalty of £82,687 on WHG (International) Limited after finding a failure to comply with a remote customer-identification requirement. The Commission's decision concerned steps required when a customer held one or more accounts with the licensee and one or more accounts with a group company. The UKGC noted that the operator self-identified the breach, reported it proactively, took remedial action and cooperated with the investigation.

The decision is operator-level evidence about a defined historic issue. It should not be attributed to a different William Hill legal entity or treated as proof of the present condition of every account or control.
