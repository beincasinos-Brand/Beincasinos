## Review evidence checklist
1. Capture the live iGaming Ontario operator entry, legal operator name, exact domain and date checked.
2. Confirm the domain and casino product are covered by the current Ontario registration; don't infer status from another province or from an international homepage.
3. Match the site's legal notice, terms, privacy policy and contracting entity to the register.
4. Capture dated Ontario-specific bonus terms, if any: eligibility, wagering, expiry, maximum bet, eligible games, payment exclusions and maximum cashout. If terms are incomplete, do not publish a bonus amount.
5. Verify payment methods, deposits, withdrawals, limits, fees, processing estimates and verification restrictions; no claims of personal testing without an actual test.
6. Verify age/location eligibility, player-protection tools, self-exclusion, support, complaints, privacy/cookies, accessibility and mobile UX.
7. Clear image rights; add affiliate disclosure; complete consumer testing, independent second review and editorial/legal approval.
