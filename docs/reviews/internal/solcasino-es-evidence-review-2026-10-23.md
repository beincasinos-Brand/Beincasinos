## Provisional assessment

SolCasino's DGOJ profile for **ESTRELLA SOL INTERACTIVE, S.A.** directly lists **solcasino.es** and singular licences for slots, blackjack and roulette. The profile records **18 March 2026** extensions for blackjack and roulette. The current first-party game contract is version **15 October 2026**, names the same legal operator, and lists licence identifiers for the Spanish product. This is a well-supported Spain-specific casino-vertical lead.

The first-party welcome page promotes up to **€1,800** across three deposit bonuses: 200% up to €600 on the first deposit, 250% up to €600 on the second, and 300% up to €600 on the third. It states a €10 minimum deposit, the three offers are available during the first 30 days after registration, each bonus lasts seven days, the wagering requirement is **40× the bonus amount**, and the maximum conversion to real balance is **1× the bonus amount**. However, the general promotions terms separately state a default **50×** rollover for casino/slots unless specific promotion terms differ. The offer-specific terms must be reconciled before describing the requirement as settled; do not hide this conflict.

The general contract says withdrawal orders are executed within 24 hours after the request, after required checks and subject to bank/payment-service availability. Additional identity/payment-owner documents can suspend that period. It also says that while identity is checked and documentary verification remains pending, a player may deposit up to a combined €150 but **cannot withdraw any prize** until the required checks are complete. These are operator-published contract terms, not independently tested payment results.

**No score or recommendation is assigned.** Current exact bonus terms, withdrawal methods/fees, full privacy notice, actual cookie-consent behaviour, accessibility, current enforcement history and independent second review remain open.

## Current contract — identity, eligibility and withdrawals

Primary source: https://solcasino.es/termsandconditions

The general game contract states version **15 October 2026** and identifies Estrella Sol Interactive, S.A. as the operator. It states that the Spanish service is for adults aged 18 or older and Spanish residents participating from Spanish territory.

Key operator-published terms:
- Before all identity/document checks are complete, the contract allows a combined deposit of up to **€150** in the described pending-document scenario but says prizes cannot be withdrawn until the required checks are complete.
- Withdrawals are made to the same payment method used for the deposit, except where that method is deposit-only; any alternative withdrawal method must be valid and owned by the participant.
- The withdrawal amount is blocked from further use while the request is verified and approved.
- Solcasino says it will execute withdrawal orders within **24 hours** of the request after checks, subject to banking/payment-service availability.
- Additional identity or payment-ownership documents (the contract gives examples such as updated identification, selfies, video calls or bank certificates) may be requested; the 24-hour period is suspended while those documents are supplied and validated.
- The contract does not establish that a withdrawal was personally tested, nor does the evidence captured here establish the current list of cashier methods, fees, per-method limits or external bank settlement times.

These points should be presented as written policy only, not as a promise that every withdrawal reaches a bank within 24 hours.

## Welcome offer — current page and terms conflict to reconcile

First-party sources:
- Welcome page: https://solcasino.es/bono-bienvenida
- General promotion terms: https://solcasino.es/terminos-y-condiciones-de-las-promociones
- Current promotions overview: https://solcasino.es/promociones

The welcome page currently states:
- First deposit: **200% up to €600**.
- Second deposit: **250% up to €600**.
- Third deposit: **300% up to €600**.
- Minimum deposit: **€10**.
- The three deposit bonuses must be claimed during the first **30 days** after registration.
- Each credited bonus is valid for **7 days**.
- Welcome page states rollover of **40× the bonus amount**.
- Maximum conversion to withdrawable real balance is **1× the bonus amount**.

**Material caveat:** the general promotions terms state a default rollover of **50×** the bonus for casino and slots, with the specific offer terms taking precedence where they differ. The welcome landing page says 40×, but the extracted public page does not provide the complete offer-specific legal text needed to confirm which terms govern every component. Do not publish a simple “40× wagering” claim without obtaining and matching the complete offer-specific terms and checking whether the promotion uses different rules by game category.

The general promotions terms also say:
- Casino, slots and betting are separate categories; using a casino bonus in slots or betting can cancel the bonus and invalidate related plays/winnings.
- General rollover contributions differ by game category: the document states 100% for slots, 25% for certain roulette bets and 5% for blackjack; some roulette bets, blackjack surrender bets and jackpot games do not count, and specified patterns of sports bets are excluded.
- In general, bonus expiry is seven days unless the specific promotion says otherwise.
- The operator reserves rights to cancel promotions/related winnings for abuse or breach of the terms.

These are important restrictions; they should be summarised in plain Spanish and checked against the actual welcome-offer terms before publication.

- A self-assessment questionnaire is offered.
- The operator describes a free, confidential safer-gambling telephone service at **951 30 50 12**, available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, staffed by trained personnel.
- The contract allows complaints to the operator within **six months** of the event giving rise to the complaint, through the published contact channels, and says a complaint may also be submitted to the DGOJ.

The contact page lists telephone support **09:30–01:00, seven days a week**, email at any time, and chat. These are published hours/channels, not tested response quality.
