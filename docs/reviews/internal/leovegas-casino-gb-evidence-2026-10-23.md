## Regulatory history — material context

The UKGC published two historic enforcement decisions involving LeoVegas Gaming PLC:

- **2 May 2018:** the Commission announced a **£600,000 penalty** following misleading advertising and failures relating to customers at the end of self-exclusion. The published account said 41 misleading adverts were identified and that funds received from the failings were to be divested. Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/leovegas-penalised-for-advertising-and-marketing-failings
- **3 August 2022:** the Commission announced a **£1.32 million penalty**, an official warning and an audit following social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures. Primary source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/online-operator-leovegas-fined-gbp1-2-million

The current UKGC sanctions page for account 39198 says no regulatory actions have been recorded for this business, but the public-register action view is not a substitute for checking older published decisions. The historic actions above must be disclosed accurately in any balanced review; do not describe the operator as having a completely clean enforcement history.
