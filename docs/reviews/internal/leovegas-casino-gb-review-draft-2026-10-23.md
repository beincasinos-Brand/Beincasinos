## Regulatory history

### 2 May 2018 — £600,000 penalty package

The Commission reported failings relating to misleading advertising and the handling of customers at the end of self-exclusion. Its summary stated that LeoVegas was responsible for 41 misleading advertisements, failed to return funds to 11,205 customers who chose to self-exclude and close accounts, sent marketing material to 1,894 people who had previously self-excluded, and allowed 413 previously self-excluded customers to gamble without speaking to them first or applying a 24-hour cooling-off period. The penalty package also included divestment of funds received as a result of failings and the Commission's costs.

Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/leovegas-penalised-for-advertising-and-marketing-failings

### 3 August 2022 — £1.32 million penalty

The Commission reported social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failings, issued an official warning and required an audit of the operator's implementation of relevant controls. Its published examples included spending triggers set at levels that were not adequately justified, an unexplained six-hour threshold before a cooling-off period, failure to act on indicators such as cancelled withdrawals or long sessions, and inadequate AML thresholds and customer financial-risk assessment.

Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/online-operator-leovegas-fined-gbp1-2-million
