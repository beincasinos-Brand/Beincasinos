## Regulatory history — mandatory contextual review

The UKGC register records regulatory actions for 888 UK Limited. At least the following decisions are surfaced in the current register and must be read in full before publication:

- **19 August 2024:** £23,000 financial penalty associated with a breach of licence condition 6.1.2 concerning use of credit cards, after an investigation into a key-event submission. The register notes the licensee self-identified the breach, reported it proactively, took immediate remedial action and cooperated. Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/272
- **7 August 2025:** £33,075 financial penalty for a failure concerning identification of individual customers across accounts with the licensee and a group company. The register notes self-identification, cooperation and remedial action. Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/272
- **18 November 2025:** £86,843 financial penalty for a breach relating to remote self-exclusion. The register notes the issue was due to human error, says controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling were not affected, and records remedial action and cooperation. Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/299
