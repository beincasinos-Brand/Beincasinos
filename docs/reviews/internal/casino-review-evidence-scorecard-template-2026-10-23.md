# Casino review evidence scorecard — internal template

**Status:** Internal editorial tool. Not a public score, ranking, or recommendation.
**Purpose:** Make evidence quality, market eligibility, and unresolved risks explicit before any review is approved.

## Gate 0 — eligibility (all must pass)

| Check | Required evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Exact market is stated | Country/territory and product scope | PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN |
| Active permission is confirmed | Primary regulator record for exact legal entity and product | PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN |
| Brand/domain mapping is confirmed | Regulator domain/trading-name record plus first-party terms | PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN |
| Contracting party is known | Current first-party terms for the target market | PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN |
| Product is lawful and eligible for intended audience | Current regulator / legal-policy checks appropriate to market | PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN |

**Rule:** Any FAIL or UNKNOWN means **HOLD**. Do not include the brand in a “best” list or add commercial recommendation links until resolved.

## Evidence quality scale

Score each dimension from 0 to 3. A score measures evidence completeness, not whether the casino is “good.”

- **0 — Missing/contradicted:** no reliable evidence or material contradiction.
- **1 — Partial:** one source, stale evidence, unclear scope, or key conditions missing.
- **2 — Strong desk evidence:** current primary evidence captured and dated; independent corroboration where appropriate.
- **3 — Verified:** strong current primary evidence plus documented direct journey testing where lawful, reproducible test notes, and independent reviewer confirmation.

## Publication gates

A review can proceed to editorial approval only if all are true:

- [ ] Gate 0 eligibility is PASS for the exact target market.
- [ ] No unresolved contradiction exists in legal entity, licence, domain or current terms.
- [ ] Every material factual claim has a source, check date and market scope.
- [ ] Policy statements are not presented as tested performance.
- [ ] No deposit, withdrawal, support or gameplay test is claimed unless it was actually performed and logged.
- [ ] Historic regulatory action is attributed to the exact licensee and the regulator's stated scope; group actions are not casually attributed to one brand.
- [ ] Safer-gambling and complaints information is complete and prominent.
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure is visible; commercial relationships do not affect factual findings or scoring.
- [ ] A second editor has checked source accuracy, fairness, clarity and originality.
- [ ] The page has a distinct reader benefit and does not duplicate another URL.
- [ ] Final page is checked for canonical, indexability, structured-data accuracy and sitemap eligibility.
- [ ] Next review date and correction owner are assigned.

## Scoring and ranking warning

Do not average this evidence-completeness score into a consumer quality rating. A brand can have extensive documentation and still be unsuitable; eligibility failures are hard stops. Any separate consumer score must use a published, market-specific method with reproducible weights and must not be for sale.
