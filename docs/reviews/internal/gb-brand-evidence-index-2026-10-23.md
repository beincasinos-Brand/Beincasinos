## Minimum bar for changing a brand to VERIFIED

A candidate is not VERIFIED until the same current market-specific domain is matched to the current contracting operator and active remote casino permission in the regulator register, the current terms and key policies are captured, the product's actual availability is checked lawfully, and the claim-level evidence is reviewed. A parent-company relationship, legacy licence, generic search result, or brand name alone is insufficient.

## Minimum bar for changing a review to APPROVED

A separate reviewer must check claim-to-source traceability, dated offer terms, payment and withdrawal claims, limitations on any hands-on testing, safer-gambling information, conflicts/affiliate disclosure, and market-specific legal/editorial requirements. A research pack is not an approved review.
