## Material UKGC action — decision dated 30 October 2025

The UKGC imposed a **£650,000 financial penalty**, a warning and an additional licence condition after finding anti-money-laundering (AML) and social-responsibility failures relating to the period October 2023 to February 2024. The regulator also required a third-party audit to assess whether the operator was effectively implementing its AML and safer-gambling policies, procedures and controls.

The UKGC described a monthly deposit-limit system that did not include the customer's initial deposit, resulting in customers exceeding their stated monthly limits. It also found that systems did not effectively identify some potentially harmful activity, and that AML risk scoring and controls failed to trigger timely scrutiny in some cases involving high-volume deposits and withdrawals and digital prepaid vouchers.
