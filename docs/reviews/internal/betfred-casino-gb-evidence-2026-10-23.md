## 2. Material regulator actions — include context

### 30 June 2026 — £900,000 settlement
The UKGC states that a review following a May–June 2024 compliance assessment found failings in remote customer-interaction/social-responsibility controls under SRCP 3.4.3. Among the findings, the business lacked sufficient processes to identify indicators of harm and ensure prompt automated action; a flagged customer could go seven days without a further review even if further indicators appeared. The regulator's public statement says one consumer lost £17,900 within 24 hours without an additional interaction. The licensee will make a £900,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty. The regulator also records that the licensee implemented interim controls and an action plan.

Primary sources:
- UKGC action register: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/actions/39544
- UKGC public statement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/petfre-gibraltar-limited-to-pay-gbp900-000-for-regulatory-failures

### 20 May 2025 — £240,000 financial penalty
The UKGC found a breach of licence condition 2.3.1 concerning remote technical standards following a key-event submission. The regulator noted that the licensee identified and proactively reported the issue, took immediate remedial action and cooperated:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/286

The Commission's public statement about the technical-standard case explains that some slot games failed to show the customer's net position clearly and celebrated returns that were less than or equal to the total stake. The operator removed affected games:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/gbp240-000-fine-for-petfre-gibraltar-limited
