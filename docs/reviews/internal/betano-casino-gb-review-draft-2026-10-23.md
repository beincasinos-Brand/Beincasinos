## Terms and withdrawal conditions

The current GB terms say a customer can generally withdraw cleared real-money balance if relevant conditions are met. Those conditions include confirmation that payments have cleared, completion of verification checks, compliance with relevant withdrawal or bonus conditions, and the absence of certain legal or regulatory restrictions or specified ongoing investigations.

The terms state a minimum withdrawal amount of £5. Where the balance is below £5 but at least £1, the customer is directed to contact customer service for assistance; balances below £1 are stated not to be processed. The terms also say withdrawals should normally be returned to the same payment method as the main funding deposit, with alternative arrangements potentially considered if that is not possible, and a reasonable processing fee may apply in specified circumstances.

These are clauses in the published terms, not an independent test of actual processing time. This draft does not claim that withdrawals are fast, slow or guaranteed by a particular deadline.
