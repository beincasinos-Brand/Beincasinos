# GB candidate evidence — LeoVegas, Mr Green and Casumo

**Checked:** 22 October 2026 (UTC). Internal evidence log only; not a consumer review or publication approval.

## 1. LeoVegas — active UKGC permission; historical enforcement must be disclosed accurately
- UKGC account 39198 (LeoVegas Gaming PLC) shows an active Casino Remote licence: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39198
- UKGC domain record lists `leovegas.co.uk` as Active, while `leovegas.com` and `www.leovegas.com` are Inactive under this record: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/39198
- The UKGC announced on 3 August 2022 that LeoVegas would pay a £1.32m penalty package for social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures; the notice described the domains/products then operated by the business: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/online-operator-leovegas-fined-gbp1-2-million
- **Interpretation:** active licence status does not erase past enforcement. Attribute the 2022 action to the regulator's stated licensee and scope; separately confirm the current live UK site and terms.
- **Decision:** HOLD pending current terms, domain/contracting-entity match, payments/withdrawals, complaint/ADR, privacy, safer-gambling and second-editor review.

## 2. Mr Green — old GB record is surrendered; do not call it currently UKGC licensed from this record
- UKGC account 39264 (Mr Green Limited) shows Casino Remote licence surrendered on 28 October 2024: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39264
- The domain record for `www.mrgreen.com` under this account is Inactive, and the trading name Mr Green is marked Inactive: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/39264
- The UKGC's 28 March 2023 group enforcement notice stated Mr Green Limited, which ran mrgreen.com at the time, would pay £3.7m as part of a £19.2m total package across three William Hill Group businesses: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/william-hill-group-businesses-to-pay-record-gbp19-2m-for-failures
- **Interpretation:** the historical GB licence record is not current authority. A new market review requires independent identification of the actual present operator and regulator for that market. Do not conflate past GB authorisation with permission elsewhere.
- **Decision:** NOT APPROVED as a currently UKGC-authorised GB casino on the cited record; retain the negative finding and investigate other jurisdictions only with separate regulator evidence.

## 3. Casumo — Casumo-branded legacy record surrendered; register points to another entity/domain route
- UKGC search results list Casumo Services Limited (account 39265) and Recro Limited (account 61549) against `casumo.com`: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/results?business-search=casumo
- Casumo Services Limited's Casino Remote permission was surrendered on 3 November 2023; its `casumo.com` domain and trading-name entries are Inactive under that record: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39265
- A UKGC freedom-of-information response published in 2025 and saved 30 September 2026 says Casumo does not have a licence in its own right and that the website is run by licensed operator Recro Limited. The response declines to confirm or deny further correspondence under the stated law-enforcement exemption: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/about-us/freedomofinformation/casumo-services-ltd
- The UKGC announced a £6m Casumo penalty package on 25 March 2021 after social-responsibility and AML failings: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/regulatory-action-against-casumo
- **Interpretation:** do not use the surrendered Casumo Services Limited record to assert current status. Verify Recro Limited's current permission, exact domain mapping and present customer terms before making a GB-specific claim. The 2021 enforcement is historical context and should be attributed accurately.
- **Decision:** HOLD pending verification of the current contracting entity/domain, live licence and all consumer-review checks.
