## Review evidence checklist
1. Capture the live PGCB listing and exact PA-authorised casino URL with date.
2. Match the consumer site's legal notice, terms and privacy policy to the certificateholder/operator relationship.
3. Capture dated bonus terms and exclusions only where the offer is live and terms are accessible; include wagering, expiry, max bet, game exclusions, payment restrictions and max cashout.
4. Verify deposits and withdrawals, limits, fees, processing estimates, identity checks and restrictions. Do not state a withdrawal was tested without a genuine authorised test.
5. Verify responsible-gambling tools, self-exclusion, support, complaints, privacy/cookies and account-security practices.
6. Check mobile usability, accessibility, image rights, affiliate disclosure, independent second review and editorial/legal approval.
