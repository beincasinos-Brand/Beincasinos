# Sweden regulator check — Betsson appeal status confirmed on 23 October 2026

This is a dated internal update to the Betsson Sweden evidence dossier, not a consumer review.

## Official current checks
1. Spelinspektionen's domain-specific licence register page for `betsson.com/sv` was fetched successfully on 23 October 2026. It lists **Betsson Nordic Ltd** and **Spin Nordic Ltd** as licence holders and shows **Kommersiellt online** (commercial online gambling) valid **24 August 2026–23 August 2031**. The same page separately lists commercial betting permissions; do not confuse betting permission with online-gambling permission. Source: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/licensurl/?url=betsson.com%2Fsv
2. The regulator's decision register labels the Betsson Nordic Ltd AML matter **“Överklagad”** (appealed). It records the original decision dated **28 May 2025**, the Administrative Court judgment dated **2 July 2026** rejecting the company's appeal and upholding the regulator's assessment, and says Betsson Nordic Ltd has appealed to the Administrative Court of Appeal. Source: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/vara-beslut/beslutslista/betsson-nordic-ltd---varning-penningtvatt/
3. The regulator's public judgment notice states that the Administrative Court found serious AML-rule violations involving insufficient customer due diligence for Betsson Nordic Ltd, Snabbare Ltd and Spooniker Ltd. Source: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/nyhetsarkiv/dom-forvaltningsratten-faststaller---varning-och-sanktionsavgift-for-tre-bolag/

## Editorial handling
- Current source check confirms the appeal remains listed as appealed on the regulator page at the time of this research; no final appellate disposition is shown there.
- Describe the 2025 warning/sanction, July 2026 Administrative Court judgment and continuing appeal separately. Do not imply the sanction has been finally settled, nor infer current operational non-compliance from the historical case alone.
- Keep the exact domain and licence holder matched to the Swedish product and customer terms. Do not count `betsson.com` and `betsson.com/sv`, or the two listed licence holders, as separate consumer brands.
- Consumer review gates remain open: current Swedish terms, offer-specific bonus conditions, payment/withdrawal limits and fees, payout timing, complaints/ADR, privacy/cookies, accessibility and evidence of the responsible-gambling controls.
- No account, deposit, gameplay, withdrawal, KYC or support test was performed.

**Disposition: HOLD. Approved reviews added: 0.**
