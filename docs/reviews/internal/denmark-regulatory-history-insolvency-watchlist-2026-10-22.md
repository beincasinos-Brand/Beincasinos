## 2. Cashpoint Limited / successor name Merkur Bets Malta Limited — historical AML enforcement

Official Danish Gambling Authority decision summary, 31 March 2023: https://spillemyndigheden.dk/en-us/rulings-and-reactions/merkur-bets-malta-limited-formerly-cashpoint-limited-reprimanded-for-breach-of-the-anti-money-laundering-act

The regulator states that on 29 March 2023 it reprimanded Merkur Bets Malta Limited (formerly Cashpoint Limited) for inadequate written procedures relating to customer due diligence in land-based betting. The described deficiency concerned not retaining a copy of ID shown by customers claiming winnings above DKK 14,870. The regulator says the procedures were subsequently revised and the breach no longer existed; it describes the reprimand as not entailing an obligation to act.

A separate older official Danish-language decision, dated 27 January 2022, records an order and two reprimands concerning AML risk assessment and business procedures: https://spillemyndigheden.dk/afgoerelser-og-domme/paabud-og-to-paataler-til-merkur-bets-malta-limited-tidligere-cashpoint-limited-for-overtraedelse-af-hvidvaskloven
