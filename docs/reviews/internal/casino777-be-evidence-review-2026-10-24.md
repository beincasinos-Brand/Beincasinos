**Editorial implication:** clearly disclose that account authentication can be a prerequisite for play and withdrawal; do not promise instant access or imply that withdrawal processing was tested.

### Deposits and withdrawals

The terms say withdrawals are processed upon request when the account is authenticated and has a sufficient positive balance, subject to the terms and checks; standard bank processing times still apply. The terms also state that the operator does not charge deposit or withdrawal fees regardless of payment method. This is a published contractual statement, not a guarantee that a player's bank or payment provider will charge no fees, and not a transaction test.

The terms describe personal deposit methods only, a default deposit-limit process, and personal limits for 24 hours, 7 days or 1 month. They state that reducing a personal limit takes effect immediately, while an increase only takes effect after 72 hours. The terms state that if the default limit is removed and no personal limit is set, a €2,000 weekly limit applies. The precise applicable default limit and any current statutory changes must be rechecked against the live account flow and Belgian rules before publication.

### Complaints and dispute route

The terms say complaints about games or winnings can be submitted through the site's Help tab or the operator's listed email. They state a **14-day** filing window after the triggering event. The terms also point players to the Belgian Gaming Commission for information requests or complaints. The short contractual window is a material term to explain neutrally; legal enforceability and interaction with statutory rights require legal/editorial review before public characterization.
