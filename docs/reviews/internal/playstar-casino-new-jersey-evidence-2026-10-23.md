# PlayStar Casino — New Jersey evidence note

**Checked:** 2026-10-23  
**Status:** Internal research only — **HOLD / not approved for publication**  
**Market scope:** New Jersey, United States only for this evidence record.

## Evidence established

1. The New Jersey Division of Gaming Enforcement (DGE) authorized-sites page lists `nj.playstar.com` under Ocean Resort Casino. The DGE states that sites not included on the list are not approved to offer internet gaming in New Jersey.
   - Primary source: https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/internet-gaming-sites/
2. PlayStar's published terms identify **PlayStar NJ LLC** as operating `nj.playstar.com`, and state that it is licensed and regulated by the New Jersey DGE for real-money internet/mobile gaming.
   - Primary source: https://playstar.com/terms-and-conditions
3. Ocean Casino Resort Holdings' Q4 2025 financial report lists the PlayStar skin as operated by PlayStar NJ, LLC, commencing in August 2022, and describes a contractual revenue arrangement with AC Ocean Walk, LLC. This is corroborating corporate reporting, not a substitute for player-facing terms.
   - Primary source: https://www.nj.gov/oag/ge/docs/Financials/QuarterlyFinRpt2025/OceanQ42025.pdf

## What this does and does not establish

- Supports a narrow claim that PlayStar is an authorized New Jersey internet-gaming brand/domain with PlayStar NJ LLC identified in its terms.
- Does **not** establish authorization in any other state or country.
- Does not establish a tested player experience, payout speed, game fairness certification, current bonus value, payment limits, or support quality.
- The casino's DGE complaint route requires customers to complain to the casino first and receive a response before filing an internet dispute form with the DGE. See https://www.njoag.gov/about/divisions-and-offices/division-of-gaming-enforcement-home/internet-gaming-information/
