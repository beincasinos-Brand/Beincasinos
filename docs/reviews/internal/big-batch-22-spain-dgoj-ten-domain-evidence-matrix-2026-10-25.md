## Required evidence before an individual review can be scored
1. Save the current DGOJ entity record and the exact general + singular game licences for the domain and product.
2. Match domain, contracting entity, company identifier and legal notice; resolve any group-company ambiguity.
3. Capture current welcome offer and full Spanish terms with date checked, wagering, eligible games, max bet, expiry, deposit threshold, excluded payment methods, max cashout and withdrawal conditions. If any critical term is missing, omit the bonus claim and keep bonus score unassigned.
4. Verify deposits and withdrawals separately: payment options, limits, fees, processing times, identity checks and complaint route.
5. Record safer-gambling controls, RGIAJ/self-exclusion information, limits, time-outs, account closure, privacy/cookies and support channels.
6. Test mobile/desktop usability and accessibility; do not imply consumer testing until actually performed.
7. Confirm image rights or use approved original artwork; a logo being publicly accessible is not proof of reuse rights.
8. Affiliate disclosure, independent second review and editorial/legal approval must be documented.
