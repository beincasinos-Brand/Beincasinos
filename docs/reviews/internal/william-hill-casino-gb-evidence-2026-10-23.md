## 2. Regulatory history

The UKGC record for account 39225 shows a decision dated **7 August 2025** with a **£82,687 financial penalty**. The regulator found a failure concerning remote customer identification across accounts held by the licensee and group companies, contrary to SRCP condition 3.9.1. The regulator noted that the licensee self-identified and reported the breach, took remedial action and cooperated:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/actions/39225
