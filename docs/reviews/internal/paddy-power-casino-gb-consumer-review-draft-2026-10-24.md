## Regulatory record — material finding

On **17 December 2025**, the UK Gambling Commission published a settlement concerning four licensees trading under the names Paddy Power and Betfair. The Commission said a compliance assessment in April and May 2024 found failings in remote customer-interaction controls under Social Responsibility Code Provision 3.4.3, including issues with identifying indicators of harm and acting in a timely manner. The four licensees agreed to a total payment in lieu of financial penalty of **£2,000,000**, plus the published statement and Commission costs.

This is a group/ multi-licensee settlement, not a finding to attribute solely to the casino product or solely to PPB Entertainment Limited. The final review must explain the entity scope, dates, findings and the regulator's recorded mitigating context, including the action plan and cooperation. It must not omit this material finding or imply that the current product has been independently retested.
