## 888casino

The UKGC register maps 888casino.com to 888 UK Limited, account 39028. The regulator's published decisions include a £23,000 penalty dated 19 August 2024 concerning the credit-card-use licence condition, a £33,075 penalty dated 7 August 2025 concerning customer identification across group-company accounts, and an £86,843 penalty dated 18 November 2025 concerning a self-exclusion code provision. The regulator's decision describes the latter as human error, notes the controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling were not affected, and records remedial action and self-reporting. Preserve each decision's scope and context in any future review.
