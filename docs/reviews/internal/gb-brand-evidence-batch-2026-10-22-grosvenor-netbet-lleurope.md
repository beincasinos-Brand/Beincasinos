# Regulatory evidence batch — Grosvenor, NetBet and L & L Europe

**Checked:** 2026-10-22. Internal research note only. Do not publish as a completed consumer review.

## Grosvenor Casinos

The UK Gambling Commission's settlement for **Grosvenor Casinos Limited**, account **614**, was decided **7 October 2026**. The regulator found AML-control failures and failures in premises-based customer interaction requirements. The agreed payment in lieu of a financial penalty was **£5,012,261**, with an external third-party audit commitment within six months of conclusion of the licence review. The public statement describes examples involving missed safer-gambling interactions, including a customer losing £50,000 during a period without the required interaction, and a returning self-excluded customer who lost £25,000 before interaction.

Primary sources:
- Settlement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/347
- Public statement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/public-statement/detail/grosvenor-casinos-limited-public-statement
- News summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/land-based-casino-operators-to-pay-gbp5m-for-regulatory-failures

**Fairness rule:** clearly identify this as a land-based premises-based action and the operator entity. Do not automatically characterize it as a failure of Grosvenor's online casino product. The online product's current operator and controls need separate evidence.

## NetBet — relevant market evidence, not currently a row in the 100-brand registry

UKGC lists **Netbet Enterprises Limited**, account **39170**, with active Casino Remote permission; `casino.netbet.co.uk` and `netbet.co.uk` are listed active. The regulator's settlement dated **5 November 2025** concerned AML/CTF and remote customer-interaction controls, marketing requirements and regulatory returns, and included a **£650,000** payment in lieu of a financial penalty.

Primary sources:
- Licence summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/39170
- Domains: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/39170
- Settlement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/293
- Public statement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/public-statement/detail/netbet-enterprises-limited-public-statement

NetBet is documented here as a research lead only because it is not one of the current 100 candidate rows. Do not silently replace an existing row or imply it has been added to the registry.

## L & L Europe Limited — portfolio risk signal, not an exact brand-level review

A UKGC settlement dated **21 October 2026** requires **L & L Europe Limited** to make a **£900,000** payment in lieu of a financial penalty. The regulator found issues with unfair withdrawal terms/withholding, AML/CTF controls, remote customer interaction and technical standards for financial limits. The regulator's news page names several sites run by the operator, including allbritishcasino.com, casinocasino.com and hypercasino.com.

Primary sources:
- Settlement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/350
- Public statement: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/public-statement/detail/l-and-l-europe-limited-public-statement
- News summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/l-and-l-europe-pays-gbp900-000-for-failures-including-withdrawal-delays

The 100-brand registry does not currently contain a clearly matching L & L Europe brand row. Record this as an operator-level watchlist lead, not as a completed review or a claim about every brand in its portfolio.
