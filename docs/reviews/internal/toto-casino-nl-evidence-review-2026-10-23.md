The notice names TOTO and Winnitt under the same legal entity and says all eight recipients meet the requirements to continue operating in the Dutch online gambling market. A material enforcement item is the Ksa's public **Wwft aanwijzing** (formal instruction/remedial order) concerning TOTO Online B.V. The decision identifies shortcomings in ongoing customer-relationship and transaction monitoring, risk classification, and source-of-funds investigation. The regulator found that one specific PEP source-of-funds issue had been remedied during the process, but the broader deficiencies remained and a formal instruction was imposed with a six-month compliance period. This was **not a monetary fine** in the decision text reviewed. The decision PDF has a date inconsistency: its cover states 28 October 2025 while the signature page says 24 July 2025; the publication/decision chronology should be independently reconciled before the date is used in public copy.

The renewal decision is a later event and should be reported separately. The Ksa says it assessed applicants' definitively established violations from the preceding five years and that all eight met renewal requirements, but the notice does not specifically state that every element of the TOTO Wwft instruction was closed. Do not imply either a clean enforcement record or that the renewal proves the historical issue was fully resolved.

**No score or recommendation is assigned.** Current full terms, complete bonus conditions, payment/withdrawal limits and fees, full complaint escalation details, live cookie-consent behaviour, accessibility testing, current enforcement/appeal status and independent second review remain incomplete.

## Regulatory history — Ksa Wwft instruction

Primary decision PDF:
https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025-28-10_01.356.880_Aanwijzing%20Wwft_TOTO%20Online_sanctiebesluit.pdf

The public decision describes a Ksa investigation into TOTO Online B.V.'s compliance with the Dutch Anti-Money Laundering and Anti-Terrorist Financing Act (Wwft). Its summary states that the regulator found a violation of Article 3(1), read with Article 3(2)(d), and issued an **aanwijzing** under Article 28 directing the company to follow specified conduct requirements within six months.

The decision's findings include:
- Ongoing monitoring of customer relationships and transactions was insufficient in important respects.
- Risk classification and the handling/documentation of transaction-monitoring flags were inadequate.
- Source-of-funds investigations were not sufficiently deep in several reviewed customer files.
- A separate PEP source-of-funds concern was considered resolved after the operator changed policy and completed the relevant checks; that does not erase the other findings.
- The Ksa required review of existing customer files and risk-appropriate follow-up measures, with clearer documentation of risk classifications, transaction flags, controls and source-of-funds investigations.

**Date caution:** the document's cover page shows 28 October 2025, while its signature page displays 24 July 2025. Confirm the decision date and publication chronology from the Ksa record before stating a single date in public copy. Also verify whether any objection, appeal, follow-up compliance check or later publication superseded/updated the status. Do not call the instruction a fine unless another primary decision establishes a monetary penalty.
