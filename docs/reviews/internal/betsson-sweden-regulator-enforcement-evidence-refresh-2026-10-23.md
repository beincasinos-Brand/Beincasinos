## Material enforcement — status updated
On 2 June 2025, Spelinspektionen issued Betsson Nordic Ltd a warning and SEK 6.5 million sanction fee over AML/customer-due-diligence deficiencies, including inadequate information about customers' source of funds. The company appealed.
- Original authority notice: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/nyhetsarkiv/betsson-nordic-ltd-far-varning-och-sanktionsavgift-for-brister-i-arbetet-mot-penningtvatt/
