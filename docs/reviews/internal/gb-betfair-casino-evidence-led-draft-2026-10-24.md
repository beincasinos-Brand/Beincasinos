## Withdrawals and payment checks

The official withdrawal-help page says payment methods have different processing timelines and that the customer is shown an expected arrival estimate when confirming a withdrawal. It describes checks that may prevent a withdrawal, including closed-loop payment requirements, account verification, and use of a method that does not accept withdrawals. The general terms also condition withdrawal on cleared payments and completion of relevant verification checks, and describe circumstances in which funds may be delayed or withheld during investigations.

Primary sources:
- [Withdrawal Queries](https://support.betfair.com/app/answers/detail/58-withdrawing-funds/)
- [Betfair General Terms and Conditions](https://support.betfair.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/10559/)

These are operator-published policy statements, not independent performance measurements. This review has not measured payout speed, verified payment availability for a particular customer, or tested a withdrawal.

## Complaints and alternative dispute resolution

Betfair's official complaints procedure describes contacting customer service first, escalating an unresolved issue to the complaints team, and a UK/Isle of Man final-response timeframe that may extend to eight weeks. It identifies **IBAS** as the UK alternative dispute resolution provider on that page. Confirm the procedure and ADR provider remain current for the specific casino product before publication.

Primary source: [Betfair's Complaints Procedure](https://support.betfair.com/app/answers/detail/6158-betfairs-complaints-procedure/).

## Safer-gambling tools

Betfair's safer-gambling information describes financial limits, time-out options and self-exclusion tools. The operator's own guidance also states that safer-gambling tools applied to a Betfair account do not automatically apply to all other brands in its wider group. A consumer review should explain this boundary clearly rather than imply that one limit automatically covers all related brands.

Primary source: [What can I do if I think I may be gambling too much?](https://support.betfair.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/90).
