## Regulatory history

The UKGC's decision dated **17 December 2025** records a combined **£2,000,000 payment in lieu of financial penalties** across four licensees: PPB Entertainment Limited, PPB Counterparty Services Limited, Betfair Casino Limited and TSE Malta LP. The Commission said a review followed a compliance assessment of remote licences and found failings in remote customer-interaction social-responsibility controls. Do **not** attribute the entire £2m to Betfair Casino Limited alone; it is a combined settlement with four named licensees.
