## 1. BresBet and Bet St George — surrendered licences; not active GB recommendations

Primary source: UK Gambling Commission update, originally 28 August 2026 and updated 16 October 2026:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/suspension-of-licences-bresbet-ltd-and-bet-st-george-ltd

The Commission reports:
- The operating licences of BresBet Ltd (bresbet.com) and Bet St George Ltd (betstgeorge.com) were suspended on 28 August 2026 during an investigation into suspected social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failings.

- The Commission later reported that some BresBet customers had difficulty withdrawing funds.
- On 16 October 2026, the Commission said administrators had been appointed for Bet St George Ltd and directed customers who believe they are owed money to the administrator contact named in the official notice.

## 2. Grosvenor Casinos Limited — recent land-based enforcement; online-product scope must be checked separately

Primary source: UK Gambling Commission announcement, 7 October 2026:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/land-based-casino-operators-to-pay-gbp5m-for-regulatory-failures/

The Commission states that Grosvenor Casinos Limited, Grosvenor Casinos (GC) Limited, and Gaming Group Limited, all owned by Rank Group PLC, were included in a £5 million settlement following an investigation into anti-money-laundering and social-responsibility failures.
