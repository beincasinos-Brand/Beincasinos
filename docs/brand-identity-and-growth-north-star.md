## Review standard required before any brand is listed

Every casino profile must identify the exact operating legal entity, domain, target market, licence/register evidence and date checked, product/market eligibility, ownership where reliably sourced, terms/bonus/withdrawal conditions, payment and complaint evidence where verified, safer-gambling controls, material limitations, review date, and any commercial relationship. Clearly separate verified facts, operator claims, editorial assessment, and unknowns. Paid placement must never buy a rating or override a negative finding. Remove or label any unverified information instead of filling gaps with guesses.

## Ambition versus verified metrics

100 million search impressions per day is a north-star ambition, not a forecast, service promise, media-kit claim, or sensible near-term operating target. It equals roughly 3 billion impressions in a 30-day month and 36.5 billion in a 365-day year. Search engines determine visibility independently; there is no ethical or technical way to guarantee this outcome.

Track real Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, indexed valid pages, non-brand query coverage, country/device splits, qualified referral traffic, user utility, corrections, and brand enquiries. Record the date range and source for every metric. Do not publish audience numbers, “top 10” claims, review counts, rankings, or media-kit promises until independently supportable evidence exists.

## Growth sequence

1. Resolve naming/trademark risk, publisher disclosures, privacy and market restrictions.
2. Build a differentiated review methodology and publish only researched, useful pages for markets where publication and promotion are lawful.
3. Complete technical SEO and real browser/mobile/accessibility checks; then obtain explicit owner approval before deployment and indexing.
4. Build sustainable search visibility through original research, expert-quality reviews, useful comparison tools, trustworthy citations, and regular maintenance—not mass-produced thin pages or fabricated backlinks.
5. Validate audience and conversion quality before offering paid listings. Disclose sponsorship; keep editorial scoring independent.
6. Expand markets only after local legal and source checks. Never assume one global “casino legal” status applies to every country/state.
