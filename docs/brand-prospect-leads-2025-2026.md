## Market scope guardrails

- **United States:** evaluate state-by-state. Never imply a brand has nationwide online-casino permission.
- **France, Germany, Switzerland, Netherlands and UK:** verify the exact domain, product, legal operator and current licence in the relevant official register. A parent company's licence or another country’s licence is not sufficient.
- **Australia:** confirm the current Interactive Gambling Act position and local advertising rules; do not assume a foreign licence authorises online casino services to Australians.
- A brand launch announcement or supplier deal is a commercial discovery signal, not proof of licence eligibility or willingness to pay BeInCasinos.
