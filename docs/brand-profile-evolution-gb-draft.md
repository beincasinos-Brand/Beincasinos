## 1. Why this dossier is separate from casino reviews

Evolution supplies casino games to operators. It should not be presented as a casino where a consumer opens an account unless separate first-party evidence establishes that product. A supplier-level regulatory event may be relevant to a review of game providers or to the licensing risk of a casino operator, but must not be attributed automatically to every casino using Evolution games.

## 2. Regulatory settlement — 23 July 2026

The UKGC announced a **£4.75 million settlement** with Evolution Malta Holding Limited after finding that its games appeared on six websites accessible to consumers in Great Britain without those sites holding a UKGC licence. The regulator said the supplier's risk assessment did not adequately identify money-laundering and terrorist-financing risks and that it failed to maintain effective controls to prevent its games being supplied to unlicensed operators. The investigation covered activity between December 2023 and November 2024.

The regulator's notice describes weaknesses in assessing business partners and identifying whether partner operators were supplying games into Great Britain without authorisation. The finding concerns the supplier's controls and the named unlicensed sites; it does not establish that all licensed casinos using Evolution games are unlicensed or unsafe.
