# Operator qualification scorecard — internal

**Status:** Internal decision aid only. Not a licence, legal opinion, public ranking, or commercial approval. Do not publish candidate scores or imply that a candidate is eligible until the evidence has been independently checked for the exact market and product.

## Hard gates (all must pass before any public commercial profile)

A candidate is **HOLD** if any hard gate is unknown or fails:

1. **Market legality:** The exact product is permitted in the intended market and the proposed advertising/affiliate activity is permitted.
2. **Official authorisation:** The regulator's current official register confirms the exact legal entity, relevant permission, and (where applicable) exact consumer-facing domain.
3. **Product identity:** The live domain, operator name, trading name, and product category match the evidence; no lookalike or redirect ambiguity.
4. **Audience suitability:** Age restrictions, location controls, responsible-gambling information, and ad restrictions can be represented accurately.
5. **Evidence quality:** Evidence is dated, linked, and captured from primary sources; unresolved contradictions are documented.
6. **Commercial transparency:** Any paid placement is separately labelled; commercial status never changes an editorial score.
7. **Privacy and tracking:** No conversion pixel, affiliate tracking, or data transfer is activated before privacy/legal approval and contract terms.
8. **Brand safety:** No unresolved material concerns about ownership, misleading claims, complaints handling, or the candidate's proposed marketing assets.

## Research scoring (100 points; only after hard gates)

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence to collect |
|---|---:|---|
| Licence and domain traceability | 25 | Current regulator register, exact domain and entity, permitted product scope |
| Terms and consumer transparency | 15 | Bonus terms, withdrawal limits, fees, identity checks, complaint route |
| Safer-gambling safeguards | 15 | Age/location restrictions, self-exclusion, limits, support information |
| Payments and withdrawals | 10 | Published methods, currencies, fees, limits, processing terms; avoid unsupported speed claims |
| Support and complaints | 10 | Contact channels, hours, escalation and regulator/dispute routes |
| Evidence freshness and completeness | 10 | Dated first-party and regulator evidence; material contradictions resolved |
| User-facing clarity/accessibility | 5 | Terms are findable, readable, and not obscured by promotional design |
| Operational reliability evidence | 5 | Only substantiated public status or documented testing; never infer from marketing |
| Commercial fit (separate from editorial score) | 5 | Relevant audience/market, named B2B contact, defined deliverables and procurement fit |

**Scoring rule:** Do not assign points for unknown facts. Mark unknown as **N/E (not evidenced)** and withhold any total until enough evidence exists. The hard gates override the numerical score. Commercial fit must not influence the editorial score or licence eligibility.

## Evidence record template

For every claim record: candidate; exact legal entity; exact domain; market; product; claim; source URL; source type (regulator / operator terms / other); source date; checked-at UTC; screenshot or archive reference where lawful; reviewer; confidence; contradictions; expiry/recheck date; decision.

## Decision outcomes

- **PASS TO EDITORIAL RESEARCH:** All hard gates pass for one specified market/product; no public ranking implied.
- **HOLD — EVIDENCE GAP:** Missing, stale, or contradictory evidence.
- **REJECT FOR MARKET:** Permission or ad model appears incompatible; record the authoritative basis and obtain qualified advice for borderline cases.
- **COMMERCIAL DISCOVERY ONLY:** A named contact or programme exists, but no willingness to pay or eligibility is established.
- **PAID PLACEMENT ELIGIBLE:** Only after legal/privacy approval, a signed agreement, verified deliverables, and visible sponsorship disclosure. Payment never buys a score or licence badge.

## Recheck cadence

Recheck regulator permission and domain mapping immediately before publication and on a scheduled cadence thereafter; recheck sooner after a regulator notice, domain redirect, ownership change, product change, material complaint, or advertising-rule change. Store dated evidence and maintain a correction/removal process.
