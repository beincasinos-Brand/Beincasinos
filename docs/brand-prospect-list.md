## Review workflow
1. Start with the official brand website and identify the precise domain and legal operator.
2. Identify the target market first. Check the relevant regulator's current register and exact domain/legal entity. Official starting points include the [Great Britain Gambling Commission business register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses), which supports searches by business/trading name and domain ([register guidance](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/page/public-register)); Spain's [DGOJ licensed-operator search](https://www.ordenacionjuego.es/en/operadores-juego/operadores-licencia/operadores); and Ontario's [iGaming Ontario regulated-operator directory](https://www.igamingontario.ca/en/operator/operators). These registers apply to their own jurisdictions; none of them proves a brand may serve every country.
3. Record the product scope, permitted territories, excluded territories, terms, withdrawals, complaints, safer-gambling tools, official partnership contact, and source dates.
4. Keep status as `prospect` or `researching` until evidence is collected. `approved_to_publish` requires market-specific review. Do not copy rankings or licence claims from third-party listicles as proof.
5. Publish only if the profile adds original utility and current source-backed information. Do not create 100 thin pages just to meet the target.
6. Record any commercial relationship separately: `none`, `paid listing`, `affiliate`, or `both`. Paid placement must be labelled and must never determine a factual licence or safety verdict.
