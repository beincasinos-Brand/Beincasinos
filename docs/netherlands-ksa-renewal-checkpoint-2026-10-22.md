## Required per-brand checks before approval

- Current Ksa entry for exact legal entity, consumer-facing website/app and licence number.
- Confirmation of casino permission, rather than assuming a sportsbook, poker or other product implies casino permission.
- Current terms, game rules, bonus offer and full bonus conditions.
- Deposit/withdrawal methods, limits, fees, verification and processing terms.
- Responsible-gambling controls and CRUKS/self-exclusion information.
- Privacy/cookie details, support and formal complaint escalation.
- Enforcement history and any current restrictions or material changes.
- Reproducible permitted UX observations; never invent account, deposit, withdrawal or gameplay tests.
- Affiliate relationship disclosure, independent second review and editorial/legal sign-off.

## Counting and release decision

Do not count two skins or related brands as separate approved reviews without evidence of distinct products and separately useful editorial evaluation. Licence status is not a rating of fairness, usability, value or service.
