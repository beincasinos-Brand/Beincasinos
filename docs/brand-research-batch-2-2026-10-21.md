## Cross-brand observations

- Similarity between Casushi and FruitKings homepage content is an identity-resolution issue to investigate, not proof of common ownership or misconduct.
- Promotional language (“fast withdrawals”, “no wagering”, “100% licensed”, game counts and bonus amounts) must be checked against current official terms and precise eligibility conditions; never convert advertising claims into independent conclusions.
- Where the website blocks a location or automation, stop and record the limitation. Do not defeat the control or infer missing facts.
