## Verification protocol

1. Open each live Kansspelwijzer record and capture the exact licence holder, permitted game categories, website/app, licence start/end dates and current status.
2. Match the live domain's legal notice and account contract to the licensee. Where one licensee has multiple brands, deduplicate correctly.
3. Confirm a house-banked casino catalogue is actually offered in the Netherlands before classifying the brand as a casino review.
4. Capture Netherlands-specific terms, casino rules, privacy/cookie notice, payment methods, withdrawal conditions, bonuses, complaints and customer-support channels.
5. Verify required player limits and CRUKS self-exclusion information from primary sources.
6. Check current Ksa enforcement decisions and any domain/product changes.
7. Require independent second review, dated evidence snapshots and a clear methodology before publication.
