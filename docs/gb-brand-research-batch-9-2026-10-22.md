## What the regulator states

The UK Gambling Commission says L & L Europe Limited runs seven websites, naming allbritishcasino.com, casinocasino.com and hypercasino.com as examples. It agreed a £900,000 regulatory settlement following findings involving consumer withdrawals, anti-money-laundering controls and social responsibility. The period described in secondary reporting is October 2024–October 2025; use the regulator's linked public statement for the definitive wording and scope.

The Commission's summary identifies:
- a term allowing only one withdrawal request at a time until the prior request was authorised, considered unfair under the Consumer Rights Act 2015;
- manual review of every fifth or tenth withdrawal for some customers, with additional information requested at withdrawal where it could reasonably have been requested earlier;
- AML controls based heavily on financial thresholds and sampling rather than customer risk profiles, including weaknesses in due diligence for newly opened bank accounts and unsubstantiated sources of funds;
- safer-gambling alerts not activated consistently across the full website suite, and no process to evaluate the effectiveness of the overall customer-interaction approach.

These are findings about the named licensee and the scope of the Commission's decision. Do not imply every named domain had identical customer-level incidents, or that the action establishes criminal money laundering. Do not describe reported remediation as independently tested by BeInCasinos.
