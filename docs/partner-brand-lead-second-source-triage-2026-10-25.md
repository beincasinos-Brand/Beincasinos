# Second source triage — partner-sourced brand leads

**Checked:** 25 October 2026  
**Scope:** preliminary identity conflict discovery only. Search results and operator self-published statements are leads, not independent proof of a current licence or market eligibility.

## Binobet — HOLD: multiple domains and inconsistent licence claims

- `https://binobet-el.com/en/terms/` states a Curaçao eGaming licence reference `8048/JAZ2020-013`.
- `https://binobetnl.com/terms-conditions/` claims a Netherlands/KSA remote gaming licence.
- Third-party records for similarly named Binobet surfaces show a different Anjouan/Comoros licence reference.

These claims cannot be combined into one legal profile. A claim of a Netherlands licence is especially material and must be checked directly against the Dutch regulator's current licensee register; a website's own terms are not enough. Identify the exact domain intended for each target market, then verify its domain/operator pairing with the regulator. No Dutch eligibility is approved by this note.

## MrPunter — HOLD: domain variants name different operators

- `https://casinomrpunter.com/terms-and-conditions/` states that MrPunter Casino is operated by Adonio N.V. and claims licence `B2C-D8D8GKS7-1668JAZ`.
- `https://mrpunter.io/` displays a separate operator statement naming MR Punter Limited and an MGA licence reference.
- `https://casinomrpunter.me.uk/responsible-gambling/` names Nexus Digital N.V. and a different Curaçao/Antillephone licence reference.

**Decision:** treat each domain and legal footer as a separate identity until a primary regulator record confirms otherwise. Do not transfer the MGA claim, operator name, bonus, or responsible-gambling claims from one domain to another. Check the Malta Gaming Authority's own register before making any MGA-authorisation statement.

## Betscore — HOLD: possible non-casino brand collision

- `https://betscore.app/disclaimer.html` describes a sports information/prediction service and says it is not a casino or sportsbook.
- `https://betscoreitaly.com/` presents a casino-branded site, but the indexed content says an Italian ADM concession number and verifiable licence number are not displayed.

**Decision:** establish what exact product the lead refers to. Do not count an affiliate directory or sports-statistics app as a casino operator. If `betscoreitaly.com` is the intended casino, do not present it as Italian-authorised without an affirmative current ADM record.

## Commercial/editorial consequence

This triage shows why the ten owner-supplied leads must not automatically become ten "verified" casino reviews. Several names resolve to multiple domains or inconsistent operator/licence statements. This is a reason to prioritise primary-source checks, not a conclusion that every similarly named service is unlawful.

For each lead, record the exact target domain and market before researching bonuses or writing a consumer verdict. A failed identity/eligibility gate should result in exclusion or a clearly documented hold, not a favourable score or a substitute domain.
