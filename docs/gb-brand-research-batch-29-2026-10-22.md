## Licence and market scope
The UKGC register search associates Sky Vegas with Bonne Terre Gaming Limited, account 65519, and lists www.skyvegas.com and m.skyvegas.com among matching domains. The official site states that Bonne Terre Gaming Limited is licensed by the UK Gambling Commission for customers in Great Britain and the Isle of Man, and also references a Gibraltar remote gambling licence. This does **not** establish permission to serve customers in Morocco or every other jurisdiction.

## Important entity distinction
The register search also returns Bonne Terre Limited, account 38718, with a matching mobile domain. Do not merge the two legal entities or infer that every historic or mobile-domain entry belongs to the same currently active permission without checking the individual licence/domain status pages. The official Sky Vegas support page describes Bonne Terre Gaming Limited as the casino-games and poker operator and SBG Sports Limited as the sports-betting operator.
