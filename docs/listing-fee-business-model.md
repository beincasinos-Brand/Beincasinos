# BeInCasinos: listing-fee-first commercial plan

## Goal
Build a useful, trusted casino-brand directory and educational resource. The initial research target is 100 brands. Monetization order: **paid listings first after audience evidence; affiliate links later and secondary**.

## Non-negotiable principles
- Resolve the name/trademark risk before public launch or further branding investment. The owner reports the domain purchase is complete; that purchase is not trademark clearance.
- Do not publish a brand as licensed, legal, safe, or available in a country without current, primary-source evidence.
- Keep research prospects separate from public profiles. No blank/near-duplicate profiles created just to reach 100 URLs.
- Mark paid placement beside the listing. Payment does not influence verification, safety language, or editorial conclusions.
- Do not promise advertisers traffic, leads, or conversions. The media kit must use measured data and disclose the reporting period.
- Affiliate links come later, after programme, market, disclosure, and privacy checks.

## Phase 0 — pre-launch name and business clearance
1. Resolve brand-name clearance, especially the “BEIN” element; consider an alternative name if qualified advice suggests material risk. The reported domain purchase does not remove this blocker.
2. Choose a first target market and language. “International” is not a legal market approval.
3. Decide the publisher identity and monitored business contact.
4. Choose a host whose terms permit the intended commercial directory and affiliate business.

## Phase 1 — research 100 prospects
Create 100 prospect records, but keep each in research status until checked. For each brand, collect:
- exact brand and official website, with access date;
- legal operating entity and official company/licence register links where applicable;
- product and geographic scope, including markets not served;
- responsible-gambling tools, complaint route, terms and withdrawal information;
- contact or partnership route from an official source;
- source citations, reviewer, last checked date, confidence, unresolved issues;
- publication decision and reason.

Do not use scraped listicles as proof of licensing. A licence must be checked against the regulator's current register and the exact legal entity/domain.

## Phase 2 — first six months
- Publish high-quality educational guides and tools; build useful internal links.
- Measure Search Console impressions, clicks, indexed pages, query intent, and country/device trends after domain verification.
- Review pages for usefulness, originality, mobile experience, broken links, and sources.
- Do not assume six months guarantees traffic. At the six-month review, decide whether the audience justifies offering paid placements.

## Phase 3 — paid listings
- Prepare a media kit using real Search Console and analytics data for a clearly stated date range.
- Define inventory, price, term, cancellation/refund policy, renewal, sponsored label, and what is included.
- Make the paid/sponsored label visible on every paid placement, not just in a policy.
- Separate paid placement from any independent verification and editorial judgement.
- Reject advertisers whose claims or market permissions cannot be substantiated.
- Never sell “verified”, “safe”, or top-ranking status as a paid add-on.

## Phase 4 — affiliate as a secondary stream
- Apply only to relevant programmes once traffic and compliance process justify it.
- Verify programme restrictions and permitted territories before publishing links.
- Disclose affiliate links near each commercial link and update privacy/cookie language before enabling tracking.
- Keep commercial status recorded per brand: none / paid listing / affiliate / both.

## Google Search Console launch checklist
1. Purchase the domain only after name clearance.
2. Deploy the reviewed site on a commercially permitted host and enable HTTPS.
3. Verify the domain property in Search Console using the DNS TXT record from Google.
4. Replace every sitemap placeholder with the final canonical domain; confirm canonical URLs use one preferred host/protocol.
5. Remove the pre-launch Disallow: / only after launch approval; make sure important pages are crawlable.
6. Submit the sitemap in Search Console, inspect the homepage and key pages, and monitor indexing and Core Web Vitals.
7. Do not expect immediate indexing or rankings; fix reported technical issues and build useful content.
8. Never share Search Console credentials; use delegated access when a collaborator needs it.

## Tracker status vocabulary
- prospect — candidate only; no verification implied.
- researching — source checks in progress.
- verified_for_review — evidence gathered, awaiting editorial/legal review.
- approved_to_publish — internal review complete for named market(s), with date.
- rejected — insufficient evidence, market risk, or other reason; record why.
- published — profile live and scheduled for review.
- paid_listing / affiliate — commercial status fields, never verification labels.
