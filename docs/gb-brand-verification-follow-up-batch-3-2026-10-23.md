## Publication-quality evidence still required

For each candidate, record dated primary-source evidence for:
1. Official product URL, current terms, and legal contracting entity.
2. Deposit and withdrawal methods, limits, fees, timing, verification/hold conditions, and any stated restrictions.
3. Bonus terms only where the article discusses a bonus; avoid unsupported or stale offer claims.
4. Customer complaint route and any applicable independent escalation route.
5. Safer-gambling tools, account limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, and support information.
6. Privacy/contact disclosures and the market-specific promotion rules.
7. Full regulator enforcement history, with outcomes described in context.
8. A second-editor check of factual claims, evidence dates, market scope, and wording.
