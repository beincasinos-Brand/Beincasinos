# BeInCasinos — casino review SEO and media acceptance checklist

**Use for internal editorial QA only. This checklist does not confer licence status or publication approval.**

## Search intent and page structure
- [ ] Page targets one jurisdiction and one exact online casino product.
- [ ] Title/H1 identifies brand and local market without “best” or “official” claims that are not evidenced.
- [ ] Intro states review date, methodology, jurisdiction and material limitations.
- [ ] Table of contents and clear sections: licence/operator, eligibility, bonus terms, payments, games/features, mobile/accessibility, support, privacy, player protection, complaints and verdict.
- [ ] Comparison claims use comparable definitions and dates; avoid unsupported numerical ratings.
- [ ] Internal links connect relevant market guides, operator profiles and responsible-gambling/legal explainers without creating duplicate thin pages.
- [ ] Canonical URL, indexability, sitemap inclusion, breadcrumbs and structured data are checked against the actual rendered page.
- [ ] Metadata is unique, accurate and not stuffed with repeated keywords. No fabricated review stars or ratings markup.

## Evidence and trust
- [ ] Primary regulator source and exact legal entity/domain are cited with checked date.
- [ ] Claims about bonuses, payment methods, limits, fees and processing times cite current binding terms or are clearly attributed as operator-published estimates.
- [ ] Tests are reproducible and accurately labelled as performed or not performed.
- [ ] Enforcement/complaints context is balanced and current; allegations are not presented as proven facts.
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure is clear before material affiliate links and near relevant recommendations.
- [ ] Responsible-gambling information is visible and appropriate for the jurisdiction; no encouragement of harmful play.
- [ ] Second reviewer records corrections, remaining uncertainty and sign-off.

## Image and accessibility controls
- [ ] Every image has documented ownership, licence or written permission; source URL and capture date are recorded.
- [ ] No hotlinking, scraped competitor screenshots or misleading mockups presented as real product evidence.
- [ ] Brand logos are used within permitted terms and not altered to imply endorsement.
- [ ] Images are compressed, responsive, dimensioned to reduce layout shift and served in appropriate modern formats where supported.
- [ ] Alt text describes meaningful content rather than keyword stuffing; decorative images use empty alt text.
- [ ] Text contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, headings, labels and zoom/responsive layout are checked.

## Final stop conditions
Keep status **HOLD** if the exact casino permission or domain cannot be confirmed, material terms are missing, media rights are unclear, consumer checks are claimed but not documented, or the second reviewer/legal-editorial approver has not signed off. A polished design, SEO score, affiliate offer or directory listing cannot substitute for evidence.
