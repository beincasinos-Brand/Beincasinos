# BeInCasinos full casino-ecosystem content map
Date: 22 October 2026
Status: internal planning document; not a claim of legal approval, published review, or ranking.

## Goal

Build a useful, evidence-led reference covering the full casino and gambling ecosystem without treating every product as legal in every country. Separate operator reviews from explainers, venues, software suppliers, free-play products, betting, and consumer-protection resources. Keep country and product permissions explicit on every page.

## Navigation and content types

1. Casino operator reviews — online casino only where the exact domain, legal entity, regulator entry, remote activity and product permissions match.
2. Game guides — slots, video slots, roulette, blackjack, baccarat, craps, poker, live dealer, RNG, RTP, volatility, paylines, game rules and table limits.
3. Other gambling verticals — sports betting, horse-race betting, bingo, lottery, fantasy sports and exchange betting; each needs a distinct legal scope and review checklist.
4. Venue directory — land-based casinos, venue address, official operator, access/age rules, opening information and source date. Never infer that a venue licence authorises online play.
5. Social, sweepstakes and free-play — clearly distinguish virtual coins from redeemable prizes and real-money gambling; keep legality unapproved until reviewed.
6. B2B ecosystem — software studios, game aggregators, live-dealer suppliers, platform providers and testing laboratories. Mark B2B profiles as suppliers, not casinos.
7. Player rights and safer play — licensing, terms, withdrawals, disputes, self-exclusion, privacy, scams, underage protection and regulator enforcement.

## Required page schema

Every operator profile must identify:
- Market, product type, exact customer-facing domain, legal operator and date checked.
- Official regulator record URL, activity scope, status and any domain/trading-name match.
- Official terms, bonus terms, deposits, withdrawals, fees, verification and account closure.
- Privacy/cookies, marketing preferences, safer-gambling tools, complaints and independent dispute route.
- Enforcement check, source excerpts or notes, unresolved conflicts, and review limitations.
- Affiliate/commercial relationship and how it is separated from editorial scoring.
- Primary reviewer, independent second reviewer, legal/editorial sign-off and next review date.
- Reproducible hands-on observations only where the team actually performed the test. Do not imply deposits, withdrawals or gameplay were tested if they were not.

## Publishing and counting rules

- A candidate is not an approved review. A draft, search result, affiliate page or regulator-list entry is not enough.
- A review counts toward 100 only when every mandatory gate passes and an independent reviewer signs off.
- Keep separate counts for (a) verified online casino operators, (b) other regulated gambling operators, (c) physical venues, (d) B2B suppliers, (e) social/free-play products, and (f) educational guides.
- Never fill a market quota with a different product category and label it an online casino.
- If the legal market has fewer than ten eligible operators, report the real count and use the remaining work for guides or other lawful verticals without inflating the casino-review total.
- Add no schema rating/review markup to draft, unscored or unverified profiles.
- Each market page must show source dates and a visible correction/update route.

## Suggested production sequence

1. Run and pass existing static checks.
2. Finish the review evidence template and ledger; maintain the current approved count until independently approved records exist.
3. Complete primary-source market scope pages, starting with the markets where exact official registers are available.
4. Turn the 30 briefs into a deduplicated keyword cluster plan using actual search-demand and Search Console data when accessible; do not invent volume estimates.
5. Draft and edit one page at a time against primary sources; use contextual internal links and breadcrumb trails.
6. Check links, canonical, robots, sitemap, metadata, structured data, accessibility and mobile rendering.
7. Deploy only after release approval; verify production URLs after deploy, then request indexing where appropriate.
