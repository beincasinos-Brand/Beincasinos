# Brand outreach email template — for use only after research verification

Use this only after the specific operator, official domain, legal entity, target market, and applicable regulatory status have been independently checked. Do not imply the recipient is licensed or legally available in a market unless the exact claim has current primary-source evidence. Do not send mass outreach to unverified addresses.

## First-contact email

**Subject:** Editorial listing enquiry — BeInCasinos

Hello [Partnerships/Business Development team],

I'm contacting you on behalf of [publisher/legal entity], which is developing BeInCasinos, an English-language information site focused on clear, source-backed casino operator information and educational tools.

We are currently researching operators and documenting official terms, permitted markets, payment/withdrawal information, complaints routes, and safer-gambling resources. We are not presenting this enquiry as an endorsement or a statement about your regulatory status.

Once the site has a meaningful, measured audience, we may offer clearly labelled paid listings. Any paid placement would be identified as advertising and would not buy a positive editorial verdict or change factual review criteria. We are not promising traffic, rankings, conversions, or launch dates.

If your team handles directory listings or media partnerships, could you point me to the appropriate contact and share any official media-kit, brand-use, market eligibility, and compliance requirements?

Thank you,

[Name]  
[Role] — [Publisher/legal entity]  
[Monitored business email]  
[Website, once live]

## Before sending

- [ ] Replace every bracketed field with accurate details; never invent a publisher identity or contact.
- [ ] Verify the official domain and use a contact address published by the operator.
- [ ] Check whether the recipient's jurisdiction and the intended outreach are lawful and compliant with applicable marketing/privacy rules.
- [ ] Record the evidence, source URLs, date, and reviewer in the research tracker.
- [ ] Respect opt-outs and do not repeatedly contact people who decline.
- [ ] Do not attach an audience media kit until real, accurately dated analytics/GSC evidence exists.
- [ ] Do not claim the website is live, licensed, approved, or has traffic unless verified.
- [ ] Do not offer or imply paid influence over regulatory status, factual accuracy, ratings, or safety conclusions.
