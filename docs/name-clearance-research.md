# BeInCasinos name-clearance workpaper

**Status: NOT CLEARED — research checklist only, not a legal opinion.**  
**Prepared:** 20 October 2026  
**Name under review:** “BeInCasinos”, “Be In Casinos”, “BEIN”, and confusingly similar word/device marks.

## Why this is a release blocker

A domain registration does not create trademark clearance. “BEIN” may overlap with existing media and entertainment branding, and the intended project is a commercial gambling-information directory. Search-engine results or a single database search cannot establish whether use is safe. Do not invest further in public branding, pay for a logo campaign, or launch under the name until a qualified review is complete.

## Search plan for the owner or trademark professional

Search exact and similar variants, including:
- BeInCasinos, Be In Casinos, BEIN, Bein Casino, Be In Casino, phonetic and logo variants.
- Marks in relevant goods/services, especially online information/publishing, advertising/marketing, business listings/directories, software/digital services, and gambling-related categories as counsel advises.
- Active, pending, expired and opposable marks; ownership, filing/priority dates, territories, goods/services and opposition history.
- Company names, unregistered/common-law use, media brands, domain use, social handles, and market-specific reputation/confusion risk.
- The proposed logo and typography separately from the word mark.

## Decision gate

Do not mark this issue closed until the owner has:
1. Received a written clearance/risk assessment for the intended name, logo, services and target jurisdictions from a qualified trademark professional.
2. Recorded the permitted usage, limitations and any required changes.
3. Decided whether to keep, modify or replace the name before public deployment.
4. Rechecked the domain/brand strategy if the name changes, including canonical URLs, metadata, logo, sitemap, social handles, and legal notices.

This workpaper deliberately records **no search result and no clearance conclusion**. It must be completed by the owner or a qualified professional.
