## 2. Regulatory settlement — 7 October 2026

The UKGC announced that the three Rank Group-owned land-based casino operators would pay **£5 million in total** following an investigation into anti-money-laundering (AML) and social-responsibility failures. The Grosvenor Casinos Limited settlement record specifies a payment in lieu of a financial penalty of **£5,012,261 on behalf of the licensee and wider group**, publication of a statement of facts, Commission costs and a commitment to a third-party external audit within six months of the conclusion of the licence review.

The Commission described AML weaknesses including outdated policies, inconsistent risk decisions, inadequate scrutiny of high-risk sources of funds and enhanced due diligence not being completed when policies required it. Social-responsibility examples included missed safer-gambling interactions during a customer's £50,000 loss, no recorded interactions with a customer who won around £260,000 in a short period and then lost around £250,000 in 12 days, and delayed interaction with a returning self-excluded customer until after £25,000 in losses.

These are the regulator's findings for the reviewed period. Do not present them as proof of the current state of every venue or every control. The regulator reports that changes were implemented and requires an external audit; verify the audit outcome and later regulatory updates before making present-day claims.
