# Partner-ready 100-brand pipeline: qualification rules

## Objective

Build a research-backed list of 100 **commercial prospects** that could realistically consider a paid BeInCasinos listing. Do not describe all 100 as willing buyers until each has explicitly confirmed interest. Willingness to pay is not reliably knowable from a brand name, launch date, or affiliate programme alone; it must be validated through a real conversation, written reply, signed order, or payment.

The prospect target includes established operators and brands launched in 2022–2026. Newness is a useful prospecting angle, not a quality or compliance shortcut.

## What “partner-ready” means

A brand only enters the qualified outreach queue after these checks:

1. **Identity:** official website, exact brand domain, operating legal entity, parent/group, and whether it is a standalone operator, a rebrand, or a skin using a shared platform are recorded.
2. **Market/product fit:** select the exact BeInCasinos audience market and product category. Verify the exact legal entity/domain against the relevant regulator's current primary-source register. For US casino products, record each state separately. Do not promote online-casino offers to AU or FR audiences as though they were locally lawful.
3. **Editorial value:** identify a real reader question the profile answers using primary evidence (licensing scope, terms, withdrawal rules, complaints, payment limits, safer-gambling tools, ownership, or material changes). No thin template-only profiles.
4. **Commercial signal:** record evidence that the business invests in distribution or partnerships: official affiliate/partner programme, public media/PR contact, B2B partnership page, sponsorship/advertising activity, or a direct reply. These are **signals of outreach fit, not proof that the brand wants a paid listing**.
5. **Reachable decision-maker:** capture a public business contact source and role/team (partnerships, affiliate, acquisition, brand marketing, PR, or commercial). Do not guess email addresses or collect personal contact details from dubious sources.
6. **Trust:** capture official source URLs, date checked in UTC, reviewer, unresolved issues, and a recheck date. Do not publish unsupported claims about licensing, safety, ownership, launch dates, or market availability.
7. **Commercial disclosure:** agree on clear “Paid listing”/“Sponsored” labeling and preserve independent editorial decisions. A fee never buys a positive verdict, a licence claim, a safety badge, or hidden ranking preference.

## Prioritisation score (0–100)

Score only from documented evidence; use unknown instead of guessing. The score estimates prospecting fit and **does not predict willingness to pay**.

| Dimension | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Legal/market fit for the intended page | 0–25 | Current primary-source regulator record, exact entity/domain, product scope and market rules |
| Reader value and differentiation | 0–20 | Specific, sourceable information that adds value beyond generic bonus copy |
| Commercial partnership signal | 0–20 | Official partnership/affiliate page, media contact, public campaign or direct reply |
| New/changed brand opportunity | 0–10 | Launch, rebrand, new market authorisation, or material product change dated 2022–2026 and verified by an attributable source |
| Contactability | 0–10 | Official route to relevant business team; contact source recorded |
| Operational maturity and transparency | 0–10 | Clear ownership, terms, complaints route, payments and safer-gambling information |
| Evidence freshness | 0–5 | Official evidence checked within the last 90 days or current regulator data captured with a dated review |

**Routing:** 80–100 = priority research/outreach candidate; 60–79 = further research; below 60 = hold or reject. A missing or adverse legal-market check overrides the numerical score: do not publish or sell a market-specific placement while the issue remains unresolved. The score is an internal heuristic and should be calibrated against actual replies and paid conversions.

## 2022–2026 new-brand research

For any claimed launch year, fill in launch_year, launch_year_source_url, and launch_type. Use:
- new launch only with credible, dated launch evidence;
- rebrand for a renamed/repositioned brand;
- new market for a brand entering a new jurisdiction;
- acquisition for a change in ownership;
- unknown if evidence is inconclusive.

Prefer official operator announcements, corporate press releases, regulator records, company filings, or reputable industry trade reporting that cites an identifiable source. Do not treat “first seen in a directory” as a confirmed launch date. Do not infer that a recent brand is more willing to pay; validate its commercial signal separately.

## Validate actual willingness to pay

Keep these states separate in the sales pipeline:
- **Prospect:** name identified; no claim about interest.
- **Researching:** identity, market and commercial signals being checked.
- **Outreach ready:** qualification evidence and contact route documented; outreach has not yet happened.
- **Contacted:** a compliant business message was sent; date and channel recorded.
- **Replied / interested:** a real response exists; preserve the date and next step.
- **Qualified buyer:** decision-maker confirms the use case, market and paid-listing option are relevant.
- **Proposal sent:** specific package, fee, term, disclosure, cancellation and approval process sent in writing.
- **Won / paid:** signed acceptance or payment evidence exists and required reviews are complete.
- **Lost / no response / do not contact:** record outcome and reason; respect opt-outs and applicable outreach/privacy rules.

Do not call a prospect “wants paid listing” until a direct, documented signal supports that statement. No cold outreach until publisher identity, monitored contact email, privacy/outreach rules, site quality, and the media kit are ready. Never imply traffic or audience numbers that have not been measured.

## Offer design: sell real value, not rankings

Potential packages to test only after audience data exists:
- **Verified basic listing:** factual company/operator profile with dated source checks; payment disclosed and does not affect findings.
- **Enhanced paid listing:** additional clearly marked company-supplied facts, contact details, product/market coverage where verified, and update SLA.
- **Sponsored educational feature:** clearly labeled, fact-checked, editorially controlled, with market-specific compliance review.

Do not set final prices or promise reach until analytics and production costs exist. Interview prospects before fixing package names, inclusions, or pricing. Offer no guaranteed rankings, licensing outcomes, conversions, or SEO results.

## Market focus for the initial 100

Prioritise partner research where the intended product and advertising route can be reviewed clearly: GB, DE, NL, CH, and individually authorised US states/products. AU and FR remain research-only for online-casino promotions unless qualified local review identifies a specific lawful category and compliant use case. This is not a finding that any particular operator is authorised.

## Useful market benchmark

Some iGaming media directories publicly offer paid company listings and describe a verification process; this demonstrates that paid B2B directory inventory exists as a model, not that casino operators will pay BeInCasinos or that the same model is appropriate for a consumer-facing directory. Validate demand directly. Example reference: https://gamblersconnect.com/advertising/

## Review gate

Before any paid offer is sent: clear the BeInCasinos name/trademark risk; identify the publisher; choose permitted hosting; complete privacy and outreach review; build a genuine media kit from measured data; publish enough independently reviewed content to demonstrate value; and get market-specific legal/compliance review for the placement. This document is an operating framework, not legal advice.
