The current UKGC register associates betmgm.co.uk with LeoVegas Gaming PLC (account 39198), which has active remote Casino and Bingo licences and active remote real-event and virtual-event betting permissions. BetMGM UK's current terms name LeoVegas Gaming PLC as the contracting operator for the UK website and cite the same UKGC account number. BetMGM is a brand, not the name of the UK-licensed contracting entity. US/Canada accounts and rewards are explicitly separate from the UK service.

The UKGC's current actions page for account 39198 says no actions have been recorded in that page's current register view. However, the Commission's 3 August 2022 announcement recorded a £1.32m penalty, official warning and audit for LeoVegas. A fair review must retain this historical regulatory context rather than imply there has never been enforcement.

## 2. Current product terms and safer-gambling evidence

The live general terms state that LeoVegas Gaming PLC operates betmgm.co.uk and associated UK applications. The help centre lists sections for account verification, payments, withdrawals, mandatory limits, safer gambling, sports, casino and bonuses. The official safer-gambling page describes deposit limits, net-deposit limits, loss limits, session limits, stake limits, time alerts and self-exclusion. These are published feature claims, not independent verification of how easy each control is to find, set, change or enforce.

The promotional-terms page contains separate casino and sports welcome offers and states that the offer terms are subject to the general terms. It also allows offers to be modified or withdrawn in specified circumstances. The live promotion must be captured and its exact monetary value, wagering requirements, qualifying deposit/stake, eligible games/markets, expiry and maximum conversion/withdrawal rules recorded before describing an offer to readers.
