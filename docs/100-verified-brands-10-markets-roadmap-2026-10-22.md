## Mandatory approval gates

1. Current official regulator record and active permission for online casino games.
2. Exact domain, trading name and contracting legal entity matched to that permission.
3. Current terms and bonus rules archived with date/version.
4. Deposit/withdrawal methods, fees, limits, verification, timing language and restrictions captured.
5. Complaints and independent dispute-resolution route checked.
6. Responsible-gambling controls and local self-exclusion routes verified.
7. Privacy controller, privacy notice, cookies/consent and accessibility reviewed.
8. Recent enforcement, sanctions, suspensions and ownership/domain changes checked.
9. Reproducible UX tests completed where lawful and possible; never claim tests that did not occur.
10. Commercial/affiliate disclosures and publisher identity verified.
11. Independent second reviewer validates sources, claims, calculations and score.
12. Final editorial/legal review signs off. Missing or stale evidence means HOLD.
