## Goal and counting rules

Target: 100 distinct, market-specific casino brands across 10 regulated markets, aiming for 10 approved brands per market. A candidate or draft does not count. A licence record does not alone establish that a brand offers online casino games. The same global brand in two countries may count as two market-specific reviews only if the product, legal entity/licence, terms, payments, bonus, game scope and user experience are materially market-specific; otherwise avoid duplicate counting.

**Current verified-review count: 0/100.** Candidate registers and draft profiles are research inputs only. Do not publish rankings or scores for brands whose review is on HOLD.

## Brand approval gates — all mandatory

Each candidate must have an evidence file and ledger row covering:

1. Official regulator record checked on a recorded date; correct licensed entity and active status.
2. Brand/domain mapped to the legal entity; lookalike domains excluded.
3. Specific permission for the relevant online casino product and permitted game categories confirmed. Sportsbook or lottery permission alone is insufficient.
4. Current full terms and game rules captured with version/date.
5. Deposit methods, limits, fees, processing language, verification requirements and availability captured.
6. Withdrawal methods, limits, fees, processing stages, KYC conditions and complaints about delays captured.
7. Current bonus terms checked end-to-end: eligibility, qualifying deposit, wagering, game contribution, expiry, max conversion/withdrawal, excluded methods and games.
8. Responsible-gambling tools and local self-exclusion routes checked against regulator/operator sources.
9. Privacy notice, controller, key data uses/retention, cookie/SDK consent and consent withdrawal checked.
10. Customer support and formal complaint/escalation routes verified.
11. Recent regulator enforcement, sanctions, suspensions, domain changes and material ownership changes checked.
12. Reproducible user-experience tests completed in a permitted context, with timestamps and no fabricated account/payment/gameplay claims.
13. Affiliate relationship and commercial disclosures confirmed; otherwise disclose no relationship if true.
14. Independent second reviewer validates sources, calculations, claims, and score.
15. Final editorial/legal review confirms the article is accurate, useful, locally appropriate and not misleading.

No gate may be silently waived. If a source is missing or stale, record the gap and mark HOLD.

## Rating and review principles

- Score evidence and consumer-relevant experience, not bonus size or affiliate payout.
- Separate operator claims from independently observed facts.
- Do not claim a withdrawal was tested unless a permitted real test was actually performed and documented.
- Explain methodology, market-specific legal scope, review date, uncertainty, and material changes.
- Never imply “safe” means risk-free. Prominent responsible-gambling information is required.
- No copied competitor text, fabricated tests, invented ratings, or automatic top rankings based only on licence status.

## Work sequence

1. Finish and validate the shared ledger/template; preserve docs-only changes on review branches.
2. Complete market-level regulator source maps for all ten markets before selecting the final 100.
3. Build a longlist of at least 15–20 eligible candidates per market to allow exclusions without quota pressure.
4. Verify distinct brands and legal/game scope; deduplicate same-operator or same-product variants.
5. Complete evidence packs in batches of 5, with a second reviewer before any status changes to APPROVED.
6. Update the ledger and board only with evidenced status; candidates and drafts remain uncounted.
7. Run public build, link/canonical/robots/sitemap/404 checks, content-quality checks, and human review against a release candidate.
8. Deploy once after explicit release approval; then validate live routes, redirects, sitemap and indexing signals. Search engine indexing/rankings/impressions are not guaranteed.
