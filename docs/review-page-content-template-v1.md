# BeInCasinos evidence-first casino review template v1

Use this for an original market-specific review after research is complete. Remove internal instructions before publication. Never fill unknown values with assumptions.

## Page metadata
- Brand: canonical consumer brand
- Market: country and state/province where relevant
- Official domain: exact verified URL
- Operator/legal entity: entity from current terms
- Licence/register: regulator, ID, direct official register URL
- Evidence checked: date in UTC
- Review updated: date
- Editorial status: HOLD / REVIEW IN PROGRESS / APPROVED
- Affiliate disclosure: clear statement of any commercial relationship
- Image rights: source, permission/licence and date

## Summary
Write an original summary explaining what was verified, for which market, and the key limitations. Avoid unsupported superlatives such as best, trusted or fastest payouts.

## Licence and market eligibility
Identify the contracting operator and explain the regulator record, permitted casino product, domain match and date checked. Link the official register. If a material point cannot be reconciled, mark HOLD.

## Casino games
List only game types confirmed on current official pages for this market: slots, live dealer, blackjack, roulette, baccarat, poker, bingo or other applicable categories. Note that catalogue availability can vary. Do not invent game counts or supplier lists.

## Welcome offer and promotions
Use a table with: offer/eligibility; minimum deposit or qualifying activity; wagering requirement; max stake; eligible games/contribution; expiry and withdrawal limits; official source and checked date. Use not verified where needed. Do not advertise an offer as current without checking it at publication time.

## Deposits and withdrawals
Describe payment methods and limits shown in official market-specific sources. Distinguish operator processing from bank/payment-provider processing. Do not promise payout times unless current terms support the exact wording and conditions are explained.

## Account, mobile and usability
Separate direct observation from documented facts. Record device/browser, date and test steps. Do not claim testing that was not performed. Never place real-money bets merely to complete editorial checks.

## Player protection and complaints
Summarise official age restrictions, deposit/time controls, self-exclusion, cooling-off, reality checks, help links, complaint steps and applicable ADR route. Record missing sources as evidence gaps.

## Strengths and drawbacks
Provide evidence-linked, market-specific bullets. Include meaningful drawbacks and avoid generic praise.

## Editorial score
Use the scoring rubric. Show dimension ratings, weights, evidence and limitations. Do not publish an overall score while mandatory gates remain open or required dimensions are unassessed.

## Methodology and updates
Explain review date, primary sources, what was and was not tested, independent feedback methodology if any, correction route, affiliate relationship and recheck date.

## Pre-publication checklist
- Correct market and canonical URL
- Exact domain/operator/register match
- Casino product permission confirmed
- Bonus terms and claims current and sourced
- Payment/withdrawal language sourced and cautious
- Complaints, ADR and player protection included
- Score reproducible; missing dimensions not concealed
- Original copy; no copied competitor text
- Image reuse rights confirmed
- Affiliate/paid-placement disclosure visible
- Internal links, canonical, metadata, headings and structured data checked
- Mobile accessibility and links checked
- Second editor signs off; publication owner approves
