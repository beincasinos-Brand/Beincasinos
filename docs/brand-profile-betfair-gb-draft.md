## 2. Regulatory record

The UKGC published a settlement dated **17 December 2025** following a compliance assessment of four remote licensees trading under the Betfair and Paddy Power names:
- PPB Entertainment Limited (39426)
- PPB Counterparty Services Limited (39439)
- Betfair Casino Limited (39435)
- TSE Malta LP (39561)

The Commission found failures in remote customer-interaction social-responsibility controls. The four licensees agreed to a **total £2 million payment in lieu of a financial penalty**. The published details describe failings under social-responsibility code provision SRCP 3.4.3.

Official decision:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/308

This is material to the review and must be explained fairly, with the exact licensees and decision date stated. Do not attribute the settlement to Betfair Limited account 2062, or to every Betfair product, unless the source supports that attribution.
