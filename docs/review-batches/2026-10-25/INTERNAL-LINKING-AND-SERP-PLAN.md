# BeInCasinos — internal linking and search plan

## Architecture
- Market hub → eligible brand review → relevant evergreen explainers (licensing, bonus terms, withdrawals, player protection).
- Only create a market hub when it provides genuine market-specific value and the legal team has approved its scope.
- Do not generate indexable near-duplicate pages by swapping a country name into a template.
- Never create a “best casino” list that recommends operators not confirmed eligible in that jurisdiction.
- Keep one canonical review per distinct brand + market scope where the legal operator/product actually differs; avoid duplicate URLs for spelling and tracking parameters.

## Search snippet rules
- Titles describe the exact brand, market and review scope.
- Meta descriptions summarize what was actually checked; do not promise winnings, safety, “best” status or a current bonus without evidence.
- H1 should match the page purpose and the visible page content.
- FAQ content must answer real user questions and should not be added just to manipulate rich results.
- Structured data must be accurate, visible on page and eligible under current search guidelines. Never fabricate AggregateRating, reviewCount, offer prices or availability.

## Internal links
- Each review links to the market hub and methodology.
- Link to source-backed explainers for wagering, payment processing, KYC and self-exclusion.
- Use descriptive anchor text and avoid repetitive exact-match anchors.
- No orphan approved review; no link to an excluded operator as a recommendation.
- Label affiliate links appropriately and separate sponsored placements from editorial rankings.

## Measurement loop
Weekly: Search Console indexing and canonical reports, sitemap errors, impressions/clicks/CTR by query and page, coverage of intended markets, and pages with no impressions after a meaningful period.
Monthly: query cannibalisation, title experiments with change log, outdated terms, regulator changes, broken sources and asset permissions.
Quarterly: review scores, internal-link relevance, stale pages, duplicate/thin pages and legal eligibility.
Do not promise a daily-impression target as guaranteed; use actual GSC trend data to prioritise fixes.
