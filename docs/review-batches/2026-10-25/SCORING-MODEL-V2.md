# BeInCasinos scoring model v2 — evidence-gated 100-point rubric

**Status:** editorial specification only. No casino in the current batch has a final score from this document alone.

## Weighting
| Dimension | Weight | Evidence expected |
|---|---:|---|
| Legal eligibility, operator identity and player protection | 25 | Exact market regulator entry, matching legal entity/domain, licence scope, self-exclusion and limits |
| Bonus transparency and fairness | 15 | Live offer terms, wagering, eligible games, expiry, max bet/cashout, excluded payments |
| Payments and withdrawal transparency | 15 | Published methods, fees, limits, processing windows, KYC rules; timed tests only if actually performed |
| Games and fairness disclosures | 10 | Catalogue evidence, studio/provider identity, published RTP/fairness information where available |
| Support and complaints | 10 | Contact channels, response policy, complaint escalation; response-time score only if tested |
| Usability and accessibility | 10 | Mobile flow, cashier clarity, keyboard/accessibility and performance checks |
| Privacy and account security | 10 | Privacy policy, data controller, security/verification information, account protection |
| Evidence quality and freshness | 5 | Primary-source links, capture dates, conflict log, independent second-editor review |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Scoring rules
- Each dimension gets a raw 0–10 grade, written rationale, evidence links, checked date and confidence.
- Weighted points = raw grade ÷ 10 × dimension weight. Show arithmetic and total.
- **NOT RATED** if critical market eligibility, operator identity, terms or payment evidence is missing.
- **HOLD** when a material source conflicts or has not been rechecked; **EXCLUDE** when the exact product is not eligible in the target market or legal review requires exclusion.
- A licence entry does not prove payout speed, customer satisfaction or fairness of every game.
- Never present a guessed value, AI-generated impression or competitor score as BeInCasinos' own measured result.
- No hands-on testing claim without dated test notes and outcome evidence.
- The affiliate team cannot alter a score; paid placement must be labelled and must not affect rating.
- Score changes require reason, new evidence, editor name and date.

## Score bands for the UI
Use only after scoring is approved: 90–100 Excellent; 80–89 Strong; 70–79 Good with caveats; 60–69 Mixed; 40–59 Weak; 0–39 Avoid/Exclude. Legal ineligibility overrides arithmetic and means do not recommend. Do not award high bands where core evidence is sparse.

## Confidence label
- High: complete current primary-source checks plus adequate repeatable hands-on test evidence where the score depends on behaviour.
- Medium: core primary sources are complete but some behaviour dimensions remain untested.
- Low: material reliance on published claims or limited/old evidence; suppress numeric rating and use NOT RATED/HOLD if a critical fact is missing.
