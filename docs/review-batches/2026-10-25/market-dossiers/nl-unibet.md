2. Read the operator legal notice and terms; reconcile company name, licence holder and domain. A foreign licence alone is not proof of local authorisation.
3. Archive current bonus terms if an offer exists. Capture wagering calculation, expiry, maximum stake, excluded games, contribution rates, cashout cap, eligibility and jurisdiction restrictions.
4. Capture payments terms separately from marketing claims: methods, limits, fees, processing window, KYC triggers and account restrictions.
5. Confirm game categories and providers from the live product catalogue. Record the date; avoid unsupported catalogue counts.
6. Record support channels, complaints escalation, self-exclusion, deposit/time limits, privacy controller and account-closure process.
