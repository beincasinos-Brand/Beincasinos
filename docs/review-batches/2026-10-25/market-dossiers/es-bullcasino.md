### 3. Licensing and operator identity
Explain what the regulator register establishes and what it does not. Match the precise domain and product, not merely a similarly named company or an overseas licence.

### 4. Bonus and terms
Translate the verified terms into plain language: wagering basis, qualifying deposit, expiry, maximum stake, excluded games, contribution rates, cashout limits and eligibility. If no current offer is verified, say so rather than inventing one.

### 7. Support, complaints and player protection
