## 1. William Hill online — Great Britain

**Source A — regulator public register:** https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register  
The UK Gambling Commission describes its public registers as the place to search licensed businesses, individuals, regulatory actions and premises. Search for the precise trading name, legal entity and domain; save the matching entry and relevant licence activities.

**Source B — operator disclosure:** https://www.williamhill.com/  
The operator page says customers in Great Britain are served by WHG (International) Limited and states UK Gambling Commission account number 39225. It separately identifies William Hill Global PLC for customers outside the named territories. The operator's own disclosure is a cross-check, not a substitute for the regulator record.

**Editorial conclusion:** record legal entity, account number, domain and remote product categories from the current register. Do not conflate the overseas operator with the Great Britain operator. Do not assign a score based only on the existence of a licence. Still required: current bonus terms, payment restrictions, support tests, complaints/enforcement check, safer-gambling controls, privacy terms, image rights and second-editor review.
