# BeInCasinos review batch — 25 October 2026

**Status: INTERNAL EDITORIAL WORKBENCH — NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

This batch establishes a consistent review build for twelve target markets. It is not evidence that any listed brand is currently licensed, available, or suitable for promotion in a given market. Candidate selection is a research queue only.

## Release gates for every review
1. Confirm current official operator/domain and exact product.
2. Verify the relevant regulator's live register and product/jurisdiction scope.
3. Capture current bonus page and binding full terms; record capture date, wagering contribution, expiry, max bet, excluded games, withdrawal cap and eligibility.
4. Verify payment methods, minimum/maximum limits, fees, withdrawal processing terms and KYC requirements from official sources.
5. Review complaints/ADR, safer-gambling tools, self-exclusion, privacy, contact details and enforcement history.
6. Use only original graphics or assets with documented permission; never hotlink competitor images.
7. Have a second editor review factual claims and the score before removing noindex.
8. Confirm country-specific advertising and affiliate-disclosure rules.

## Scoring policy
Do not assign a numeric score while any critical licensing, terms, safety or evidence gate is unresolved. Once verified, score each dimension from 0–10 using recorded evidence:
- Legal eligibility and operator transparency: 25%
- Bonus clarity and fairness: 15%
- Payments and withdrawal transparency: 15%
- Games/product information: 10%
- Player protection: 15%
- Support and complaints handling: 10%
- Usability/accessibility: 5%
- Privacy/security transparency: 5%

Weighted score = sum(dimension score × weight). A failed legal eligibility gate means **EXCLUDE**, not a low score. Critical evidence missing means **HOLD — NOT RATED**. Never call a score a hands-on test unless a documented test was actually performed.

## SEO and page quality checklist
- Unique title and meta description; one clear H1; logical H2s.
- State the target market and review's evidence-check date.
- Original intro, pros/cons, operator/licensing section, bonus terms, banking, games, mobile/usability, support, safer gambling, FAQs and verdict.
- Link directly to primary sources; add affiliate disclosure before commercial links.
- Canonical points to the exact intended page; keep draft pages noindex until approved.
- Do not promise winnings, imply risk-free gambling, or use unsupported superlatives.
- Images need a source, licence/permission note, descriptive alt text, responsive sizing and no misleading bonus overlays.

## Counting rule
Candidate dossiers, market-specific duplicates and SEO briefs do **not** count as approved reviews. Count a review only after primary-source verification, completed content, a valid score (or explicit no-rating decision), image-rights check and independent sign-off.
