# Batch 86 — Australia: candidate product research
**Candidates:** The Lott, Sportsbet, Crown digital properties. These are research leads, not three confirmed online-casino brands.

## The Lott — category-first review brief
**Question to resolve:** Which specific lottery products are offered, by which legal entity, and under what state/territory rules? Do not count a lottery platform as an online casino.  
**Evidence:** official product pages, operator legal notice, state/territory eligibility, current draw/product rules, responsible-gambling information.  
**Potential useful review:** explain the product category, eligibility, official costs and rules, payment/account conditions, and responsible play. Do not force casino bonus language into a lottery review.

## Sportsbet — sportsbook/casino separation
**Question to resolve:** Which products are currently offered to eligible Australian customers, and which exact page is being reviewed? Sports betting is not the same as an online casino.  
**Evidence:** official terms, operator identity, product-specific rules, age/identity requirements, deposit and withdrawal disclosures, account controls.  
**Publication rule:** if the review scope is sportsbook only, categorise and title it accordingly; exclude it from the online-casino count.

## Crown digital properties
**Question to resolve:** identify the exact official digital product, legal operator, permitted category, and current Australian availability. Do not assume a land-based venue's reputation or licence extends to an online casino.

## Market gate
Australian online-casino services offered to people in Australia face different rules from sports betting and lotteries. Start with ACMA: https://www.acma.gov.au/online-gambling. Verify the current legal position before recommending any product. No bonus, rating, payout claim, or signup CTA until evidence is complete.
