## Shared quality standard
A review may state “not verified” where evidence is absent. Never fill gaps with assumed licensing, copied bonuses, made-up payout speeds, invented player ratings, game counts or borrowed logos. The UK Gambling Commission explains that online games must be tested by approved test houses and operators must monitor performance after launch; use primary regulator materials when describing regulatory requirements. Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-and-players/guide/page/how-we-ensure-online-gambling-games-are-fair
