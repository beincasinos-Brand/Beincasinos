# Batch 94 — Germany: review copy and terms checks
**Scope:** strengthen German-market candidate reviews without implying authorisation from a brand name.

## Candidate names to reconcile
Merkur-Spiel, NOVOLINE, Löwen Play. Resolve the exact legal operator, online consumer domain, and permitted category. Manufacturers and land-based venue operators are not automatically online casino operators.

## Evidence checklist
- Current GGL whitelist entry for exact operator/domain/category.
- German terms and eligibility.
- Deposit limits, identity checks, game rules, bonus terms, and complaint process.
- Responsible-gambling controls and support information.
- Official source URL and review date.

## Content requirements
Explain bonus conditions in plain language and avoid unsupported payout-speed claims. Don't create a recommendation page if the product is not currently eligible for the target market. If category scope is ambiguous, set status to pending and exclude from the published leaderboard.

## SEO
Use useful German localisation rather than translated boilerplate. Add relevant links to official regulator information and operator terms. No hidden keywords, fake reviews, fabricated ratings, or structured data unsupported by visible content.
