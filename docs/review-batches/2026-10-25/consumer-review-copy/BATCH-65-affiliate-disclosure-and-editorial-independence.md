# Batch 65 — Affiliate disclosure and editorial independence
**Purpose:** protect reader trust and prevent commercial relationships from being presented as regulatory or editorial verification.

## Disclosure principles
- Clearly disclose affiliate relationships near relevant links and in a site-wide disclosure page.
- Explain that a commission may be earned if a reader signs up or takes an eligible action, without implying that the commission changes a licence or product's legal status.
- Do not describe a paid listing as an independent endorsement.
- Keep any future listing-fee product distinct from editorial scoring. Payment must not guarantee a higher rating, “verified” badge or top ranking.
- Mark sponsored placements clearly and keep them visually separate from organic editorial rankings.
- If a commercial relationship exists, record it in the editorial CMS and ensure the public disclosure is visible.

## Evidence and corrections
- Legal status must be checked against the relevant regulator, not an affiliate manager or commission agreement.
- Bonus claims must link to current official terms.
- Corrections to expired offers, domains, operator names or licence status should be dated and logged.
- If evidence conflicts, pause the recommendation and explain the uncertainty instead of choosing the more commercially favourable claim.

## Scoring independence
Use the same published rubric for all brands. The score must reflect documented criteria, not CPA rate, listing fee, partner status, brand familiarity or advertiser preference. Keep the evidence ledger and scoring notes available for internal audit.

## User safety and responsible wording
Do not promise income, “guaranteed wins”, risk-free gambling or guaranteed withdrawals. Include age and market eligibility where relevant, and link to verified player-protection resources for the jurisdiction.

## Future paid listing policy
If BeInCasinos later introduces paid listings, identify the placement as sponsored and retain separate organic review criteria. No payment should create a fabricated licence check, fake user review, invented score or false claim of first-hand testing.
