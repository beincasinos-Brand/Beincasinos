## Shared review methodology and publication rules
1. **Identity gate:** exact domain, redirect path, legal entity and current terms.
2. **Licence gate:** current regulator register, matching domain, operator and casino product scope.
3. **Market gate:** local legality, site eligibility, marketing/affiliate restrictions and age requirements.
4. **Commercial gate:** current bonus terms, cashier methods, fees, limits, KYC and withdrawal conditions.
5. **Product gate:** dated live-lobby observations, provider/game details and mobile usability.
6. **Protection gate:** local limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, complaints, privacy and support.
7. **Editorial gate:** source log, image permissions, affiliate disclosure, last-verified date and independent review.

Suggested score weights only after all critical legal gates pass: local authorisation and eligibility 25%; payment transparency 20%; bonus fairness 15%; games/provider transparency 15%; mobile usability 10%; responsible gambling and complaints 10%; support evidence 5%. If operator identity, licence scope or local eligibility remains unresolved, use “Not rated / Not verified” rather than inventing a score, and do not add Review structured data that implies a verified rating.
