# Batch 55 — Canonical inventory, evidence ledger, scoring, images and SEO QA
**Purpose:** editorial controls for all existing candidate drafts. This is not a new casino brand and must not count toward the 100-brand target.

## 1. One canonical identity per brand
Store canonical name, official domain per market, legal operator, parent company if verified, product category, country/province/state scope, regulator and permit reference, source URL and check date. Use statuses: candidate, identity unresolved, market blocked, evidence pending, evidence checked, publish ready. A country page, provincial subsite, alternate spelling or duplicate batch must not be counted as a separate unique brand.

## 2. Evidence ledger
For every factual claim record: claim; exact source URL; source type; UTC check date; market; result (verified, contradicted, inaccessible, unresolved); page section supported; and scope caveat. Prefer regulator registers for permission, operator terms for offers, official cashier/help pages for payments, and direct product inspection for UX. Third-party affiliate directories do not replace regulator records.

## 3. Consistent rating rubric
Only score when the legal and product gates pass. Rate each dimension 0–5 with a written rationale:
- Regulatory and market clarity: 25%.
- Terms transparency: 20%.
- Product and usability, based on dated inspection: 20%.
- Payments clarity: 15%.
- Player protection and support: 20%.

Weighted score is the sum of each dimension's 0–5 rating multiplied by its weight, then converted to a 0–10 scale. If a critical licence, product or eligibility question is unresolved, display “Not rated — verification pending.” Never invent a score or claim the score is a regulator endorsement. Date the assessment and explain the method.

## 4. Offers and cashier
For each current offer store official URL, access date, market, eligibility, qualifying deposit, wagering base/multiple, game contribution, expiry, exclusions, cashout cap and withdrawal restrictions. If unavailable, mark “not verified”; do not infer that a bonus exists. Distinguish deposit method availability from withdrawal availability and processing time. Never promise “instant” or “fast payouts” without current product-specific evidence.

## 5. Image rights
Use operator-owned assets only where reuse is permitted, licensed stock, or explicitly licensed media. Record asset URL, owner, licence/permission, date captured, allowed commercial use and attribution. A logo visible on a website is not automatically free to reuse. Do not copy competitor screenshots or hotlink game-provider art without permission. Write descriptive, non-keyword-stuffed alt text.

## 6. SEO and structured data
Use one canonical URL per unique page. Use hreflang only for genuine market-specific equivalents. Titles and descriptions must match the exact product and market. Avoid thin country/state permutations and near-duplicate doorway pages. Add review or FAQ structured data only when visible content supports it and applicable search policies permit it; never manufacture ratings or review counts. Date-check volatile offers and licence details, and remove expired promotions.

## 7. Publication gate
A page can be publish-ready only after:
- unique brand identity reconciled;
- exact domain and operator confirmed;
- current regulator record and product category checked;
- market eligibility and age/residency constraints written clearly;
- live lobby and mobile UX inspected or limitations disclosed;
- bonus terms verified or no bonus claim published;
- cashier and withdrawal wording sourced;
- player-protection and complaint information verified;
- score justified by the same rubric;
- image rights recorded;
- title, meta, headings, canonical, internal links and schema QA passed;
- unsupported claims, duplicate brand counts and invented evidence removed.
