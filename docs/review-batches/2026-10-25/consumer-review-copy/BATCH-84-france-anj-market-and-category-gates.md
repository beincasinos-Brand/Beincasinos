# Batch 84 — France: ANJ and product classification
**Candidates:** Winamax, Partouche, JOA. Brand recognition alone does not establish that every product or domain is authorised for online casino games in France.

## Verification
Start with the French National Gaming Authority (ANJ): https://anj.fr/
Verify exact legal entity, domain, and permitted category. Distinguish online sports betting, poker, lottery, land-based casino services, and online casino games. Don't call a sportsbook or poker product a licensed online casino where that classification isn't supported.

## Review checklist
- Scope and category of the actual France-facing product.
- Legal operator and official domain.
- Current offer and official terms, where relevant and lawful.
- Payment and withdrawal information from official sources.
- Age and eligibility restrictions.
- Responsible-gambling and complaint resources.
- Date and evidence URL for each material claim.

## Publishing rule
If product category is not the one the page title promises, either change page category/title to match evidence or exclude the candidate from the online casino list. Never use a generic international website to imply French authorisation.

## SEO
A helpful category explainer may clarify the difference between permitted online gambling products and land-based casino services. Avoid claims like “France's top online casinos” unless the list matches the legal product category and current evidence.
