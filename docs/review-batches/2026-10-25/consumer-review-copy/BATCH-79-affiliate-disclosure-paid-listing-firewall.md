# Batch 79 — Affiliate and paid-listing firewall
**Policy:** commercial relationships must not determine editorial ratings, inclusion eligibility, or factual claims.

## Required reader disclosure
Place plain-language affiliate disclosure near the first relevant commercial link and maintain a permanent disclosure page. Explain that BeInCasinos may earn a commission if a reader uses a link, and that this does not guarantee a positive review. Adapt wording to applicable law and platform requirements.

## Separate three concepts
- **Affiliate link:** tracking or commission relationship.
- **Paid listing:** a commercial placement that must be clearly labelled.
- **Editorial rating:** an evidence-based assessment using the published rubric.

These are not interchangeable. A paid placement must not be displayed as an editorial award or “best” badge. A brand must not be described as verified merely because a partnership manager accepts it.

## Controls
1. Maintain a commercial relationship register accessible to editors.
2. Keep scoring sheets separate from commission rates and sales negotiations.
3. Mark sponsored cards visibly where used.
4. Don't let an advertiser purchase a higher score or suppress a material safety/eligibility caveat.
5. Provide a correction and appeal process without promising a favourable result.
6. Review disclosures for every target jurisdiction before publication.

If a candidate is ineligible for the page's target market, block the recommendation even if it is a partner. Record the reason and source evidence.
