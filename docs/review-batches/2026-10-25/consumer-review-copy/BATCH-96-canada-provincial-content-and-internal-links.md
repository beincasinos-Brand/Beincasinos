# Batch 96 — Canada: provincial content architecture
**Scope:** make Canadian reviews useful without suggesting a single national market.

## Candidate names to reconcile
PlayNow, OLG.ca Casino, Espacejeux. Record the exact province and product each page covers. A brand can have different rules or be unavailable across provincial borders.

## Content modules
- Province-specific eligibility and operator identity.
- Product category and supported games/services.
- Official current bonus terms, if offered.
- Deposits, withdrawals, verification, and fees.
- Safer-gambling tools and official provincial support.
- Update history and official links.

## Internal links
The Canada overview should link to provincial pages only when the page adds material, verified information. A province page should link only to products eligible in that province. Do not generate 13 near-identical pages with a province name swapped in.

## Quality checks
Verify legal operator and domain; deduplicate aliases; separate lottery and sports products from casino; disclose affiliate links; use licensed images; keep ratings evidence-based. If evidence is missing, leave the candidate pending rather than filling a blank with invented details.
