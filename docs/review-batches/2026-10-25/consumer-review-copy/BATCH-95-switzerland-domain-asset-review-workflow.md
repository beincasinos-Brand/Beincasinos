# Batch 95 — Switzerland: domain, assets, and evidence workflow
**Scope:** supplementary workflow for Swiss online-casino candidates.

## Candidate names to reconcile
Swiss Casinos, Grand Casino Luzern / mycasino, Jackpots.ch. Treat these as candidates until the exact current online operator, domain, and Swiss authorisation are evidenced.

## Evidence packet
1. Official ESBK listing or other relevant primary-source record.
2. Operator legal notice and current terms.
3. Exact Swiss-facing product and customer eligibility.
4. Current promotion evidence, if any.
5. Payment/withdrawal conditions and verification rules.
6. Responsible-gambling and complaints pages.
7. Licensed image source or neutral fallback decision.
8. Evidence timestamp and reviewer notes.

## Review content
A useful Swiss review should distinguish legal availability, product category, game catalogue, terms, account tools, and usability. Do not infer licence status from a land-based casino concession or the brand's international site. Do not use a global bonus on a Swiss page unless official terms clearly apply.

## Release gate
No score or “verified” badge until the packet is complete. Do not use copied competitor screenshots. Link to ESBK and official operator terms.
