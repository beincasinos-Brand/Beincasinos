# Batch 74 — Bonus evidence ledger and offer QA
**Purpose:** prevent invented, stale, or misleading welcome-offer copy across the 100-review programme.

## Required fields per offer
- Brand canonical ID and exact official domain
- Market / jurisdiction / eligible customer type
- Offer headline from the official page
- Bonus type: match, free spins, cashback, free bet, or other
- Minimum deposit and qualifying payment methods
- Wagering requirement and qualifying activity
- Maximum bonus / spins / winnings cap
- Eligible games and contribution rates
- Expiry, opt-in code, and time limit
- Withdrawal restrictions and verification requirements
- Terms URL, capture date, reviewer, and next recheck date
- Evidence status: unverified, official terms found, tested, expired, or not offered

## Publication rules
1. Never calculate a “value” headline without explaining the conditions.
2. Never present free-spin amounts without expiry, eligible games, and winnings cap where applicable.
3. If the official site shows no promotion, don't infer one from an affiliate network or old search snippet.
4. Mark market restrictions prominently; a global offer can differ by country.
5. Treat a bonus as expired when official terms have expired or the offer can no longer be verified.
6. Keep screenshots or archived evidence only where legally and operationally appropriate; never store customer personal data.

## Reader-first phrasing
“Promotions can change by country and account. Check official terms before depositing.” Add a last-checked date only after a real check.

## Quality control
A second reviewer should verify offer headline, terms, and jurisdiction before publication. Automated schema must not contain bonus values absent from visible, current page copy.
