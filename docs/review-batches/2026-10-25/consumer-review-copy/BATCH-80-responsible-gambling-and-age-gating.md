# Batch 80 — Responsible-gambling and age safeguards
**Purpose:** ensure reviews are not purely promotional and provide practical risk information.

## Required page elements
- Age and jurisdiction eligibility reminder based on relevant official rules.
- Clear access to deposit limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, reality checks, and account closure where documented.
- Direct links to official local support resources, selected after verifying the reader's market.
- Statement that gambling involves financial risk and winnings are not guaranteed.
- No implication that gambling solves money problems, provides reliable income, or creates financial freedom.
- No “risk-free,” “guaranteed win,” or misleading urgency claims.

## Review-specific checks
Document whether safer-gambling tools are present in the operator's official account/help pages. Don't state a feature exists because competitors commonly offer it. If it cannot be checked, label it “not verified” and exclude it from scoring.

## Market-sensitive publishing
Don't assume the same support number, self-exclusion programme, legal age, or operator eligibility applies across Australia, Canada, Great Britain, the US, Switzerland, Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Spain, Italy, France, and Belgium. Maintain jurisdiction-specific links and check dates.

## UX
Keep safety information readable on mobile. Don't bury it under promotional banners. Avoid preselected deposit amounts and manipulative countdown timers. Don't target minors or use youth-oriented imagery.

A page lacking verified market eligibility or presenting misleading risk claims must not be published, regardless of SEO potential.
