# Batch 70 — Pre-launch integrity gate for the 100-review programme
**This is a release checklist, not a brand or a claim that the site is launch-ready.**

## Content integrity
- Every review maps to one canonical brand ID.
- The exact market, domain, operator and product category are stated.
- Current primary-source evidence supports licence and eligibility statements.
- Current official terms support each bonus and payment claim.
- Game counts, payout times, ratings and review counts are not invented.
- Unavailable evidence is clearly marked; no implied hands-on test if the product was not inspected.
- Every page has unique reader value, not merely swapped brand names in a template.
- Pros/cons follow from evidence, not affiliate commission or brand reputation.

## Visual integrity
- Brand logo and screenshots have recorded reuse rights.
- Images are correctly associated with the reviewed brand and market.
- No generic stock image is presented as an official brand asset.
- Alt text is descriptive and accessible.
- Mobile layout, image dimensions and load behaviour are checked before launch.

## Technical SEO
- Unique, stable canonical URL for each unique review.
- Accurate title and meta description; no unsupported market claims.
- Internal links connect only relevant market/category pages.
- No indexable duplicate pages created by alternate slugs, query strings or thin state/province variations.
- Sitemap contains only intended canonical URLs after publication.
- Structured data reflects visible page content and current search policies; no fabricated aggregate ratings.
- 404s, redirects, robots directives and canonical targets are checked on staging.
- Search Console sitemap and indexing are reviewed after the site is genuinely ready, not during draft work.

## Legal and market QA
- Current regulator record is saved with access date.
- Product-specific permission is confirmed; licence for a sportsbook, poker or lottery product is not treated as casino permission.
- US pages name the exact state; Canadian pages name the province where relevant.
- French online casino classification is checked carefully.
- Australian offshore online casino access is not presented as authorised without primary-source evidence.
- Age/residency/geolocation requirements are visible where relevant.

## Commercial integrity
- Affiliate disclosure is clear near monetised links.
- Future paid listings are labelled sponsored and cannot buy a higher editorial score.
- Affiliate link destinations are tested and do not misrepresent market eligibility.
- Expired offers are removed or updated promptly.

## Launch decision
Do not merge or deploy until the canonical list has 100 distinct brands, all planned pages pass the above gates, image rights are recorded, the score rubric is applied consistently and technical QA is complete. Keep production unchanged while these conditions remain unmet.
