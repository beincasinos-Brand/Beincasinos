# Batch 93 — France: online product category review standards
**Scope:** prevent misclassification of French-facing gambling brands.

## Candidate names to reconcile
Winamax, Partouche, JOA. For each, verify the actual online product, legal entity, official domain, and permitted category before creating a casino review.

## Category gate
France's online gambling permissions differ by product. A sports-betting or poker offering, a lottery product, and a land-based casino are not interchangeable with an online casino games product. If the candidate does not match the page promise, reclassify it or exclude it.

## Required facts
- Official ANJ evidence for the relevant operator/product category.
- Current terms and exact eligibility.
- Promotions only where applicable and legally offered.
- Payments, identity checks, limits, customer support, and complaint routes.
- Responsible-gambling information.
- Evidence URLs and last-check dates.

## Editorial copy
Describe what the product is, who may use it, what terms matter, and what remains unverified. Don't borrow generic casino review language if the actual product is poker or sports betting. Don't publish made-up bonus amounts or numerical scores.
