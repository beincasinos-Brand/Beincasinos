# Batch 75 — Evidence-based review score rubric
**Status:** rubric proposal; no brand score is assigned by this document.

## Proposed 100-point rubric
- Legal and market eligibility evidence: 20
- Terms transparency and bonus clarity: 15
- Payments and withdrawal transparency: 15
- Game catalogue and provider transparency: 15
- Account security, privacy, and identity-check clarity: 10
- Responsible-gambling controls: 10
- Usability and accessibility from a documented test: 10
- Support availability and complaint process: 5

## Non-negotiable gates
- If exact domain/operator/market eligibility is unresolved, do not publish a recommendation or numeric overall score.
- A product with unresolved serious legal concerns is blocked, not merely penalised.
- Do not award points for claims that cannot be evidenced.
- Never score a casino based on bonus size alone.
- Affiliate commission, listing fees, and commercial relationships must not affect scoring.

## Evidence levels
- **A — Primary verified:** current regulator or official operator evidence.
- **B — Directly tested:** documented test with date, conditions, and reproducible steps.
- **C — Corroborative:** reputable independent source; useful context but not a substitute for primary evidence.
- **D — Unverified:** no reliable evidence; exclude from score and public assertions.

## Calibration
Apply the same rubric to comparable product categories within a jurisdiction. Don't compare a lottery product, sportsbook, poker room, land-based venue, and online casino on one undifferentiated leaderboard. Publish a short methodology explainer and disclose material limitations.

## Example score presentation
“Score pending verification” is correct while evidence is incomplete. Don't fabricate 9.8/10 or 98/100 merely to make a review card look finished.
