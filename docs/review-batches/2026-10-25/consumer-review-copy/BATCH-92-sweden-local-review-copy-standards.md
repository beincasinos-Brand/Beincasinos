# Batch 92 — Sweden: local review copy standards
**Scope:** evidence and wording for Swedish-market review candidates.

## Candidate names to reconcile
Svenska Spel, ATG, LeoVegas. Confirm the specific Swedish-facing product, current licence status, exact domain, and category for each. Don't count lottery or horse-racing products as online casino reviews without a separate qualifying casino product.

## Evidence fields
- Current licence/operator evidence from Spelinspektionen.
- Exact domain and product scope.
- Current Swedish offer and terms, or no-offer/unverified status.
- Payment methods, withdrawal conditions, identity checks, limits, and complaint route.
- Responsible-gambling tools and applicable local support.
- Mobile usability test date and notes.

## Writing rules
Use Swedish terms accurately and explain restrictions in accessible language. Do not use “safe,” “guaranteed,” “fastest payout,” or “best” without clear, current evidence. Keep any affiliate disclosure near commercial links. Do not let a paid listing influence rating.

## SEO
Create Swedish pages only where localisation adds real value. Avoid machine-translated duplicates and generic market pages that merely repeat a bonus table. Link to the regulator and official operator terms.
