# Batch 30 — Evidence grid and market release checklist
**Internal companion to existing brand drafts.** Use this to prevent duplicate counting and accidental publication of unverified claims.

## Market-by-market release matrix
Complete a separate row for every brand and every target market (AU, CA, GB, US, CH, DE, NL, SE, ES, IT, FR, BE). A brand that is eligible in one country must not inherit that status in another.

| Field | Required evidence | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical brand identity | Official consumer-facing URL and product identity | Do not count as a casino-operator review |
| Contracting entity | Current official terms or account terms naming the entity | Hold |

| Market eligibility | Official terms or regulator/operator statement | Do not target the market |

## Scoring worksheet
For a fully checked market-specific page, score each category from 0 to its maximum and write a one-sentence evidence-based rationale:
- Licensing, operator transparency and market fit: /25
- Promotion transparency: /20
- Payments and withdrawal clarity: /15
- Game catalogue and supplier transparency: /15
- Usability and mobile experience: /10
- Support and complaints: /10
- Responsible-gambling tools and accessibility: /5
- **Total:** /100

Rules:
1. Never score legal authorisation by intuition or popularity.
2. Missing evidence means “not assessed”, not an invented score.
3. If legal status or exact operator identity is uncertain, the page fails release even if other categories look strong.
4. A score is a transparent editorial assessment, not a guarantee of safety or winnings.
5. Publish methodology and date checked alongside the score; update when material terms change.

## Bonus copy checklist
Before publishing a bonus card, verify: currency, qualifying deposit, bonus cap, wagering base, wagering multiple, game contribution, excluded games, expiry, maximum cashout/winnings, withdrawal restrictions, payment exclusions, new-customer rules, country scope, source URL and check date. Do not copy offers from affiliates or comparison pages when official terms are available.

## Review page content structure
1. Short neutral verdict with market and review date.
2. At-a-glance facts: official URL, operator, licence status, supported market, product type — only verified facts.
3. Pros and cons with evidence, not generic praise.
4. Game categories and lobby usability.
5. Bonus terms in plain language, with restrictions prominent.
6. Payments, verification and withdrawal steps.
7. Support, complaint process and account controls.
8. Responsible-gambling guidance and self-exclusion.
9. Score breakdown with rationale, only after assessment.
10. Sources and “last checked” date.
11. Affiliate disclosure where links are commercial.
12. Correction contact and editorial methodology.

## Image rights register
For each asset store:
- Brand/game and asset filename.
- Source URL and retrieval date.
- Rights basis: written permission, approved affiliate media kit, or commercial licence.
- Required attribution and allowed territories/media.
- Expiry/withdrawal conditions.
- Alt text and page usage.
If no rights basis is recorded, do not use the asset in production. Do not assume a search result, CDN image, public logo or screenshot is free for commercial use.

## Duplication and counting rules
- One brand family with supplemental drafts counts once, not once per file.
- Country-specific variants count as market versions of one brand unless the user explicitly defines the target as separate licensed entities and the editorial architecture clearly explains why.
- Aliases, old names, product formats and game providers do not count as separate casino operators.
- Keep unresolved candidates in a “research / replace” queue; do not pad the 100 with them.
- A “review draft exists” status is not equivalent to “verified and publish-ready”.
