# Batch 63 — Reader-first casino review template for unique, evidence-backed pages
**This is a reusable editorial template, not a new brand.** It must be filled with current evidence for each distinct brand and market; do not publish as a thin mass-produced page.

## Page structure
1. **At-a-glance:** brand, exact market, verified operator, product categories, last checked date and verification status.
2. **Verdict in plain English:** concise, evidence-backed strengths and limitations. No “best” claims without comparative research.
3. **Market and eligibility:** who can legally access the exact product; age/residency/geolocation restrictions; link to official regulator record.
4. **Games and experience:** observed game categories, navigation, mobile experience, rules access and limitations. State the date inspected and do not guess counts.
5. **Welcome offer:** only show if current official terms were checked. Include eligibility, qualifying deposit, wagering base/multiple, contribution, expiry, exclusions and cashout restrictions. Otherwise say “No current offer verified.”
6. **Payments and withdrawals:** distinguish deposit methods from withdrawal methods, list fees/limits if sourced, explain identity checks and avoid promises about processing speed.
7. **Player protection:** actual limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, support and complaints verified from official sources.
8. **Pros and cons:** only facts supported by the page's evidence ledger.
9. **How we scored it:** weighted rubric, dimension-level reasoning, last-reviewed date and any unavailable evidence.
10. **Alternatives:** only other distinct brands that passed the same market eligibility gate.
11. **Sources and disclosures:** primary sources, dates checked, affiliate disclosure where relevant, image credits/rights.

## Reusable editorial scoring
- Market/operator clarity: 25%.
- Transparency of terms: 20%.
- Observed product and usability: 20%.
- Payment clarity: 15%.
- Player protection/support: 20%.

Each dimension gets a 0–5 rating with written reasons. Convert to a 0–10 total only after required evidence is present. If operator identity, legal scope, or user eligibility is unresolved, use “Not rated — verification pending.” A high score must never be implied to be a regulator endorsement.

## Visual and image standards
Use a licensed operator logo only where reuse is permitted. Use genuine product screenshots only with permission or a valid legal basis. Do not use a generic casino image as if it were a specific brand. Every image needs source, rights status, alt text and market relevance.

## SEO standards
Use one canonical URL for one unique review; market-specific variants must have meaningful differences. No thin city/state doorway pages. Titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and structured data must reflect the exact product and jurisdiction. Add review schema only where the content and current search policies support it; never invent review count or rating. Link to sources near volatile claims and show last checked date.

## Publication gate
No page is publish-ready until identity, market eligibility, current terms, cashier, safeguards, score rationale, image rights, canonical, internal links and structured data have been checked. This template is not permission to duplicate near-identical reviews at scale.
