# Batch 83 — Sweden: licence and product verification
**Candidates:** Svenska Spel, ATG, LeoVegas. Each candidate must be reviewed as the exact product offered in Sweden, not a generic global brand.

## Verification
Begin at the Swedish Gambling Authority: https://www.spelinspektionen.se/
Find current operator licence information and match legal entity, product type, and official domain. Confirm whether the product is online casino, sports betting, horse-racing betting, lottery, or land-based service.

## User-facing review sections
- Operator and market eligibility
- Exact product category and available games/services
- Current promotions with complete Swedish terms, or note that no current offer was verified
- Payments, identity checks, withdrawals, and fees based on official disclosures
- Customer support and complaint handling
- Limits, self-exclusion, and responsible-gambling tools
- Mobile usability based on a dated test

## Category distinction
A brand known primarily for horse racing or lottery must not automatically be counted as an online casino. Where a company offers several products, state which one the review covers and use a separate record only if the consumer product is distinct.

## Publication and SEO
No score until licence/domain verification is complete. Don't advertise a bonus as universal if terms or eligibility vary. Use Swedish-market keywords naturally and link to regulator information.
