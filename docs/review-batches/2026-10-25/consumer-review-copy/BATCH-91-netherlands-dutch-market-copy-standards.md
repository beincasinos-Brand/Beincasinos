# Batch 91 — Netherlands: local review copy and checks
**Scope:** a quality-control supplement for Dutch-facing pages and any candidate considered for the Netherlands.

## Page-level fields
- Dutch-facing official domain and exact legal operator.
- Current KSA licence evidence and product category.
- Date checked and links to the official register and terms.
- Current welcome offer or a transparent statement that no current offer was verified.
- Payment/withdrawal and identity-check terms.
- Safer-gambling controls, complaint route, and eligible-customer restrictions.

## Candidate names to reconcile
Holland Casino Online, Kansino, BetCity. These names must not be counted as verified merely because they are familiar or appear in old search results. Check current operator/domain data and deduplicate any rebrand or acquisition.

## Copy style
Use clear Dutch wording and explain wagering restrictions in plain language. Never reduce a promotion to a headline amount without the qualifying deposit, wagering requirement, expiry, eligible games, and cashout restrictions. Don't claim instant payouts unless a current, testable source supports that exact claim.

## SEO integrity
One genuinely useful local guide is better than dozens of thin keyword permutations. Internal links should connect only eligible products. Include an update date only when a check was actually performed.
