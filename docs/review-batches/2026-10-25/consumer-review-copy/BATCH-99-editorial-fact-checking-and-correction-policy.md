# Batch 99 — Editorial fact-checking and corrections
**Purpose:** keep reviews credible as operators, offers, domains, and laws change.

## Fact-checking
Every material statement should map to a source and check date. Primary sources should establish operator identity, authorisation, official terms, and responsible-gambling features. Independent sources can add context but should not replace regulator evidence.

## Correction levels
- **Critical:** eligibility/licence error, misleading bonus term, incorrect payment claim, or safety issue. Remove or block the affected CTA/page until corrected.
- **Material:** operator/domain change, outdated offer, inaccurate game/provider claim. Correct promptly and update evidence record.
- **Minor:** typo or non-material wording issue. Correct during routine editorial maintenance.

## Change log
Record page, claim, old wording, corrected wording, source, date, reviewer, and whether rating changed. Don't silently preserve an old rating after the evidence underlying it changes.

## Reader feedback
Provide a visible route to report errors. A commercial partner may submit evidence but cannot dictate editorial conclusions. Explain that correction requests are reviewed independently.

## Scheduled checks
Set review intervals based on volatility: bonuses and domains require more frequent checks than stable methodology pages. Any regulator alert, operator rebrand, or acquisition should trigger an immediate review.
