# Batch 28 — Canonical review scorecard, evidence rules and image rights
**Internal editorial standard — not a consumer review page.** Apply this to every candidate in the 100-brand set before a review is marked publish-ready.

## 1. Canonical identity record
Every brand must have one canonical row with:
- Canonical brand name and approved URL.
- Exact contracting/legal operator for each target country.
- Regulator, licence/reference number, status and allowed product scope, verified directly in the regulator register.
- Countries where the specific online product is legally available, plus exclusions.
- Official terms URL, bonus URL (if any), cashier/help URL, safer-gambling URL, complaint route.
- Date and reviewer for every check.
- Duplicate/alias notes: keep alternate spellings and supplemental drafts attached to the canonical record; do not count them as separate brands.

## 2. Evidence labels
Use one of these labels beside every material claim:
- **Verified — primary source:** current operator page, regulator register or official help/terms page directly supports the statement.
- **Verified — independent corroboration:** a reliable secondary source supports the claim, but primary-source confirmation is still required for legal status or commercial terms.
- **Unverified:** evidence not found, stale, inaccessible or ambiguous.
- **Conflict:** credible sources disagree. Hold the affected claim and investigate.
- **Not applicable:** only when the reason is recorded.

A brand cannot be described as “verified” merely because an affiliate network accepts it or because the name is familiar.

## 3. Rating framework — score only after evidence
Do not publish a score until all critical legal and product-identity checks pass. Suggested 100-point rubric:
- Licensing, operator transparency and market fit — **25 points**
- Terms and promotion transparency — **20 points**
- Payments and withdrawal clarity — **15 points**
- Game catalogue and supplier transparency — **15 points**
- Usability and mobile experience — **10 points**
- Support and complaint handling — **10 points**
- Responsible-gambling tools and accessibility — **5 points**

For each dimension, keep a short rationale and evidence URL/date. If a dimension cannot be assessed, mark it **Not assessed** and withhold the aggregate score rather than treating missing data as zero or inventing a neutral score. Legal uncertainty is a publication blocker, not a cosmetic deduction.

## 4. Bonus fact sheet
For each current promotion, capture:
1. Headline and currency.
2. New/existing customer eligibility.
3. Minimum deposit and qualifying payment method.
4. Bonus amount/cap and wagering multiple, including what the multiple applies to.
5. Eligible and excluded games and contribution rates.
6. Expiry, maximum cashout/winnings and withdrawal restrictions.
7. Country restrictions and any account-specific conditions.
8. Official terms URL, capture date and reviewer.

If no promotion is verified, say “No current offer independently verified for this market” or omit the bonus claim. Never fabricate an offer to fill a card.

## 5. Image and logo rights
- Do not hotlink competitor images or scrape casino/game art into production.
- Use assets supplied with explicit affiliate/media-kit permission, written permission, or a licence that covers the intended commercial use.
- Store the source URL, rights basis, allowed use, attribution requirement, retrieval date and local asset path.
- If rights cannot be established, use a designed text/brand placeholder; do not imply that a placeholder is an official logo.
- Keep alt text descriptive and non-promotional. Do not create fake screenshots of a live casino lobby.
- Third-party image search results are discovery leads, not proof of usage rights.

## 6. SEO and consumer-trust checks
- One canonical URL per unique brand/market page; do not create thin duplicates by copying the same review into every country.
- Add market-specific content only when the operator, product and terms genuinely differ.
- Keep title/meta description accurate and distinct; avoid unsupported “best”, “safe”, “licensed everywhere”, “instant payout” and “no. 1” claims.
- Disclose affiliate relationships clearly where relevant; do not present paid placement as independent ranking.
- Use FAQ content only when it answers genuine questions. Structured review/rating markup must reflect visible, evidence-supported content and must not fabricate scores or user reviews.
- Ensure the page has clear date reviewed/updated, author/editorial method, corrections contact and responsible-gambling information.
- Do not publish market targeting or signup CTAs where eligibility or legal scope remains unresolved.

## 7. Release gates
**Gate A — Identity:** exact domain, operator and country identified.
**Gate B — Legal:** regulator register checked and scope matches product.
**Gate C — Facts:** bonuses, payments, games and support checked against current official sources.
**Gate D — Editorial:** balanced pros/cons, original writing, clear uncertainty and responsible-gambling context.
**Gate E — Assets:** commercial image rights recorded.
**Gate F — Technical:** canonical, internal links, mobile rendering, metadata, accessibility and structured data validated.
**Gate G — Release:** reviewer approval and sitemap/indexing change only after the page passes all previous gates.

A candidate that fails Gate A or B remains an internal research draft. Do not inflate the “100 completed reviews” count with duplicate supplements, unverified brands, or blank templates.
