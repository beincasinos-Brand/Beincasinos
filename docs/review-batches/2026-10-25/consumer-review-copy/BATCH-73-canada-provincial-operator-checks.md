# Batch 73 — Canada: province-specific casino products
**Status:** candidates requiring current primary-source checks.

## Candidate set
- **PlayNow** — investigate provincial operator, supported province, and product scope before calling it a casino.
- **OLG.ca Casino** — establish the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation product and current eligibility conditions.
- **Espacejeux** — establish the operator, Quebec product scope, and exact current legal terms.

## Eligibility rule
Canadian online gambling is not a single nationwide market. Record province, operator, domain, product category, and source date for each candidate. Don't imply a government-operated platform is available in every province or describe an operator's entire portfolio as casino gaming if it includes lottery, sports, or other products.

## Required checks
- Official provincial operator site and legal/about page.
- Current eligibility and geolocation terms.
- Official bonus terms, including whether an offer exists at all.
- Payment options, identity checks, withdrawal terms, limits, and support.
- Responsible-gambling resources and provincial self-exclusion mechanisms.
- Accessibility, language support, and mobile experience as actually tested.

## Public review wording
**Availability:** state the exact province and official eligibility rule.  
**Promotions:** don't display an unverified bonus amount. If no offer has been confirmed, say it has not been independently verified rather than inventing one.  
**Score:** hold until evidence collection is complete; brand recognition is not service quality.

## SEO
Target province-plus-brand searches only where there is a real, useful, current page. Avoid broad “best Canada casino” language implying universal availability. Include an update date and source links only after actual checks.

Use the operator's official terms and the relevant provincial government/lottery authority. Secondary affiliate pages can identify leads but do not establish legal status or bonus terms.
