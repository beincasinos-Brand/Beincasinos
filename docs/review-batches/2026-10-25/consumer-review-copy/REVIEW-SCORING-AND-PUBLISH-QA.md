# BeInCasinos — Review scoring and publish QA
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Apply one consistent, auditable standard to the 100-brand review batch. This is an editorial control, not a claim that any casino has been tested.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Score only a market-specific product with an identified legal operator and a current regulator/operator source trail.
- Never infer a numeric casino-quality rating from the brand name, affiliate payout, promotional headline, or another country's licence.
- Do not claim hands-on testing unless the test log identifies date, market, device, account state, test steps, and outcome.
- Do not publish a bonus as currently available unless the official offer and full terms were checked on the same date; state eligibility, expiry, wagering, game restrictions, maximum cashout and other material limits when the terms disclose them.
- Every image must have a recorded source and rights/permission basis. Otherwise use a designed brand-neutral fallback, not a copied logo or casino screenshot.
- Mark a market unavailable/blocked when official rules do not permit the product. Do not imply availability merely because a domain loads.
- Affiliate/commercial relationships must be disclosed near decision-making content.

## Consumer-facing score rubric (100 points)
Scores are assigned only when evidence exists for the relevant factor. If a factor cannot be verified, mark it **Not verified** and do not silently award full points.

| Factor | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Regulatory status and operator transparency | 25 | Regulator register, exact legal entity, product and domain match, check date |
| Terms and promotional clarity | 15 | Official full terms, eligibility and material restrictions |
| Deposits, withdrawals and fees | 20 | Official payment/withdrawal policy with limits, fees and processing caveats |
| Player protection and complaints | 15 | Official safer-gambling tools, self-exclusion, complaints/ADR routes |
| Product information and usability | 10 | Product-specific facts supported by dated evidence; hands-on label if tested |
| Customer support and accessibility | 5 | Published contact routes and clearly described test status |
| Transparency and evidence quality | 10 | Source links, timestamps, limitations, affiliate disclosure and editorial traceability |
| **Total** | **100** | Publish a numeric score only when the evidence coverage is sufficient to support it. |

## Evidence coverage gate
Before assigning a consumer score, require verified sources for: (1) operator/licence/domain, (2) market eligibility, (3) current promotion or an explicit “no offer verified” note, (4) payments and withdrawals, (5) safer gambling and complaints, (6) affiliate disclosure, (7) image rights/fallback, and (8) editorial review. If any critical legal/eligibility point is unresolved, use **HOLD — not scored**.

## Required review-page sections
1. Market and product scope, checked date, and status
2. Operator identity and regulator evidence
3. Bonus/offer — exact headline, eligibility, terms and expiry, or “not verified”
4. Deposits, withdrawals, fees and limits — sourced or explicitly not verified
5. Games/product scope — sourced; no invented counts
6. Player protection, self-exclusion and complaints
7. Mobile/usability and support — distinguish published information from testing
8. Pros and limitations supported by evidence
9. Score breakdown and rationale, or a clear “not scored” explanation
10. Official sources, methodology, affiliate disclosure and responsible-gambling notice

## Editorial state definitions
- **CANDIDATE:** brand identity not yet fully established.
- **HOLD:** material evidence or legal/market eligibility unresolved.
- **EVIDENCE-COMPLETE:** required source trail assembled; copy still needs editorial review.
- **EDITORIAL-APPROVED:** claims, links, score, disclosures and presentation reviewed.
- **PUBLISH-READY:** all above passed; image rights/fallback confirmed; canonical URL and internal links checked.

## Release checklist
- [ ] One unique consumer brand; no duplicate locale counted as another brand
- [ ] Exact legal entity, regulator, product and domain align
- [ ] Intended market is lawful and eligible
- [ ] Offer and full terms checked on the stated date
- [ ] Payment/withdrawal details sourced or marked not verified
- [ ] Responsible-gambling and complaint routes included
- [ ] No unsupported score, testimonial, speed claim or “tested” language
- [ ] Image rights recorded or brand-neutral fallback used
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure visible
- [ ] Canonical URL is the clean directory URL (not /index.html)
- [ ] Page links, mobile layout, metadata and internal navigation checked
- [ ] Editorial approval recorded in the master register

**Release principle:** do not expose HOLD pages as completed reviews, and do not submit draft pages in the sitemap.
