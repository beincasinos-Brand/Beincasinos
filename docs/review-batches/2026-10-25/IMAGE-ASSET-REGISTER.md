## Workflow
1. Prefer original commissioned artwork, brand-provided press assets with explicit editorial-use permission, or a compatible stock licence.
2. Save the original licence/permission text and its date, allowed media, territories, attribution and expiry.
3. Keep the image filename, dimensions, source, rights holder, usage purpose and alt text together.
4. Do not copy competitor screenshots, game art, logos or promotional banners without permission.
5. Do not embed current bonus values or ratings in image files; they can become stale and are not accessible to all users.
6. Produce responsive WebP/AVIF where supported, preserve legibility, compress, and check mobile crops and layout shift.
7. Use alt text that describes the image accurately; do not keyword-stuff.
8. On rights expiry/revocation, remove or replace the asset and check cached variants.
9. No asset may be marked CLEARED without a recorded permission/licence and reviewer check.
