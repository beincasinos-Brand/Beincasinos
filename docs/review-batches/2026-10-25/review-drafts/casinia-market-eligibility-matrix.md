## Evidence required before writing a verdict

1. **Operator and legal eligibility:** primary regulator record, legal entity, exact domain, permitted activity and checked date.
2. **Terms:** current general terms, bonus terms or a clearly sourced statement that no offer was verified; record wagering, expiry, max-bet, excluded games, payment exclusions and cashout restrictions where applicable.
3. **Payments:** official cashier/payment policy with methods, limits, fees, processing windows, KYC and withdrawal rules. Separate published estimates from measured outcomes.
4. **Games and fairness:** dated catalogue/provider evidence; include RTP only when the exact game/version and source support it.
5. **Player protection:** limits, self-exclusion, age/identity checks, complaint route, privacy/data controller and support channels.
6. **Original evaluation:** meaningful pros and cons tied to evidence, user-serving FAQ, limitations and a dated update note. Do not invent customer sentiment or test results.
7. **Images:** licensed/permissioned logo or original art, asset URL, rights holder, permitted use, attribution, retrieval date and factual alt text.
8. **SEO and publication:** unique title/meta/H1, self-canonical, visible affiliate disclosure, safer-gambling note, accurate structured data, relevant internal links and mobile QA.
