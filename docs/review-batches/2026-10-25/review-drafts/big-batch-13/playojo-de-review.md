## Legal eligibility gate
1. Locate the exact domain in the current GGL whitelist.
2. Record legal operator, product category, first/renewal authorisation dates and geographic scope.
3. For online casino table games, separately confirm the relevant state-level concession and state scope; GGL explains these are handled by the responsible state gambling authorities, not the GGL itself.
4. Confirm the operator's own homepage displays the required permission statement and issuing authority.
5. If the entry is for virtual slots only, do not label the brand a fully licensed online casino without clear qualification. If scope cannot be proven, HOLD or EXCLUDE.
