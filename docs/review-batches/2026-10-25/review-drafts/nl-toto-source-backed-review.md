The Ksa's 17 September 2026 announcement names TOTO Online B.V. (TOTO and Winnitt) among eight operators granted a follow-up remote-gambling licence for 1 October 2026 through 30 September 2031, including casino games against the licence holder and peer-to-peer casino games. The regulator separately published an enforcement decision concerning advertising directed at young adults; that record should be reviewed and fairly contextualized in any final review.

The Kansspelautoriteit (Ksa) says operators must hold a Dutch remote-gambling licence to offer regulated online gambling. The regulator's licence register and current Kansspelwijzer must be checked against the exact domain and product at publication. A licence for one entity or product does not establish permission for unrelated brands, other jurisdictions or every game type.

## Fair-review requirements

A final review must show the current offer and wagering base, expiry, max bet, game restrictions, payment exclusions and withdrawal interaction, or state that no offer was verified. Payment processing times must be labelled as operator-published unless independently measured. Include meaningful pros and cons, the legal operator, local eligibility, support and complaint routes, privacy details, safer-gambling controls and any relevant regulator decisions. Enforcement history must be described neutrally with the current procedural status; it must not be omitted to protect an affiliate relationship.

Do not invent player sentiment, a payout success rate, test results or a numeric score. Secure permission for logos/screenshots or create original artwork.
