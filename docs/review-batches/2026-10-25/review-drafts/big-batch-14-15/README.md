# Big batches 14–15 — Sweden and Netherlands workbenches

These batches add 12 Sweden-market research workbenches and 12 Netherlands-market research workbenches. Brand variants may overlap earlier market dossiers, so do not count these files as 24 new unique brands.

Regulatory starting points:
- Sweden: [Spelinspektionen](https://www.spelinspektionen.se/en/)
- Netherlands: [Kansspelautoriteit](https://kansspelautoriteit.nl/)

For every candidate, capture the exact current legal entity, local licence status, product scope and approved domain. Then capture current offer terms, cashier/payment rules, player protection, privacy/support, image rights and editorial review. A regulator homepage is only a starting point, not proof of any candidate's eligibility.

All workbenches remain HOLD / NOINDEX / NOT RATED. These are research templates, not finished consumer reviews. No production pages or deployment resulted from these batches.
