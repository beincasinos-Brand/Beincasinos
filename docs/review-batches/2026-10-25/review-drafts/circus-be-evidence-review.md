## 3. Deposits, withdrawals and payment transparency
Capture the local cashier and written payment policy. For each method, record currency, minimum and maximum deposit/withdrawal, fees, stated processing window, external bank/provider delays, verification rules and any requirement to withdraw through the deposit method.

Do not state “instant withdrawals” or a typical payout time without test evidence. For any permitted hands-on test, maintain a dated log of method, amount band, account verification state, request time, approval time, receipt time, support interactions and unexpected fees. Keep operator-stated processing time separate from measured end-to-end time; disclose sample size and exceptions.

## 7. Safer gambling, privacy and account controls

Never frame gambling as a source of income; include adult-only and responsible-gambling context.
