# Score calculation protocol — internal, not a brand score

Do not copy a competitor's score, infer a score from reputation, or fill unknown categories with guessed values. A published score must be reproducible from dated evidence and checked by a second editor.

## Score calculation
Use the approved 100-point rubric:
- Legal eligibility and operator identity: 25
- Bonus fairness: 15
- Payments and withdrawals: 15
- Games and fairness: 10
- Support and complaints: 10
- Usability and accessibility: 10
- Privacy and account security: 10
- Evidence freshness: 5

Each category receives a 0–5 evidence-backed rating. Weighted points = (category rating / 5) × category weight. If a critical legal eligibility check is unresolved, set status HOLD and do not publish a total even if other categories are scored. If a category lacks evidence, mark it PENDING, not zero. Do not treat PENDING as a numerical value.

## What evidence is needed
- Legal: current official regulator register, exact operator, exact domain, exact product category and target market.
- Bonus: live offer page and full terms captured with timestamp; eligibility, wagering, expiry, max bet, cashout cap and excluded games.
- Payments: current cashier/policy, fees and limits, KYC terms; any measured payout needs a dated test record with method, sample size and timeline.
- Games: eligible-market catalogue and source for any RTP claim.
- Support: dated inquiry, channel, question, response time and answer quality.
- UX/accessibility: device/browser, task checklist, observed defects and reproducible steps.
- Privacy/security: current privacy policy and account controls, with factual observations.
- Freshness: source date, last checked date, and second-editor confirmation.

## Internal illustration only
If all categories are genuinely rated, category ratings of 4, 3, 3, 4, 3, 4, 4, 5 would produce weighted points of 20 + 9 + 9 + 8 + 6 + 8 + 8 + 5 = 73/100. **This is a made-up arithmetic example, not a rating for any casino. Do not publish it as a real brand score.**

## Consumer-facing labels
- **NOT RATED:** evidence incomplete.
- **HOLD:** legal/product-scope or critical safety evidence unresolved.
- **EXCLUDE:** verified ineligibility or serious disqualifying issue, with documented rationale.
- **RATED:** complete evidence pack and second-editor sign-off.

## Release checklist
A score may be shown only after a dated evidence pack is saved, all critical gates pass, the math is checked, and the final visible review explains the score and limitations. No AggregateRating structured data unless a genuine, visible, supported rating is present.
