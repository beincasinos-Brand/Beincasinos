## Evidence quality standard

Prefer current primary sources for licensing, operator terms, payment policies, bonus rules, game rules and responsible-gambling controls. Use independent secondary sources for context, not as a substitute for regulator evidence. Cite material claims close to the claim. Preserve a date checked because terms and permissions can change.
