# BeInCasinos — SEO, visual asset and page QA checklist

## Search quality
- The page answers a distinct market-specific query; it is not a thin location swap of another page.
- Title communicates brand + market + review intent without stuffing.
- Meta description describes the actual page and does not promise winnings or safety guarantees.
- Intro states the evidence-check date, operator/domain scope and key legal limitation.
- Heading structure follows user tasks: eligibility, terms, payments, games, protection, support, verdict.
- Every material claim has a source note or dated hands-on test log.
- Add original comparison tables only where their fields are verified and useful.
- Include author/reviewer, editorial policy, corrections contact, affiliate disclosure and responsible-gambling information.
- Do not create doorway pages for markets where the product is unavailable or not authorised.
- Do not publish multiple near-identical pages solely to capture country keyword variants.
- No fabricated testimonials, review counts, ratings, licence claims, awards, or test results.
- Internal links connect to relevant explainers and market hubs; avoid orphan pages.
- Canonical points to the approved URL; XML sitemap includes only indexable, approved pages.
- Structured data matches visible content. Do not add aggregateRating/reviewCount/Offer fields unless genuinely supported and eligible.
- Search Console monitoring: indexing status, canonical selection, sitemap errors, impressions/clicks, CTR, and query/page cannibalisation.

## Review score transparency
- Display a numeric score only after all critical checks pass.
- Show the weighted score breakdown and what each dimension measures.
- Include evidence confidence, last verified date and known limitations.
- A regulator record proves only the facts in that record; it does not establish payout speed or customer-service quality.
- Keep a distinction between operator claims, official register data, independent reporting and BeInCasinos hands-on tests.
- Record who checked the score and who independently reviewed it.

## Image/visual asset workflow
1. Prefer original brand-provided media where terms permit editorial use, commissioned art, or properly licensed stock.
2. Record asset URL, rights holder, licence/permission, permitted uses, attribution requirement, download date and expiry/revocation conditions.
3. Do not scrape or hotlink competitor images, review screenshots or copyrighted game art without permission.
4. Never place unverified bonus amounts, ratings or “licensed” claims into image text.
5. Use a rights-cleared logo only when its use is allowed; avoid implying endorsement.
6. Use meaningful alt text; decorative art gets empty alt text in the implementation.
7. Export responsive sizes, modern formats where supported, and compress without damaging legibility.
8. Check crop, contrast, mobile layout, image loading and layout shift.
9. Do not use a generic stock photo as proof of a casino’s games, software providers, licence or offer.
10. Keep an asset inventory so revoked permissions can be handled.

## Review page visual hierarchy
- Header: brand + market + review title.
- Legal status: clear status and date, no badge that implies approval without evidence.
- Score: only when approved; link to transparent breakdown.
- Bonus: only current verified offer; terms and last-checked date prominent.
- Quick facts: operator, regulator, payment limits and review date.
- Evidence-led pros/cons.
- Detailed review sections and sourced FAQs.
- Affiliate disclosure near commercial links; safer-gambling note visible.

## Mobile and accessibility QA
- Test common narrow viewport sizes and Android browser.
- Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic headings and readable link labels.
- Sufficient text/background contrast and no information conveyed by colour alone.
- Tap targets and accordions usable on touch screens.
- No sticky commercial UI obscures terms/disclosures.
- Verify page speed, image payload, layout shift, broken links and redirects.
- Check language and currency are appropriate to the target market.

## Release gates
Do not publish/index if any of these are unresolved: legal eligibility, exact operator/domain, current material terms, misleading score, unlicensed image, missing affiliate disclosure, duplicate/thin copy, or missing independent editor sign-off.
