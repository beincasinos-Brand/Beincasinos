# BeInCasinos — editorial independence and update policy

## Editorial vs commercial
- Affiliate commissions or future listing fees do not determine the score, verdict or organic ranking.
- Paid placements are labelled and visually separated from independent rankings.
- The commercial team cannot edit a score; all changes require evidence and an editorial audit trail.
- No casino can buy a positive verdict, remove a substantiated criticism or skip legal checks.

## Review authorship
Every approved review should show the reviewer, independent editor, last checked date, market scope, evidence limitations and a link to the scoring method. AI-assisted research or drafting does not replace human primary-source verification.

## Update triggers
Recheck immediately after licence changes, regulator action, domain changes, new bonus terms, material payment/withdrawal changes, repeated credible complaints or a failed test. Otherwise schedule regular review based on traffic and risk.
For each update, record previous claim, new evidence, change, editor and timestamp. Do not silently change a score.

## Corrections
Provide a correction route. Review disputed factual claims against primary evidence, document the decision and amend the score only if the evidence affects scoring. Retain an internal history of material corrections.

## Safer gambling
Do not describe gambling as a source of income, encourage chasing losses or promote a product to a person known to be self-excluded. Use market-appropriate age/legal notices and relevant support information.
