# Batch 103 — Twelve-market review approval matrix

**Status: working checklist; no market is pre-approved.** This matrix prevents a brand being treated as globally eligible just because it has a licence in one country. Each row must be completed with current primary-source evidence for the exact consumer product, legal operator and domain before publication.

## Required evidence fields for every candidate
- Consumer brand, official domain, product category, legal contracting operator and parent group.
- Intended target market and, where relevant, subnational jurisdiction.
- Regulator register or official whitelist URL, date checked, matching operator/domain/product and result.
- Official terms URL, promotion URL or explicit “no verified offer”, check date, eligibility and material restrictions.
- Payments/withdrawal evidence: supported methods, minimum/maximum limits, fees and processing terms where published.
- Complaints and escalation route, safer-gambling tools and support information, privacy/cookie policy.
- Image source, asset URL, permission/licence or a designed non-infringing fallback.
- Source notes, evidence gaps, reviewer, score calculation, affiliate disclosure and editorial sign-off.

## Market-specific release gates

| Market | Primary verification path to document | Mandatory caution before approval |
|---|---|---|
| Australia (AU) | Current official federal/state/territory regulatory guidance relevant to the exact product and operator | Do not assume offshore online casino services are lawful or available because a sportsbook or land-based venue exists. |
| Canada (CA) | Province/territory regulator or official government operator list for the target province | Record province explicitly; availability and legal framework differ. Do not label a brand “Canada-wide” based on one province. |
| Great Britain (GB) | UK Gambling Commission public register and exact licensed entity/domain/product | Confirm the exact domain and product, not just a similarly named company or brand. |
| United States (US) | Official regulator for the named state and the approved operator/skin/product list | State-specific only. A national brand or tribal/retail presence does not establish online casino eligibility in another state. |
| Switzerland (CH) | ESBK and, where relevant, cantonal/official approved online casino information | Verify the actual licensed Swiss online casino and domain; a foreign licence is not a substitute. |
| Germany (DE) | Gemeinsame Glücksspielbehörde der Länder (GGL) official whitelist/register and product permissions | Verify the exact domain, operator and authorised product type; avoid inferring casino approval from a different gambling vertical. |
| Netherlands (NL) | Kansspelautoriteit (KSA) official licensee register and authorised domain/product | Confirm exact domain and current permission; do not promote unlicensed offers as locally legal. |
| Sweden (SE) | Spelinspektionen official licensee register and permitted product | Confirm the named operator and consumer domain; capture applicable Swedish terms and safer-gambling tools. |
| Spain (ES) | Dirección General de Ordenación del Juego (DGOJ) official operator/licence information | Match domain, operator and the authorised online product category; keep copy aligned with Spanish requirements. |
| Italy (IT) | Agenzia delle Dogane e dei Monopoli (ADM) official concession/domain information | Confirm the currently authorised domain and concession; do not infer legality from an international brand name. |
| France (FR) | Autorité Nationale des Jeux (ANJ) official guidance on permitted categories and authorised operators | Do not describe online casino games as locally licensed where the category is not authorised; distinguish permitted online verticals. |
| Belgium (BE) | Belgian Gaming Commission official licence and domain lists for the relevant class/product | Match the online domain, operator and applicable licence class; check local restrictions before presenting an offer. |

## Portfolio-wide decisions
1. A regulator search result is evidence to inspect, not automatic approval. Save the exact source and the conclusion.
2. If evidence is missing, contradictory, stale or cannot be matched to the exact domain/operator, set status to **BLOCKED / NEEDS VERIFICATION** and withhold numeric score.
3. If the product is not legally authorised for the intended market, do not publish a promotional review targeting that market. Re-scope or remove the candidate.
4. Do not copy bonuses across countries or domains. Store exact conditions and the checked date; if no current official offer can be confirmed, say so plainly.
5. Use only cleared brand assets or a bespoke editorial fallback. Publicly accessible images are not automatically reusable.
6. Distinguish documented facts from actual hands-on testing. Never claim account creation, deposits, withdrawals or support testing unless records exist.
7. Keep affiliate relationships visible and editorial scores independent of commercial terms.
8. Only approved pages may enter the review hub, internal link graph and sitemap. No bulk indexing request for drafts.

This file standardises the market approval process for all 100 candidates. It does not assert that any specific brand has passed a gate, and it does not change the sitemap or trigger deployment.
