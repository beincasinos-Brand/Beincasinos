## 1. One scoring system for all reviews

Award points only after evidence is captured for the exact market, consumer domain, product and contracting entity. Record the source URL, access date, finding and reviewer initials beside each score. N/A is allowed only where a feature genuinely does not apply; it must not conceal missing research.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Licence, operator and market eligibility | 20 | Current regulator record, exact domain, legal entity and casino-product permission |
| Payments and withdrawals | 18 | Current methods, currencies, limits, fees, processing caveats and verification requirements |
| Player safety and controls | 15 | Age/identity controls, limits, self-exclusion, reality checks and help |
| Casino product and games | 12 | Current catalogue/provider evidence, product scope and sample checks |
| Bonus fairness and clarity | 10 | Offer page plus full conditions, eligibility, wagering, expiry, max cashout and exclusions |
| Complaints and dispute handling | 10 | Complaint process, escalation/ADR or regulator route, contact availability and enforcement context |
| Usability and accessibility | 8 | Documented mobile/desktop checks, keyboard spot checks, navigation, legibility and error states |
| Privacy and account data | 4 | Current privacy/cookie notices, data controller and relevant disclosures |
| Evidence quality and recency | 3 | Dated primary-source bundle, reproducible findings and second-editor review |
| **Total** | **100** | **No points awarded merely because a URL exists.** |

### Score-to-rating conversion
- 90–100: 4.5–5.0 / 5, only if mandatory eligibility and safety gates pass.
- 80–89: 4.0–4.4 / 5.
- 70–79: 3.5–3.9 / 5.
- 60–69: 3.0–3.4 / 5.
- Below 60: below 3.0 / 5, with weaknesses explained.
- Not scored: any required criterion is unassessed, evidence is stale, or an eligibility/safety gate is unresolved.

The star rating must be derived from the final total. Affiliate commission, commercial relationships and paid placement must never affect the score.

## 2. Image quality and rights register

For every image, record exact source URL, retrieval date, asset owner/licence or permission, intended use, local asset path, format, dimensions, file size, alt text, cropping permissions and attribution requirements.

**Fallback:** an original neutral casino-review illustration or typographic tile created for BeInCasinos, clearly labelled as editorial artwork—not an official logo, real casino photograph or endorsement. Do not hotlink random image-search results or competitors' assets. Prefer optimized local WebP/AVIF with dimensions reserved to avoid layout shift. Use meaningful alt text; decorative artwork should have empty alt text.

Image QA:

## 4. Required sections in every final review

1. At-a-glance card: exact market, official domain, operator, earned score/rating only, review date and eligibility caveat.
2. Verdict: who the product may suit and material limitations; no blanket “best” or “safe” claims.
3. Licence and eligibility: official regulator link and territorial/product limits.
4. Games/providers: current first-party evidence and documented sample methodology; no invented counts.
5. Bonus and terms: dated offer plus full conditions, wagering, expiry, eligibility and exclusions.
6. Deposits/withdrawals: methods, currencies, limits, fees, verification and stated processing windows.
7. Safety/support: age requirement, limits, self-exclusion, complaint route, support and regulator escalation.
8. Privacy/accessibility: concise findings and links to current policy.
9. Pros/cons: each supported by evidence.
10. Methodology and commercial disclosure: weights, evidence date, testing limitations, affiliate disclosure and corrections contact.
11. FAQ: useful questions answered from evidence, not keyword filler.
12. Image provenance: internal rights record; no unsupported trust badges.
