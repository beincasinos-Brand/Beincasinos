## 2. Regulatory settlement — 25 March 2026

The Commission recorded a £26,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty, plus Commission costs and publication of a statement of facts. The review found failings in:
- AML/CTF risk assessment and controls (LC 12.1.1); and
- remote customer interaction / social responsibility controls (SRCP 3.4.3).

The public statement describes an AML risk assessment that omitted relevant risks and was not demonstrably reviewed annually; over-reliance on financial triggers; insufficiently detailed enhanced due diligence; gaps in identifying vulnerability and behavioural harm indicators; untimely customer interactions; insufficient tailoring/documentation of actions; and inadequate evaluation of intervention effectiveness. The statement also says the operator put an action plan in place, cooperated and accepted failings at an early stage. Include both the findings and the stated mitigation; do not portray the settlement as a revocation.

## 3. Consumer-facing review status

This file is an evidence dossier, not a completed hands-on review. Before writing a consumer verdict, independently verify:
- current operator identity in the site's own terms and footer;
- registration and account controls, including deposit/loss/time limits;
- current bonus terms, wagering, eligible games, expiry and withdrawal restrictions;
- payment methods, minimums/maximums, fees and published withdrawal times;
- identity checks, complaint route and ADR details;
- current game catalogue and supplier availability;
- safer-gambling tools and the operator's current interaction procedures where publicly evidenced;
- independent complaint evidence and date-stamped user feedback;
- a second-editor review of every claim and score.

Do not invent test deposits, withdrawals, customer support contacts, game counts, ratings or payment speed. If a function cannot be tested, label it “not independently tested.”
