The Netherlands has a strict national licensing regime. Every brand/domain must be checked against the Dutch Gambling Authority (Kansspelautoriteit, Ksa) register immediately before publication or outreach. A licence belongs to a legal operator and does not automatically authorize every brand/domain or other markets.

## Qualification rules

1. Search the Ksa register by **operator, brand, domain, and product**. Save the date checked and a direct record link.
2. Keep a brand separate from its licence holder. Record both.
3. Do not call a rebrand a new operator; do not call a supplier partnership a launch.
4. Record regulatory history and responsible-gambling requirements separately from licensing status.
5. Paid listing status must be recorded as **Unknown** until an official commercial response, rate card, media kit, or other direct evidence confirms it.
6. Do not imply Dutch permission means permission in the UK, Australia, US, France, Germany, or Switzerland.

## Outreach qualification

Before contacting the brand, verify the legal operator and use an official partnership, media, or corporate contact. Ask whether paid directory listings or sponsored profiles are offered, request current commercial terms and approved markets, and state that payment cannot buy a positive review or bypass legal/editorial checks.
