## Batch conclusion

- Official source entry points were validated, but no named casino has been marked licensed from a landing page alone.
- UKGC, KSA and GGL source descriptions reinforce the need to match the exact entity, domain, status and product permission.
- ACMA's register concerns interactive wagering providers; it must not be treated as blanket authorisation for online casino products.
- The DGOJ URL recorded previously in English is stale/404; use the current operator section above as a starting point, not as proof of an individual licence.
