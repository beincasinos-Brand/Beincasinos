**Purpose:** Turn the owner's ambition to compete with Casino.org and leading casino-review publishers into an evidence-led product roadmap. This is a working benchmark, not a definitive market ranking, independent audit, or claim that BeInCasinos currently matches established publishers.

## Executive direction

BeInCasinos should compete on a distinct proposition: **market-specific operator verification + transparent review evidence + unusually clear commercial disclosures + practical tools + documented change monitoring**. Do not imitate competitor copy, layouts, logos, proprietary scoring, or unverified audience claims.

The incumbent sites have material advantages in brand recognition, established audiences, teams, first-hand testing, player feedback, complaint systems, and update operations. A new static site cannot honestly claim parity until these capabilities exist and are evidenced.

## Non-negotiable product capabilities

### 1. Evidence-backed operator profiles
Each publishable profile needs exact brand domain, legal entity, regulator record, product permission, target territory, official terms, withdrawal information, complaints/support route, safer-gambling information, dated sources, reviewer notes, and unresolved questions. A regulator record for a parent company or a different domain must not be represented as direct proof for a brand without the relationship being evidenced.

### 2. A hard eligibility gate before a score
Do not let a high usability or bonus score compensate for failed or unresolved licensing and market eligibility. If the gate fails, withhold the score and explain why. Country, state, product, and advertising restrictions need separate treatment. Australia and France need special restrictions review; US coverage must be state/product-specific.

### 3. Real testing, honestly labelled
Create an internal testing record for account registration, deposits, bonuses, withdrawals, support, mobile experience, and responsible-gambling tools. Until a real test is performed, label the field “not tested” or “operator-stated”; never write “tested” based on desk research alone. Never ask reviewers to gamble beyond approved legal/compliance and risk controls.

### 4. Maintenance, not just publication
Use a dated review history, next-check date, correction route, change log for licence/ownership/terms/withdrawal changes, and a defined escalation path for material complaints. Automate reminders only after the data and operating process exist.

### 5. Useful tools and content depth
Prioritise genuinely helpful tools: bonus wagering calculator with visible assumptions, terms checklist, licence verification guide, market-eligibility explainer, payment/withdrawal comparison, and glossary. Every tool must be tested for edge cases, keyboard use, mobile layout, and clear limitations.

### 6. Transparent commercial model
Potential revenue streams to research:
- Fixed fee for a defined, visibly labelled sponsored listing or advertising inventory.
- CPA / revenue share under an affiliate contract, where allowed.
- Hybrid fixed fee plus performance commission, where allowed.
- Sponsored educational content only with conspicuous labels and editorial safeguards.

No model is live until the owner approves the contract terms, market scope, privacy/tracking design, measurement/reporting, disclosure, and legal review. Affiliate partnership evidence does not prove willingness to buy a paid listing. No commercial payment may buy a score, licence conclusion, positive review, or guaranteed organic ranking.

## Prioritised roadmap

**P0 — launch integrity**
- Resolve brand/name clearance and publisher/legal identity.
- Finish market-specific advertising/affiliate review; select the first legally supportable market(s) rather than assuming all seven can launch at once.
- Confirm monitored contact, privacy/cookie implementation, hosting commercial terms, and owner launch approval.
- Keep crawlers blocked until approved launch.

**P1 — review product**
- Create one reusable profile template with structured source citations and evidence states.
- Add visible last-checked date, operator identity, market eligibility status, criteria scores, limitations, and corrections link.
- Publish only profiles that pass all eligibility checks; retain research-only candidates privately.

**P2 — audience value**
- Build original guides, comparison pages, tools and FAQs around specific reader questions.
- Ensure every page has a distinct purpose and non-duplicative content; no mass-generated thin brand pages.
- Add analytics only after privacy and consent review; build a real dated baseline before pitching audience reach.

**P3 — commercial readiness**
- Validate commercial demand with authorised contacts and record explicit responses.
- Produce a media kit from real reports only.
- Define listing, CPA, revshare and hybrid contract templates; specify territory, permitted assets, disclosures, link attributes, attribution window, payment, cancellation, disputes, and audit/reporting.
- Accept no money and publish no paid placement until gates are approved.

## Scorecard for internal quarterly review

Track actual evidence, not vanity claims:
- Share of public operator profiles with current official primary-source evidence.
- Share of material claims with source and check date.
- Age of last verification and overdue reviews.
- Correction acknowledgement and resolution time.
- Number of profiles withheld due to unresolved eligibility.
- Tool completion/error rates if lawfully measured.
- Organic impressions/clicks and engaged visits from dated analytics, once available.
- Commercial enquiries, qualified conversations, signed deals, and realised revenue—kept distinct from prospects and affiliate-program existence.
- Complaints, corrections, privacy incidents, and regulatory changes.
