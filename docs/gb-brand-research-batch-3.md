## Limitations and release decision

This batch is not a consumer test, complete legal audit, bonus/withdrawal test, or quality ranking. No hands-on testing or customer outcome has been claimed. A regulator's licence summary is only one part of the evidence; it does not establish product availability in other jurisdictions or guarantee customer experience.

**Publication approved from this batch: 0.** The research value here includes identifying historic surrendered licences and preventing a brand from being presented as currently licensed without evidence. Keep public review pages unpublished until all evidence and market eligibility gates pass.
