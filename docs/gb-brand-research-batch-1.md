## Primary source interpretation

The UKGC's [business register](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses) says its domain names and trading names are provided by gambling businesses and that it cannot guarantee their accuracy. It defines licence-status fields and makes business, trading-name, domain and licence datasets available. The register should therefore be treated as essential primary evidence, but not as a substitute for matching the exact record and checking current terms or player experience.
