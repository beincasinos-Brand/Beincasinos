# Paid placement order form and contract checklist — internal draft

**Status:** Commercial operations template only. Not a binding contract, legal advice, or an offer to any operator. Do not send, sign, invoice, or accept payment using this draft until qualified counsel adapts it to the publisher entity, governing law, tax/invoicing position, market, product, privacy terms, and advertising rules.

## Gate before an order is offered

- [ ] Publisher legal identity and invoice/payment details are confirmed.
- [ ] Relevant market/product/operator/domain passed the market compliance gate and has dated primary-source evidence.
- [ ] Name/trademark and public publisher details are cleared for launch.
- [ ] Site is publicly launched with real, measured audience evidence; proposed placement and report are deliverable.
- [ ] Privacy/data flow, cookie/tracking, and disclosure controls are approved.
- [ ] The proposed fee and deliverables are approved by the owner; any rate remains a test, not a guaranteed ROI claim.
- [ ] Legal review approves the final agreement, tax terms, cancellation/refund wording, liability/indemnity, dispute resolution, and electronic signature process.

If any required gate is incomplete, do not offer or activate the placement.

### Compliance and editorial independence
- Operator warrants that it has authority to contract and that supplied claims, licences, domains, terms, logos and creative are accurate and may be used; final warranties require legal review.
- The operator must disclose material changes to licence, legal entity, domain, product, bonus/withdrawal terms, market permissions, enforcement, or responsible-gambling controls promptly.
- Publisher may pause or remove a placement if evidence is stale, a regulator or legal issue arises, a claim is unsupported, the offer becomes unavailable/unlawful, or a disclosure/privacy safeguard fails.
- Payment does not affect editorial eligibility, review score, factual conclusions, safer-gambling language, organic ranking or inclusion in an editorial top list.
- The operator cannot require suppression of substantiated factual criticism or control the publisher's independent conclusions.
- Claims and creatives must comply with the rules of each targeted market, age/vulnerability protections, and responsible-gambling requirements. The exact obligations require market-specific legal review.
- All tracking, personal-data processing, cookies, audience sharing, and reporting must be described and approved before activation; no personal player data is to be shared unless separately lawful and contractually approved.
- Publisher and operator contacts, security incident notifications, correction requests, and removal process: **[record]**.

### Cancellation, termination and disputes
Counsel must draft the actual clauses for cancellation, pro-rata/refund treatment, renewal/auto-renewal, cure periods, immediate suspension, termination, unpaid invoices, removal of assets/links, post-termination tracking reconciliation, liability limits, indemnities, force majeure, governing law, dispute forum, notices, and survival. Do not improvise these terms in an email.

## Hard stop rules

1. Never backdate an agreement or claim that an operator is a partner before signature.
2. Never accept payment for a market or product that has not passed the documented eligibility gate.
3. Never activate a tracking link before programme terms, privacy, consent and disclosure requirements are approved.
4. Never sell editorial influence, a positive score, a licence badge, “safe” status, or guaranteed traffic/conversions.
5. Never fabricate metrics, partner references, test results, invoices, tax details or legal approval.
6. If a legal or regulator status is unclear, set the order to **HOLD** and do not publish.
