## Scope and controls

- The DGE site list is the authoritative starting point for New Jersey internet-gaming site approval. This list must be rechecked immediately before publication; a current page fetch is not a permanent authorisation guarantee.
- The DGE list groups consumer sites under land-based casino permit holders and affiliate relationships. Do not infer that all brands in a corporate group share the same permit, product scope, domain approval, or consumer terms.
- The register intentionally includes casino and poker-facing brands/sites only. Sportsbook-only offerings are outside this register's review scope unless a separate sports-betting review is created with its own scope.
- The permit-holder PDF identifies nine listed internet-gaming permit-holder rows, including separate Caesars-affiliate relationships for Boardwalk Regency, Harrah's and Tropicana. A parent casino licence or a brand name alone is not sufficient proof of a particular website's approval.
- No account creation, deposit, withdrawal, game, customer-support, or safer-gambling-control testing is claimed. No consumer offer, payout timing, safety ranking or score is approved.
- Before approval, capture exact site/domain and legal entity; current general and NJ-specific terms; offer-specific terms if applicable; deposits/withdrawals, fees, KYC and limits; privacy/cookies; safer-gambling controls; complaints/dispute path; official enforcement history; reproducible UX observations only if actually performed; affiliate disclosure; independent second-editor review; and legal/editorial sign-off.
- Keep this document internal. It is a discovery register, not public advice to gamble and not a substitute for a legal review.
