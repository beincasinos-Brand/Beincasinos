## Scope and counting rules

ADM's register lists remote-gambling concessionaires and associated consumer sites. A listing is only a first-stage authorisation signal: it does not by itself prove the exact site currently offers a house-banked casino product. Confirm the exact concession, current legal entity/domain and permitted game categories before counting a brand. Do not count betting-only, poker-only, bingo-only, multiple domains under one licensee, or brands with materially overlapping products as separate casino reviews without a documented editorial reason.

The register is paginated and sortable. Rows below reflect the official ADM register pages found during this research pass; the current record and consumer product must be rechecked immediately before any publication. All candidates remain HOLD.

## Required checks for every brand

1. Open the current ADM record and record concession number, legal entity, associated domain and current status/date.
2. Visit the exact Italy-facing domain and match its legal notice to the ADM record.
3. Confirm the live casino product and the permitted game categories; exclude brands that only offer betting, poker, bingo or lotteries from the casino count.
4. Archive Italy-specific terms, casino rules, privacy/cookies, complaints, account closure and responsible-gambling pages.
5. Verify deposit/withdrawal methods, minimums, fees, limits, verification requirements and processing stages from primary sources; do not state operator estimates as independently tested.
6. Verify bonuses and wagering terms, including expiry, maximum bet, excluded games and withdrawal restrictions.
7. Check self-exclusion, spending/time controls, age verification, customer support and current regulator measures.
8. Record dated source snapshots and have an independent second reviewer confirm the evidence before scoring or publishing.
