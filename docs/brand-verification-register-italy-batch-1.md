## Scope and counting rules

ADM's concessionaire register is the starting point for Italy-facing remote gambling authorisation and lists concessionaire legal entities with associated sites. A listing alone does not complete a casino review: check that the current official site is live, that the concession and relevant casino product are active, and that the brand offers the casino games being reviewed. Do not treat betting-only, poker-only, bingo-only, or multiple sites under one concessionaire as independent casino brands without additional evidence.

The register is paginated and has appeared with multiple sort orders in search results; verify every selected record directly on the current ADM source before publication. Candidate list below is a shortlist for verification, not a certified count of unique, casino-active brands.

## Required checks for every brand

1. Open the current ADM record and record concession number, legal entity, associated domain and current status/date.
2. Visit the exact Italy-facing domain and match its legal notice to the ADM record.
3. Confirm the live casino product is available in Italy and not only betting, poker or bingo.
4. Archive Italy-specific terms, casino rules, privacy/cookies, complaints, account closure and responsible-gambling pages.
5. Verify deposit/withdrawal methods, minimums, fees, limits, verification requirements and processing stages from primary sources; do not state operator estimates as independently tested.
6. Verify bonuses and wagering terms, including expiry, maximum bet, excluded games and withdrawal restrictions.
7. Check self-exclusion, spending/time controls, age verification, customer support and current regulator measures.
8. Have a second reviewer confirm the evidence and freshness before scoring or publishing.
