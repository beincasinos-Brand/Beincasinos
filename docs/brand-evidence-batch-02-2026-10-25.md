## Google quality gate

Google's people-first guidance asks whether pages offer original research/analysis, comprehensive information, trustworthy sourcing and clear authorship; it warns against mass-produced pages that add little value. It specifically notes that reviews are more trustworthy when the process is explained and test results are supported with evidence. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

Implication for BeInCasinos:
1. Do not label a desk-researched profile “hands-on tested.”
2. Display “not verified” for bonuses and payment performance until evidence is captured.
3. Record a dated source, exact domain, operator entity, market and product scope for every licensing claim.
4. Keep scores unassigned if any weighted criterion or eligibility gate is unassessed.
5. Add a transparent methodology, author/editor identity and correction policy before opening indexing.
6. Avoid publishing 100 near-identical pages that only restate operators' own terms; each review needs genuinely useful comparative analysis.
