## 2. Terms and welcome offer

The official terms page shows an effective date of **16 December 2025**. The current welcome offer advertises a £30 slots bonus and 50 free spins after a new player selects the offer and makes a first deposit of at least £15. The published offer terms state:
- the offer is for new customers aged 18+ who can view it in the account's Promos section;
- Paysafecard deposits do not qualify;
- bonus funds and bonus wins are restricted to selected games;
- the qualifying £15 deposit plus £30 bonus must be wagered 10 times before bonus-balance winnings can be withdrawn, which the terms calculate as £450 total wagering;
- claiming the offer excludes eligibility for other Regal Wins welcome offers.

The homepage also advertises 10x wagering and a £50 maximum win. Capture the complete live offer terms to verify expiry, game contribution, maximum conversion, and any further restrictions before publication.
