# Paid-listing sales pipeline (internal template)

Use this tracker only after the site has real, reportable audience evidence and the owner approves commercial outreach. Do not populate it with guessed contacts or send bulk unsolicited messages. Follow applicable anti-spam/privacy rules and each operator's official contact preferences.

## Qualification gate before outreach
- [ ] Brand and exact operating entity have been researched for the intended market.
- [ ] Current primary-source evidence and last-check date are recorded.
- [ ] The site has a real audience report with dates and limitations.
- [ ] The publisher identity, monitored email, commercial disclosure, offer terms, and privacy notice are ready.
- [ ] The owner has approved the offer and outreach approach.

## Pipeline stages
- `not_contacted`: research only; no message sent.
- `qualified`: suitable candidate, contact route sourced from official channel.
- `draft_ready`: tailored draft reviewed; no message sent yet.
- `contacted`: a legitimate, relevant first message was sent and date recorded.
- `follow_up_due`: follow-up is permitted and due; respect opt-outs and reasonable frequency.
- `replied_interested`: a real reply explicitly indicates interest; preserve the date and source.
- `qualified_buyer`: the relevant decision-maker confirms the paid-listing use case is relevant.
- `in_discussion`: operator responded and discussion is active.
- `proposal_sent`: written offer and terms sent.
- `won`: agreement signed, invoice/payment recorded as paid or complete, and placement details documented.
- `lost`: declined, unsuitable, or no further contact.
- `do_not_contact`: opt-out, complaint, or compliance reason; never re-contact for sales.

## Commercial integrity
- Listing fees buy clearly labelled placement/inventory only. They do not buy licence verification, a favourable review, “safe” status, or an undisclosed ranking advantage.
- Never promise traffic, clicks, leads, conversions, rankings, or revenue. Quote only measured data with reporting period and methodology.
- Record agreed price, currency, taxes, duration, placement, sponsored label, cancellation/refund terms, link attributes, tracking, invoice status, start/end dates, and reviewer.
- Do not activate a paid placement until agreement, payment terms, disclosures, market eligibility, and publication review are complete.
- Do not add affiliate tracking as part of a listing without separate programme approval and privacy review.

## Review cadence
Review active conversations weekly. Review paid placements before start, at renewal, and when the operator's status or permitted market may have changed. Retain only necessary business contact information and apply a documented retention policy.
