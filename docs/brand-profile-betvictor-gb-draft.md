# BetVictor (Great Britain) — evidence dossier / draft

**Status:** Research in progress — HOLD. Not an approved consumer review, not a recommendation, and not counted among the 100 approved reviews.  
**Research date:** 23 October 2026  
**Market scope:** Great Britain only. Do not generalise this assessment to Ireland or other jurisdictions.

## Identity and regulatory match

- **Consumer brand:** BetVictor.
- **Legal operator shown in GB terms:** BV Gaming Limited, registered in Gibraltar (company number 42734).
- **UK Gambling Commission account:** 39576.
- **Official register status:** the UKGC licence summary currently lists active remote licences for casino, bingo, gambling software, real-event betting and virtual-event betting.
- **Domain match:** UKGC lists `betvictor.com` and `betvictor.mobi` as active domains for account 39576.
- **Trading-name match:** UKGC lists `betvictor` as an active trading name.
- **Jurisdiction warning:** the same legal entity has other trading names and white-label domains in the register. Do not treat those brands/domains as interchangeable with BetVictor or assume their product terms are identical.

## What the current sources establish

1. The GB terms identify BV Gaming Limited as the operator and name UKGC account 39576.
2. The UKGC register currently lists the above remote licence activities as active.
3. The official domain and trading-name records match BetVictor's principal domain and brand.
4. The UKGC regulatory-actions page currently says no regulatory actions have been recorded for this business. This is a narrow statement about that register page at the time checked; it is not a guarantee of service quality or a claim about all regulators and jurisdictions.
5. The GB terms state that identity documents may be required before withdrawals and that access is restricted in certain territories. This is a reason to examine the full current verification and withdrawal rules, not proof that a particular withdrawal will be delayed.
