## 2. Regulatory action — 21 October 2026

The UKGC announced a **£900,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty**, Commission costs and publication of a statement of facts. The review covered failings between October 2024 and October 2025 across fair terms/consumer treatment, withdrawal processing, AML/CTF controls, social responsibility/customer interaction and technical standards.

The Commission reported that:
- a term restricted customers to one pending withdrawal at a time until it was authorised, which the regulator considered unfair;
- some customers were asked for further information at withdrawal even though it could reasonably have been requested earlier;
- AML controls leaned heavily on financial thresholds and sampling rather than sufficiently risk-based profiles;
- safer-gambling indicators and customer-interaction processes were not consistently applied across the operator's websites;
- testing of financial-limit functionality was not effective in some cases.

The regulator's statement says the operator amended the withdrawal process so withdrawals are no longer withheld unless a regulatory or legal requirement applies, and created a standalone Great Britain MLTF risk assessment. These remediation statements must be attributed to the regulator's published account; do not imply a current independent audit has verified every control.
