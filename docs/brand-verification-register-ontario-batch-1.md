- Treat each exact Ontario-facing domain and legal operator as a separate verification unit. If a brand also appears in another market's register, do not automatically count it as another distinct brand. A market edition can count separately only where the legal entity, applicable terms, permitted products or consumer conditions materially differ and the page clearly states its territory.
- No hands-on account, deposit, withdrawal, game, support or control testing is claimed in this register.
- Do not publish a ranking, score, “best” claim, bonus amount, withdrawal promise, or safety endorsement from this register.
- Before approval, capture the current Ontario terms, welcome-offer terms where relevant, payment methods and limits, withdrawal rules, verification/KYC, privacy/cookies, safer-gambling controls, complaint route, relevant enforcement history, and dated screenshots/exports. Complete an independent second review and legal/editorial sign-off.
