## Market boundary

This register is for Ontario only, not all of Canada. iGaming Ontario's official operator directory said its listing was accurate as of 1 September 2026 and showed 49 operators and 84 gaming websites when checked. Players must be physically located in Ontario to play. A site being available elsewhere in Canada does not establish Ontario authorization.
