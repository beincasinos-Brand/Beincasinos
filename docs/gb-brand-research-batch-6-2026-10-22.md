## 1. QuinnBet (Gibraltar) Limited — settlement dated 20 August 2026

Primary source: UK Gambling Commission announcement:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/quinnbet-gibraltar-limited-to-pay-gbp609-104-for-regulatory-failures

The Commission says QuinnBet (Gibraltar) Limited, which runs quinnbet.com, agreed to pay £609,104 as part of a settlement following an investigation into anti-money-laundering and social-responsibility failures. The Commission's summary includes weaknesses in controls for customers aged 18–24 and failures to identify and flag potential signs of gambling harm in a timely way.

## 2. Evolution Malta Holding Limited — supplier and unlicensed-site risk (not an operator review)

Primary source: UK Gambling Commission announcement dated 23 July 2026:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/evolution-malta-holding-limited-to-pay-gbp4-75m

The Commission says Evolution Malta Holding Limited will pay a £4.75 million settlement after an investigation found that games appeared on six websites accessible to Great Britain consumers without the operators holding a Commission licence. The Commission identified shortcomings in the supplier's risk assessment, controls intended to prevent games being supplied to unlicensed operators, and customer due diligence.

This concerns a game supplier and its compliance responsibilities. It is not, by itself, evidence that every licensed casino using Evolution games has committed a breach, nor does it identify every casino site that a publisher should include or exclude.

Editorial handling:
- Do not use a game-provider logo or catalogue as evidence that a casino operator is licensed.
- Verify the casino operator/domain and active permission independently with the UKGC.
- Only attribute a site-specific finding if an official source explicitly connects that site to the case.
- Status: contextual evidence only; does not add a brand to the verified list.
