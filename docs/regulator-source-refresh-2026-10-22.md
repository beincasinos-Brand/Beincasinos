## Sweden — Spelinspektionen

- Official licence register: https://www2.spelinspektionen.se/licens-o-tillstand/licensregister/
- English licence and permit directory: https://www2.spelinspektionen.se/en/licence-and-permit/licence-and-permit-directory/
- The regulator describes this register as listing actors with valid licences/permits and includes web addresses for online-gambling licensees.
- **Candidate rule:** a brand only advances after the active record, licensed legal entity, exact consumer-facing domain and online-casino licence/product scope are confirmed from the live register. A sports-betting licence or a third-party comparison page is not enough.
- **Research status:** candidates only; no brand approval is inferred by this source check.

## Great Britain — Gambling Commission

- Official public register: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/page/public-register
- Official licensees hub: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/licensees-and-businesses/home
- The Commission states its public register covers licensed businesses and includes a separate regulatory-actions register.
- **Candidate rule:** confirm the current operating-licence holder and remote casino scope, map the exact domain to the operator, and inspect the Commission's regulatory actions register. Do not rely on a brand's generic international licensing page alone.
- **Research status:** existing GB candidates/drafts remain HOLD pending all 15 review gates.

## Evidence handling rules

1. Save the source URL, access date, the exact legal entity, licence/permit type, domain, status and any expiry/restriction in the per-brand evidence pack.
2. Prefer the regulator's record for permission and the operator's own current pages for terms; do not use one to substitute for the other.
3. Record material contradictions between the regulator, operator terms and live product. Keep the candidate on HOLD until resolved.
4. Recheck at publication time; regulator registers and operator terms can change.
5. This note records discovery/refresh only. It does not certify any brand, product, bonus, payment method, or consumer experience.
