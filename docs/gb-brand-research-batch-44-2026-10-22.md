# GB brand research batch 44 — Stakemate / Bet Theory (Malta) Limited and Bet Theory UK Ltd
**Checked:** 22 October 2026 (UTC)  
**Status:** internal conflict note; HOLD until operator and terms history is reconciled.

## Official register evidence
The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) register lists **Bet Theory (Malta) Limited**, account **66151**, with active remote bingo, casino, gambling software and real-event betting permissions from 2 May 2025. It lists **www.stakemate.com** as an active domain and the trading name **stakemate** as active. The official actions page currently says no regulatory actions have been recorded for account 66151.

## Material entity conflict
The current indexed Stakemate terms page identifies Bet Theory (Malta) Limited and UKGC account 66151, with a terms version effective 15 September 2025. However, an older page on the same brand domain (stakemate.com/termsconditions) identifies **Bet Theory UK Ltd**, account **60991**, and an older terms version effective 4 June 2024. This may reflect a historic entity/domain transition, but the old page is still discoverable and the relationship must not be guessed. Current live terms, app store disclosures, privacy, customer contract entity and domain/account linkage must be reconciled before publication.

## Consumer-facing terms observations
The indexed current terms state that customer funds are held separate from company funds but are **not protected in the event of insolvency** (described as “not protected segregation”). They describe a complaints path to customer service and referral to IBAS if unresolved after eight weeks, subject to scope and deadlines. The current site also describes deposit limits, reality checks, time-outs and self-exclusion. These are disclosures in the published terms/site, not hands-on tests; archive the current pages and recheck their version before using these statements publicly.
