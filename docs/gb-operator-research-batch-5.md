## Evidence standard

The UK Gambling Commission public register can establish the named legal entity and licence status shown on its page. It does **not**, by itself, establish the correct consumer brand, the exact website, the availability of a product to every market, current terms, payment/withdrawal performance, customer service quality, or a review score. Match the consumer brand and domain to the legal entity using the regulator's domain/trading-name records and the operator's own terms before drafting a public review.

## Publication gate (all must pass)

1. Exact consumer brand, official domain, legal entity and licence record matched with dated sources.
2. Product permission and target-market legality checked for the page's stated jurisdiction; never imply a UK licence authorises play in other countries.
3. Current official terms reviewed for deposits, withdrawals, bonus wagering, identity checks, fees, limits and account closure.
4. Complaint route, safer-gambling controls, self-exclusion and support contacts verified.
5. Regulatory actions and material changes checked; all claims dated and cited.
6. Independent editorial review completed; no invented player experience, withdrawal tests, quotes, ratings or unsupported structured data.
