# BeInCasinos — editorial quality rubric

Use this rubric before approving any casino review. A page must pass the critical legal and factual gates; a high word count cannot compensate for failure.

## Gate A — stop/publish decision
**Automatic HOLD / NOINDEX** if any of these are unresolved:
- Exact operator, domain and casino product eligibility in the target jurisdiction.
- Material offer/payment claims have no current primary source.
- Page implies hands-on tests that were not performed.
- Numeric rating is unsupported or the score arithmetic is missing.
- Image usage rights are unknown.
- Affiliate relationship is undisclosed.
- Page duplicates another brand/domain page without a distinct user purpose.
- Required safer-gambling information is missing.

## Gate B — score quality (0–5 each)
1. **Accuracy:** current primary sources and transparent unknowns.
2. **Specificity:** real brand and market facts, not country-name substitutions.
3. **Decision usefulness:** helps compare eligibility, bonus restrictions, fees, payout policy, support and controls.
4. **Original analysis:** interprets evidence and explains practical consequences; does not paraphrase marketing.
5. **Transparency:** test method, dates, sample size, reviewer and limitations.
6. **Trust and accountability:** editorial independence, affiliate disclosure, corrections process.
7. **Readability/accessibility:** scannable structure, plain language, mobile and keyboard checks.
8. **Technical completeness:** metadata, canonical, internal links, image optimization, sitemap and structured-data QA.
9. **Visual quality:** useful, rights-cleared imagery and consistent cards without misleading badges.
10. **Maintenance readiness:** named evidence owner and recheck trigger/date.

Target: at least 40/50 plus every critical gate passed. Scores are internal QA, not a public casino rating. If the page falls short, return it for revision rather than stuffing extra words into it.

## What a final review must say
- What exactly was reviewed and for which jurisdiction.
- What primary sources prove and what they do not prove.
- What was personally tested, what was not, and why.
- What the current terms mean in practical language.
- What could make the product unsuitable or unavailable.
- How the score was derived and how uncertain inputs were treated.
- Where a reader can get help, complain or self-exclude.
- How commercial links work and whether the publisher may earn a commission.

## What must never appear without proof
- “Guaranteed safe,” “best,” “fastest,” “instant withdrawals,” “fully licensed” without exact scope, “verified by BeInCasinos” without documented verification.
- Invented bonus amounts, payout speeds, customer support experiences, review counts, player testimonials, game counts or RTP claims.
- Fake stars, AggregateRating/reviewCount markup, awards, regulator badges or image permissions.
- A claim that every page will be indexed or that SEO can be 100% guaranteed.

## Competitor-quality comparison without copying
Study competitor page structure and user questions, but create original text, original graphics and an independent scoring system. Useful differentiators include a clearly dated source panel, local domain eligibility, plain-language bonus math, separate operator estimate vs observed payout, limitations, an auditable scorecard and a transparent corrections log. Never copy their prose, screenshots, proprietary card art or scores.
