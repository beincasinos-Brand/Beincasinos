### Regulatory history — material and market-specific
The UKGC action page currently records three financial penalties for **888 UK Limited**:

1. **19 August 2024 — £23,000:** breach of licence condition 6.1.2 concerning credit-card use. The regulator notes the licensee identified and reported the breach, acted to remedy it and cooperated.
2. **7 August 2025 — £33,075:** failure to take all reasonable steps concerning customer identification across accounts held with the licensee and a group company. The regulator notes self-reporting, remedial action and cooperation.
3. **18 November 2025 — £86,843:** breach of the remote self-exclusion social-responsibility code. The regulator says the failure resulted from human error, did not affect controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling, and was followed by remedial action; the licensee self-reported and cooperated.
