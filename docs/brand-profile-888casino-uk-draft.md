### Regulatory history — material disclosure
The UKGC account action page displayed three financial-penalty decisions at the recorded check:
- **19 August 2024 — £23,000:** breach of licence condition 6.1.2 concerning use of credit cards. The regulator noted self-identification/reporting, remedial action and cooperation.
- **7 August 2025 — £33,075:** breach related to remote customer-identification requirements across group-company accounts. The regulator noted self-reporting, remedial action and cooperation.
- **18 November 2025 — £86,843:** breach of SRCP 3.5.3 concerning remote self-exclusion. The regulator noted self-reporting, cooperation and remedial action, and stated the failure was due to human error and did not affect controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling.

Any eventual public treatment must link each primary decision, describe its precise scope, include relevant context and mitigation recorded by the regulator, and avoid implying that a financial penalty automatically means a current licence suspension. Recheck the account's current licence status and each action before publication.

### Withdrawals and protection of funds
The UK customer agreement describes withdrawals as subject to cleared payments, completed verification checks and relevant investigations. It also discusses payment-method thresholds and says the full available balance will be processed even below a threshold, potentially with customer-service assistance. Do not turn written policy into a claim about actual payout speed: no payout test has been conducted.

The agreement describes Great Britain customer funds as held in a designated client account at a stated **medium protection** level and explicitly says repayment in insolvency is not guaranteed. It also says funds temporarily held by a payment provider before reaching that account are not covered by the account during transit. Any summary must retain these qualifications rather than imply a guarantee.

### Safer-gambling tools
The UK-specific responsible-gaming policy describes a control centre, deposit limits, time-spent reminders, self-exclusion, “Take a break” and promotional preferences. These are operator policy descriptions only; this draft has not tested whether each control is accessible or functions correctly in the current account journey.

### Privacy and complaints
The privacy policy names 888 UK Limited as data controller for users of the 888 brand and mentions the UK GamProtect Single Customer View trial. The UK agreement describes a complaints path beginning with customer-service live chat, escalation to the “Second Opinion” team, and possible eCOGRA adjudication if dissatisfied or no final response within eight weeks. Before saying ADR is available for a particular case, confirm the current eCOGRA approved-site listing and the applicable eligibility requirements. Recheck the live privacy notice, cookie controls, support route and escalation links.

### Bonuses and promotional terms
