# GB brand research batch 38 — Lottofy domain and product-scope discrepancy
**Checked:** 2026-10-22 (UTC)  
**Purpose:** internal identity and domain review. Not a completed consumer review or publication approval.

## Executive finding
The UKGC domain register lists **lottofy.co.uk** as Active for **Clobet Limited**, account **100831**. However, the currently indexed `lottofy.co.uk/about` page describes itself as an informational source about the UK National Lottery and explicitly says it is not affiliated with official lottery operators. Meanwhile, `lottofy.com` publishes gambling terms naming Clobet Limited and its Malta Gaming Authority licence. These facts must not be blended into one consumer brand profile without resolving the exact current purpose, ownership and product status of each domain.

## UKGC primary evidence
- UKGC licensee: Clobet Limited, account 100831.
- Official licence summary: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/100831
- Official domain record: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/domain-names/100831 lists `lottofy.co.uk` as Active.
- Official trading-name record to recheck: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/business/detail/trading-names/100831

The UKGC domain register says domain/trading-name data is provided by the licensee and is not guaranteed by the Commission; an Active row is not, by itself, a complete product or editorial verification.

## Consumer-facing domain evidence
- `lottofy.co.uk/about`: https://www.lottofy.co.uk/about currently presents informational content about the UK National Lottery and states it is not affiliated with official lottery operators.
- `lottofy.com` terms: https://www.lottofy.com/en/tos/ identify Clobet Limited as the contracting party for services offered through Lottofy websites and describe a Malta Gaming Authority licence (MGA/B2C/762/2019). The page is dated/updated in 2026 but contains multiple version references; archive the exact live terms and resolve version inconsistencies before any public claims.
- `lottofy.com/about`: https://www.lottofy.com/en/about describes Clobet Limited and an MGA licence. This is a different domain from the UKGC-listed .co.uk address.

## Material discrepancy / risk
Do not assume that the UKGC-listed `lottofy.co.uk` informational site is the same active gambling product as `lottofy.com`. Verify whether the .co.uk domain currently redirects, has changed use, is controlled by the same operator, or is simply a legacy domain entry. The public .co.uk page's disclaimer is inconsistent with treating it as an active consumer gambling brand page without further evidence.

## Publication decision
**Status: HOLD — exact domain purpose, operator relationship and product scope unresolved.** Do not count Lottofy as a verified active consumer casino/lottery brand until:
1. The live .co.uk route and redirect behaviour are checked directly.
2. The UKGC register and current domain control are re-confirmed.
3. The legal footer/terms for each domain are captured separately.
4. The operator explains or primary evidence resolves the informational-site disclaimer and the licence/domain listing.
5. The target market and product legality are verified separately.
6. Full terms, payments/withdrawals, complaints/ADR, support, privacy and safer-gambling controls are reviewed if the domain offers gambling.

No hands-on account, deposit, lottery participation, gameplay, withdrawal, support or complaint test was performed.
