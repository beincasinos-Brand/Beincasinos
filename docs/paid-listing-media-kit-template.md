## Available inventory
For each placement, define exact location, label, duration, links, creative rules, update frequency, and cancellation terms. Any paid listing must display “Paid listing” or “Sponsored” beside the placement.

## Editorial independence
- Fees buy placement/inventory only, not licence verification, a positive review, “safe” status, or undisclosed ranking priority.
- A brand must still pass the same source-based publication checks as non-paying brands.
- Reject unverifiable claims and placements that cannot legally target the relevant market.
- Keep a record of contract, invoice, campaign dates, labels, and review history.

## Six-month review
Do not automatically start charging after six months. Review actual audience evidence, brand demand, operating costs, legal requirements, and editorial capacity. If evidence is weak, continue building the resource instead of promising exposure.
