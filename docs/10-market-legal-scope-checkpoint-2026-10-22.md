# 10-market brand programme — legal market scope and counting rules

**Updated:** 22 October 2026

**Purpose:** Prevent the 100-brand target from turning into promotion of unauthorised casino sites.

## Requested markets and feasibility

| Market | What may count toward a casino-review target | Initial disposition |
|---|---|---|
| AU — Australia | Do not create a “10 legal online casinos in Australia” list. Australian law prohibits providers from offering online casino services to people in Australia; the regulator also publishes prohibited providers and warns about affiliate promotion. | **BLOCKED for 10 legal online-casino brands.** Use a consumer-safety/legal-market explainer instead; do not market offshore casino brands as lawful Australian options. |
| CA — Canada | Canada is not one unified online-casino licensing market. Each candidate must be mapped to a province/territory and its applicable regulator. Ontario candidates cannot be described as licensed across Canada. | **RE-SCOPE by province.** Start with Ontario and separately assess other provinces only against their own official sources. |
| GB — Great Britain | Candidate must have the exact current Gambling Commission permission for remote casino and the relevant domain/product. Betting-only or other permissions do not qualify. | Candidate market; verify each site in the current UKGC register. |
| CH — Switzerland | Count only the ten casino platforms listed by the ESBK/FGB as online-capable and verify individual game permits. Do not fill a ten-brand target with foreign/offshore sites. | Ten official platforms exist in the ESBK source; each review still requires all editorial gates. |
| FR — France | Online casino games are prohibited. ANJ says foreign “international” licences do not make an online casino lawful in France. Approved online verticals are limited (notably poker, sports betting and horse-race betting). | **BLOCKED for 10 legal online-casino brands.** Publish a legal-market explainer, or redefine as licensed poker/betting reviews with explicit scope—not casino reviews. |
| IT — Italy | Verify each domain, concessionaire, concession status and current ADM casino-game permission. | Candidate market; current official ADM checks required. |
| DE — Germany | Separate GGL-authorised virtual slots and poker from state-authorised table-game/casino offers. A slots listing does not authorise roulette/blackjack across Germany. | Candidate market; review exact domain, game type and authority. |
| AT — Austria | Verify current Austrian federal legal framework and the exact authorised online product/operator. Do not treat an EU/foreign licence as Austrian authorisation. | **Scope checkpoint required before candidate counting.** |
| NL — Netherlands | Verify exact domain and legal entity in the current Ksa register, including active permit and product scope. | Candidate market; recheck the live register. |
| SE — Sweden | Verify exact domain, licensee, licence type/validity, responsible-gambling requirements and enforcement history in Spelinspektionen's live register. | Candidate market; current regulator evidence required. |

## Counting policy

The requested arithmetic is 10 markets × 10 brands = 100, but it is not an instruction to fabricate legal casino brands where none are authorised. AU and FR cannot responsibly be populated with ten legal online casino operators under the current official guidance. Canada must be counted by province, not as a single nationwide licence. CH has ten platforms in the regulator's online-casino list; listing is only the first gate, not final editorial approval.

For every candidate, complete all applicable gates in `docs/brand-review-evidence-template.md`, obtain an independent second review and record the decision in `docs/100-brand-verification-ledger.md`. Candidate leads and draft files do not count as approved reviews.

## Release block

**Approved reviews: 0/100.** Do not publish or deploy a claim of 100 verified brands until the market definitions are resolved, each review has current primary-source evidence and independent sign-off, and the release candidate passes QA.
