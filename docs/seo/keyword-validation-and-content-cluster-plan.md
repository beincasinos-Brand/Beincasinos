# Keyword validation and content-cluster plan

**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Status:** Research workflow; not a claim of measured demand or ranking.

## Goal

Turn the 1,000-row candidate keyword backlog into a smaller, evidence-backed publishing queue. Do not publish 1,000 pages just because 1,000 seed terms exist. Each proposed page must have a clear user need, lawful market scope, a distinct answer and evidence that the site can deliver something better than the current SERP.

## Validation workflow

1. **Market and eligibility gate:** confirm the target country and the exact operator/domain/product before making availability or recommendation claims. Hold uncertain brands.
2. **SERP capture:** for each candidate query, record date, locale, device, top organic result URLs, page type, intent, freshness, publisher type, title/H1, snippet features and whether results are dominated by operators, affiliates, regulators or forums.
3. **Demand evidence:** use Search Console query/page impressions and clicks where available, Google Trends for directional interest, and an approved keyword provider for volume/difficulty/CPC. Preserve the source, locale, metric and date. If unavailable, write “unknown”; never fill with guesses.
4. **Cannibalization:** group synonyms and near-identical intents into one canonical page. Do not create separate pages for “review”, “ratings”, “is it legit” and “is it safe” unless the SERP and content needs are materially different.
5. **Opportunity score:** use the transparent formula below as a prioritization aid, not as a forecast:
   - Intent fit: 0–3
   - Evidence available: 0–3
   - SERP weakness/opportunity: 0–3
   - Existing site relevance: 0–3
   - Effort efficiency: 0–3
   - Legal/market risk: 0–3 penalty
   Score = first five dimensions minus risk penalty. Record each component and reviewer.
6. **Content brief:** include the exact question, country, intended reader, evidence links, required unique facts, comparison set, title/H1 draft, internal links, schema eligibility, freshness trigger and what must not be claimed.
7. **Publish gate:** an editor checks originality, source accuracy, market restrictions, affiliate disclosure, responsible-gambling language, canonical/indexability and whether the page duplicates an existing page.
8. **Measure:** after indexing, assess Search Console query/page pairs, CTR, impressions, qualified visits, internal-link engagement and update needs. Do not treat a sitemap submission or indexing request as proof of indexing.

## Suggested clusters (GB only after licence and eligibility validation)

- **Brand review cluster:** one canonical, specific page per eligible brand, only after the operator, licence, domain and current terms are verified.
- **Terms literacy:** wagering requirements; bonus expiry; game contribution; max-bet and max-cashout clauses; KYC and source-of-funds checks.
- **Payments literacy:** payment methods; withdrawal limits; fees; verification; policy versus observed payout time.
- **Trust and redress:** UKGC register guide; how to read a licence; complaints and ADR; how to interpret historic regulatory actions.
- **Player protection:** deposit limits; time-outs; GAMSTOP/self-exclusion; account closure; safer-gambling tools and support.
- **Product transparency:** game providers; RNG/RTP explanations; live-casino terminology; mobile accessibility.
- **Comparison pages:** compare only eligible operators and state criteria, market, date and evidence confidence. Avoid “best” claims unsupported by a reproducible comparison.

## Recommended content template

- Page title and clear country/product scope.
- Short answer and “last checked” date.
- Operator legal name, licence number and primary regulator link.
- Domain and contracting-party evidence.
- Terms/offer summary with expiry and restrictions.
- Payments and withdrawal policy, with policy vs observed result separated.
- Safer-gambling and complaint/ADR routes.
- Relevant historic regulatory action, with date, licensee and regulator-stated scope.
- Named author, fact-checker and correction route.
- Sources and material limitations.
- Related guides and contextual internal links.
- Affiliate disclosure next to commercial links and globally.
- No unsupported star ratings, “tested” labels, guarantees, fabricated user reviews or invented traffic metrics.

## Acceptance criteria for the 1,000-row backlog

A keyword moves from backlog to brief only when:
- [ ] Market and intent are specified.
- [ ] SERP is captured with date and locale.
- [ ] Demand metrics have a cited source or are explicitly unknown.
- [ ] Canonical target and cannibalization decision are recorded.
- [ ] Page can provide unique evidence-backed value.
- [ ] Legal and brand eligibility gates are satisfied.
- [ ] Reviewer and next review date are assigned.

**Current limitation:** the seed backlog contains 10 intent templates for each of 100 candidates; it does not contain validated search volume, difficulty, CPC, live SERP measurements or guaranteed traffic. Those values must be collected from actual sources before prioritization.
