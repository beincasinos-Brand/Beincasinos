# BeInCasinos — commercial outreach pipeline

**Purpose:** turn verified brand research into evidence-based conversations about paid listings.  
**Status:** template only. No messages have been sent and no prospect has agreed to pay.

## Pipeline stages

| Stage | Entry criteria | Required evidence / action | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Discovered | A credible source identifies a brand | Capture brand, domain, source, and date found | Exact domain is checked |
| 1 — Legal verification | Exact domain is known | Verify legal operator, applicable regulator register, product scope, market, and regulatory actions | Pass, or move to Hold |
| 2 — Editorial review | Legal verification passed | Check ownership, product claims, responsible-gambling tools, consumer complaints where credible, and conflicts | Editorial decision recorded |
| 3 — Commercial contact verified | Public official company/partnership contact found | Save official source URL and contact role; avoid guessed email addresses | Contact is suitable for commercial inquiry |
| 4 — Outreach ready | Stages 1–3 complete | Personalize email and record why the brand fits the directory | Owner review before first contact |
| 5 — Contacted | Message actually sent by project owner | Log date, channel, recipient source, and exact message/version | Reply or follow-up date reached |
| 6 — Discussion | Brand replies | Request rate card, media kit, permitted markets, contract terms, and disclosures | Written terms received or prospect declines |
| 7 — Commercial review | Terms received | Assess price, deliverables, disclosure, duration, cancellation, legal market scope, and editorial independence | Approve, negotiate, or decline |
| 8 — Approved partner | Written terms accepted and legal/editorial checks complete | Signed agreement, invoice/payment record where applicable, disclosures, asset rights, and review date | Listing goes live only after final checks |
| Hold / Reject | Missing or adverse evidence | Record reason and re-check date if appropriate | Explicitly resolved; never silently promote |

## Mandatory fields for the master tracker

- Prospect ID
- Brand and exact domain
- Legal operator and registration jurisdiction
- Market(s) and product(s)
- Regulator, licence number/type/status, official register URL, date checked
- Regulatory action check and source
- Brand launch date and primary evidence
- Official commercial contact role, source URL, and contact method
- Discovery grade (A/B/C/Hold)
- Editorial decision and reviewer
- Commercial interest: Unknown / Asked / Interested / Declined / Written terms received
- Willingness-to-pay evidence URL or email record
- Last contact, next action, and follow-up date
- Commercial terms, placement disclosure, approved markets, contract status
- Last review date and next re-verification date

## Suggested contact cadence

Use this only after legal and editorial checks pass and an owner approves the first message.

1. **Day 0:** send a short, tailored email to a verified official commercial contact.
2. **Day 5–7:** one polite follow-up if no reply.
3. **Day 12–15:** final brief follow-up, then mark “No response” and pause.
4. Do not scrape personal emails, guess addresses, mass-message unrelated recipients, or keep contacting someone who declines or opts out.

Dates are guidelines, not automated actions. The project owner must send and log messages.

## Commercial and editorial safeguards

- Do not write “willing to pay” until a direct response, official rate card, media kit, or equivalent evidence supports it.
- Do not promise rankings, positive reviews, dofollow links, guaranteed traffic, or guaranteed SEO outcomes.
- Label paid placements prominently and separately from editorial ratings.
- A sales decision must never override a legal, licensing, safety, or responsible-gambling rejection.
- Check each target market separately. US permissions are state-specific; a license in one country does not imply permission elsewhere.
- Re-verify license and domain status before publication and at scheduled reviews.
- Do not publish a profile solely because the operator paid.
