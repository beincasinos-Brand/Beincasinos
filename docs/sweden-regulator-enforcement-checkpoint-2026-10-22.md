## Mandatory Sweden-specific checks

1. Look up the exact consumer domain in the current Spelinspektionen register and record the legal licensee.
2. Confirm the current licence category covers commercial online games/casino, not merely betting or another product.
3. Capture licence validity dates and any conditions from the individual register result.
4. Review current decisions/enforcement notices for the legal entity, domain and associated brand. The authority's illegal-gambling page lists recent prohibition decisions, including a 14 September 2026 decision concerning stakes.com and July 2026 decisions concerning Softon Ltd and RBGAMING N.V.; these are examples of why enforcement review must be date-stamped, not a claim about the candidate brands in our register.
5. Confirm required responsible-gambling features and visible Spelpaus access; do not infer compliance from a logo alone.
6. Verify terms, payments, bonus terms, privacy/cookies, complaints/support, and any ownership/domain changes from current primary sources.
7. Perform only permitted, reproducible UX checks; record what was actually observed. No account, deposit, withdrawal or gameplay tests are claimed by this source checkpoint.
8. Require affiliate disclosure review, an independent second reviewer and final editorial/legal sign-off.
