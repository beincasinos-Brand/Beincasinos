# BeInCasinos SEO research and high-value content plan
**Prepared:** 21 October 2026  
**Scope:** English-language editorial strategy, with Great Britain as a research priority only where lawful and appropriate. This is a planning document, not a claim of keyword volumes or rankings.

## Executive decision

Do not pursue 100 million daily impressions by mass-publishing thin brand pages. The site currently has a useful base of 17 guides and two tools, but the Reviews directory explicitly says that no casino profiles have been scored. Search Console data available to the project connector returned no Search Analytics rows for the periods checked; therefore we cannot responsibly report a current impressions baseline or calculate growth. The 32-URL sitemap was submitted without reported errors, but sitemap acceptance is not proof of indexing.

Priority: build original, verifiable tools and research pages that answer a specific question better than existing results, improve internal linking and trust signals, then use Search Console query/page data to decide what to expand. Keep the research market-specific. Never imply a licence in one country grants access in another.

## What the competitor sample shows

A small public sample was checked on 21 October 2026. This is a qualitative SERP/site sample, not a full backlink or keyword-gap analysis.

### 1. BestCasino.co.uk — https://www.bestcasino.co.uk/reviews/
Observed page elements:
- Broad A–Z review directory and a stated review scope covering licensing, withdrawal speed, bonus terms, game selection, mobile play and support.
- Named reviewer/checker and visible “last updated” date; stated monthly review cadence and updates when offers change.
- Recent-update log (new reviews and retests) and an advertising disclosure.

What BeInCasinos can do better:
- Publish a transparent evidence ledger for each claim: source URL, check date, market, operator/legal entity, and whether a statement is operator marketing or independently tested.
- Show a real update history and reviewer/editor identity only where true.
- Do not claim “tested”, measured withdrawal speed or reviewed a brand unless the testing happened and the method is documented.

Observed result dated October 2026 promotes a ranked list and describes methodology around licensing, game fairness, payments, bonus clarity, customer support and payout benchmarking.

What BeInCasinos can do better:
- Avoid copying a ranked-list format without real tests. Use transparent comparison tables for verifiable terms and regulator status; separate observed facts from subjective assessments.
- Build tools that let readers calculate wagering obligations and compare restrictions without requiring a signup.

### 3. TNT Sports — https://www.tntsports.co.uk/betting/casino/
Observed page describes a multi-criteria methodology and compares licensing, game selection, withdrawals, offer terms, mobile experience and support.

What BeInCasinos can do better:

- Correct and explain mismatches in operator/domain/licence references rather than repeating a marketing claim.

### 4. Official Google Search guidance
Google says content should be people-first and add original information, research, analysis or substantial value. Its spam policies specifically warn against mass-generating unoriginal pages primarily to manipulate rankings. Sources:
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

## Highest-value pages to build or upgrade first

### P0 — existing assets (no new URL needed)
1. **Wagering calculator** — explain formula, stake assumptions, eligible-game contribution, maximum-bet and expiry caveats; add two worked examples and a printable checklist. Clearly state that actual terms override generic examples.
2. **Terms checklist** — organise by bonus, withdrawal, identity verification, fees, limits, game restrictions, closure and complaints. Include a downloadable/copyable checklist if technically safe.
3. **Licence-check guide** — direct readers to official regulators; explain legal operator vs brand/domain vs software supplier and why a licence does not mean “available everywhere”.
4. **Withdrawal guide** — distinguish operator processing time from payment-provider/bank time; cover pending withdrawals, KYC, limits, fees and complaint escalation without promising a speed.
5. **Review methodology** — add evidence standards, score eligibility gates, date-stamped source checks, retest cadence, affiliate disclosure and correction process. Keep “no profiles scored” clear until true.

### P1 — original research pages
6. **Regulator register directory** — a small, carefully maintained list of official registers and what each register proves; each jurisdiction gets a clear scope warning and last-checked date.
7. **Casino bonus terms comparison dataset** — compare actual terms from a small set of eligible operators, with source URLs and check dates. Do not rank until evidence coverage is comparable.
8. **Withdrawal terms comparison** — compare published limits, fees, processing estimates and verification triggers; label all figures as published terms, not tested performance.
9. **Complaint escalation map** — for each included jurisdiction, show operator complaint stage, independent ADR (where applicable) and regulator role using official sources.
10. **Evidence-led review change log** — explain what changed on a profile and link to sources, rather than refreshing dates without substantive updates.

### P2 — scale after measurement
11. Expand market guides only when a regulator’s official records, lawful market scope and maintainable editorial capacity are established.
12. Add individual brand pages only when the full profile checklist passes; prospects in the internal tracker do not count as published reviews.
13. Add new tools only when they solve a distinct task and can be tested/accessibly maintained.

## Internal linking and technical SEO actions

- Create a hub-and-spoke structure: Guides hub → topic guides → related tools and methodology; every guide should link to 2–4 genuinely relevant pages, not an arbitrary sitewide keyword list.
- Add contextual links from each calculator to its explanatory guide and from guides back to the useful tool.
- Check every canonical URL, indexability, title, H1, description, and HTTP status; ensure sitemap lists only canonical, indexable, useful pages.
- Add visible author/editor and correction details where accurate; publish no fabricated author credentials, test dates or first-hand claims.
- Add structured data only when the page genuinely qualifies; markup must match visible content. Avoid review/rating schema for unscored pages.
- Keep images lightweight and descriptive, and preserve mobile usability, keyboard navigation and contrast.
- Do not create near-duplicate pages targeting spelling variants or many regions with the same body.

## Measurement and decision gates

Weekly, use Search Console to export:
- clicks, impressions, CTR and average position by query/page/country/device;
- indexed status and exclusions using the URL Inspection / Pages reports where available;
- sitemap status and crawl anomalies.

Tag every content task to a query cluster and a page URL. After enough data accrues, prioritise pages with impressions but weak CTR, positions near page one, and queries where the current answer misses the user's intent. Do not claim growth from sitemap submissions or “site:” searches.

Suggested milestones (not guarantees):
- First: confirm a reliable baseline and indexing coverage.
- Then: improve the pages already earning impressions and validate title/snippet intent.
- Next: publish a small batch of original research/tools, measure outcomes, and only then scale what works.
- Treat 100 million daily impressions as a long-term aspiration, not a forecast. It would require enormous sustained demand, broad relevance, strong authority and evidence of actual performance.
