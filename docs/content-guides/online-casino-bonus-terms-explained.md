# Online Casino Bonus Terms Explained: A Practical Checklist

**Editorial status:** educational guide draft; review for target-market laws and local advertising requirements before publication.

## The short answer
A welcome bonus is not the same as cash you can immediately withdraw. Its real value depends on the full terms: what amount must be wagered, which games count, how long the offer lasts, what bets are permitted, and whether a cashout cap applies. Read the terms before depositing, and skip the offer if the conditions are unclear or unsuitable.

## 1. Find the wagering requirement and calculation base
A term such as “30x wagering” is incomplete unless you know what the multiplier applies to. It might apply to the bonus amount, the deposit, or the combined amount. For example, if a hypothetical bonus is €20 and the requirement is 30x the bonus, the required qualifying turnover would be €600. If the same multiplier applied to a €20 deposit plus a €20 bonus, the required turnover could instead be €1,200. These are examples of the math only, not claims about any operator’s current offer.

Ask: Is the multiplier applied to bonus, deposit or both? Does it apply only to eligible play? Are some games excluded or weighted differently? Is there a maximum bet while the bonus is active? Can winnings be withdrawn before completion?

## 2. Check expiry and maximum-bet restrictions
Check when the expiry clock starts (claim, deposit or activation), the time zone and what happens when it ends. A maximum-bet rule may apply until wagering is complete; exceeding it may affect eligibility under the terms. Do not assume a restriction is harmless because it is buried in a long document.

## 3. Understand game contribution
Not every game necessarily contributes equally. A slot might contribute fully while live dealer or table games contribute less or not at all. Check the contribution schedule in the current offer terms. If contribution is unclear, treat the offer’s value as uncertain.

## 4. Look for conversion and cashout caps
Some promotions limit the amount that can be converted to withdrawable funds or cap bonus winnings. Check whether the cap applies to bonus winnings, all winnings or one promotion. The headline amount alone does not reveal these restrictions.

## 5. Check eligibility and payment exclusions
Offers may be limited to new customers, specific countries or account types. Some deposit methods may not qualify. Identity verification may be required before withdrawal. Verify the offer on the exact local domain for your jurisdiction.

## 6. Compare offers fairly
| Term | What to record | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Bonus amount | Currency and amount | Headline value only |
| Wagering | Multiplier and calculation base | Determines qualifying turnover |
| Expiry | Deadline and time zone | Time available |
| Max bet | Limit during bonus play | May affect eligibility |
| Game contribution | Percentage by category | Changes how wagering counts |
| Cashout cap | Maximum withdrawable winnings | Can limit value |
| Payment restrictions | Eligible/excluded methods | Deposit route may affect offer |
| Eligibility | Market and customer type | Offer may not be available to you |
| Verification | KYC and withdrawal conditions | May affect release of funds |

Do not rank offers by headline amount alone. Compare the same fields for every operator and mark missing information “not verified” rather than assuming it is favorable.

## 7. Red flags
- Promotion headline and full terms disagree.
- Material restrictions are missing, contradictory or hard to locate.
- Local domain or product eligibility is uncertain.
- Expiry, wagering base, max bet or cashout cap is unclear.
- Support cannot explain a material term.
- A review promotes an offer without linking its terms or stating when they were checked.

## 8. FAQs
### Does 30x wagering mean I must deposit 30 times?
Not necessarily. The multiplier’s calculation base is defined in the terms; it may apply to bonus, deposit or both.

### Are all games counted equally?
No. Contribution can differ by category or title. Check the promotion terms.

### Can I withdraw the bonus immediately?
A bonus may be subject to conditions before funds become withdrawable. Check wagering and cashout rules.

### Should I accept the biggest bonus?
Not based on amount alone. Compare wagering base, expiry, contribution, max bet, cashout cap, eligibility and payment restrictions.

## Responsible decisions
Ask whether you would still choose the product without the promotion. Do not deposit money you cannot afford to lose or treat wagering as income. If gambling becomes difficult to control, use limit, time-out or self-exclusion tools and seek appropriate local support.
