# How to Compare Online Casinos: A Practical Checklist

This guide helps readers compare gambling services using verifiable facts rather than headline bonuses or unsupported rankings. It is general information, not legal advice. Gambling involves risk; never gamble money needed for essentials.

## Start with legal eligibility

Before comparing games or promotions, establish whether the exact operator and product are permitted where you live. A familiar brand name is not enough: domains, operating companies, licences and permitted product types can differ by country or state. Check the official regulator's register and the operator's legal terms. If the operator, domain, or product permission cannot be confirmed, do not treat it as eligible.

## Compare like for like

For every candidate, record the exact domain; legal company named in the terms; regulator and current licence status; product and geographic restrictions; bonus terms; cashier methods and fees; withdrawal process; games and rules; support routes; privacy policy; account controls; accessibility; and the date each claim was checked.

A large advertised bonus may be less useful than a smaller offer with clearer restrictions. Calculate the wagering amount and check expiry, eligible games, contribution rates, maximum bet, withdrawal cap, excluded payment methods and eligibility. | Field | What to record |

## How to read a review score

A score is meaningful only when its methodology is published and evidence supports each dimension. Legal eligibility is a gate, not a reward that can be offset by attractive design. A service whose eligibility cannot be established should be marked HOLD or EXCLUDE, not given a high overall rating. A review based on public information alone must say so; it must not imply that a deposit, withdrawal or support test occurred when it did not.

## Questions to ask before creating an account

1. Is this exact operator and product permitted where I live?
2. Can I understand the bonus terms without contacting support?
3. What identity checks may be required, and at what stage?
4. Which withdrawal methods are actually available to my location?
5. Where are account limits, time-outs, self-exclusion and complaint procedures explained?
6. What happens to a bonus balance if I cancel a promotion or withdraw?

## FAQs

### Is the biggest bonus the best offer?
No. Wagering, expiry, game contribution, maximum bet, withdrawal caps and eligibility rules can outweigh the headline amount.

### Does a licence guarantee a good experience?
No. It is an important eligibility and oversight check, not a guarantee of fast payments, helpful support or a complaint-free experience.

### Can a review site guarantee a withdrawal time?
Not responsibly unless a clear, current source supports the claim and the conditions are explained. Operator processing and bank/payment-provider settlement are different stages.

### What if information is missing?
Treat it as unknown. Do not invent a value, award points for absent evidence or publish a definitive recommendation.
