# Tier-1-only strategy and market prioritisation

**Owner decision:** BeInCasinos targets Tier 1 audiences only, initially AU, US, FR, DE, CH, GB, and NL. “Tier 1” is an informal marketing classification, not a legal category, and lists differ by network. This strategy does not mean every gambling product is lawful or advertisable in every selected market.

## Positioning

Build a trusted, English-first educational directory about operator identity, terms, payments, withdrawal rules, licensing, and safer gambling. Prioritise usefulness and verifiable primary-source evidence over volume of thin brand pages.

### Language approach
- **English first:** shared core guides and tools, with clear market-specific caveats.
- **French (FR):** only commission localised pages after native-quality editing and French-specific legal/regulatory review.
- **German (DE and relevant CH audience):** only commission localised pages after native-quality editing and separate German and Swiss market review.
- **US:** where relevant, distinguish individual states and game types; never label an operator broadly “legal in the US” based on one state's authorisation.
- Do not machine-translate regulatory/legal claims and publish them without a qualified human review.

## Seven target markets: editorial gating

| Market | Internal priority | Non-negotiable guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| GB — Great Britain | Initial priority candidate | Verify exact entity, domain, active Gambling Commission licence, and relevant remote activity. Check advertising rules. |
| DE — Germany | Initial priority candidate | Match exact entity/domain and game category to the current GGL whitelist; do not infer all products are covered. |
| NL — Netherlands | Initial priority candidate | Match exact operator and website/product to the current Ksa licence register; review local advertising and duty-of-care rules. |
| CH — Switzerland | Initial priority candidate | Verify current Swiss authorisation and domain; foreign licences alone are not enough. |
| US — United States | State-by-state only | Record each state and product separately; no generic nationwide online-casino permission claim. |
| FR — France | Restricted for online-casino promotions | Do not promote offshore online casinos as legal in France. Separate casino products from categories specifically authorised by ANJ. |
| AU — Australia | Restricted for online-casino promotions | Online casino services covered by the Interactive Gambling Act are prohibited. Do not promote offshore online-casino offers as available to Australian readers. |

“Initial priority candidate” is an operational sequence, not a finding that a particular operator is legal or that this market is necessarily the most profitable. Revisit priority using audience evidence, legal advice, publisher capacity, and official source checks.

## Research tracker rules

For every brand, record the status separately for each market:
- unknown — insufficient or stale evidence; do not publish a market-availability claim.
- verified for review — official primary-source evidence captured and reviewed; this is not by itself publication approval.
- approved to publish — editorial and market-specific compliance review completed and signed off.
- rejected — evidence indicates the operator/product is not suitable for that market or fails editorial standards.
- published — page is live and has an assigned next-review date.

Required evidence per market: official source URL, exact legal entity, domain, licence/authorisation number where relevant, permitted product category, current status, date checked in UTC, reviewer, decision, and unresolved issues. For US entries, include the state and regulator. Keep evidence for advertising and affiliate permission distinct from evidence of operator licensing.

## Commercial approach

1. No sales claims before audience measurement. Never fabricate traffic, audience geography, rankings, leads, or conversion rates.
2. Do not sell placement for a market where the operator's permission or the placement's legality is unresolved.
3. Label paid inventory clearly as “Paid listing” or “Sponsored”. Fees buy inventory only—not licence verification, a positive review, “safe” status, or hidden ranking preference.
4. Add affiliate links only after reviewing programme terms, allowed territories, advertising restrictions, tracking, and privacy obligations.
5. Do not treat six months as a revenue deadline. Review real Search Console/analytics evidence and business costs before making commercial commitments.
