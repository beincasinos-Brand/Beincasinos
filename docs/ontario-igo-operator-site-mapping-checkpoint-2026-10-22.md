## Ontario-specific evidence requirements

1. Current iGaming Ontario operator and site listing, exact legal entity, domain and category.
2. Confirm Ontario-specific access/eligibility and applicable terms; do not generalise Ontario permission to all of Canada.
3. Current full terms, game rules, bonus eligibility, wagering and expiry restrictions.
4. Deposit/withdrawal methods, limits, fees, verification and processing stages.
5. Responsible-gambling tools and current self-exclusion/opt-out resources.
6. Privacy controller, cookies/SDK consent, support and complaint escalation.
7. Relevant AGCO/iGaming Ontario enforcement and material changes.
8. Reproducible UX testing limited to permitted observations; no fabricated account, payment or gameplay testing.
9. Affiliate relationship disclosure, independent second reviewer and legal/editorial sign-off.

## Counting and release decision

Multiple sites under the same legal operator may be separate products, but they must not be counted as separate approved reviews unless each review has a distinct, useful and independently evidenced consumer-facing evaluation. Casino, poker and sportsbook verticals must not be conflated.
