# Operator outreach playbook (internal)

**Not ready to send by default.** No commercial offer or paid-placement pitch should be sent until the owner approves it, the publisher identity and monitored business email are set, the website is stable, audience evidence is available for any performance claims, and the recipient/market/contact route have been checked. A narrowly scoped pre-launch discovery interview may be considered without audience metrics only if it is clearly research (not a sale), lawful for the recipient/channel, and separately approved by the owner. This is a process template, not legal advice. Apply the recipient's country-specific marketing/privacy rules and honour objections and opt-outs.

## Positioning

Sell a clearly labelled, factual directory placement—not a positive review, licence certification, recommendation, ranking boost, guaranteed traffic, or promise of conversions. Early on, request a short discovery conversation rather than selling an audience size that does not yet exist.

Do not imply BeInCasinos already has meaningful reach unless dated analytics/Search Console evidence supports that statement. Do not tell brands that they were “selected as legal/safe” based on a search result. If the brand or product cannot be verified for the intended market, do not pitch a placement for that market.

## Research before any first message

- [ ] Identify exact brand, official domain, operating legal entity and parent company.
- [ ] Verify current primary-source regulator status for the exact market and product; for US online casino, check each state separately.
- [ ] Record an official business contact source and why the recipient's role is relevant.
- [ ] Record the source URL, date checked, reviewer, and unresolved issues.
- [ ] Verify that contact is permitted under applicable marketing/privacy law and the site's stated contact preferences.
- [ ] Personalise the note with one true, specific observation; never invent familiarity or prior contact.
- [ ] Do not send if the recipient opted out, the contact source is questionable, or legal/market status is unresolved.

## Follow-up policy

- One short follow-up at most, and only where lawful and appropriate; otherwise no follow-up.
- Respect any unsubscribe, objection, complaint or do-not-contact request immediately.
- Do not buy lists, guess email addresses, use scraped personal data, or automate bulk outreach.
- Log the sent date, contact source, message version, outcome, next action and opt-out status in the sales pipeline.
- If the recipient asks for a media kit before reliable metrics exist, explain that the site is pre-launch/early-stage and do not fabricate figures.

## Discovery questions (if a brand replies)

1. Which legal entity and markets would the listing relate to?
2. Who owns approval of directory advertising and legal/compliance review?
3. What official sources should be used for current brand, domain and product facts?
4. What disclosure, link, creative, tracking, renewal and cancellation requirements does the operator have?
5. Which models does your team consider: fixed-fee directory inventory, CPA, revenue share, or a hybrid—and what programme rules, attribution/reporting, market restrictions, and approval steps apply?
6. Is a paid directory listing or future performance-based arrangement of interest in principle, subject to market eligibility, actual measurement capability, and transparent written terms?

A positive reply is only an initial signal. Mark the pipeline stage replied_interested only when the reply explicitly indicates interest; use qualified_buyer only when the relevant decision-maker confirms the paid-listing use case. Mark won only after a signed agreement and recorded payment/invoice status.

## Do not send until all release gates pass

- [ ] Brand/name risk reviewed and publisher identity approved.
- [ ] Website launched and stable, with privacy/disclosure/contact pages matching actual implementation.
- [ ] For any commercial pitch, real audience evidence and its limitations are available. If the site has no measured audience, only a clearly labelled research/discovery note may be considered, with separate owner approval and no offer, traffic claim, or performance promise.
- [ ] Proposed market and exact product reviewed; no prohibited or unlicensed offer is promoted.
- [ ] Pricing, taxes, term, labels, link attributes, cancellation/refund terms and invoicing approved.
- [ ] Outreach is lawful for the recipient and channel; opt-out handling is operational.
- [ ] Owner approves this specific outreach batch.
