# Target-market regulatory source matrix

**Internal research aid — not legal advice and not publication approval.** Prepared 20 October 2026 from official regulator pages linked below. Laws, registers, licence scopes, advertising restrictions, and market access can change. Re-check the primary source on the date each operator profile is reviewed. This matrix is a starting point, not a substitute for market-specific legal advice.

## Target markets

The project owner has selected these intended audience markets: Australia (AU), United States (US), France (FR), Germany (DE), Switzerland (CH), Great Britain (GB), and the Netherlands (NL).

Do not use a single “international licence” as proof of legality in all seven markets. Record the exact operating entity, website/domain, game vertical, legal permission, allowed territory, source URL, date/time checked, and reviewer for each market separately.

## US state-by-state research requirement

Before publishing or selling a placement aimed at US readers, create one row per state and product combination. At minimum record:

- State and regulator name.
- Official statute/regulator URL and last-checked timestamp (UTC).
- Product permission: online casino, online poker, sports betting, or other; do not combine categories.
- Exact operator legal entity, domain, licence/approval number and current status.
- Whether the intended content, referral link, affiliate relationship, and advertising are permitted.
- Reviewer, evidence file/link, decision, and unresolved questions.

A generic “US licence” field is not sufficient. If state-specific status cannot be verified, set availability to **unknown / not approved for publication**.

## Editorial and commercial controls

1. Keep all 100 brand prospects marked as prospects until primary-source checks are complete.
2. Never infer permission from a brand's marketing claims, an offshore licence, search results, or absence from a blacklist.
3. Separate factual operator profiles from paid inventory. Payment cannot change the licensing conclusion or buy a positive review.
4. Do not link to or promote a service where local legality, affiliate permission, or advertising rules remain unresolved.
5. Display the last-checked date and link to the official source. Recheck before publication, before a paid placement starts, and on a scheduled refresh.
6. Remove or pause a profile/placement when a source changes or the evidence cannot be refreshed.
7. Do not publish this internal matrix as personalised legal advice; obtain qualified advice before operating a commercial gambling directory across these markets.
