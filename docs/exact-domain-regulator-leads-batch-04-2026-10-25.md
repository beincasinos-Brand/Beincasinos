## Great Britain — register process check

Source: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses  
The UK Gambling Commission register was last updated 25 October 2026 when checked. It supports search by business name, trading name, domain or account number and exposes licence status and activity information. It also warns that domain/trading names are supplied by businesses and may not be guaranteed accurate.

For each GB review, record:
1. Exact domain as displayed in the register and whether active/inactive/white label.
2. Legal account name and account number.
3. Active status and relevant remote casino activity.
4. Activity-from/until dates and regulatory actions.
5. Date checked, plus a screenshot/export reference in the internal evidence store.

Do not infer GB authorization from an overseas licence, a similarly named corporate group, or an affiliate marketing page.
