## 2. Regulatory history — material context

On 4 April 2024, the UK Gambling Commission announced a combined £582,120 regulatory settlement relating to AML and social-responsibility failures found in a March 2022 compliance assessment:
- Hillside (UK Gaming) ENC, associated with casino and bingo: £343,035.
- Hillside (UK Sports) ENC, associated with betting: £239,085.

The regulator described weaknesses that included customer interactions not being tailored to individual journeys or levels of harm, inability to demonstrate that early-risk detection and customer interactions were effective, ineffective AML/KYC triggers, missing financial-sanctions checks before first deposits, and over-reliance on customers' annual self-verification. The Commission also noted remedial action in determining the settlement. The review must present both the findings and the recorded remediation fairly, and must not imply the 2024 settlement is a current licence suspension or proof of present-day product quality.
