# Commercial brand prospect qualification

**Purpose:** Build a defensible shortlist of 100 casino brands for a potential paid-listing offer from BeInCasinos. This is a research and outreach document, not a claim that any brand has agreed to pay.

## Non-negotiable qualification rules

1. **Market fit:** record each target market separately: Australia, United States (state-by-state), France, Germany, Switzerland, United Kingdom, and Netherlands. “Tier 1” is a commercial shorthand, not a legal permission.
2. **Legal status:** verify the exact operator entity, domain, licence, permitted product, and permitted audience against the relevant regulator's current register. A licence in one country does not establish permission in another.
3. **Brand identity and dates:** distinguish a new brand, a rebrand, a new domain, and a new licence/operator. Record the source and the date checked. Only use 2022–2026 as the launch window when evidence supports it; do not infer a launch date from a licence date alone.
4. **Commercial plausibility:** look for observable signals such as a public affiliate/partner programme, an operator group managing multiple brands, an active PR/marketing function, or a stated B2B partnership route. These are prospecting signals, not proof of willingness to buy a listing.
5. **Payment willingness:** mark “unconfirmed” until the brand or its authorised representative explicitly responds or publishes a paid directory/media-buying option. Never present a prospect as a paying customer before that.
6. **Editorial independence:** a fee may buy clearly labelled placement only. It cannot buy licence verification, a favourable safety verdict, an undisclosed ranking, or removal of material risk information.
7. **Market restrictions:** exclude or hold any brand whose target-market eligibility, advertising permission, or applicable affiliate rules are unclear. Do not direct users to unlicensed operators in restricted jurisdictions.

## Commercial offer discipline

Use the internal [paid-listing offer framework](./paid-listing-offer-framework.md) only as a hypothesis for research interviews. The example prices are not validated rates and must not be presented as proven market pricing. Do not sell placements until real audience data, publisher identity, privacy/contact terms, legal market scope, and owner approval are ready. UK candidate checks should follow the [dated UKGC register spot-check audit](./ukgc-register-audit-2026-10.md) and the current official register, not third-party launch trackers alone.

## Evidence grades

- **A — Verified:** current official regulator entry and official brand/operator source both checked; exact domain/entity matched; launch or market-entry date supported by a dated primary source.
- **B — Strong lead:** official source supports the brand and operator, but one material fact (often launch date, domain, or territory) still needs verification.
- **C — Research queue:** surfaced by a secondary industry source only. Not ready for public listing or outreach claims.
- **Hold / Exclude:** licensing mismatch, unclear market permission, contradictory evidence, material regulatory concern, or no credible brand/operator identity.

## Prospect record fields

For each candidate capture:

- Brand name and canonical domain
- Legal operator / parent group
- Target market(s), one record per market
- Regulator and licence reference; verification URL
- Domain status and product permission
- Brand launch date and evidence URL (separate from licence date)
- Official partner/affiliate/advertising contact URL
- Commercial signals and supporting evidence
- Evidence grade and unresolved questions
- Last checked date (UTC), researcher, next review date
- Outreach status: not contacted / contacted / replied / declined / interested
- Paid-listing status: unconfirmed / explicitly interested / terms requested / agreed / paid
- Disclosure and editorial review notes

## Outreach readiness gate

A candidate is ready for outreach only when its official identity and target-market eligibility are verified, the commercial contact route is credible, and the pitch describes the site's real current value without invented traffic, audience, ranking, or conversion metrics. If the site's traffic is not yet measurable, offer an honest pre-launch conversation or future launch package; do not promise reach or performance.

A candidate is ready for a **paid listing** only after the business explicitly confirms interest and the placement, fee, duration, renewal, labelling, and editorial-independence terms are documented.
