## Proposed page copy

### Clear, labelled listings. Independent editorial standards.

BeInCasinos is developing an English-language learning resource that helps readers understand operator identity, licensing claims, bonus conditions, withdrawal rules, and safer-gambling tools.

We may offer clearly labelled paid directory placements when the site has enough measured audience evidence to describe its reach honestly. A paid placement is advertising inventory—not a licence verification, endorsement, positive review, or guarantee of performance.

### What a paid listing may include

The final package, placement, duration, renewal, cancellation, price, tax treatment, link attributes, and reporting (if any) will be described in a written proposal before an agreement is signed. No package is available until the publisher approves those terms and confirms the site is ready to accept payment.

### Editorial independence is non-negotiable

- Every operator profile must pass the same source-based publication checks, whether or not the operator pays.
- Fees cannot change a factual licence or market-availability conclusion.
- Paid placements will be labelled “Paid listing” or “Sponsored” beside the placement.
- We will not promise or imply that a fee buys a favourable rating, “safe” status, a licence conclusion, or undisclosed ranking priority.
- Market eligibility and applicable advertising restrictions must be reviewed before a placement is accepted.
- We may decline or remove claims that cannot be supported by reliable evidence.

### Evidence before sales

We will not advertise audience size, search visibility, clicks, leads, or conversions without dated source data and a clear measurement method. Any media kit will state the reporting period and limitations. If there is not enough evidence to support a useful proposal, we will continue developing the resource rather than overstate its reach.
