## Reporting rules

- Never invent values, audience sizes, country mix, CTR, leads, conversion rates, or projected revenue.
- Distinguish observed data from estimates; preferably do not use estimates in the initial media kit.
- Do not promise future traffic, clicks, leads, sales, rankings, or search results.
- Keep reporting period, timezone, metric definition, source, and limitations attached to every figure.
- Do not disclose personal data or small cohorts that could identify individuals.
- Do not add analytics or tracking until the real implementation and applicable privacy/consent requirements are reviewed.
- If a metric is unavailable, write “Not measured” or “Not recorded”; do not replace it with a guess.
- Share the report with a prospect only after owner approval and only when it accurately represents the live site.

## Minimum evidence to consider a paid-listing pilot

1. A live, stable site with name/domain, publisher identity, privacy/contact, legal and editorial gates cleared.
2. A complete reporting window with source files retained and measurement limitations documented.
3. A relevant inventory page with enough actual traffic to evaluate honestly; no universal minimum is assumed in advance.
4. A prospect-specific market/product review and a clear sponsored label.
5. Written deliverables, price, term, cancellation/refund conditions, link attributes, tracking/privacy approach, and reporting schedule.
6. Owner approval and signed terms before placement goes live.
