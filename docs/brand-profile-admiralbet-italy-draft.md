### Payment evidence — operator-stated, not independently tested

The operator's payment FAQ says card deposits using Visa, Mastercard and Postepay are credited instantly. It says card/Postepay withdrawals are supported when the card can receive payouts, and notes that bank or issuer limits can apply. Its general FAQ describes e-wallet withdrawals as typically processed within 24 hours and bank-transfer/card withdrawals as potentially taking 3–5 working days. These are published estimates, not independently observed outcomes.

A 2024 terms PDF lists multiple withdrawal methods and notes that method-dependent commissions may apply. Do not make a blanket “all withdrawals are free” claim; check the current method-specific fees and terms.
