## Official sources checked

- Great Britain Gambling Commission, business register: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses — register searchable by business/trading name, domain, or account number; source notes domain/trading-name data may be supplied by businesses and is not guaranteed by the Commission.
- Australian Department of Social Services, gambling support services: https://www.dss.gov.au/gambling/gambling-support-services — National Gambling Helpline 1800 858 858, free/confidential, 24/7; BetStop scope is licensed online and phone wagering services.
- Australian Communications and Media Authority, Interactive Gambling Act: https://www.acma.gov.au/interactivegambling — online casinos are among prohibited services for Australian users and prohibited services must not be advertised in Australia; the page records 2026 reforms with most measures commencing 1 January 2027.
- US National Council on Problem Gambling, helpline: https://www.ncpgambling.org/help-treatment/about-the-national-problem-gambling-helpline/ — current service branded 1-800-MY-RESET, with call/text/chat and referrals; check the official page for the latest contact methods.
- France Service-Public.fr, Joueurs Info Service: https://lannuaire.service-public.gouv.fr/gouvernement/0572bff4-2153-4b2e-9ca3-e15fdd27e3bc — lists 09 74 75 13 13 and published phone hours; verify live service details before use.
- Germany Check-dein-Spiel / BIÖG: https://www.check-dein-spiel.de/kontakt and https://www.check-dein-spiel.de/hilfe-fuer-spieler/telefon-beratung/ — official telephone counselling contact and published hours.
- Switzerland Federal Gaming Board, player protection: https://www.esbk.admin.ch/de/sozialschutz — describes casino social-protection duties and links to national/cantonal support services.
- Government of the Netherlands, gambling-problem support: https://www.government.nl/themes/justice-security-and-defence/games-of-chance/question-and-answer/help-with-gambling-problems — signposts 24/7 Loket Kansspel support and CRUKS for Dutch-licensed services.
- France National Gaming Authority, illegal offers: https://anj.fr/index.php/joueurs/offre-illegale — states that online casino games such as roulette, blackjack and slots are not authorised in France; a foreign online-casino licence is not recognised as French authorisation.
- France National Gaming Authority, permitted online gambling categories: https://anj.fr/foire-aux-questions/offre-de-jeu-legale-et-illegale/quels-sont-les-jeux-dargent-et-paris-sportifs — lists permitted categories and confirms online casinos are not authorised in France.
