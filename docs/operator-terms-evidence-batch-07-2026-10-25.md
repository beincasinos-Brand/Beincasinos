## Cross-brand conclusions
1. None of the above pages alone supports an overall quality score. Operator-published terms can populate a source-backed facts section, but payment speed, support quality, fairness in practice, complaint handling and usability require independent checks or hands-on testing.
2. Current bonus cards must be sourced from the exact locale/market landing page and offer terms at the time of publication. Where headline and detailed terms conflict (Kokobet), hold the offer rather than choosing the more attractive number.
3. Exact domain + legal entity + regulator register + permitted product category + target jurisdiction must all match before a brand can pass the market-specific legal gate. A Curaçao/Anjouan claim is not permission for AU, US, GB, FR, DE, NL, CH, SE, ES, IT, BE or Canada.
4. No real-money account was opened, no deposits were made, no withdrawals were attempted, no support interactions were conducted, and no gambling products were tested in this research batch.
5. These are internal research notes only; do not expose this file in the public site build. Preserve “Not rated / evidence incomplete” on public profiles until all required criteria have evidence.
