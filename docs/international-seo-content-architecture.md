# International SEO and review content architecture

**Status:** Internal working specification. This is not a traffic forecast or permission to promote gambling in any market. Recheck the regulatory matrix and legal position before every market-specific launch.

## Objective

Build BeInCasinos into a high-trust, international casino-review and research publication. The owner's top-10 ambition and 100 million daily search-impressions goal are north stars only. Search rankings and impressions are controlled by search engines and cannot be guaranteed.

## Reader promise

Every useful page should help a reader answer one specific question with current evidence:
- Who is the legal operator behind this domain?
- What exactly does a regulator's register say, and for which product and territory?
- What do the bonus, wagering, withdrawal, and account-verification terms actually mean?
- Which restrictions, exclusions, complaints routes, and safer-gambling tools matter?
- What remains unverified, and when was the information last checked?

## Content clusters

### 1. Brand/operator reviews
Create a profile only after the internal brand-profile publication gate is complete. Required page elements:
- exact brand and domain, legal entity, target jurisdiction, and review date;
- linked primary regulator record and clearly bounded licence/product scope;
- terms and withdrawal findings sourced to official current pages;
- complaint and safer-gambling routes;
- concise pros/limitations grounded in evidence, with no unsupported “best”, “safe”, or “fully licensed everywhere” claims;
- prominent paid-listing and affiliate disclosures, if applicable;
- correction route and next review date.

### 2. Regulatory literacy
Build evergreen explainers on regulator registers, licence scope, legal entity/domain matching, product permissions, geographic restrictions, and how to report a concern. Prefer official regulators and statutes. Never reuse one market's rules as a global answer.

### 3. Terms and money mechanics
Maintain useful explainers and tools for wagering requirements, bonus exclusions, maximum cashout, withdrawal limits, identity checks, fees, payment processing, and complaint escalation. Explain calculations with transparent examples and limitations.

### 4. Consumer protection
Maintain market-specific safer-gambling resources, age restrictions, self-exclusion routes, blocking tools, and official help contacts. Check every contact route and eligibility statement before publication.

### 5. Research and comparison
Publish original methodology, source-backed market explainers, update logs, and comparisons that explain their scope. Do not fabricate user reviews, operator tests, first-hand experience, or statistical samples. Do not use mass-produced near-duplicate pages to inflate page count.

## International market gates

| Market | Working approach |
|---|---|
| GB | Check current UKGC register, exact domain/operator and licence/product scope; follow advertising and affiliate rules. |
| DE | Verify current GGL whitelist and relevant state/product scope; never imply blanket nationwide availability. |
| NL | Check current Ksa licence register, domain and permitted product; review current advertising restrictions. |
| CH | Verify current ESBK/FGB authorised online offer and exact operator/domain. |
| US | Treat each state and product as separate. Do not infer nationwide legality from one state licence. |
| AU | Research-only unless a specific category and promotion are cleared under current Australian law; do not promote prohibited offshore casino offers to Australian users. |
| FR | Research-only unless the precise product and promotional activity are confirmed lawful; do not present offshore online-casino offers as authorised for France. |

This table is an editorial guardrail, not legal advice. Use the target-market-regulatory-matrix.md document for official starting points and re-check the sources at publication time.

## Technical SEO release requirements

- Unique, accurate title and meta description for every indexable page; OG description matches the meta description.
- One clear H1, descriptive internal links, accessible keyboard navigation, meaningful focus states, and responsive mobile layout.
- Canonical URLs point to the chosen HTTPS domain and match the actual production host.
- Sitemap contains only intended public canonical pages that resolve to HTML.
- Do not enable crawling or submit Search Console until launch approval and the release gates are complete.
- Add structured data only when it truthfully describes visible page content. Do not add aggregate ratings, reviews, or organization details that are not supported.
- Add hreflang only after genuinely localized equivalents exist and reciprocal relationships can be maintained. English pages targeting several countries are not automatically separate localized pages.
- No paid link schemes, fabricated testimonials, fake author credentials, cloaking, doorway pages, or automated thin review pages.

## Quality and measurement loop

1. Before publication: source-check, legal/market-check, edit for original utility, accessibility pass, disclose commercial status.
2. After publication: retain the dated evidence log; schedule rechecks for licence, terms, complaints, and support links.
3. Monthly: record Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, top queries/pages, country mix, indexing issues, and corrections. Compare like-for-like periods and annotate changes.
4. Improve pages based on actual reader intent and evidence. Do not promise impressions to partners; do not convert impressions into assumed visitors or revenue.
5. Retire, update, or clearly mark a profile if the licence, domain, market availability, or source evidence changes.

## Commercial independence

A listing fee can buy only the clearly described placement package. It cannot buy a licence claim, positive review, score, search ranking, or inclusion in a “top” ranking. Editorial scoring, corrections, and market eligibility must remain independent of sales. A prospect is not a customer until a documented agreement and payment exist.
