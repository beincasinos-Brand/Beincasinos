## What counts as fully verified

Every candidate remains **HOLD** until the profile contains and an independent second editor checks:
1. Current regulator record, active licence type, exact trading name/domain, and market scope.
2. Current full operator terms and version/date.
3. Payment methods, fees, withdrawal thresholds/timelines, verification rules, and a clear distinction between operator processing and bank settlement.
4. Current bonus terms only if bonuses are discussed: wagering, exclusions, expiry, maximum bet/cashout, and withdrawal restrictions.
5. Complaint route and escalation options, privacy notice, operator contact, and safer-gambling controls/resources.
6. Public regulatory actions/enforcement history and any applicable illegal-site/block list.
7. Source URLs and timestamps, unresolved discrepancies, editorial limitations, and second-editor sign-off.
8. BeInCasinos' own publisher identity, privacy, advertising/affiliate, domain and market-launch gates.

Do not assign scores, claim hands-on testing, imply all games are licensed, or publish generic/thin pages based solely on a register entry.
