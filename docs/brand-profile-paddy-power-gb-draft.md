## 2. Regulatory history — material evidence

The UKGC published a regulatory settlement announcement on **17 December 2025** covering four remote licensees trading under the Paddy Power and Betfair names:
- PPB Entertainment Limited
- PPB Counterparty Services Limited
- Betfair Casino Limited
- TSE Malta LP

The four licensees agreed to a **£2 million total payment in lieu of a financial penalty** after a 2024 compliance assessment found social-responsibility failures in remote customer-interaction controls. The announcement describes examples where customer activity and harm indicators were not identified or acted on promptly.

Official UKGC notice:
https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/paddy-power-betfair-to-pay-gbp2m-for-regulatory-failures

The UKGC notice also says Paddy Power Betfair faced a **£490,000 fine in 2023** for marketing to vulnerable consumers. Retrieve the original 2023 notice and public statement before publication; do not merge this into the 2025 settlement or attribute every group action to every single entity without checking the decision scope.
