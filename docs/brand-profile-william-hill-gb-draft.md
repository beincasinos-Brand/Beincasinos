## 2. Regulatory history — material context

### 28 March 2023 — £19.2 million group enforcement

The UKGC announced £19.2m in total payments across three William Hill group entities for social-responsibility and anti-money-laundering failures:
- WHG (International) Limited: £12.5m.
- Mr Green Limited: £3.7m.
- William Hill Organisation Limited: £3m.

The Commission described examples of customers being able to spend large sums before adequate checks or intervention, failures in customer interaction and risk identification, and ineffective controls that allowed 331 customers to gamble with WHG despite self-excluding with Mr Green. These findings relate to the historical period investigated; they must be stated accurately and not presented as proof of current product behaviour.

### 7 August 2025 — £82,687 penalty

The UKGC imposed an £82,687 financial penalty on WHG (International) Limited after finding a breach of the group-account identification requirement. The regulator noted that the licensee self-identified and proactively reported the breach, took remedial action and cooperated with the investigation. Both the breach and these mitigating details belong in any fair summary.

The UKGC's current register shows active licences, but active status alone is not a consumer-quality rating and does not replace a review of current terms, payments, complaints and safer-gambling controls.
