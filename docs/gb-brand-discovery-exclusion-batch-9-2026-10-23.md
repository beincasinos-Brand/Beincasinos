## Purpose and evidence bar

This is a cautious discovery batch from the UK Gambling Commission public register. A licence entry is not an endorsement or proof of service quality. Domain/trading names are supplied by licensees and may be inaccurate. Do not publish a review until the live domain, terms/footer entity, consumer product, exact remote casino permission, regulatory history, payments/withdrawals, complaints and safer-gambling features are evidenced and independently checked.
