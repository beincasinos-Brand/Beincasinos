The UKGC's current action record identifies two material actions for this licensee:
- **20 May 2025 — £240,000 financial penalty:** breach of remote technical standards (LC 2.3.1). The regulator's published account says affected slot games failed to show the customer's net position and some celebrated losses as wins; the licensee reported the issue and removed the affected games.

The regulator described shortcomings in identifying indicators of harm, acting promptly when those indicators were strong, and a process that delayed further review for seven days after an account was flagged. The regulator's news statement says one customer lost £17,900 within 24 hours without an additional interaction. It also records interim controls, remedial work and cooperation.
