# GB brand research batch 42 — Bet Ahoy / Bet Ahoy Ltd
**Checked:** 22 October 2026 (UTC)  
**Status:** internal evidence lead only; not publication approval.

## Identity and permitted scope
The UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) public register lists **Bet Ahoy Ltd**, account **100094**, with active Remote Bingo, Casino, General Betting Standard — Real Event, and General Betting Standard — Virtual Event permissions from 17 December 2025. The UKGC domain register lists **www.betahoy.co.uk** as an active domain. A separate current brand-by-brand check is still required to confirm the live site's legal disclosures, trading-name entry and exact product scope before publication.

## What this establishes — and what it does not
The regulator record is useful evidence of the operator's currently recorded GB remote permissions and one registered domain. It does not establish that every product or promotion advertised is available to every user, that withdrawals are fast, that support is effective, or that the site is eligible for affiliate promotion in any jurisdiction outside the confirmed market.
