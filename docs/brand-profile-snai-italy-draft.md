### Withdrawals — operator-stated, not independently tested

SNAI's withdrawal FAQ lists method-specific thresholds and states that many methods are generally processed immediately, subject to sample checks that may take around 12 hours. It describes card settlement as dependent on whether the issuing bank supports real-time payment protocols; where it does not, the bank may take 2–3 working days, and checks can extend the overall period. It lists bank transfer with a €10 minimum and a stated daily maximum of €30,000, and describes postal domiciliation as taking 4–5 working days. These are operator-published statements, not verified test results; recheck every method and footnote on publication day.

The FAQ says withdrawals are limited to winnings, identity documents must be provided, and the withdrawal instrument/account holder must match the gambling-account holder. Bonuses and unused deposits are not withdrawable under the described rules, subject to the account-closure exception for unused deposits.

Source: https://www.snai.it/faq/prelievi

SNAI's responsible-gambling page links to help resources and states customer-care availability as 24/7. Verify current self-limitation, time-out, self-exclusion and national exclusion controls, and confirm the current support channels and hours before publication.
