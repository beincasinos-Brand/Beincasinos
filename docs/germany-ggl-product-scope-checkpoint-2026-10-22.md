## Critical product-scope distinction

The GGL whitelist separates **virtual slot games** from **online casino games** such as roulette, blackjack and baccarat. The GGL explains that online-casino table-game permissions are handled by the relevant German states; the GGL's whitelist also includes offerings overseen by other German authorities. Therefore, a brand appearing with a virtual-slots permission is not evidence that the same site may offer every table game or a full online casino in every German state.

The whitelist is updated as needed, at least monthly. Its 2 October 2026 snapshot is a dated reference only. Recheck the current whitelist and the relevant state authority before publication.

4. Confirm that the consumer-facing site currently offers the product being reviewed in the relevant state.
5. Verify current terms, bonus conditions, deposits/withdrawals, responsible-gambling controls, privacy/cookies, support and complaints.
6. Check recent regulatory changes and enforcement.
7. Only record UX/payment/gameplay testing that was actually performed and permitted.
