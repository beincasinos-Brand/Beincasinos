## Important limitations

- This is a first-pass official-register evidence log, not a complete legal audit, consumer test, terms audit or quality ranking.
- A listed domain, trading name, or active licence for a related entity does not establish that every casino product is permitted under that account.
- The UKGC register is specific to Great Britain. It must not be presented as proof of legality in Morocco or any other market.
- No customer support, bonus, withdrawal, account-opening or payment test has been performed for this batch. Do not write first-hand claims.
- No numeric score and no public review is approved from this evidence alone.
