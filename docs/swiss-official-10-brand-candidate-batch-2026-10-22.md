## Counting and publication controls

- This batch contains **10 regulator-listed candidate platforms**, not 10 completed reviews.
- Do not infer that every game is authorised merely because the platform is listed: ESBK states that an online licence extension and permits for individual games are required.
- Do not add ratings, “best” claims, bonus amounts, payment-speed claims or first-hand testing claims until evidence has been captured and independently reviewed.
- Capture the current terms version/date, promotion terms (if discussed), deposit/withdrawal rules, KYC, payment-method restrictions, privacy and cookies, responsible-gambling controls, customer support and complaints/ADR route, and relevant regulator actions.
- Require an independent second editor and legal/editorial sign-off. Keep each candidate HOLD until every gate is evidenced.
- Official count toward the programme's **approved reviews remains 0/100** until the approval ledger is updated under the repository's hard gates.
