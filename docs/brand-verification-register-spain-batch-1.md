## Scope and evidence rules

The DGOJ finder associates legal entities with reported consumer domains. An entity appearing in the finder or a listed `.es` domain does **not**, by itself, establish permission for every game type. Before a brand can be reviewed or counted toward the 100-brand target, verify the current legal entity, exact consumer domain, general licence, relevant singular licence for online casino games, current terms, payment/withdrawal rules, bonus terms, safer-gambling controls, privacy/contact details, complaints process, and any material regulator action. Preserve source URLs and check dates. No testing deposit or withdrawal has been performed.

Multiple domains associated with one legal entity are not automatically separate brands. This batch intentionally contains candidates for distinct consumer-facing brand/domain investigations, not a claim that all entries have current casino-game permissions.
