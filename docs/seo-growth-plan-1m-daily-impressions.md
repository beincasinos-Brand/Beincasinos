# SEO growth plan — 1,000,000 daily Google Search impressions

**North-star ambition:** 1,000,000 Google Search impressions per day. This is an ambition, not a forecast or guarantee. No baseline Search Console data is available yet, the domain is not confirmed live, and no pages should be described as indexed until verified.

## What the metric means

Search Console impressions count eligible appearances of a site's links in Google Search; impressions are not clicks, users, revenue, or proof of ranking stability. Track impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, query, page, country and device together. Use a 28-day and 90-day view and annotate releases. Source: [Google Search Console performance reports](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/10268906?hl=en).

## Release gates — do not bypass

1. Owner verifies domain and mailbox access, Cloudflare account security, and current DNS records.
2. Complete publisher identity, privacy, advertising/affiliate disclosure, trademark, and applicable market legal reviews.
3. Keep Australia and France online-casino promotion blocked pending qualified review; verify US state/product eligibility and exact operator/domain before market-specific claims or outbound commercial links in GB, DE, CH and NL.
4. Keep pre-launch crawl block in place until the owner explicitly authorizes public launch after acceptance testing.
5. Publish operator profiles only after exact domain, legal operator, licence/product scope, territory, terms, withdrawal policy, complaint route, safer-gambling tools and evidence date are verified from primary sources.

## Growth principles

- Build a focused, English-language evidence and consumer-literacy resource before expanding into translated markets.
- Prioritise original explanations, practical checklists, calculators, transparent methodology, dated primary-source references, and content that answers a genuine reader question.
- Do not mass-publish near-duplicate city/state/country pages, scraped operator descriptions, fake first-hand reviews, unsupported scores, or pages made only to capture keyword variations.
- Do not buy links, manufacture reviews, or promise rankings to commercial partners.
- Do not use a paid listing as a reason to alter editorial conclusions. Label commercial placements clearly and keep them separate from evidence-based findings.
- Treat high-risk gambling information with stronger sourcing, expert review, and explicit jurisdiction limits. A general educational page is not a licence or legal clearance.

## Measurement milestones

These are checkpoints, not promised deadlines or traffic forecasts.

| Phase | Evidence required | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — Pre-launch | DNS/domain verified, legal/privacy gates documented, preview acceptance passed | Only then consider public launch |
| 1 — Discovery | Search Console verified, sitemap submitted after launch, crawl/index reports reviewed | Fix discovery, canonical, robots and coverage issues |
| 2 — Baseline | First 28 days of valid Search Console data | Record impressions/clicks/CTR/position by page, query, country and device |
| 3 — Early learning | Repeatedly appearing queries and pages identified | Improve pages with relevant impressions and weak CTR; fill demonstrated content gaps |
| 4 — Topic authority | Multiple useful pages in clusters earn impressions and natural citations | Expand only where data and original expertise justify it |
| 5 — Scale | Sustained 90-day trend, no major policy/security issues, content quality maintained | Increase editorial capacity and carefully review additional languages/markets |
| 6 — North-star review | Rolling daily impressions measured in Search Console; compare 28/90-day trends | Report distance to 1M/day honestly; do not substitute indexed-page counts for visibility |

## Content architecture

1. **Trust and methodology:** about the publisher, author/reviewer credentials, editorial policy, affiliate disclosure, corrections, evidence dates, privacy and contact.
2. **Licensing literacy:** exact legal entity vs brand, domain matching, licence scope, jurisdiction differences, how to verify regulator records, what a licence does not mean.
3. **Terms literacy:** wagering requirement arithmetic, eligible games, maximum bet rules, expiry, maximum cashout, withdrawal cancellation, terms-change records.
4. **Payments literacy:** verification steps, processing stages, limits, fees, payment-method ownership, how to document a delay, formal complaint routes.
5. **Game maths literacy:** RTP, theoretical vs observed RTP, volatility, probability vs return, house edge, why short sessions differ from long-run averages.
6. **Consumer research methods:** evaluating complaint data, distinguishing allegations from findings, source quality, date and denominator, comparing review methodologies.
7. **Tools:** calculators and checklists that explain assumptions and limitations, with no promise of winnings or advice to gamble.
8. **Market pages:** create only after a qualified market-specific review and current official source verification; no blanket claims that a licence applies worldwide.

## Operating cadence after launch

- Weekly: review Search Console queries/pages/countries/devices, crawl/index coverage, security/manual-action reports and errors.
- Monthly: refresh pages only where primary sources changed or the content materially improved; record what changed and why.
- Quarterly: review the topic map, legal/market scope, disclosures, internal links, and which content genuinely helps readers.
- Every material operator claim: keep primary source URL, exact entity/domain, market/product scope, evidence date and next review date.
- Every content experiment: define a question and success measure before changing the page; compare periods fairly and avoid claiming causation from small samples.
