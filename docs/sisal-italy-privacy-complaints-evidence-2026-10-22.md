## Operator-published privacy observations

Sisal's current privacy summary identifies Sisal Italia S.p.A. as a data controller for relevant online gaming activities. It describes rights requests through privacy_sisal@legalmail.it, a cookie/SDK consent-management route, and retention periods that include ten years after the gaming contract ends in the stated circumstances and two years for certain responsible-gambling monitoring and profiling purposes. These are operator-published statements; this file does not replace a legal review of the full applicable privacy notice, cookie inventory, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, or actual consent behaviour.

## Complaints and player support

The official contact page says customers should first contact Sisal customer care about an account matter, then use the ADM reporting portal via SPID or CIE if the answer is unsatisfactory or no response is received. It also lists support contact details and a national responsible-gambling support number with weekday hours. These details should be rechecked at publication, and the review must distinguish an operator complaint channel from an independent dispute-resolution service.
