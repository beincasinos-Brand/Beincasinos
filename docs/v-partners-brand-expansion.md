## Initial first-party findings

- The network's official portfolio currently presents the seven brands above.
- Its individual pages publish launch-year, game-provider, language, currency and restricted-geography information. Treat those as network-published statements with a check date, not as regulator-confirmed facts.
- Examples of explicit restrictions shown on first-party pages include Great Britain for Slotoro, FieryPlay, Hit’n’Spin, Verde Casino, ICE Casino and FS Casino; additional restrictions differ by brand. Recheck each page and the intended market before publication. Never imply that a brand is available in a market merely because its site loads.
- The network's naming update has associated former Vulkan brand names with V.Partners' newer names. Keep legacy-name/redirect checks in the research log so old domains, operator terms and historical enforcement records are not accidentally attributed to the wrong current brand.

## Required verification for every brand

1. Capture the exact official consumer domain by following the network's own “Visit website” link; record redirects and the final canonical URL.
2. Save the current consumer terms and identify the contracting legal entity, company number/address and applicable governing law.
3. Check the relevant official regulator register for the entity, active licence, permitted product, exact domain and target-market scope. A network listing or a generic “licensed” statement is insufficient.
4. Reconcile any legacy brand names, redirects, mirrored domains and historical enforcement records.
5. Review current bonus terms, wagering rules, withdrawal limits/timing/fees, payment methods, complaints/ADR, privacy, account closure and safer-gambling tools.
6. Compare the brand's own restricted-geography list with regulator rules and the site's actual market terms; record conflicts rather than resolving them by assumption.
7. Record dated primary-source URLs, unresolved conflicts, commercial/affiliate disclosure and the exact market being reviewed.
8. Require second-editor approval before a public review, rating, recommendation or “verified” label.

## Publication decision

All seven brands remain **HOLD**. These are research leads, not seven approved reviews and not proof that every brand is legally available in any specific country. Do not count them as approved brands until all identity, licence, market, terms, evidence and second-editor gates pass.
