# Editorial review and ranking rubric

**Internal policy draft — 20 October 2026.** Use only after editorial/legal review. Scores must not be generated for an operator until the eligibility gates below pass for a specific jurisdiction and product.

## Non-negotiable eligibility gates (pass/fail, not points)

A brand is ineligible for a market-specific recommendation/ranking if any of these cannot be evidenced:
- Exact domain and legal operating entity are identified.
- Current official regulator/register evidence confirms the relevant entity/domain/product scope where a licence is required.
- The product is permitted for the intended market and the site's planned description/promotion is lawful.
- Current official terms and withdrawal rules can be reviewed.
- Material red flags, restrictions, regulatory actions, or unresolved identity mismatches are documented.
- Required commercial disclosures and safer-gambling information can be presented clearly.

If a gate fails or evidence is missing, label the profile “Not scored — verification incomplete” or do not publish it. Do not turn uncertainty into a low numeric score that could be mistaken for approval.

## Weighted editorial assessment (100 points after eligibility gates)

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence examples |
|---|---:|---|
| Terms clarity and fairness | 25 | Clear bonus conditions, wagering base, game restrictions, expiry, max cashout, and accessible full terms. |
| Payments and withdrawals transparency | 20 | Clear limits, processing stages, fees, verification requirements, complaint steps, and no misleading certainty. |
| Safer-gambling controls and information | 20 | Visible limits/time-outs/self-exclusion, age information, support signposting, and clear product restrictions. |
| Complaints and customer-support transparency | 15 | Clear support routes, formal complaint process, applicable escalation route, and response expectations where evidenced. |
| Operator and licence transparency | 10 | Clear legal entity, exact domain, current licence details, and links to official register evidence. Eligibility gate still applies separately. |
| Usability and information accessibility | 10 | Terms are findable/readable, important restrictions are not hidden, and key help/account controls are reasonably accessible. |

## Criterion rating anchors (0–5)

Use the same anchors for each of the six criteria, supported by criterion-specific evidence notes:

- **0 — Material failure documented:** current primary evidence shows a serious failure or a key required safeguard/information is absent after a direct, documented check.
- **1 — Very weak:** substantial gaps or materially unclear information remain despite a completed check.
- **2 — Weak:** several meaningful gaps, restrictions, or ambiguities are evidenced.
- **3 — Adequate:** the main information and controls are present and usable, but limitations remain.
- **4 — Strong:** clear, accessible and comparatively comprehensive information/control evidence, with only limited gaps.
- **5 — Exemplary transparency:** unusually clear, complete, accessible, and current evidence for the criterion, with the scope and limitations stated.

Do not score a criterion from absence of research, inaccessible sources, or an unverified assumption. Mark it “not assessed” and withhold the total. A 0 is reserved for a documented finding, not missing evidence. Record examples and source links for every assigned rating.

**Calculation:** criterion points = (criterion rating ÷ 5) × criterion weight. Add all six criterion points for a total out of 100. Do not round individual criterion points; round only the final displayed total to the nearest whole number. A total is publishable only when all six criteria are assessed and all eligibility gates pass.

**Interpretation bands:** 90–100 strong documented transparency; 80–89 good documented transparency with stated limitations; 70–79 mixed evidence or meaningful gaps; 60–69 significant weaknesses; below 60 material weaknesses in assessed criteria. These are editorial descriptions only, not legal, safety, or regulatory ratings. Eligibility gates and material red flags always override a numeric total.

## Evidence and scoring rules

- Every criterion requires a short evidence note, primary-source URL, date checked, and reviewer.
- Separate observed facts, operator claims, third-party reports, and editorial interpretation.
- Do not claim first-hand play, deposit, withdrawal, customer-service tests, or mystery shopping unless the test was actually performed and documented.
- If a criterion cannot be assessed, mark it “not assessed” and withhold the total score; do not silently redistribute weights or invent a neutral score.
- Scores describe the reviewed product and jurisdiction at a stated date, not a universal guarantee of safety, legality, fairness, or future performance.
- Set a re-review trigger for licence changes, terms changes, domain/operator changes, regulatory action, or credible complaints. Once the publication is operating, schedule a routine evidence refresh at least every 90 days, keep a visible “last checked” date and next-check target, and mark/suspend a profile if a material check is overdue or cannot be re-verified. This cadence is a planned operating commitment, not proof that any current profile is monitored.
- Do not combine products/markets into one score if legal scope or terms differ materially.

## Ranking and “top 10” rules

- Publish a top-10 ranking only for a named market/product scope, after enough eligible profiles have completed the same rubric.
- State the methodology, scope, date range, eligibility gates, and material limitations next to the ranking.
- Do not call a list “international top 10” if entries are not comparable across legal markets and product scopes. Prefer clearly bounded market-specific rankings.
- Commercial fees, affiliate commission, sponsorship, operator outreach, and revenue must never change scores, ranking order, eligibility, or review conclusions.
- If paid placement exists, separate it visually from editorial rankings and label it “Paid listing.” Do not sell guaranteed inclusion in a top-10 ranking.
- Keep rejected/ineligible brands out of ranked lists, even if they pay for a listing.

## Corrections and conflicts

Reviewers must disclose any relevant personal, financial, or commercial conflict to the editor. Correct material errors promptly and keep a dated internal change log. If evidence is disputed, link the primary sources and state what is unresolved rather than asserting certainty.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Market/product scope named.
- [ ] Every eligibility gate evidenced and passed.
- [ ] All six criteria scored from documented evidence.
- [ ] No criterion marked not assessed.
- [ ] Score math checked and reviewed by a second person.
- [ ] Commercial relationship checked and disclosed.
- [ ] Last checked and next review date assigned.
- [ ] Copy, links, mobile layout, keyboard use, and corrections route checked.
- [ ] Ranking language is scoped and supported; no guaranteed “best/safest” claim.
