## Editorial and compliance gates
The UKGC explains that the business register's domain and trading-name information is provided by licensees and may be inaccurate: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/businesses/. A live homepage plus terms supports an identity/product lead, but is not a substitute for a complete review dossier. The official consumer guidance also advises checking licence identity, domains, and the product terms before relying on them: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-and-players/guide/what-to-look-at-before-you-gamble.

Before a brand is approved for publication, record dated evidence for: legal entity and licence scope; site/domain and redirect status; bonus terms; payment methods and withdrawal rules; identity/KYC; customer-support channels and test results; responsible-gambling tools; complaints/ADR; regulatory history; editorial disclosures; and any hands-on testing actually performed. Do not invent ratings, payout speed, personal experience, or availability in a target country.
