## Regulatory actions requiring accurate attribution
The UKGC regulatory-actions page for account 39028 records:
1. **19 August 2024 — £23,000 financial penalty:** breach of licence condition 6.1.2 concerning use of credit cards. The regulator notes the licensee self-identified the issue, took remedial action and cooperated.
2. **7 August 2025 — £33,075 financial penalty:** failure to comply with customer-identification requirements across accounts held with the licensee and a group company. The regulator notes self-identification and remedial action.
3. **18 November 2025 — £86,843 financial penalty:** breach of SRCP 3.5.3 concerning self-exclusion. The regulator describes the failure as human error, says it did not affect controls preventing self-excluded customers from logging in or gambling, and notes the licensee self-reported, cooperated and took remedial action.

These are regulator-recorded findings for **888 UK Limited**, not proof that every 888-branded service or operator in every country has the same legal entity or regulatory history. Present the regulator's context alongside the outcome; do not overstate the finding.
