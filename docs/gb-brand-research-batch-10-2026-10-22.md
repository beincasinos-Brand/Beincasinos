# Great Britain brand research batch 10 — Stakelogic BV primary enforcement record

**Checked:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** internal evidence only; supplier/operator mapping required before any consumer-facing attribution.  
**Primary source:** UK Gambling Commission, Stakelogic BV regulatory action, decision date 25 June 2026: https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/public-register/regulatory-action/detail/329

## Primary-source findings

The UKGC public-register decision records a £122,835 payment in lieu of a financial penalty, Commission costs and a public statement. Stakelogic BV reported that an online slots game offered to Great Britain consumers did not comply with Responsible Product Design Remote Technical Standard 14D, which required at least 2.5 seconds from starting one game cycle to commencing the next.

The Commission's record states:
- the licence-condition breach related to RTS 14D between 31 October 2021 and 30 October 2025;
- a further 15 games were non-compliant by smaller margins;
- Stakelogic disabled all games available to the GB market once the scope became clear;
- the issue arose partly because timing checks used a manual stopwatch, which the Commission considered open to significant inaccuracy;
- internal quality-assurance and incident-management controls fell short of expected standards.

The decision says the fault had been corrected by the time the licensee informed the Commission. Treat that as the regulator's record of the operator's report, not as independent BeInCasinos testing. Check the linked public statement for full context before publishing detailed claims.

## Attribution limits

This is a supplier-level regulatory action about Stakelogic BV's games and controls. It does **not** establish that every casino offering games from Stakelogic had the same defect, knew of the defect, or breached its own licence. A consumer brand profile should mention the matter only where material and accurately scoped to the game supplier and relevant period. Do not use this event as a proxy score for every operator.

For any game/provider-related page:

2. identify whether the reviewed operator currently offers affected games and whether the claim remains relevant;
3. distinguish historic technical non-compliance from current availability and remediation;
4. avoid unsupported claims about game fairness, current speed, certification or present product status.
