# Market eligibility gate — BeInCasinos target geographies

Scope: AU, CA, GB, US, CH, DE, NL, SE, ES, IT, FR, BE. This is an editorial control, not legal advice and not a statement that any named operator is authorised.

## Required record per brand-market pair
Record market and state/province where applicable; official regulator and direct register URL; licensed legal entity and licence ID; exact consumer domain and redirect/white-label relationship; authorised remote activity including casino games; register status/date; whether residents are accepted; official market-specific terms and bonus rules; exclusions, unresolved questions, reviewer and evidence date.

## Market-specific cautions
- AU: confirm applicable federal/state/territory rules and whether the specific online casino service may lawfully be offered. Offshore accessibility is not permission.
- CA: determine province and product model. Ontario status must not be inferred for other provinces.
- GB: match exact domain and contracting entity to the UK Gambling Commission register and remote casino permission; check enforcement history and terms.
- US: specify state. Verify state regulator, operator/skin relationship and online casino permission. Sportsbook availability is not casino authorisation.
- CH: verify current Swiss authority approved casino/operator/domain information and product scope.
- DE: check current German regulator records, permitted offer, exact domain and entity.
- NL: verify Kansspelautoriteit register, entity, domain and product scope.
- SE: verify Spelinspektionen register, entity, domain and product scope.
- ES: verify DGOJ authorisation for exact operator, domain and game types.
- IT: verify current ADM concession/operator/domain and online casino products.
- FR: do not assume online casino games are authorised because sports betting or poker is licensed; verify the exact product's legal status.
- BE: verify current Belgian Gaming Commission licence, linked domain and relevant casino-class permission.

These are focus points, not substitutes for current official-register checks. Re-check primary sources immediately before publication.

## Outcomes
PASS: market, domain, entity, licence and casino product permission evidenced. HOLD: a material field is missing, contradictory, inaccessible or stale. EXCLUDE: official evidence indicates the service is not permitted or identity cannot be reconciled. RECHECK: a passed item is stale or material rules/terms changed.

Do not claim legal status, licensing, availability or safety based only on marketing copy, affiliate listings, an offshore licence or search snippets. Cite the official regulator record and identify the legal entity and product scope.
