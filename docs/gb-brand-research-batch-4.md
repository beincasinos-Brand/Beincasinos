## Evidence limits

- The official register is a primary source for recorded licence status and declared trading names/domains, but the Commission warns that business-supplied names and domains are not guaranteed to be accurate.
- This batch is not a complete legal audit, terms audit, payment/withdrawal test, support test, or quality ranking.
- No hands-on play, account opening, deposit, withdrawal, complaint outcome, or customer-support test has been performed. Do not claim otherwise.
- A Great Britain licence does not establish permission in Morocco, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Germany, or any other jurisdiction.
- A brand with unresolved identity, product, market or terms evidence stays **HOLD**; do not assign a score or publish a recommendation.
