## 2. Terms and promotion snapshot

The official terms page shows version 1.0.0, effective 15 September 2025. It names Bet Theory (Malta) Limited as the contracting entity and says the services for people located in Great Britain are licensed by the UK Gambling Commission.

The homepage's sportsbook welcome offer at the time of checking says “bet £10 get £20 free bets”: a qualifying £10 bet at minimum decimal odds 2.0 must settle; cash-out is excluded; the reward is four £5 free bets; each free bet requires at least three selections at combined odds of 3.0; returns exclude the free-bet stake; payment exclusions apply. These are headline conditions only. The complete live promotion terms must be archived and checked before any consumer-facing review.

The promotions page also lists a separate casino welcome promotion (“Deposit & Wager £10, Get 50 Free Spins on Big Bass Blast”). Eligibility, expiry, wagering and withdrawal restrictions must be captured from the full terms before this is described as a recommendation.

## 3. Payments, complaints and customer protection

- The terms say accepted deposit methods are listed in the app's Deposits and Withdrawals screen and may change. This research did not create an account or test payment flows, fees, limits, or withdrawal timings.
- The homepage advertises “super fast withdrawals” and says withdrawals are processed straight away. This is an operator marketing claim, not independently measured evidence; do not repeat it as a verified fact.
- The terms summary states that **customer funds have no protection** (terms clause 11.2). This material qualification must be stated plainly if the dossier is later turned into a review.
- The terms summary says customers can contact support@stakemate.com; if the complaint is not resolved within eight weeks, the customer may refer it to the Independent Betting Adjudication Services (IBAS), subject to the stated contractual-scope conditions. Recheck the full current clause and IBAS eligibility before publication.
