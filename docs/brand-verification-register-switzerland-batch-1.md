## Counting rules — do not inflate the target

- **10 regulator-listed candidate platforms** in this Swiss batch.
- **0 fully publication-approved brands** in this batch at the time of this register.
- A brand only counts toward the goal of 100 verified brands after: exact current regulator entry and jurisdictional scope; operator/legal entity; current full terms; withdrawals and fees; current bonus conditions if discussed; game-permit scope; complaints/escalation route; privacy/contact; safer-gambling controls and resources; enforcement/blocked-list check; dated source log; and independent second-editor approval.
- A regulator listing verifies only the limited claim that the platform appears in that regulator's current list. It does not prove every individual game is permitted, payout claims are true in practice, the operator has no enforcement history, or the brand is legal in other countries.
- Never infer test results, ratings, payout speed, customer-service quality, commercial willingness-to-pay, or affiliate relationships from public copy alone.
- Do not create thin public pages solely to increase page count. Keep these records in internal docs until BeInCasinos' own publisher identity, privacy, advertising/affiliate, domain, and market-launch gates are approved.
