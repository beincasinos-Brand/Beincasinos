## Official regulator evidence

Its official [Online gambling page](https://www.esbk.admin.ch/en/online-gambling), published 17 March 2026 and crawled again in October 2026, lists ten online platforms, their web addresses, and the start date of each online operation. The official [unauthorised online games page](https://www.esbk.admin.ch/en/unauthorised-online-games), updated 25 August 2026, explains that Switzerland publishes a list of unauthorised platforms and requires access restrictions for covered sites.
