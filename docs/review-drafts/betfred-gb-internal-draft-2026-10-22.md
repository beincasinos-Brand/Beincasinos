## Regulatory record and safer-gambling context

The UKGC published a settlement on 30 June 2026 requiring Petfre (Gibraltar) Limited to make a £900,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty, plus Commission costs and a public statement. The review concerned remote customer-interaction/social-responsibility controls. The Commission described gaps in identifying and responding quickly to indicators of harm, including a case where a customer lost £17,900 within 24 hours without an additional interaction. The Commission also said the licensee had introduced interim mitigating controls and delivered an action plan. A fair review should report the findings and the regulator's stated remedial steps together, without treating either as proof of current performance.

A separate UKGC action dated 20 May 2025 imposed a £240,000 financial penalty for a technical-standards breach following a key-event report. Keep this distinct from the 2026 settlement.
