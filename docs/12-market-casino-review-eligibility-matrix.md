# 12-market casino review eligibility matrix

**Scope from owner:** AU, CA, GB, US, CH, DE, NL, SE, ES, IT, FR, BE. This is a research checklist, not legal advice. A market label must never be inferred from a casino's language, currency, international licence or affiliate availability.

## Gate for every brand-market pair

- Match the exact consumer domain to the legal operator in a primary source.
- Find the current official regulator register for the relevant territory and verify remote casino permission for the relevant product (not merely sports betting, land-based activity or a historic licence).
- Record source URL, date checked, licence/account identifier, permitted products and any territory restrictions.
- Check official terms and responsible-gambling/complaints pages for that same domain and market.
- If the evidence is blocked, contradictory, outdated or not product-specific, use **HOLD**. Never infer permission from a search snippet.
- Record market availability as one of: confirmed permitted; confirmed excluded; unclear/blocked; not researched. Only the first can progress to review-ready.

## Market-specific research notes

| Market | Required primary-source check | Editorial handling |
|---|---|---|
| Australia (AU) | Current federal and relevant state/territory official gambling-law guidance; verify product type and local restrictions | Do not assume an overseas online casino is lawful or available to Australian residents. Distinguish online casino from permitted wagering products. |
| Canada (CA) | Provincial/territorial regulator and official operator lists; for Ontario use the relevant provincial regulator and registered-operator evidence | Do not generalize Ontario status to all Canada. State the province explicitly. |
| Great Britain (GB) | UK Gambling Commission public register and licence conditions for the exact operator/domain/product | GB eligibility is not interchangeable with the wider UK or an offshore licence. |
| United States (US) | State regulator register and state-specific online-casino permission | Name the state on every page; a national US casino claim is insufficient. Do not conflate sportsbook permission with online casino permission. |
| Switzerland (CH) | Federal Gaming Board (ESBK/CFMJ) or Intercantonal Gaming Supervisory Authority (GESPA) official lists, according to product/type | Verify whether the operator/domain is authorised for the specific Swiss product; offshore availability is not proof of permission. |
| Germany (DE) | Gemeinsame Glücksspielbehörde der Länder (GGL) official whitelist and product-specific permissions | Check the exact domain and permitted game category. Do not treat a generic licence statement as sufficient. |
| Netherlands (NL) | Kansspelautoriteit (Ksa) official licence holder list and current licence scope | Verify the exact legal entity, domain and licensed product; check current restrictions and advertising rules. |
| Sweden (SE) | Spelinspektionen official licence register and licence type | Confirm the consumer brand/domain is covered and distinguish online casino from other licence categories. |
| Spain (ES) | Dirección General de Ordenación del Juego (DGOJ) official operator/authorisation records | Confirm Spanish authorisation and exact product category; Spanish-language content alone is not evidence. |
| Italy (IT) | Agenzia delle Dogane e dei Monopoli (ADM) official concession/operator records | Verify the current concession and domain. Treat blocked or unavailable official terms as an unresolved evidence gap. |
| France (FR) | Autorité Nationale des Jeux (ANJ) official guidance and authorised-operator/product scope | Do not imply online casino slots are authorised simply because an operator is known for other regulated gambling products. |
| Belgium (BE) | Belgian Gaming Commission (Kansspelcommissie / Commission des jeux de hasard) official licence lists and domain/product scope | Verify the correct licence class and authorised website. Check local age and advertising requirements. |

## Editorial disposition

- **REVIEW-READY:** official source supports the precise brand/domain/operator/product/market match; consumer terms and protection evidence collected.
- **HOLD:** any material ambiguity, inaccessible official page, incomplete source set or domain mismatch.
- **OUT OF SCOPE:** confirmed not eligible for the stated market/product; do not publish a recommendation page implying availability.
- **CANDIDATE:** not yet researched; not a verified brand.

Review each market separately. The matrix is a workflow guardrail, not a substitute for checking the current rules at publication time.
