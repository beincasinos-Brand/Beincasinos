## Publication rules
1. Never show a numeric bonus unless the amount, currency, eligibility and core restrictions are confirmed on the exact market/domain.
2. If the banner conflicts with detailed terms, pause the offer card and document the conflict. Do not select the most attractive number.
3. Do not infer a promotion for a market from another locale.
4. Do not convert “up to” into a guaranteed amount or free spins into cash.
5. Never imply a withdrawal time has been tested unless an actual dated test was performed and the method/account conditions are documented.
6. Use “Bonus not verified” when the page is inaccessible, blocked, stale, expired or unclear.
7. Do not scrape or republish copyrighted artwork; record image source and rights.
8. Show affiliate disclosure clearly and do not let commission affect the review score.
9. Check the exact legal domain/operator/product for the target jurisdiction before presenting a brand as available there.
10. Recheck after terms changes, new campaign banners, operator/domain changes, or reported disputes.

## Suggested UI states
- Verified: display sourced offer details and “Terms checked [date]” with link to full terms.
- Conflict: suppress the promotional amount; show a neutral “Offer terms are inconsistent; check operator terms directly” note only if useful.
- Unavailable: “Current bonus not verified.”
- Expired: remove the offer from active cards; archive only when useful and label historical.
- Market mismatch: do not show the offer on that market page.
