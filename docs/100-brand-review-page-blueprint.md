# Premium casino review page blueprint

Use this outline for every approved brand-market page. Write original, evidence-led copy; never duplicate a generic paragraph across dozens of brands and change only the name.

## Page identity

- **H1:** [Brand] Casino Review — [Market] (only after eligibility is confirmed)
- Short factual introduction: what product was reviewed, for which market, and when evidence was checked.
- Visible review/update date, author/editor, methodology link and commercial disclosure.
- A clear availability note based on the market gate. If not confirmed, do not publish as an available casino recommendation.

## Above-the-fold comparison card

Include only verified values:
- Brand name and licensed operator (with source link).
- Market status and regulator source.
- Overall score only after the scorecard is complete and independently reviewed.
- Bonus headline only if the exact current offer and terms were verified; otherwise show “No current offer verified”.
- Main verified game categories.
- Payments/withdrawals: link to official policy; do not promise a speed.
- Main advantage and main limitation, both evidence-backed.
- CTA text must not imply guaranteed winnings or pressure readers to gamble.
- Affiliate label adjacent to monetised CTA; never let commission change rank.

## Main review sections

1. **Verdict and suitable audience:** evidence-backed strengths, limitations and who should avoid it.
2. **Licence, operator and availability:** exact entity, domain, market and product permission.
3. **Bonus and terms:** offer date, eligibility, minimum deposit, wagering multiplier and calculation base, max bet, expiry, excluded games, withdrawal cap and other material terms. Include a direct official source.
4. **Games:** verified categories and named providers only where current evidence exists; explain catalogue varies by market.
5. **Deposits and withdrawals:** supported methods, minimum/maximum, fees, stated processing windows, verification requirements and missing evidence.
6. **Mobile and usability:** distinguish direct testing from published specifications; record device/browser/date for testing claims.
7. **Customer support:** channels, hours and actual test results only if documented.
8. **Player protection:** age rules, self-exclusion, limits, reality checks, support links and complaint/ADR process.
9. **Pros and cons:** concise, specific and sourced.
10. **Score breakdown:** category scores, weights, rationale, evidence links and limitations.
11. **FAQ:** answer only questions supported by current official evidence; avoid invented payout, bonus or licence claims.
12. **Sources and updates:** primary-source list, last checked date, correction route and material changes log.

## Editorial and SEO safeguards

- A market-specific page must add meaningful market-specific information, not merely country-name substitutions.
- Keep one canonical URL per true brand-market intent. Do not create near-identical country pages without distinct evidence and utility.
- Avoid doorway pages, keyword stuffing, fake first-hand testing, fake customer reviews, unsubstantiated “best” claims and invented review counts.
- Use structured data only for content visible on the page and eligible under the relevant search-engine rules. Never mark up fabricated aggregate ratings.
- Add contextual links to methodology, local eligibility guidance, payments explainer, safer-gambling page and relevant comparison pages.
- Compress and optimise images; use descriptive alt text that describes the image, not a list of keywords.
- Add a clear corrections/contact route and review cadence. Update when licence, terms, domain, bonus or product status changes.
