## Editorial / SEO safeguards

1. Keep each candidate at HOLD until the brand-domain-licensee chain is explicit.
2. Record the exact evidence date and source URL for licence status, domain mapping, regulatory actions, terms, payments, withdrawal rules and complaints.
3. Distinguish an editorial review from a verified hands-on test. Never fabricate play, deposits, withdrawals, customer support contacts, bonus outcomes, or screenshots.
4. Include responsible-gambling and jurisdiction eligibility information; do not target self-excluded or underage users.
5. No unsupported "best", "safe", "fastest payout", or "guaranteed win" claims. Score only on documented criteria and disclose affiliate/commercial relationships.
6. UKGC says domain names and trading names are supplied by the gambling business and may not be accurate; corroborate them rather than treating a register entry as proof of user experience.
