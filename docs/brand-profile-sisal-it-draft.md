## Responsible-gambling and service-contract evidence

Sisal's responsible-gambling FAQ explains that outcomes in chance-based games are independent and that repeated play does not improve the odds of recovering previous losses. Its online-gaming concession contract includes a player complaint process and states that the operator must respond to account-related complaints within 72 hours; if the player is dissatisfied or receives no response, the document describes escalation to ADM's designated portal. The same contract text describes winnings withdrawal timing obligations, but the public-facing review must check the currently applicable agreement and not assume every case follows a single timetable.

These are operator-published materials and contractual text, not an independent test of support response times or withdrawals.
