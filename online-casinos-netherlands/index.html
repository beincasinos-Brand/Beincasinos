Online casinos in the Netherlands: only with a KSA licence

Since 1 October 2021 online casino games are legal in the Netherlands only for operators licensed by the Kansspelautoriteit.

How to check an operator in the Netherlands

The regulator is the Kansspelautoriteit (KSA). Always use its own register, not a badge on a website.

  1. Find the company in the casino's terms and conditions.
  2. Search for it on the KSA register of licence holders.
  3. Check that the exact domain is listed.
  4. Confirm the operator connects to Cruks, the exclusion register.
  5. Read bonus, withdrawal and complaint terms.

What licensed operators must provide

Licensed operators must offer limits, connect to Cruks, verify age and identity and follow Dutch rules on advertising and bonuses.

Frequently asked questions

Are online casinos legal in the Netherlands?

Yes, but only with a Dutch licence from the Kansspelautoriteit.

What is Cruks?

The central register for self-exclusion. Licensed operators must check it.

Is a Malta or Curaçao licence enough?

No. An operator targeting the Dutch market needs a Dutch licence.
