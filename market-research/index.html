Research framework

Check the market before you publish.

“International English” is a language strategy, not a legal permission. Eligibility and marketing rules must be reviewed market by market.

Market readiness checklist

  • Identify the target country and the audience’s age requirements.
  • Find the relevant regulator and read current official rules.
  • Verify whether the operator and its domain may legally serve that market.
  • Review the affiliate program’s allowed territories and promotional restrictions.
  • Check rules for bonuses, comparisons, testimonials, claims, and paid traffic.
  • Review privacy, cookies, tracking, and marketing-consent obligations.
  • Publish the real publisher identity and clear commercial disclosures.
  • Verify support resources and escalation routes for that country.
  • Document the source, date checked, reviewer, and next review date.

Our twelve target geographies

BeInCasinos targets only these twelve geographies: Australia (AU), Canada (CA), Great Britain (GB), United States (US), Switzerland (CH), Germany (DE), Netherlands (NL), Sweden (SE), Spain (ES), Italy (IT), France (FR), and Belgium (BE). No other country is in the active brand-review target. Authorisation is product- and jurisdiction-specific; a US state or Canadian province licence must never be described as nationwide authorisation. Every brand needs current local permission and exact domain/legal-entity matching before publication.

  • Australia (AU): ACMA — Interactive Gambling Act. Online casino services are generally prohibited; do not promote offshore casino offers as locally legal.
  • Canada (CA): AGCO and the relevant provincial regulator. Confirm province-by-province eligibility and licence scope; do not infer nationwide permission.
  • Great Britain (GB): Gambling Commission business register. Verify entity, active remote casino licence, and domain/trading name.
  • United States (US): New York State Gaming Commission is one state-level starting point. Verify the specific state, product, operator, and current approval; there is no single nationwide online-casino licence.
  • Switzerland (CH): Federal Gaming Board — online gambling. A foreign licence is not Swiss authorisation.
  • Germany (DE): GGL — official whitelist of permitted providers. Match exact domain, entity, and product scope.
  • Netherlands (NL): Kansspelautoriteit — register of licensees. Verify the exact licensed entity and product.
  • Sweden (SE): Spelinspektionen — gambling regulator. Verify active licence and product scope.
  • Spain (ES): DGOJ — gambling regulator. Verify the operator and permitted product in the current register.
  • Italy (IT): ADM — Customs and Monopolies Agency. Verify current concession, domain, and product.
  • France (FR): ANJ — illegal online gambling offers. Online casino games are not generally authorised; do not present offshore casinos as legal in France.
  • Belgium (BE): Belgian Gaming Commission. Verify the exact operator and product permission.

Record what you verify

For each market, keep a short evidence record: regulator, applicable law or guidance, operator identity, permitted products, affiliate conditions, required disclosures, source links, date reviewed, and unresolved questions. Do not publish a market as “approved” until the evidence is current and reviewed.
