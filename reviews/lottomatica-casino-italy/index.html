Italian domain and ADM concession

The Italian Customs and Monopolies Agency (ADM) lists concession code 16010 for Goldbet Better Organization Italy S.p.A. (GBO Italy S.p.A.) and associates that concessionaire with www.lottomatica.it. Lottomatica's own account-opening help page also identifies GBO Italy S.p.A. as the concessionaire and says an Italian tax code and identity verification are required. This is the specific Italian relationship checked for this page; it must not be transferred to another country domain or sister brand without separate evidence.

Sources: ADM authorised remote-gambling concessionaires · Lottomatica account-opening guidance · Lottomatica official site.

A concession entry establishes a regulator relationship for the listed entity and domain; it does not by itself establish bonus value, payout speed, support quality or a positive editorial recommendation.
