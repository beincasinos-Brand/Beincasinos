Regulatory and domain evidence

The Spanish Directorate General for the Regulation of Gambling (DGOJ) provides an official operator finder that maps licensee entities to reported consumer domains. The DGOJ finder associates www.codere.es/ with CODERE ONLINE, SAU. This is a useful entity/domain check, but a domain match is not by itself proof that every product or game is authorised.

Primary source: DGOJ — official operator finder. Official brand website: www.codere.es/. Before relying on the listing, confirm the current general licence and the singular licence for the exact game type in the live register.

Product scope

This page is limited to the Spanish domain. Do not transfer licence, bonus or product claims from another country or another domain with a similar brand name. Exact game availability and the relevant singular authorisation have not been independently verified for this review.

Bonus and promotional terms

We did not verify a current promotion and therefore do not publish a bonus figure. Read the live terms for minimum deposit, wagering requirements, eligible games, maximum stake, expiry, maximum cash-out and withdrawal restrictions. Do not treat an advertising headline as withdrawable value.

No deposit or withdrawal was made by BeInCasinos. Payment methods, limits, fees, identity checks, processing estimates and bank-side delays have not been independently confirmed. Operator estimates should not be presented as measured payout results.

Player protection and complaints

We have not independently tested account limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, customer support or complaints handling. Review the operator’s current player-protection information and the DGOJ’s official guidance before making a decision.

Editorial conclusion

The DGOJ operator finder is a starting point for checking a Spanish gambling domain, not a substitute for matching the entity, domain and relevant game licence. BeInCasinos withholds a numeric rating until current terms and product-level evidence can be checked.
