Before a profile goes live

Verify the facts that matter.

We check the exact website and operating entity against the relevant regulator's current register, then review product scope, territory restrictions, terms, withdrawals, complaints, and safer-gambling tools. Every material claim needs a source and a check date.

Licensing and permitted products vary by jurisdiction. A record in one regulator's register is not proof that a brand may serve every country, state, or product category.

Our review checklist

  • Exact domain and legal operator matched.
  • Relevant primary regulator source checked for the named market.
  • Product scope and territorial limits stated clearly.
  • Official terms, withdrawal rules, complaints route, and support resources reviewed.
  • Unknowns and limitations shown instead of guessed.
  • Review date and next-check date recorded.
  • Paid placement and affiliate relationships disclosed separately.

Transparent rating system

How a BeInCasinos score is earned

Our planned editorial score uses six weighted criteria out of 100. A score is published only after the market-and-product eligibility checks pass and every criterion has dated evidence. It is not a popularity vote or a paid ranking.

!
Eligibility comes before points

The exact domain, legal operator, current regulator evidence, product permission for the named market, current terms and withdrawal rules must be checked first. If a required check is missing or fails, the profile remains “Not scored — verification incomplete”; a high score in another area cannot override this gate.

Terms clarity and fairness

25% weight

Bonus conditions, wagering basis, expiry, restrictions, maximum cashout and accessible full terms.

Payments and withdrawal transparency

20% weight

Published limits, processing stages, fees, verification requirements and complaint steps—not untested speed claims.

Safer-gambling controls

20% weight

Visible limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, age information, support resources and product restrictions.

Complaints and customer support

15% weight

Support routes, formal complaint process and relevant escalation route where evidence exists.

Operator and licence transparency

10% weight

Named legal entity, exact domain, current product/territory permissions, direct official-register links, and relevant published enforcement decisions with any recorded follow-up. This does not replace the eligibility gate.

Usability and accessibility

10% weight

Whether important terms and restrictions are findable, readable and presented clearly on mobile.

Score bands: 90–100 Excellent evidence-backed experience · 80–89 Strong · 70–79 Good with limitations · 60–69 Mixed · below 60 Significant shortcomings. These bands apply only after eligibility passes. Any unassessed criterion means no total score. Each score must state market, product scope and check date. Affiliate commission, listing fees and sponsorship never change a score or order.
