Operator identity and Swiss regulatory scope

The Swiss Federal Gaming Board (FGB) lists Casino Lugano and swiss4win.ch among the online gambling operations in Switzerland, with an online start date of 1 March 2021. Swiss law requires a casino licence extension and a permit for each online game, so the regulator’s casino-level listing should not be read as blanket approval for every possible game.

Primary source: Swiss Federal Gaming Board — online gambling list. Official website: swiss4win.ch. A regulator listing is not an endorsement or a guarantee of service quality and does not establish that the site is legal in other countries.
