Operator and licence context

TOTO's official site identifies TOTO Online B.V. as the company licensed by the Dutch Gambling Authority (Kansspelautoriteit) to organise online sports betting and casino games. The Ksa's 17 September 2026 announcement says TOTO Online B.V. received one of the first continuation permits for online gambling, running from October 2026 through September 2031. That is a useful current regulatory fact, but it is not a quality rating and does not replace reading the terms for a particular product.

Sources: TOTO official site and licence statement · Ksa continuation-permit announcement, 17 September 2026 · Ksa register of licensees.

Current casino promotion shown on the official site

The official casino welcome page checked for this review advertised 200 free spins for Pirots 5, stated value €40. The page says the customer must claim the offer in the account first, make a minimum deposit of €20, and wager that €20 in TOTO Casino before the spins are credited. The offer is limited to new players aged 24 or older. TOTO also states that welcome offers change regularly, so these details must be rechecked on the live promotion page.

The headline is not the full contract. Before relying on an offer, read all linked conditions for eligible accounts, claim deadlines, game restrictions, spin expiry and any other requirements. We have not tested the offer in an account and do not infer that every visitor will see identical promotions.

Age restrictions and player protection

The published offer is limited to people aged 24 or older. TOTO's site also links to responsible-play information and explains that Dutch customers may set limits. Regulatory history: a material caveat

In November 2022, the Ksa announced a €400,000 fine against TOTO Online B.V. after finding that advertising had been directed at young adults, contrary to the applicable Dutch rules. The Ksa page says the decision became final because no objection was filed. This is historical regulatory context; it should not be misrepresented as a new 2026 penalty, nor should it be omitted when discussing the operator's record.

Source: Ksa decision concerning TOTO Online B.V..
