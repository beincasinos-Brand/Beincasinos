Michigan-specific authorisation

Michigan's regulator lists BetMGM among the legal internet-gaming websites permitted in the state. The MGCB's 2024 annual report identifies MGM Grand Detroit with BetMGM as its platform provider. This is Michigan-specific evidence and should not be used to infer authorisation in another state or for a different product. The exact live player domain and current contracting terms should be checked against the product being accessed.

Sources: MGCB legal vs. illegal gaming list · MGCB annual report, operator/platform information.

Regulatory listing is not a quality endorsement. It establishes the local legal context, not whether the bonus terms are favourable, the interface is easy to use or withdrawals arrive within a particular time.

Complaint route in Michigan

The MGCB's patron-dispute instructions require a player to first try to resolve the issue directly with the internet-gaming provider. The regulator says to allow at least 10 days for the provider to respond. If the complaint is not addressed after 10 business days, the player may return to the MGCB page to file a dispute alleging a terms or legal violation. The regulator provides provider-specific complaint contact information, including an address for BetMGM formal complaints.

This describes the published process, not a complaint filed or tested by BeInCasinos. Keep copies of the original complaint, reference number and response when using the procedure.

Source: MGCB internet gaming and sports betting complaint process.
