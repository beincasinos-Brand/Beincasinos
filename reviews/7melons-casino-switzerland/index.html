Operator, domain and regulatory scope

The Swiss Federal Gaming Board (FGB) lists Casino Bern and 7melons.ch among the online gambling operations in Switzerland, with an online start date of 15 September 2020. Swiss law requires a casino licence extension and a permit for each game offered online, so a casino-level listing should not be read as a blanket approval for every possible game.

Primary regulator source: Swiss Federal Gaming Board (FGB) — online gambling list. The official brand website is 7melons.ch; current offer details were checked against the official promotions page and terms. A regulator listing confirms only the facts and scope recorded by that authority; it is not a regulator endorsement, a guarantee of service quality, or proof of legality in another country.
