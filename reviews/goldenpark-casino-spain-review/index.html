Regulatory and domain check

The Spanish Directorate General for the Regulation of Gambling (DGOJ) maintains an official finder linking authorised legal entities with consumer-facing domains. The register associates www.goldenpark.es/ with GOLDEN PARK GAMES, S.A.. A domain/entity match is only one part of the check; it does not automatically confirm permission for every game or promotion.

Primary regulator source: DGOJ official operator finder. Operator domain: www.goldenpark.es/. Verify the current general licence and the singular licence for the specific game type in the live regulator record.
