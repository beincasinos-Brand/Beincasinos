Who operates PlayNow in British Columbia?

PlayNow's official legal page states that it is the official gaming site for British Columbia residents and is operated by British Columbia Lottery Corporation (BCLC). The casino page says BCLC owns and operates PlayNow on behalf of the province and identifies the Independent Gambling Control Office (IGCO) as its regulator. The same page states that the service is available exclusively to people located in BC.

Sources: PlayNow legal information · official BC online casino page.

This review is limited to the British Columbia service. PlayNow-branded sites operated by other provincial entities are not the same product and must be assessed separately.

Age and location restrictions

PlayNow's official casino page states that the minimum age is 19 and that age is verified before play. It also states that the site is available only to players physically located in British Columbia. A brand or account name is not permission to use the service from another province or country; the platform's location and eligibility checks apply.

Source: PlayNow BC casino information.

Player protection and GameSense

PlayNow's official materials describe GameSense, BCLC's responsible-gambling programme, as a way to support informed decisions and limits. The operator says players must set a weekly deposit limit before making a deposit. Its help materials also describe casino loss-limit tools, time-management tools, session reminders and account/session history. The Game Break programme lets a person take a break from gambling and restrict access for a chosen period.

These are descriptions of the published controls, not evidence that BeInCasinos tested each control in an account. Readers should review the current programme and select limits before depositing if they choose to use the service.

Sources: PlayNow positive-play information · official help and Game Break guidance · registration flow and weekly deposit-limit information.

Games and fairness information

PlayNow describes a casino catalogue that includes slots, poker, baccarat, roulette and blackjack, and says casino outcomes are determined by random-number generators within game software.
