Operator and licensing evidence

The Dutch Kansspelautoriteit’s 17 September 2026 announcement names Betent B.V. (BetCity) among operators receiving a renewed remote-gambling permit for October 2026–September 2031.

Primary source: Dutch Kansspelautoriteit — renewed permit announcement. Official brand website: www.betcity.nl/. A regulator record is evidence only for the legal entity, licence activity, domain and territory it actually covers. It is not an endorsement and does not transfer to other countries.

Product scope and availability

Dutch-licensed online gambling within the permit scope. Availability may depend on where a customer resides, age and verification status, and the exact domain used. Do not infer that a product available under the same brand in another country is available or permitted in this market.

Welcome offers and bonus terms

The current promotion and full terms were not verified sufficiently to publish a reliable offer amount. Before relying on any offer, check the minimum deposit, wagering requirement, eligible games, maximum stake, expiry, maximum cash-out, withdrawal restrictions and whether the promotion is optional. An advertised headline is not the same as the value a customer can realistically withdraw.

Payments and withdrawals

No deposit or withdrawal has been performed by BeInCasinos. Payment methods, minimum and maximum amounts, fees, verification steps, processing estimates and bank-side delays have not been independently confirmed for this review. Operator-published estimates should not be described as independently measured payout speed.

Player protection, complaints and regulatory history

The permit renewal is not a consumer-service rating. Current offers, bonus terms, payment routes, withdrawal processing, complaint outcomes and safer-gambling account controls need further independent verification.

Before registering, read the current responsible-gambling, account-limit, time-out, self-exclusion and complaints information. We have not independently tested those workflows for this page. If a regulator publishes enforcement history, it is relevant context and should be read directly in the source rather than reduced to a vague “licensed” badge.

What is verified — and what is not

  • Verified from the linked primary regulator record: the specific permit or licence evidence described above.
  • Not verified: current promotional value, complete bonus terms, cashier restrictions, actual withdrawal speed, support quality and end-to-end account experience.
  • Not claimed: a personal account test, successful withdrawal, independent game-fairness audit, or numeric score.

Editorial conclusion

The regulator record is a useful eligibility check, not a complete product assessment. BeInCasinos withholds a numeric rating until current terms, player-protection controls and product-level evidence can be checked and documented. Readers should verify the exact domain, local rules and current operator terms before making a decision.
