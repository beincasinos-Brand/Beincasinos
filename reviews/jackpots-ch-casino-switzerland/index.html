Operator and Swiss authorisation

The official Jackpots.ch terms identify Grand Casino Baden AG as the operator of the online casino and state that it is supervised by the Swiss Federal Gaming Board (ESBK) and holds Swiss concession no. 516-003. The ESBK's current online-gambling overview lists Casino Baden and the domain jackpots.ch, with online operations beginning on 5 July 2019. Switzerland requires a casino to hold a concession extension and separate permits for individual games offered online, so a general brand statement is not a substitute for checking the official list.

Sources: Jackpots.ch terms and conditions · ESBK list of Swiss online casinos · ESBK information on unauthorised online gambling.

Bonus conditions: wagering can be substantial

Jackpots.ch's published bonus guidance says the default deadline to complete wagering is one month unless a particular promotion states otherwise. The standard playthrough is described as 30× the original bonus amount for slots and typically 300× for table games. Game contributions differ: the help page lists regular slots at 100%, while roulette, blackjack, poker, baccarat, live casino, game shows and arcade games generally contribute 10%; jackpot games, high-theoretical-payout slots and slots with spin-memory features contribute 0% under the general rules.

The operator says the real-money balance is used first, and bonus credit converts to real money once the applicable wagering requirement is met. The published terms also state that bonuses cannot be withdrawn before their playthrough is complete and that active bonuses may need to be cancelled before withdrawing real-money balance. Specific promotions can override the default values, so read the individual offer and account-specific conditions rather than assuming all offers are identical.

Sources: Jackpots.ch bonus conditions · official help centre · general terms.

Withdrawals and financial verification

Jackpots.ch's official help information says withdrawals can be made only to a Swiss bank or PostFinance personal account, not to a credit card. It also says that where identity and address have already been verified, additional documents such as bank statements may be requested for deposits or withdrawals totalling CHF 4,000 within 24 hours, to establish the economic beneficiary. The operator explains that legal affordability/financial-risk checks may require further documentation; if required information is not supplied, the account can remain blocked and withdrawals may not be possible until checks are completed.

These are published policy statements, not an observed withdrawal result. BeInCasinos has not made a payment or measured the time funds took to arrive. Check the live help centre and account-specific instructions before relying on the policy.

Source: Jackpots.ch help centre.

Responsible gambling and limits

The operator's responsible-play page encourages players to set deposit and loss limits, take breaks and avoid trying to win back earlier losses. It says reductions or new limits take effect immediately, while increases or removals take effect after 24 hours. The site also describes playing breaks from 15 minutes up to six months and access to account history showing deposits, withdrawals, stakes, wins, losses and net results. The terms describe Swiss-wide exclusion obligations and financial-risk checks.

These controls are important features of the published policy, but we have not independently tested the interface or the account-level implementation. Readers should consider setting limits before gambling and should not treat them as a reason to play more.

Sources: Jackpots.ch terms · responsible-gambling guidance.
