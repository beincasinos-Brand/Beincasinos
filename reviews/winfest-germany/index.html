What the German whitelist confirms

The current GGL whitelist lists Rabbit Entertain IT Limited in the category Virtuelle Automatenspiele and includes winfest.de. The entry shows an initial authorisation date of 5 September 2025. This is meaningful market-specific evidence for the listed category, but it must be read narrowly: a virtual-slots entry does not establish authorisation for every casino format, such as roulette, blackjack or live dealer games.

Source: GGL official whitelist of permitted gambling providers.

The whitelist is updated periodically. Readers should check the current entry directly and compare the exact operator/domain with the legal terms shown on the live product. A regulator listing is not a recommendation and does not establish bonus value, payout performance or customer-support quality.

Product scope: do not assume a full online casino

For this Germany-specific review, the supported scope is virtual slot games as described in the GGL entry. We do not describe Winfest as authorised for live casino or online table games unless a separate current official record supports that claim. This distinction matters because the permitted product categories and geographic reach differ by licence type.
