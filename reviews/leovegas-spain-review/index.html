Regulatory and domain check

The Spanish Directorate General for the Regulation of Gambling (DGOJ) publishes an official operator finder linking licensed legal entities with consumer-facing domains. The register lists LEOESP, S.A. in connection with www.leovegas.es/. This is an entity/domain reference, not a blanket guarantee that every game, promotion or product is authorised.

Primary regulator source: DGOJ official operator finder. Operator site: www.leovegas.es/. Confirm the current general licence and the singular licence for the specific game type in the live regulator record.

Regulatory history and enforcement

Material historical action — ES/2025/007. The DGOJ published a 2025 sanction resolution concerning use of technical systems that were not homologated or authorised (Article 40.k). The resolution records acknowledgement of responsibility and a voluntary €75,000 payment after reductions. The DGOJ register later records the transfer of the Spanish licences from LEOVEGAS GAMING PLC to LEOESP, S.A. in 2025 and lists leovegas.es under LEOESP. This historical action is relevant to operator-level assessment; it does not by itself establish that the current licence is inactive. Read the official DGOJ sanction resolution.
