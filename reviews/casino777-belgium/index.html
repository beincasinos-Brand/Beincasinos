Account age and verification

The terms dated 19 October 2026 state that a player must be at least 21 years old. They also say the account must be authenticated before a customer can access games or withdraw money. That means readers should complete requested verification and understand the policy before making assumptions about access or withdrawal timing.

Source: official terms, account opening and authentication sections.

Deposit limits and safer play

The published terms describe personal deposit limits and say a default weekly limit of €2,000 applies where a player has not set a personal limit, subject to the rules and any applicable regulatory process described in the terms. A requested increase to a personal limit is not immediate: the terms say it becomes effective after 72 hours. Reductions are stated to take effect immediately. These are important controls to read in full, not a reason to gamble up to a limit.

The terms also state that a player who has chosen self-exclusion cannot access the account or its functions during the exclusion period. Read the operator's current responsible-play pages and the Belgian exclusion rules if you need to restrict access.
