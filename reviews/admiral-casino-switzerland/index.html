Operator identity and Swiss regulatory scope

The Swiss Federal Gaming Board (FGB) lists Casino Mendrisio and admiral.ch among the online gambling operations in Switzerland, with an online start date of 16 July 2025. Swiss law requires a casino licence extension and a permit for each online game, so the regulator’s casino-level listing should not be read as blanket approval for every possible game.

Primary source: Swiss Federal Gaming Board — online gambling list. Official website: admiral.ch. A regulator listing is not an endorsement or a guarantee of service quality and does not establish that the site is legal in other countries.

Welcome bonus and terms

The current promotion and complete terms were not independently verified for this review, so we do not publish a promotional headline. What is verified — and what is not

  • Not verified: current bonus, full terms, specific game permissions, cashier details, withdrawal timing, complaint outcomes or hands-on usability.
  • Not claimed: personal account experience, successful payouts, independent fairness testing or a numeric rating.
