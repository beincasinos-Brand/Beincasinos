Swedish licence and exact-domain evidence

The Swedish Gambling Authority (Spelinspektionen) URL register lists comeon.com under ComeOn Sweden Ltd. The record shows commercial online gambling and betting licences running from 30 October 2025 through 29 October 2030. This is the primary source for the current Swedish licence lead; readers should check the live register for changes and verify the actual route and terms displayed to their account.

Sources: Spelinspektionen domain-specific licence record · licences and permits.

A Swedish licence is not a guarantee of a flawless operator or a quality rating. The regulator itself notes that licensed operators can still be subject to sanctions when they fail to comply with rules.

Regulatory history: a finalised SEK 13 million sanction

ComeOn Sweden Ltd has a material enforcement history that should be disclosed in a balanced review. In 2021 the regulator warned the company and imposed a SEK 35 million sanction related to bonus offers and duty-of-care concerns. Following appeals, the administrative court rejected the company's appeal in 2022; the administrative court of appeal reduced the sanction to SEK 13 million in 2024. On 13 December 2024, the regulator reported that the Supreme Administrative Court would not grant leave to appeal, so the appellate decision stood. The final sanction was therefore SEK 13 million, not the original SEK 35 million.

The regulator's summary says the case involved money gifts made to a customer's gambling account at a time when there were indications of risky gambling, and found issues involving prohibited bonuses and duty of care. This is historical context; it should not be misrepresented as a new 2026 action. It is relevant when evaluating how the operator's player-protection controls and bonus practices should be scrutinised.

Sources: regulator's case summary · final appeal outcome, 17 December 2024.
