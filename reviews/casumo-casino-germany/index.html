Operator, domain and regulatory scope

The GGL whitelist lists casumo.de under Rabbit Entertain IT Limited for “Virtuelle Automatenspiele” (virtual slot games), with an initial permission date of 3 July 2024. That entry is not proof that live dealer, roulette, blackjack or other online-casino products are authorised on this German domain.

Primary regulator source: German Joint Gambling Authority (GGL) whitelist. The official brand website is casumo.de. A regulator listing confirms only the facts and scope recorded by that authority; it is not a regulator endorsement, a guarantee of service quality, or proof of legality in another country.
