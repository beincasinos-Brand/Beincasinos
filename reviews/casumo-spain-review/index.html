Regulatory and domain evidence

The Spanish Directorate General for the Regulation of Gambling (DGOJ) provides an official operator finder that maps licensee entities to reported consumer domains. The DGOJ finder associates www.casumo.es/ with CASUMO SPAIN, PLC. This is a useful entity/domain check, but a domain match is not by itself proof that every product or game is authorised.

Primary source: DGOJ — official operator finder. Official brand website: www.casumo.es/. Before relying on the listing, confirm the current general licence and the singular licence for the exact game type in the live register.

Product scope

This page is limited to the Spanish domain. Do not transfer licence, bonus or product claims from another country or another domain with a similar brand name. Exact game availability and the relevant singular authorisation have not been independently verified for this review.
