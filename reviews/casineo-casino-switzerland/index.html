Operator identity and Swiss regulatory scope

The Swiss Federal Gaming Board (FGB) lists Casino Locarno and casineo.ch among the online gambling operations in Switzerland, with an online start date of 17 March 2026. Swiss law requires a casino licence extension and a permit for each online game, so the regulator’s casino-level listing should not be read as blanket approval for every possible game.

Primary source: Swiss Federal Gaming Board — online gambling list. Official website: casineo.ch. A regulator listing is not an endorsement or a guarantee of service quality and does not establish that the site is legal in other countries.
