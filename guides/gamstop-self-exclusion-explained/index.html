GAMSTOP Explained: Free Self-Exclusion for UK Online Gambling

GAMSTOP lets you exclude yourself in one step from online gambling companies licensed in Great Britain.

How it works

You register once, choose a period, and licensed operators must stop marketing to you and prevent you from opening or using accounts.

Periods

You choose a fixed period. When it ends, you can ask to be removed only after the period has expired.

Limits to know

It covers GB-licensed sites, not offshore ones, and it does not block land-based venues. Consider pairing it with blocking software for devices.

Get support

If gambling is causing harm, free confidential help is available from services such as BeGambleAware and GamCare.

Frequently asked questions

Is GAMSTOP free?

Yes.

Does it cover all sites?

It covers online gambling companies licensed in Great Britain.
