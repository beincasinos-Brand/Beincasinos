GAMSTOP Explained: Free Self-Exclusion for UK Online Gambling

GAMSTOP lets you exclude yourself in one step from online gambling companies licensed in Great Britain.

How it works

You register once, choose a period, and licensed operators must stop marketing to you and prevent you from opening or using accounts.

Periods

You choose a fixed period. When it ends, you can ask to be removed only after the period has expired.

Limits to know

It covers GB-licensed sites, not offshore ones, and it does not block land-based venues. Consider pairing it with blocking software for devices.

Get support

If gambling is causing harm, free confidential help is available from services such as BeGambleAware and GamCare.

Before you register: what to expect

GAMSTOP is a free service. You choose a period of six months, one year or five years, and once it starts it cannot be shortened or cancelled early. That is deliberate: the tool is meant to protect you in a moment when you might want to reverse the decision.

Registration covers online gambling companies licensed by the UK Gambling Commission. It does not cover sites outside the UK licensing system, which is why a casino that accepts UK players but is not UKGC-licensed is a warning sign in itself.

What to do alongside GAMSTOP

GAMSTOP blocks access to accounts; it does not remove the urge to gamble. Consider also removing saved cards from your browser and phone, installing gambling-blocking software on your devices, and telling someone you trust.

Free, confidential help is available from the National Gambling Helpline and BeGambleAware. If you have already registered and a licensed operator lets you open an account, tell the operator and the Gambling Commission.

Frequently asked questions

Is GAMSTOP free?

Yes.

Does it cover all sites?

It covers online gambling companies licensed in Great Britain.
