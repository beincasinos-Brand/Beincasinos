How to Write a Casino Complaint That Gets Results

A complaint that is short, factual and well documented is more likely to be resolved quickly, and it also helps if you need to escalate.

Start with the facts

State your account name, the date, the amount and exactly what happened. Keep it in order and avoid emotion; the reader needs to understand the timeline in one minute.

Quote the terms

Name the clause you believe applies, and explain why. If the operator has relied on a clause, ask which one and how it was calculated.

Attach evidence

  • Screenshots of the terms on the day you accepted them.
  • Game history or transaction history.
  • Chat transcripts and emails.
  • A clear list of the documents you have already sent.

Say what you want

Be specific: for example, that the withdrawal of a stated amount be processed, or that a bonus be reinstated, by a given date.

A simple structure

Use four short parts: what happened, which terms apply, what evidence is attached, what outcome you ask for. Send it through the official complaints channel, and keep a copy.

If you are not satisfied

Licensed operators must tell you which dispute service to use. In the UK that is often IBAS or another approved service. Check the time limits, and keep your complaint factual.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a casino have to respond?

It varies by regulator and operator; check the complaints policy in the terms.

Should I complain publicly?

Use the official process first and keep a record; public posts do not replace it.

Can a regulator resolve my complaint?

Regulators generally do not resolve individual disputes; use the approved dispute service.
