Legal Online Casinos in Germany: GGL Licence, Limits and OASIS

Since the Interstate Treaty on Gambling took effect on 1 July 2021, online slots and some other casino products can be offered in Germany only with a licence from the GGL (Gemeinsame Glücksspielbehörde der Länder).

Check the licence and the whitelist

The GGL publishes a list of authorised providers and products. Match the operator, the domain and the product, because a licence for one product does not cover another.

Rules players notice

  • Deposit limit across licensed providers, checked through a central system.
  • Reduced stakes per spin on online slots and a pause between spins.
  • Participation in the OASIS exclusion system.
  • Restrictions on features such as autoplay and some jackpots.

Details change; check the GGL for the current numbers.

What this means for bonuses

Germany's rules shape what operators can offer. Do not assume UK or Malta terms apply.

Unlicensed sites

Offers without a GGL authorisation for the product are not covered by German player protection.

Frequently asked questions

Are online casinos legal in Germany?

Online slots and certain casino products are legal only with a GGL licence; check the whitelist.

What is OASIS?

The German central self-exclusion system licensed operators must query.

Does a Malta licence work in Germany?

It does not replace a German licence for the products it covers.
