Cruks Explained: Self-Exclusion for Online Gambling in the Netherlands

Cruks is the Centraal Register Uitsluiting Kansspelen. Registered people are excluded from online gambling with licensed Dutch operators and from many land-based venues.

How registration works

You can register yourself, and in some cases a third party or a court can request registration. Licensed operators must check Cruks before letting you play.

Periods

Exclusion periods start at six months and can be much longer.

What it does not cover

It covers the regulated Dutch market. Unlicensed sites are outside it, which is why checking the licence matters.

Support

If you want help, Dutch addiction care and helplines are available; ask your GP or find them via the KSA.

Frequently asked questions

What is Cruks?

The Dutch central register to exclude yourself from gambling.

Do licensed operators check it?

Yes, they must.
