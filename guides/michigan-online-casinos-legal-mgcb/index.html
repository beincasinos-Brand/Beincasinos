Are Online Casinos Legal in Michigan? MGCB Rules Explained

Michigan regulates internet gaming under the Lawful Internet Gaming Act. Operators are licensed by the Michigan Gaming Control Board (MGCB) and serve adults located in Michigan.

Check the operator

The MGCB publishes the list of licensed internet gaming operators and platform providers. Match the brand to the licensed entity.

Rules players notice

  • Players must be 21 or older.
  • Geolocation confirms that you are physically in Michigan.
  • Operators must offer responsible gaming tools and self-exclusion.

State by state

US online casino law is state-specific. A licence in one state does not authorise play in another, so we review each state separately.

Offshore sites

Sites that are not on the MGCB's list are not licensed in Michigan and the MGCB has taken action against illegal operators.

How Michigan regulates online casinos

Michigan legalised online casino gaming and the Michigan Gaming Control Board licenses and supervises the operators. Players must be 21 or older and physically located in Michigan when they play, which is checked by geolocation.

Only operators on the MGCB's list are legal in the state.

What to check as a player

Check the operator against the MGCB's official list, confirm the site is the operator's own, and use the responsible-gaming tools such as deposit limits and self-exclusion.

Offers and rules differ by state and change often, so read the current terms for Michigan on the operator's own page before you accept any bonus.

Frequently asked questions

No. Geolocation confirms you are in the state.
