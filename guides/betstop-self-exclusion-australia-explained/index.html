BetStop Explained: Australia's National Self-Exclusion Register

BetStop lets you exclude yourself from all licensed Australian online wagering providers at once. It does not make offshore casino sites legal or safe.

What BetStop is

BetStop is the National Self-Exclusion Register, run by the Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA). Once you register, licensed online wagering providers in Australia must not let you open or use an account, and they must stop marketing to you.

What it covers

BetStop covers licensed online wagering, such as sports and racing betting, with Australian licensed providers. It is free and you choose the period, from a few months up to permanent. Check the current options and rules on the official BetStop site before you register.

What it does not cover

Online casino games such as pokies, roulette and blackjack are generally prohibited from being provided to people in Australia under the Interactive Gambling Act. Offshore sites that offer them are not licensed in Australia, so BetStop does not apply to them. If you are tempted to use such sites, contact a support service instead.

Other steps

  • Remove saved payment details from betting apps and browsers.
  • Use bank gambling blocks where your bank offers them.
  • Install blocking software on your devices.
  • Tell someone you trust.

Where to get help

Gambling Help Online offers free, confidential support. In Australia, call 1800 858 858 or use the website. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services.

Frequently asked questions

Is BetStop free?

Yes.

Does BetStop cover online casinos?

No. Online casino games are generally prohibited for people in Australia, and offshore sites are outside the register.

Can I end my registration early?

Rules depend on the period you chose. Check the official BetStop terms before you register.
