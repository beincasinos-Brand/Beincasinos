Casino KYC: Which Documents You Will Be Asked For and Why

Verification is a legal requirement for licensed operators, not an optional extra. Knowing what to expect avoids the most common withdrawal delay.

Typical documents

  • Photo ID such as a passport or driving licence.
  • Proof of address such as a recent utility bill or bank statement.
  • Proof of the payment method, for example a card or account statement with some details hidden.
  • In some cases, evidence of source of funds.

Why it often happens at withdrawal

Operators may request documents when you first withdraw or when thresholds are reached. Doing it earlier removes delay.

Submit safely

Use the official upload area inside your account, not email attachments to an unknown address. Never send documents to anyone who contacts you first.

If your documents are refused

Ask which requirement was not met and keep the conversation in writing. If unresolved, see our complaint guide.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a casino ask for ID?

Licensed operators must verify age and identity and comply with anti-money-laundering rules.

Can a casino refuse to pay while verifying?

Terms usually allow holding withdrawals until checks are complete.
