Casino bonus expiry dates and conditions

A deadline is only one part of a promotion. Check when the clock starts, what activity qualifies, what happens when time runs out, and which terms apply in your jurisdiction.

Expiry can mean several different things

A promotion may have an activation window, a period in which qualifying activity must happen, a deadline to use free spins, or a deadline for completing wagering requirements. These are not interchangeable. Record the exact event that starts each clock and the consequence the terms say follows when it ends.

Six questions to answer before accepting

  1. When does the clock start? It might begin at registration, opt-in, bonus credit, deposit, or the first eligible action. Do not assume.
  2. What is the exact deadline? Check date, time, time zone, and whether the deadline is a fixed timestamp or a duration such as 7 days after activation.
  3. What must be completed by then? The deadline may apply to claiming an offer, using spins, meeting a qualifying deposit, completing wagering, or another condition.
  4. Which funds or winnings are affected? Terms may distinguish the bonus, winnings linked to the bonus, and the customer's own deposited cash. Do not assume all balances have the same restrictions.
  5. What exceptions and limits apply? Look for eligible games, contribution percentages, maximum stakes, maximum cashout, excluded payment methods, and withdrawal or cancellation effects.
  6. What happens after expiry? The terms should explain whether an unused bonus expires, whether restricted winnings are affected, and how remaining cash or already-earned winnings are treated. If this is unclear, ask for a written explanation before opting in.

Illustrative timeline—not an offer

Imagine a hypothetical promotion says it must be activated within 48 hours and any free spins must be used within 5 days after activation. The first deadline is an activation window; the second starts only after activation. Neither statement tells you how long wagering takes, which games qualify, or how remaining winnings are treated. Those points need separate terms.

Keep the terms and the timestamp

Save the offer page, full terms, account message, and the date and time you viewed them. If the offer changes, preserve both versions. Check whether the terms explain how changes are notified and whether the revised rules apply to an already-accepted offer. Do not rely on a banner headline alone.

What official guidance says

For Great Britain, the UK Gambling Commission's free offers and bonuses guidance tells consumers to check restrictions and explains that businesses should make significant conditions clear. Its fair and transparent terms guidance says promotional conditions should be accessible and understandable. These are Great Britain sources; do not assume they define the law in every country.

Important Great Britain rule on wagering requirements

For Great Britain, the Gambling Commission’s updated Social Responsibility Code 5.1.1 took effect on 19 January 2026. It requires covered licensees to make incentive terms clear, transparent, fair and readily accessible. It also prohibits wagering requirements that make a customer play through bonus funds more than 10 times, and prohibits combining more than one type of gambling product (such as casino and betting) in a single incentive. These rules are specific to the relevant Great Britain licence framework; they should not be assumed to apply to an offer in another jurisdiction or to an operator/product outside the rule’s scope. Read the official Licence Condition 5.1.1 and check the current terms for the exact offer.

Do not infer that “10x” means every bonus is good value: the calculation base, eligible games, maximum stake, expiry, and treatment of winnings still matter. Record the exact wording and ask the operator to explain any ambiguity before accepting.

If the deadline or consequence is unclear

Ask the operator in writing which deadline applies, when it starts, what exactly expires, and how your own cash and any winnings are treated. Keep the response. If you believe a term was hidden or applied unfairly, use the operator's formal complaint process and consult the regulator or dispute-resolution route for your jurisdiction.

Quick comparison checklist

  • Activation window and opt-in requirement
  • Expiry timestamp and time zone
  • Qualifying activity and contribution rules
  • Separate treatment of cash, bonus funds and winnings
  • Maximum stake, eligible games and maximum cashout
  • Consequences of expiry, cancellation or withdrawal
  • How terms changes are communicated
  • Official complaint route if a dispute occurs

Practical rule: if the deadline or loss of funds is not clear, do not accept the promotion until you understand it. You do not need to take an offer.
