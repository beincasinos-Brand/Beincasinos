How to Compare Casino Welcome Offers: A 7-Point Method

The biggest headline is rarely the best offer. Use the same seven checks on every offer and the comparison becomes clear.

1. Value actually available

Separate the maximum from what you would realistically claim. A “100% up to a large amount” offer only pays 100% of what you deposit.

2. Wagering and its base

Check the multiple and what it applies to: bonus only, or deposit plus bonus. Use the wagering calculator to turn it into turnover.

3. Time limits

Note the window to opt in, the days to complete wagering and the expiry of free spins.

4. Game contribution and exclusions

Slots, table games and live games often contribute differently, and some games are excluded.

5. Maximum bet while a bonus is active

Breaking a stake limit can void winnings. Find the number before you play.

6. Maximum cashout or winnings cap

Some offers limit what you can withdraw from bonus winnings.

7. Withdrawal conditions

Check verification, method rules and any minimum balance. A good offer on a restrictive payout policy is a poor offer.

Score your own shortlist

Write the seven answers side by side for each offer. If an operator does not state one of them, treat that as a negative until it is clarified.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best casino welcome bonus?

There is no universal best; the best offer for you has terms you can meet comfortably. Compare using the seven points.

Should I choose a bigger bonus or lower wagering?

Run both through the same calculation of turnover required relative to the value you could keep.

Are bonuses mandatory?

No. You can decline any bonus.
