Live Dealer Casinos: What to Check Before You Play

Live casino is the same regulated product with a video dealer. The checks are about rules and limits, not about the stream.

Table limits

Every table shows minimum and maximum stakes. Pick one that fits your budget.

Rules and payouts

Rules differ by variant: for example, blackjack payouts and roulette zero rules change the house edge. Open the rules before you play.

Bonus contribution

Live games often contribute little or nothing to wagering. Check before you play with a bonus.

Licensing

Operators must hold the right licence for the product and market. See our licence guide.

Control

Use deposit and time limits. Live games move quickly, so set limits before you begin.

Check the table before you sit down

Live casino games are streamed from a studio. Check the table limits, the game variant and the house rules, such as blackjack payouts of 3:2 versus 6:5, since small rule differences change the long-run cost of playing.

Look for the game provider's name and use the in-game information screen, which usually shows the rules and the theoretical return.

Bonuses and live games

Live games often count for little or nothing toward wagering requirements. If you are working through a bonus, check the game contribution table in the terms before you play, because a live bet that does not count is wasted progress.

Set a deposit limit, keep an eye on the clock, and remember that a real dealer does not change the odds.

Frequently asked questions

Is live casino fair?

Licensed operators and studios are regulated and tested, but the house edge still applies.

Do live games count for wagering?

Check the contribution table in the bonus terms.
