Are Online Pokies and Casinos Legal in Australia?

The short answer: online casino games for real money are generally prohibited from being offered to people in Australia, and licensed online wagering is a separate category.

What the law says

The Interactive Gambling Act 2001 generally prohibits providers from offering online casino-style games, including real-money pokies, roulette and blackjack, to people physically in Australia. The Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA) enforces the Act.

What is allowed

Licensed online wagering, such as sports and racing betting with an Australian licence, is a different category with its own rules. Lotteries have their own framework. Being licensed for one product does not make another product legal.

Why a website that opens is not proof

Many offshore sites can be opened from Australia. That does not mean they are lawful or licensed here. If an operator is outside the Australian system, you have weaker protections for complaints, payouts and exclusion.

Risks for players

  • No Australian licence or regulator to complain to.
  • BetStop self-exclusion does not apply.
  • Payment providers and banks may block transactions.
  • Withdrawal disputes are hard to resolve.

If you want to gamble

Use only products that are legal and licensed in Australia, set limits and treat gambling as entertainment. If it is causing harm, free support is available from Gambling Help Online on 1800 858 858.

Frequently asked questions

Is online gambling legal in Australia?

Licensed online wagering is, within its rules. Online casino games such as pokies are generally prohibited from being provided to people in Australia.

Can I be prosecuted for playing?

The Act targets providers. Players still face risks of losing money with no protection; check the ACMA guidance.

Are offshore casinos licensed in Australia?

No. They are outside the Australian system.
