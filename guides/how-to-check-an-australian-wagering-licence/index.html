How to Check an Australian Betting Licence

Online wagering in Australia is licensed by states and territories. A quick match of name, licence holder and URL tells you whether a bookmaker is part of the system.

Written and reviewed by Anass Amir · Last reviewed 2026-10-30

Written and reviewed by Anass Amir · Last reviewed 2026-10-30

Why licence checks matter

Licensed wagering providers must follow Australian rules on identity checks, advertising and consumer protection and must participate in BetStop. An unlicensed site does none of that.

The checks

  • Open ACMA's official page for checking operators and the national register.
  • Match the trading name and the licence holder.
  • Match the exact website address you are on.
  • Confirm the provider participates in BetStop.
  • Check limit and deposit tools.

Casino games are different

A wagering licence is not permission to offer online casino games. Pokies, roulette and blackjack online are generally prohibited from being provided to people in Australia.

Red flags

  • No Australian licence information.
  • A licence from another country only.
  • Casino games on a sports-betting brand.
  • Pressure to deposit quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Who regulates betting in Australia?

State and territory regulators license wagering, and ACMA enforces the federal rules on interactive gambling.

What is BetStop?

The national self-exclusion register for licensed wagering providers.

Are bookmakers allowed to offer pokies online?

No. Online casino games are generally prohibited under the Interactive Gambling Act.
