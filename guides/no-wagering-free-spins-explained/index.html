No Wagering Free Spins: What They Are and What to Check

“No wagering” means the winnings from the spins do not have to be staked a set number of times before withdrawal. It does not mean there are no conditions.

What “no wagering” removes

With a normal bonus, winnings are locked until you stake a multiple of an amount (the wagering requirement). With no-wagering spins, winnings are paid as cash or withdrawable balance without that multiple. Read the offer page to see exactly how the operator words it.

What can still apply

  • A minimum deposit or a qualifying spend to unlock the spins.
  • A claim window and an expiry for the spins themselves.
  • A fixed game or stake per spin.
  • A maximum amount you can win or withdraw from the spins.
  • Verification before payout.

Example of how to read an offer

If an offer says 50 free spins with a 10p stake, no wagering and a £10 minimum deposit, then the value of the spins is bounded by the stake and the game's payout structure, and you must meet the deposit rule first. This is an illustration, not a current offer.

Why it matters in Great Britain

The UK Gambling Commission limits wagering requirements on incentives to 10 times, which has made no-wagering and low-wagering offers more visible. Rules differ elsewhere, so check your market.

Frequently asked questions

Are no wagering free spins really free?

They may still require a deposit, have an expiry and cap winnings. Read the offer page.

Can I withdraw free spin winnings immediately?

Under a true no-wagering offer you can, subject to verification and any stated cap.

Is there a limit on winnings?

Some offers cap them. The cap, if any, is in the terms.
