Legal Online Casinos in the Netherlands: How to Check KSA Licences

The Dutch online gambling market opened to licensed operators on 1 October 2021 under the Remote Gambling Act. A legal operator needs a permit from the Kansspelautoriteit (KSA).

The legal test

Check that the operator holds a KSA permit for remote casino games, that the legal entity matches the one in the terms, and that the domain is the one the operator uses under that permit.

Where to look

The KSA publishes information on permit holders and on enforcement decisions. Use it, not an operator's footer, as the source.

Player protections you should see

  • Registration with Cruks, the central exclusion register, is checked by licensed operators.
  • A mandatory deposit limit and tools for time and spending.
  • Age rules: 18+ in general, with specific rules on advertising to young adults.
  • A formal complaints procedure.

What to avoid

Sites without a KSA permit that still accept Dutch players are illegal to market in the Netherlands, and players have no Dutch regulatory protection there.

Frequently asked questions

Are online casinos legal in the Netherlands?

Yes, when operated under a permit from the Kansspelautoriteit.

What is Cruks?

The Centraal Register Uitsluiting Kansspelen, where you can register to be excluded from gambling.

Can I play at foreign casinos from the Netherlands?

Only licensed ones are lawful to offer there; unlicensed offers carry no Dutch protection.
