IBAS: How to Escalate a UK Casino Complaint

If you cannot resolve a complaint with a UK-licensed operator, an independent service can review it. IBAS is one of the approved providers.

When you can use it

  • You have complained to the operator first.
  • The operator has given a final response, or eight weeks have passed without resolution.
  • The operator participates in the scheme.

What to prepare

  • Account details and a timeline.
  • Copies of the terms in force at the time.
  • Screenshots and communications.
  • Your desired outcome.

What it can and cannot do

An adjudicator reviews the evidence and issues a decision. It is not a court and cannot consider every type of dispute.

Other routes

Other approved ADR providers exist; the operator's terms name the one that applies.

Before you contact IBAS

IBAS (the Independent Betting Adjudication Service) is a free alternative dispute resolution service for licensed gambling operators. You normally have to complain to the operator first and give it the chance to resolve the issue.

Gather your evidence: account details, dates, screenshots of the terms you accepted, chat transcripts and any emails.

What happens next

If the operator does not resolve the complaint or you are not satisfied with the response, you can ask IBAS to review it. IBAS looks at the evidence from both sides and issues a decision.

IBAS does not handle every kind of dispute, and the Gambling Commission does not resolve individual complaints. Check IBAS's current rules and time limits on its official site, and keep your complaint factual and in writing.

Frequently asked questions

Is IBAS free?

It is free for players.

How long before I can go to IBAS?

After the operator's final response or eight weeks without resolution, according to the typical route.
