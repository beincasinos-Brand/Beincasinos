Casino Cashback Bonuses Explained: Net Loss, Caps and Wagering

Cashback sounds simple, but the definition of “loss”, the period and the form of payment decide its value.

The three questions

  • How is net loss calculated and over what period?
  • Is the cashback paid as cash or as bonus money with wagering?
  • Is there a minimum loss and a maximum payout?

Example

If a terms page offers 10% weekly cashback on net losses, and your net loss is 100 units, you would receive 10 units. If it is paid as bonus with wagering, those 10 units carry conditions. This is an illustration.

Why it is not a safety net

Cashback reduces a loss only partly and can encourage extra play. Set limits first.

Frequently asked questions

Is cashback better than a deposit bonus?

It depends on the terms: cash cashback without wagering is simpler than bonus-money cashback.

When is cashback paid?

The terms state the period and the payout day.
