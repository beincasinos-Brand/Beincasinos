Casino Closed My Account: What You Can Do

A closed account is stressful, especially if there is a balance. Staying calm and documenting everything gives you the best chance of a fair outcome.

Why accounts get closed

Common reasons include failed identity checks, suspected multiple accounts, using someone else's payment method, breaching bonus terms, or concerns about safer gambling. Operators must follow their terms and the law.

First steps

  • Ask support in writing for the exact reason and the clause relied on.
  • Ask what will happen to any balance and when it will be paid.
  • Save screenshots of the account, balance and any messages.
  • Do not open a new account to get round the closure.

Your balance

Where the operator closes an account without a breach on your part, it should normally return the balance. If it alleges a breach, it should explain the grounds and the amount.

If you disagree

Use the operator's complaints process, then the alternative dispute resolution service named in its terms. Keep your messages factual and attach evidence.

If you closed it yourself

If you asked for closure because of gambling harm, ask the operator to confirm it in writing, and consider self-exclusion schemes such as GAMSTOP. Support is available if you need it.

Frequently asked questions

Can a casino close my account without notice?

The terms usually give the operator that right in some cases, but it should explain why when asked and handle your balance properly.

Will they pay my winnings?

It depends on the reason and the terms; ask for a written explanation.

Can I reopen it?

That is the operator's decision, and a closure for safer gambling reasons may be long-lasting.
