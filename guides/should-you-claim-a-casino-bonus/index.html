Should You Claim a Casino Bonus? When to Take It and When to Skip It

A bonus adds conditions to your money. Whether it is worth it depends on how comfortable you are with those conditions.

When a bonus can make sense

  • The wagering base and multiple are low and clearly stated.
  • You are happy with the eligible games and the stake limit.
  • Expiry fits how often you play.
  • You can read the full terms and they are fair.

When to skip it

  • You may want to withdraw soon, because active bonuses can restrict withdrawals.
  • Terms are long, vague or change often.
  • A cashout cap makes the potential gain small.

You can always decline a bonus or opt out before depositing.

A quick test

Work out the turnover required with the calculator, and decide whether you would be comfortable playing that much.

Remember

A bonus is not free money and does not change the odds of the games.

Frequently asked questions

Is it better to play without a bonus?

It keeps withdrawals simple. A bonus can still be fine if you accept the conditions.

Can I cancel a bonus?

Often before you wager; the terms say how and what happens to winnings.
