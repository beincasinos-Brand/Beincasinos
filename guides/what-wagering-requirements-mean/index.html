What wagering requirements mean

A multiplier can look simple, but the eligible amount and other restrictions determine how it works.

The basic calculation

A wagering requirement is often written as a multiplier. If the terms specify a 10× requirement on a 15-unit bonus, the arithmetic is 10 × 15 = 150 units of eligible wagering. The terms may instead apply the multiplier to a deposit or to the combined deposit and bonus, so read the wording carefully.

Eligible wagering matters

Not every game or transaction necessarily contributes equally. Terms may exclude certain games, cap stakes, or count only a percentage of eligible activity. The simple calculation does not capture these details.

Jurisdiction-specific rules matter

For incentives covered by the relevant Great Britain licensing framework, the updated UK Gambling Commission Social Responsibility Code 5.1.1 took effect on 19 January 2026 and prohibits wagering requirements that require customers to play through bonus funds more than 10 times. It also restricts combining different types of gambling products in a single incentive. This is not a worldwide rule; applicability depends on the licence, product and jurisdiction. Read the official Licence Condition 5.1.1 and the current offer terms.

For the wider checklist—including eligible games, expiry, stake limits and maximum cashout—see our bonus terms guide and bonus expiry guide.

It is not a prediction

Completing a wagering requirement does not guarantee a profit, a withdrawal, or that a player will retain any balance. Gambling outcomes are uncertain and money can be lost.

Try the arithmetic carefully

Use the wagering requirement calculator to multiply the eligible amount stated in the terms by the multiplier. If the terms do not clearly say whether the base is the bonus, deposit, or combined amount, do not guess—seek written clarification or decline the promotion.

Questions to answer first

  • What exact amount is multiplied?
  • Which games count, and at what contribution?
  • What is the completion deadline?
  • Are there maximum stake or cashout rules?
  • What happens if you withdraw or the deadline passes?

Practical rule: if the calculation or the conditions are unclear, decline the promotion. A promotion is optional.
