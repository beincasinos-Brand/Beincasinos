How to Find a Legit Online Casino in the UK

“Legit” has a testable meaning in Great Britain: the exact site must be run by an entity that holds a UK Gambling Commission licence covering remote casino. Here is the quick way to test it.

Why a logo or badge proves nothing

Any site can display a licence badge. The only proof is the regulator's own public register. A badge, a footer sentence or a review site saying “licensed” is a pointer, not evidence.

The five checks

  • Domain: the address you are on must appear against the operator's record (the register lists domain names).
  • Legal entity: the terms and conditions name the company that contracts with you. Find the same name on the register.
  • Licence type: the record must include remote casino, not only betting or bingo.
  • Status: look for an active licence and read any regulatory actions or financial penalties listed.
  • Terms: withdrawal rules, bonus conditions and a complaints route should be easy to find.

Where to look

Use the Gambling Commission's public register and search by operator or licence account number, which operators must show in the site footer. If the number or company does not match, stop.

What a licence does not tell you

A licence shows the operator is regulated, not that it is the best value or that every payout will be quick. Regulators also fine licensed operators; read the regulatory history before you decide.

Red flags

  • No licence number, or one that does not match the company in the terms.
  • A domain that is not listed under the operator's record.
  • Promises of “no verification” or “no limits”: UK licensees must verify customers and offer safer-gambling tools.
  • Pressure to pay by methods that are not in your own name.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a UK online casino is licensed?

Match the exact domain, the legal entity in the terms and the licence number to the UK Gambling Commission public register, and confirm the record includes remote casino.

Is a Malta or Curaçao licence enough for UK players?

To offer gambling to people in Great Britain, an operator needs a Gambling Commission licence. Other licences do not replace it.

Does a licence guarantee fast payouts?

No. It shows regulation, not performance. Read withdrawal terms and complaint routes separately.
