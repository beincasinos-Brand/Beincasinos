Swiss Online Casinos: Which Are Legal and How to Check

In Switzerland, online casino games may be offered only by casinos that hold the right concession, supervised by the Federal Gaming Board (ESBK).

Who can offer online games

The ESBK lists the licensed providers and their online offers. In practice these are Swiss land-based casinos with an online extension.

Blocking of foreign sites

Under the Money Gaming Act, internet providers block access to unauthorised foreign gambling sites based on a list. A foreign site that is not on the licensed list is not authorised for Swiss players.

How to check

  • Open the ESBK's list of authorised online casinos.
  • Match the brand, domain and the licensed casino named in the terms.
  • Read the safer-gambling tools and exclusion options.

Frequently asked questions

Are online casinos legal in Switzerland?

Yes, when operated by a casino with an ESBK concession and online authorisation.

Can I use foreign casinos from Switzerland?

Foreign providers without Swiss authorisation can be blocked and offer no Swiss protection.
