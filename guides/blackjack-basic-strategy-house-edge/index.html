Blackjack House Edge and Basic Strategy: What It Does and Does Not Do

Blackjack has one of the lowest house edges in the casino, but only if you play sensibly and choose tables with fair rules.

What basic strategy is

Basic strategy is a set of mathematically derived decisions for each hand: when to hit, stand, double or split, given your cards and the dealer's up-card. It reduces the house edge compared with guessing.

What it cannot do

Basic strategy does not beat the house over time and cannot change the result of any one hand. It lowers the cost of playing; it does not make blackjack a source of income.

Rules that matter more than you think

  • Blackjack paying 3:2 is better than 6:5, which raises the house edge noticeably.
  • Whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17.
  • Whether doubling after a split is allowed.
  • The number of decks used.

Live and online tables

Online and live tables show their rules in the game information screen. Check them before you sit down, and do not assume every table has the same payout.

Keep it in perspective

Side bets usually carry a much higher house edge than the main game. Set a limit and stop when you reach it.

Frequently asked questions

Does basic strategy guarantee wins?

No. It reduces the house edge but cannot remove it.

Is 6:5 blackjack worth playing?

Usually not; it is worse for the player than 3:2.

Does card counting work online?

It is not practical on continuously shuffled online games, and operators can restrict accounts they suspect.
