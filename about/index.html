About the project

Clarity before claims.

BeInCasinos is an English-language educational resource about online gambling terms, licensing, payments, and safer gambling. The site is live, while its casino-review directory remains in development.

What we aim to do

We explain terminology and show readers how to find primary sources. We do not treat gambling as income, suggest that outcomes can be predicted, or pressure readers to sign up.

What this site is not

This site is not a regulator, law firm, financial adviser, or gambling treatment provider. General information cannot replace local legal advice or support from a qualified professional.

Transparency and current scope

The site currently publishes educational guides and tools. No casino profiles are currently scored or ranked, and we do not claim independent testing of operators. Any future profile must be supported by dated evidence and market-specific checks before publication. The publisher display identity is Anass Amir.
