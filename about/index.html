About the project

Clarity before claims.

BeInCasinos is an English-language educational resource about online gambling terms, licensing, payments, and safer gambling. It publishes structured reviews, a bonus register and plain-language guides for players in Great Britain, the Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland and the United States.

What we aim to do

We explain terminology and show readers how to find primary sources. We do not treat gambling as income, suggest that outcomes can be predicted, or pressure readers to sign up.

What this site is not

This site is not a regulator, law firm, financial adviser, or gambling treatment provider. General information cannot replace local legal advice or support from a qualified professional.

What is on the site

The review directory covers 29 operator profiles. Each profile follows the same audit file: licence and domain checks, bonus terms, payments, safer-gambling tools, support and usability. A numeric score out of 100 is shown only when every eligibility check passes and every criterion has dated evidence; until then the profile says what is still open instead of guessing. The method is on the how we score page.

Independence and money

We do not accept payment for scores, rankings or the wording of a review. Some links may later become affiliate links, for which we may earn a commission; this never changes a score. See the affiliate disclosure. Advertising, if added, will be labelled and disclosed in the privacy notice first.

Corrections

Terms and licences change. Each review shows when its evidence was checked. If you spot an error, email us and we will verify it against the primary source and correct the page. The publisher display identity is Anass Amir.
