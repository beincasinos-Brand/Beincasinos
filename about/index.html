About the project

Clarity before claims.

BeInCasinos is being built as an English-language educational resource about online gambling terms, licensing, payments, and safer gambling.

What we aim to do

We explain terminology and show readers how to find primary sources. We do not treat gambling as income, suggest that outcomes can be predicted, or pressure readers to sign up.

What this site is not

This site is not a regulator, law firm, financial adviser, or gambling treatment provider. General information cannot replace local legal advice or support from a qualified professional.

Transparency during development

This project is in pre-launch review. Publisher identity, editorial review records, market-specific compliance, and privacy implementation must be completed before public launch. We do not claim independent testing or publish fabricated operator scores.

Contact

Contact details will be added once a monitored publisher address is established. Until then, the site should not be treated as a fully launched publication.
