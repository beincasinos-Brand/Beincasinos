Online casinos in Germany: GGL licence, whitelist and OASIS

Since the 2021 Interstate Treaty on Gambling, virtual slots and online poker in Germany may be offered only with a German licence.

How to check an operator in Germany

The regulator is the Gemeinsame Glücksspielbehörde der Länder (GGL). Always use its own register, not a badge on a website.

  1. Find the company in the casino's terms.
  2. Search for it in the GGL whitelist of authorised providers.
  3. Check the domain and that the product (for example virtual slots) is covered.
  4. Confirm connection to OASIS and the LUGAS deposit-limit system.
  5. Read bonus and withdrawal terms.

What licensed operators must provide

Licensed providers apply a cross-provider monthly deposit limit, a per-spin stake limit and a minimum spin duration on virtual slots, and query the OASIS exclusion system.

Frequently asked questions

Are online casinos legal in Germany?

Online slots and certain products are legal only with a German licence. Check the GGL whitelist.

What is OASIS?

The central self-exclusion system licensed providers must query.

Does a Malta licence work in Germany?

Not for the products that require a German licence.
