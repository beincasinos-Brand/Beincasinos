Market guide · Switzerland

Online casinos in Switzerland: only licensed Swiss casinos

Only casinos with a Swiss concession may offer online games to players in Switzerland. The ESBK blocks access to unlicensed foreign sites.

How to check an operator in Switzerland

The regulator is the ESBK / Federal Gaming Board. Always use its own register, not a badge on a website.

  1. Check the casino on the ESBK's list of authorised providers.
  2. Confirm you are on the licensed operator's own domain.
  3. Check the player protection tools: limits and self-exclusion.
  4. Read bonus and withdrawal terms.
  5. Avoid sites that are blocked or not listed.

Frequently asked questions

Are online casinos legal in Switzerland?

Yes, when run by a licensed Swiss land-based casino with an online concession. Check the ESBK list.

Are foreign casinos blocked?

Access to unlicensed foreign offers is blocked by Swiss authorities.

How do I check a Swiss casino?

Use the official ESBK list and the operator's own domain.
