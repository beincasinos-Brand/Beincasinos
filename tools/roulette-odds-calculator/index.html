How the calculation works

Chance = numbers covered ÷ pockets on the wheel. Payout = 36 ÷ numbers covered − 1 (to 1). House edge = 1 − chance × (payout + 1). A straight-up bet on a 37-pocket wheel wins 1 time in 37 but pays 35 to 1, so on average the casino keeps 2.70% of every stake.

European or American

The American wheel adds a 00 pocket without changing the payouts, which raises the edge to about 5.26%. Choose a European or French wheel whenever you have the choice.

No bet is better than another

On a given wheel every standard bet has the same edge, so betting on red, a dozen or a single number changes volatility, not the average cost. No betting system changes the edge, since every spin is independent.

Common questions

What are the odds of winning at roulette?

A single number wins 1 in 37 on a European wheel and 1 in 38 on an American wheel. Outside bets cover more numbers and pay less.

Which roulette has better odds?

European, because it has one zero instead of two.

Do roulette systems like Martingale work?

No. They change how wins and losses are distributed but not the house edge, and they can lead to very large losses.

Does this include La Partage or En Prison?

No. French rules on even-money bets lower the edge on those bets, so check the table rules.
