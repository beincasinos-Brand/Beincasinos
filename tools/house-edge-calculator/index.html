How the calculation works

Total staked = stake × bets per hour × hours. Expected loss = total staked × house edge, where house edge = 100% − RTP. A slot at 96% RTP keeps 4% of everything staked, on average. Because staked money is recycled with every spin, the amount staked is much larger than the amount you deposit.

Why fast games cost more

The edge applies per bet, so the more bets you place per hour, the more it costs. A game with 600 spins an hour at 96% RTP costs far more per hour than a slower game at the same RTP.

Averages and real sessions

Results vary widely around the average. You may win in a session, but over many sessions the average cost tends to appear. Decide a budget before you play, set a deposit limit and treat the cost as the price of entertainment.

Common questions

What is the house edge?

The share of each stake the game keeps on average, equal to 100% minus the RTP.

Is expected loss what I will lose?

No. It is a long-run average. A single session can end higher or lower.

What is a typical RTP?

Slots often list figures from the mid-90s to the high-90s per cent, and European roulette is about 97.3%. Check the game's own information screen.

Can a strategy reduce the edge?

In some games, such as blackjack, correct play lowers the edge, but it never removes it.
