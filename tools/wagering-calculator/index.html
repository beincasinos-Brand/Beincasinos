How the calculation works

Required turnover = eligible amount × wagering multiplier. For example, if the terms say a 35× multiplier applies to a $100 bonus, the simple calculation is $3,500 in qualifying turnover. Bonus wagering calculator: common questions

What does a wagering requirement mean?

A wagering requirement sets how much qualifying turnover must be recorded before a bonus or related winnings may become withdrawable. The exact rule depends on the operator's current terms and the offer.

What amount should I enter?

Enter only the amount that the written terms say the multiplier applies to. It may be the bonus, the deposit, or a combined amount. Do not assume which one applies if the wording is unclear.

Does required turnover mean the amount I will lose?

No. Turnover is the total value of qualifying bets or plays counted under the rules. It is not a prediction of losses, winnings, the chance of completing a requirement, or the amount you should deposit.

Why might the real requirement differ from this estimate?

Game contribution rates, excluded games, maximum-bet rules, expiry dates, caps and other offer conditions can change what counts. This simple calculator applies one multiplier to one eligible amount; it does not model every operator rule.
