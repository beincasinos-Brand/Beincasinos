What this does not tell you

This figure is not the amount you should deposit, an estimate of losses, or a guarantee that a bonus can be completed. Game contribution rates, caps, time limits, excluded bets, and other terms may change the calculation.

Before relying on a number: confirm whether the multiplier applies to the bonus, the deposit, or the deposit plus bonus. Also check game contribution rates and maximum-bet restrictions. If the terms are unclear, do not assume the most favourable interpretation.

How the calculation works

Required turnover = eligible amount × wagering multiplier. For example, if the terms say a 35× multiplier applies to a $100 bonus, the simple calculation is $3,500 in qualifying turnover. If only some games contribute toward the requirement, actual play may count differently under the operator’s rules.
