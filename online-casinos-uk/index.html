Online casinos in the UK: check the licence, then read the evidence

Any casino serving Great Britain needs a UK Gambling Commission licence. Start with the register, then compare what each operator actually publishes.

How to check an operator in Great Britain

The regulator is the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC). Always use its own register, not a badge on a website.

  1. Find the legal entity named in the casino's terms and conditions.
  2. Search that company on the UKGC public register and match the licence number shown in the site footer.
  3. Check that the exact domain you are on is listed against the operator.
  4. Confirm GAMSTOP is offered and that deposit limits and reality checks are available.
  5. Read the bonus terms: wagering base, maximum bet, expiry and cashout cap.

What licensed operators must provide

Licensed operators must offer GAMSTOP, deposit limits, time-outs and reality checks, verify your age and identity, and handle complaints through an approved dispute service such as IBAS.
