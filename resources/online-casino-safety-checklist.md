# Online Casino Safety Checklist

This checklist is a general consumer-research aid. It is not legal advice, does not certify any operator, and is not a recommendation to gamble.

## Before creating an account

- **Check the exact operator.** Match the brand's legal entity and domain to the regulator's official register; similar names can belong to different companies.
- **Check local rules.** Confirm whether the operator is authorised to offer the specific gambling product in your country or state. A licence in one jurisdiction does not automatically authorise service everywhere.
- **Read the terms before depositing.** Look for bonus wagering requirements, maximum bet limits, game exclusions, expiry dates, withdrawal limits, dormant-account fees, and identity-verification rules.
- **Understand payment conditions.** Check supported deposit and withdrawal methods, processing times, fees, currency conversion, and whether the same method must be used to withdraw.
- **Review account controls.** Look for deposit, loss and session limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, and links to independent support.
- **Protect personal information.** Use a unique password, enable multi-factor authentication if offered, and avoid sharing identity or payment documents through unofficial channels.

## When comparing reviews

- Prefer reviews that name the legal operator and cite current primary sources.
- Treat bonus values and processing times as changeable; check the operator's current terms directly.
- Look for a transparent scoring method and clear disclosure of affiliate or commercial relationships.
- Be cautious of guaranteed-win claims, pressure tactics, or claims that gambling is a way to earn reliable income.
- Remember that a review score is not proof of licensing, safety, or suitability for every reader.

## If something goes wrong

Save relevant terms, transaction records, and support correspondence. Use the operator's formal complaints process and consult the relevant regulator or an appropriate consumer-support body for your jurisdiction. If gambling stops feeling manageable, stop and seek independent support; self-exclusion and blocking tools may help.

## Scope and maintenance

This is a general checklist, not a live licence database. Verify all material claims with the relevant regulator and the operator's current terms before making a decision. Do not treat this checklist as confirmation that any particular casino is authorised or safe.
