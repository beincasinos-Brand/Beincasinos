About

Anass Amir runs BeInCasinos, an English-language information site about online casinos for readers in Great Britain, the Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland and the United States. He designed the site's scoring method and writes the guides, tools and operator profiles.

His approach is simple: believe the regulator's register, not a badge. Each operator is matched to its legal entity, exact domain and licence record. Bonus and withdrawal terms are read in full and dated. If something cannot be verified from an official source, the page says so and leaves it unrated instead of guessing.

What he covers

  • How to check a casino licence in each market
  • Bonus terms, wagering requirements and cashout caps
  • Withdrawals, verification and complaints
  • RTP, house edge and game odds
  • Self-exclusion and safer-gambling tools

Standards he works to

Read the editorial policy, the scoring method and the affiliate disclosure. Scores are never sold, and commercial relationships do not change ratings.

What he does not claim

He is not a lawyer, financial adviser, regulator or gambling-treatment provider. The site gives general information, not legal or financial advice, and it does not claim hands-on testing unless a page says it was done and shows the date.

Corrections and contact

If you find an error, email contact@beincasinos.online with the page address and a link to the official source. Corrections are checked against that source and published openly. See the contact page.
