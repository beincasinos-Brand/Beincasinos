Our process

Evidence before conclusions.

This page describes the evidence standards intended for future market-specific operator profiles. It is not a claim that every page has already been independently tested or that any casino has been scored.

Methodology version 2.0 · Updated 30 October 2026 · Tier 1 reviews (Great Britain, Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland, Michigan) follow this method. A review shows a total only when every eligibility check and all six criteria are evidenced; open items are listed on each page.

Source-first research

Regulatory claims should be checked against the regulator’s own register or current official guidance. Offer terms should be checked directly with the relevant operator and recorded with a date. Where information cannot be verified, it should be omitted or labelled as uncertain.

Eligibility before scoring

Before a casino can be considered for a market-specific review or ranking, we must identify the exact domain and legal operator, verify current official regulator evidence and product scope where required, confirm the market-specific publication gate, and review current terms and withdrawal rules. If a mandatory check is unresolved, the profile is not scored or is not published. A licence in one jurisdiction is not worldwide permission.

Published scoring framework

How a score is calculated

The score is a weighted editorial assessment out of 100, but it is available only after every eligibility gate passes for the exact operator, domain, product, and market. The six criteria are scored individually from 0 to 5 against recorded evidence; each criterion's points are calculated as (criterion rating ÷ 5) × its weight. The six weighted results are then added. A missing criterion means no total score—not a zero and not a guessed average.

CriterionWeightWhat evidence must show
Terms clarity and fairness25%Current full terms, bonus conditions where applicable, restrictions, expiry, and cashout rules.
Payments and withdrawals transparency20%Published limits, fees, processing stages, verification requirements, and dispute steps.
Safer-gambling controls and information20%Visible limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, age information, and support resources.
Complaints and customer-support transparency15%Working support routes, formal complaint steps, and applicable escalation information.
Operator and licence transparency10%Exact legal entity/domain, current relevant register evidence, product/territory scope, and material published enforcement decisions with documented follow-up where available.
Usability and information accessibility10%Terms and restrictions are findable, readable, and key controls are reasonably accessible.

Each criterion is rated from 0 to 5 against recorded evidence: 0 = material failure documented; 1 = very weak; 2 = weak; 3 = adequate; 4 = strong; 5 = exemplary transparency. A low rating requires a documented finding; missing research is marked “not assessed” and blocks the total score. Criterion points equal (rating ÷ 5) × the criterion weight. The six weighted results are added, with rounding only on the final displayed total.

How to interpret the total

  • 90–100: Excellent evidence-backed experience.
  • 80–89: Strong.
  • 70–79: Good, with limitations.
  • 60–69: Mixed.
  • Below 60: Significant shortcomings.

These bands apply only after eligibility passes. Every score states its market, product scope and check date.

These bands are editorial descriptions, not regulator ratings. A high total cannot override a failed eligibility gate, a market restriction, or a material unresolved red flag. The score must show the market, product, evidence date, and source links beside it. No hands-on testing is implied unless the test was actually performed and documented.

Why a profile may say “Not rated”

“Not rated” is an intentional evidence status, not a zero score. It means at least one mandatory gate or scoring criterion has not yet been supported by sufficient current evidence, so showing a number would create false precision. A bonus marked “Not verified” is likewise not an offer claim. We will not fill those fields with estimates to make cards look complete.

Once a profile passes every eligibility gate, our planned editorial assessment uses the same weighted criteria for comparable products in the same market. Scores describe the evidence reviewed on a stated date; they are not guarantees of legality, safety, fairness, or future performance.

Each score needs evidence notes, source links, a check date, and editorial review. We do not claim to have tested deposits, withdrawals, customer service, or gameplay unless a documented test was actually performed. If a criterion cannot be assessed, we withhold the total score rather than inventing points.

“Top 10” lists, if published, will be limited to a named market and comparable product scope after enough eligible profiles have passed the same rubric. We will not present an unqualified worldwide ranking where products and laws are not comparable.

Commercial fees, affiliate commission, sponsorship, and sales outreach cannot affect eligibility, scores, or editorial order. Paid placement must be visibly labelled and cannot buy a positive review or guaranteed top-10 inclusion.

Tools and calculations

Interactive tools should show their assumptions, formulas, and limitations in plain language. A result must not be presented as a prediction, legal conclusion, safety rating, or recommendation. Test inputs at zero, blank, invalid, and unusually large values; check keyboard access and mobile layouts before release.

Corrections and updates

Once publication begins, our intended review schedule is to re-check operator identity, domain, and relevant licence/register evidence at least every 90 days, and sooner when a regulator notice, operator change, material terms update, or credible correction request gives us reason to act. We will record the last-checked date and a next-check target for each profile. A date is a record of when evidence was checked, not a guarantee that the information remains unchanged.

If a material check becomes overdue or a key claim cannot be re-verified, we will mark the profile as needing review, suspend its score/ranking where appropriate, or remove it until the evidence is resolved. Material corrections should be made promptly and documented rather than silently preserving a misleading claim. This is a planned operating standard; it must be resourced and followed before the site can claim an active review database.

Current publication state

The reviews are published as evidence files. Where a check or criterion is not yet verified from an official source, the page says so and shows “Not scored: verification incomplete” instead of a number. This page is not certification, endorsement, or a guarantee of operator safety.
