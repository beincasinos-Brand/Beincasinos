Our process

Evidence before conclusions.

This page describes the evidence standards intended for future market-specific operator profiles. It is not a claim that every page has already been independently tested or that any casino has been scored.

Methodology version 1.0 · Updated 21 October 2026 · Planned standard; no live casino profiles are scored or ranked.

Source-first research

Regulatory claims should be checked against the regulator’s own register or current official guidance. Offer terms should be checked directly with the relevant operator and recorded with a date. Where information cannot be verified, it should be omitted or labelled as uncertain.

Eligibility before scoring

Before a casino can be considered for a market-specific review or ranking, we must identify the exact domain and legal operator, verify current official regulator evidence and product scope where required, confirm the market-specific publication gate, and review current terms and withdrawal rules. If a mandatory check is unresolved, the profile is not scored or is not published. A licence in one jurisdiction is not worldwide permission.

Published scoring framework — 100 points

After every mandatory eligibility gate passes, each eligible profile is assessed against the same nine weighted criteria for the same product and market. The rubric below is the source of truth for the production ledger and public scorecards. Scores describe documented evidence checked on a stated date; they are not guarantees of legality, safety, fairness, or future performance.

  • Licence and operator transparency — 20 points: exact domain, legal entity, current regulator record, relevant product permission and market match.
  • Payments and withdrawals — 18 points: available methods, fees, limits, stated processing stages, verification requirements and withdrawal conditions.
  • Safer-gambling controls — 15 points: visible limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, age information and help resources.
  • Product and games — 12 points: published game categories, named providers where verifiable, search/filter usability and market-specific availability.
  • Bonus terms — 10 points: offer eligibility, wagering basis/multiplier, expiry, maximum conversion/cashout, excluded payment methods and full terms.
  • Complaints and support — 10 points: support contact routes, formal complaints process and applicable independent escalation route.
  • Usability and accessibility — 8 points: navigation, mobile readability, keyboard access and findability of important information, based on recorded checks.
  • Privacy transparency — 4 points: identifiable data controller, privacy notice, cookie/data disclosures and account-data information.
  • Evidence quality — 3 points: primary-source coverage, dated source records, claim traceability and explicit treatment of unknowns.

Eligibility is not a tenth score category. It is a mandatory pass/fail gate. If the exact domain, operator, relevant current permission, product scope or market publication status is unresolved or fails, the profile is withheld and no total score is published. A high bonus or usability result can never compensate for a failed eligibility check.

Every awarded category score needs evidence notes, source links, a check date, and editorial review. We do not claim to have tested deposits, withdrawals, customer service, gameplay or mobile performance unless a documented test was actually performed. If material evidence is missing, we mark the criterion unassessed and withhold the total score rather than filling the gap with invented points.

“Top 10” lists, if published, will be limited to a named market and comparable product scope after enough eligible profiles have passed the same rubric. They will show the score date, meaningful limitations and any commercial relationship. No operator can buy eligibility, points, a positive review or a guaranteed position.

Commercial fees, affiliate commission, sponsorship and sales outreach cannot affect eligibility, scores or editorial order. Paid placements must be visibly labelled and separated from independent rankings.

Tools and calculations

Interactive tools should show their assumptions, formulas, and limitations in plain language. A result must not be presented as a prediction, legal conclusion, safety rating, or recommendation. Test inputs at zero, blank, invalid, and unusually large values; check keyboard access and mobile layouts before release.

Corrections and updates

Once publication begins, our intended review schedule is to re-check operator identity, domain, and relevant licence/register evidence at least every 90 days, and sooner when a regulator notice, operator change, material terms update, or credible correction request gives us reason to act. We will record the last-checked date and a next-check target for each profile. A date is a record of when evidence was checked, not a guarantee that the information remains unchanged.

If a material check becomes overdue or a key claim cannot be re-verified, we will mark the profile as needing review, suspend its score/ranking where appropriate, or remove it until the evidence is resolved. Material corrections should be made promptly and documented rather than silently preserving a misleading claim. This is a planned operating standard; it must be resourced and followed before the site can claim an active review database.

Current publication state

The site currently publishes educational information and tools; no live casino profiles are scored or ranked. Before an operator profile is published, its content must be fact-checked, the publisher and contact details maintained, privacy practices implemented, and target-market and affiliate requirements reviewed. Do not interpret this page as certification, endorsement, or a guarantee of operator safety.
