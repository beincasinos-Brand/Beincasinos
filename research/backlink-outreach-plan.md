## Submission principles

- Pitch first when the publisher asks for a pitch. Do not send a full article where the policy explicitly prohibits unpitched submissions.
- Make each submission unique to the publisher and its audience. Do not syndicate identical articles.
- Disclose BeInCasinos' relevant affiliate/commercial relationships honestly.
- Ask for a link only when it is genuinely useful to readers. Do not demand followed links or exact-match anchor text.
- Do not pay for a link intended to pass ranking credit. Paid sponsorship links should be appropriately qualified by the publisher.
- Do not claim credentials, first-hand testing, readership figures, or regulatory verification that we cannot substantiate.
- Confirm every current editorial rule before submitting.

## Suggested first pitch

**Working title:** Beyond Casino Ratings: A Practical Checklist for Evaluating Online Casinos

**Reader problem:** Star ratings and headline bonuses alone do not help readers verify an operator, understand restrictions, or compare consumer protections.

**Outline:**
1. Verify the legal operator and licence using the regulator's official register.
2. Read bonus eligibility, wagering, expiry and withdrawal conditions.
3. Compare payment methods, verification steps, fees and processing estimates.
4. Find support escalation and complaint routes.
5. Check limit-setting, cooling-off and self-exclusion tools.
6. Use a transparent checklist and clearly separate verified facts from opinion.

**Source plan:** Use current official regulator registers, operator terms and recognised public consumer-protection resources. Add country-specific sources only after the publisher agrees to the target jurisdiction.

**Link request:** If editorially appropriate, one natural brand citation to https://beincasinos.online/; accept no link or a qualified link if that is the publisher's policy.

## Status definitions

- **Researching:** official page found; details still need confirmation.
- **Ready to pitch:** author, angle, sources and disclosure are ready.
- **Pitched:** message actually sent; record date and recipient.
- **Accepted / Rejected / Published:** use only after confirmation from the publisher.
- **Live URL:** record the actual published page; never mark a pitch as a backlink.

## Quality over quantity

A public GitHub file, profile link, or self-published post is not the same as an independent editorial endorsement. Prioritise genuine audience value and publisher fit over a target number of links. Google identifies large-scale guest articles created mainly to manipulate ranking signals as a link-spam risk: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
