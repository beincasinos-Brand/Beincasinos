 Details
 Where possible all withdrawals will be processed back to the same card used to deposit. Where we are unable to withdraw back to your Debit Card, funds will be returned to you via bank transfer. 
 We aim to process your withdrawal within 2 hours of your request. Once received by your bank, they may process your winnings instantly.
 How to Withdraw
 To make a Withdrawal, select from the top right of your screen and choose Bank, then Withdraw.
 Enter your withdrawal amount and select withdrawal. Some withdrawals may take up to 2 banking days to be received and funds processed to an international Debit Card may take up to 5 banking days. If you have deposited using a UK Mastercard and your bank is eligible, you may have the option to complete a fast withdrawal via bank transfer

 How to Withdraw
 To make a Withdrawal, select from the top right of your screen and choose Bank, then Withdraw.
 Select your Bank Country from the list and enter the required bank details. Ensure all information is accurate and the bank account is in your name, or this may prevent your withdrawal being received.
