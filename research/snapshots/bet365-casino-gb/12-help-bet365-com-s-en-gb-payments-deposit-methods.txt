 Debit Card
 Details
 Details
 You can deposit to us instantly using your Visa, Mastercard, Electron or Maestro Debit card.
 How to Deposit
 To make a deposit, select the My Account icon from the top right of your screen and choose Deposit.
 Select Debit Card from the available payment options.
 Enter your card details. The card must be registered in your name.
 Enter your deposit amount.
 On your first deposit with a new card, you will need to read and acknowledge the terms provided then select Deposit. For subsequent deposits, simply select Deposit.
 Your card issuer may require you to authorise your card payment with 3D Secure or enter additional security information.
 Once the deposit is authorised by your card issuer, your bet365 account balance will be credited instantly.
 Why was my Debit Card deposit unsuccessful?

 Isle of Man
 Ireland (Rep. Of)

 UK
 Please Note - You will be unable to deposit with a Credit Card, Pre-Paid Card or Corporate Card.

 Details
 Deposit with online banking from any of the following banks:
 Bank of Scotland, NatWest, Barclays, Lloyds, Santander, Nationwide, HSBC, Monzo Bank, TSB, Starling Bank, Danske Bank and Bank of Ireland.
 You can save your bank details within your Trustly account to make future deposits more convenient.
 How to Deposit
 To make a deposit, select from the top right of your screen and choose Deposit.
 Select Trustly from the available payment options.
 Enter your deposit amount and select Deposit.
 After selecting Deposit, you will be redirected to Trustly to select your preferred bank from the list provided. If you have previously saved your bank account on the same device, move on to step 8. Alternatively, click Change and select your preferred bank.
 If using a mobile device, press Go to (bank name). If your banking app is installed on the device, it will automatically open. If not, you will be redirected to your Bank's login page.
 On desktop, choose Open (bank name) mobile app to generate a QR code. Scan the code with your mobile device, which will automatically open your bank app. Alternatively, choose Continue on this Device to be redirected to your Bank's login page.
 Login to your online banking account, where a summary of the payment will be displayed.
 Follow the instructions to confirm your payment.
 You will be redirected back to your bet365 account and in most instances, the funds will be instantly credited to your account balance.
 Processing Times
 The majority of Trustly deposits are credited to your bet365 account instantly, however, on some occasions the deposit can take up to three banking days to credit.
 If your deposit has not been credited by the third working day, please [https://help.bet365.com/s/en-gb/contact-us] Contact Us with the below information:
 The amount of the deposit
 The time and date the deposit was made
 Whether the funds have left your account

 Details
 Choose PayPal to deposit instantly from a variety of methods, without having to fund your PayPal wallet prior to depositing with us. You'll also have the option to set up future payments to be completed in just one click.
 You can fund your PayPal deposit via your PayPal account balance, a debit card or instant bank transfer.
 To use PayPal to deposit with bet365, you will need to ensure your PayPal Payer Status (your account status) is Verified. To confirm your verification status, or for more information please visit [https://www.paypal.com] www.paypal.com.
 How to Deposit
 To make a deposit, select from the top right of your screen and choose Deposit.
 Select PayPal from the available payment options.
 Enter your deposit amount. If you wish to complete future deposits instantly without logging in to your PayPal account, tick the box under the Deposit button.
 Select Deposit. If you have previously agreed to the PayPal Billing agreement to complete your deposits in one click, the funds will be instantly credited to your bet365 balance after clicking Deposit.
 If you have not set up one-click deposits or you have ticked the option for the first time, you will be redirected to the PayPal log in page where you will be given the option to opt-in. This means any future deposits with PayPal will be completed automatically, and you will not need to log in each time.
 Log in to your PayPal account or select Create an Account and follow the on-screen instructions.
 Select your preferred funding method or add a new method that will be saved to your PayPal account for future deposits.
 Select Complete Purchase and you will be returned to the bet365 deposit page, where your balance will be credited instantly.
 If your default payment method has insufficient funds, then the deposit amount will be taken from another method registered to your PayPal account.
 Please Note - You will be unable to use Mastercard as your default method for automatic payments.

 Bank Transfer
 Details
 Details
 Deposit directly from your online bank account. 
 If you are depositing from a bank account outside of your residential country or in a non-local currency your deposit may take 2-10 banking days to credit.
 How to Deposit
 To make a deposit, select from the top right of your screen and choose Deposit.
 Select Bank Transfer from the available payment options. You will be provided with the bank details to make your payment to. Copy the banking information directly from the Deposit page by clicking the ‘Copy’ icon.
 Login to your online banking to make a payment. 
 Enter the bank account details you were provided with and ensure your bet365 username is added as the payment reference. If you do not include your username, the funds will not automatically credit your bet365 account. 
 Confirm your payment and return to your bet365 account. You will be notified by email once the funds have cleared into your bet365 account. 
 If you have deposited by Bank Transfer, withdrawals will be processed back by bank transfer. See the Wire Transfer withdrawal section for more details.
