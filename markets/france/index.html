France online gambling rules: authorised categories and online casino restrictions

How to distinguish the gambling products that are authorised in France from online casino offers that are not.

Reviewed 26 October 2026 · Editorial research, not legal advice.

Key point: online casino games are not an authorised online gambling category in France. The French regulator, Autorité Nationale des Jeux (ANJ), lists the authorised online categories separately. BeInCasinos will not describe offshore online slots, roulette or blackjack offers as legal French online-casino services.

Which online gambling categories are authorised?

According to the ANJ's official explanation, the authorised categories include:

  • Sports betting offered online by operators holding the relevant ANJ approval.
  • Horse-race betting offered online by approved operators.
  • Online poker in tournament or cash-game formats, where the operator and product are authorised.
  • Lottery games operated online under the relevant state-monopoly framework.

This list is not a blanket approval for every website or every game within a broad brand. The exact operator, website, product and current authorisation still need to match the official record.

Why online casino pages need a different approach

ANJ explicitly states that online casinos and e-sports betting are not authorised in France. A foreign licence, a website available in French, a euro balance or a welcome bonus does not establish French authorisation. Physical casinos operate under a different legal framework; their existence does not mean that an online casino version is authorised.

For that reason, we do not publish a “best legal online casino in France” ranking, and we do not present offshore casino promotions as locally approved. Any future France-facing gambling content must stay within the exact product category and approval scope supported by current primary sources.

How to check a claim

  • Start with the ANJ official website and its current lists and guidance.
  • Match the operator's legal name and exact domain; similar brand names are not proof of authorisation.
  • Confirm the relevant category: sports betting, horse-race betting, poker or lottery. Do not infer casino-game permission from a different category.
  • Check the current terms, age rules, safer-gambling tools, complaint route and the date the source was reviewed.
  • Be wary of sites that claim an overseas licence makes every product legal in France.

Our publication rule for France

We will not promote online casino bonus offers as legal in France. Where a page concerns a permitted category, it must document the applicable ANJ approval for the exact operator and product. If the evidence does not establish authorisation, the page must say so plainly and must not assign a score that could imply regulatory approval.
