Jurisdiction-first research

Twelve target geographies. No automatic approvals.

BeInCasinos researches market rules before assessing any casino brand. A target geography is a research scope, not a claim that online casino products or affiliate promotion are legal there.

Important: legal permission depends on the exact product, operator, domain, licence, audience, and advertising activity. This page is a starting-point directory, not legal advice or a live declaration that any brand is approved. Each review remains unpublished until current primary-source evidence and independent editorial checks are complete.

Our 12 target geographies

  1. Australia (AU). Start with ACMA — Interactive Gambling Act. Online casino services are generally prohibited under Australia's federal framework. Do not promote offshore casino offers as locally authorised.
  2. Canada (CA). Start with AGCO and the relevant provincial or territorial regulator. Permission is jurisdiction-specific; a licence or registration in one province is not nationwide authorisation.
  3. Great Britain (GB). Start with the UK Gambling Commission. Match the exact domain, contracting entity, active remote-casino permission, and applicable marketing rules. Great Britain is not the same as every UK jurisdiction.
  4. United States (US). Start with the relevant state regulator, for example the New Jersey Division of Gaming Enforcement for its approved internet-gaming sites. There is no single nationwide online-casino licence; check state, tribal, product, and operator scope.
  5. Switzerland (CH). Start with the Swiss Federal Gaming Board — online gambling. Verify the current licence extension, exact domain, and any game-specific permit. A foreign licence alone is not Swiss authorisation.
  6. Germany (DE). Start with the GGL official whitelist. Match the current permitted provider, domain, legal entity, and product category.
  7. Netherlands (NL). Start with the Kansspelautoriteit register of licensees. Verify the exact licensed entity, website, product, and current licence status.
  8. Sweden (SE). Start with Spelinspektionen. Confirm active permission for the exact operator and product, and check current marketing restrictions.
  9. Spain (ES). Start with the DGOJ. Verify the operator, website, and authorised product in the current official register.
  10. Italy (IT). Start with ADM — Customs and Monopolies Agency. Confirm current concession, authorised domain, product, and applicable advertising rules.
  11. France (FR). Start with ANJ information on illegal online gambling offers. Online casino games are not generally authorised in France; do not present offshore casino sites as locally legal.
  12. Belgium (BE). Start with the Belgian Gaming Commission. Check the exact operator, licence class, domain, and product permission before considering publication.

What a market check must prove

  • Current regulator evidence for the exact operator, product, and domain—not just a brand name or a foreign licence.
  • Applicable affiliate and advertising rules, including restrictions on bonuses, comparisons, paid placements, and audience targeting.
  • Consumer terms, deposit and withdrawal conditions, fees and limits, complaint routes, responsible-gambling controls, privacy/cookies, and accessibility.
  • A dated evidence log, explicit unresolved questions, independent second-editor review, and legal/affiliate-disclosure sign-off.

Publication status

The twelve geographies above define the research scope only. They are not twelve launch approvals. Australia and France require especially strict exclusion of prohibited online-casino offers; Canada and the United States require province/state- or other jurisdiction-specific checks. If primary evidence is missing, conflicting, inaccessible, or stale, keep the candidate on hold.
