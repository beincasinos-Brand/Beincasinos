Jurisdiction-first research

Online gambling rules, market by market.

A licence is not a universal passport. Before a brand, website or game is described as authorised, match the local regulator, legal entity, exact domain, product category and territory.

Editorial status: this is a primary-source directory and research starting point, not legal advice, a licence certification, or a casino ranking. Laws, licences, domains and permitted products change. Recheck the official source and date it before relying on a claim or publishing a brand profile.

What counts as evidence?

  • Regulator evidence: save the official register record, exact URL, legal entity, licence or concession identifier, product scope, date checked and any limitations.
  • Domain evidence: match the consumer-facing domain to the regulator record and the operator's current legal notice and terms. Similar names are not enough.
  • Product evidence: distinguish casino games, sports betting, poker, bingo, lottery, land-based venues, social/sweepstakes products and B2B game suppliers. Permission for one does not prove permission for another.
  • Consumer evidence: separately check current terms, deposits and withdrawals, bonus rules, privacy and cookies, safer-gambling tools, support and complaints.
  • Independent review: record what was actually observed and what remains untested. Never present operator marketing as an independently verified test.
  • Publication decision: unresolved legal or material factual gaps mean HOLD, not a provisional recommendation or score.
