How to check online casino licensing in Great Britain

Use the UK Gambling Commission’s public register to match casino domains, legal entities and remote-casino permissions.

Editorial research guide · Updated 25 October 2026 · Check primary sources again before relying on a market or operator claim.

Publication gate: REGISTER CHECK REQUIRED — Great Britain only; do not extend this to the whole UK or other countries.

The Gambling Commission regulates gambling provided to consumers in Great Britain, including remote gambling activities that require a licence. A useful check is more precise than spotting a regulator logo: match the exact consumer domain to the correct legal entity and confirm that the entity holds the relevant remote-casino permission. Sports betting and casino permissions are not interchangeable.

How to check a casino claim properly

  1. Match the exact domain. Record the customer-facing URL and redirects. A brand family can use different domains for different countries.
  2. Identify the legal operator. Read the current terms and legal footer. Compare the contracting entity with the regulator record; do not rely on a parent group name alone.
  3. Confirm the product permission. Casino games, poker, sports betting and virtual slots may have separate permissions. Never substitute one for another.
  4. Keep territory precise. State, province, canton or other local boundaries must remain visible in the wording. A permission in one place is not a universal licence.
  5. Read consumer terms. Check deposits and withdrawals, verification, fees, limits, complaints escalation, safer-gambling tools and the full terms for any promotion.
  6. Record the evidence date. Registers, domains, licence statuses and rules can change. Recheck before publication and schedule a new review date.

What BeInCasinos will and will not claim

A regulator listing can support a narrow factual statement when the exact operator, domain, product and territory match. It does not by itself prove that an operator is good value, has fast withdrawals, offers a valid bonus, or has passed hands-on testing. Those claims require separate evidence.

Market-specific editorial gate

Record the register URL, legal entity, active status, domain, licence type and date checked. Investigate regulatory settlements and enforcement actions with context. Do not infer permission for Northern Ireland, Morocco, or any other market from a Great Britain record.

Questions to ask before trusting a listing

  • Does the official register identify this exact website or a clearly matching domain?
  • Does the listed entity match the entity named in the current consumer terms?
  • Does the permission cover online casino games specifically?
  • Does the permission cover this territory and the current date?
  • Can the reader find current withdrawal, complaints and safer-gambling terms?
  • Is the page clear about what has not been tested or verified?
