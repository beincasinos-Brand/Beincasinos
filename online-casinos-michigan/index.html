Online casinos in Michigan: licensed by the MGCB

In Michigan online casino gaming is regulated by the Michigan Gaming Control Board. Players must be 21 or older and physically in the state.

How to check an operator in Michigan

The regulator is the Michigan Gaming Control Board (MGCB). Always use its own register, not a badge on a website.

  1. Check the operator on the MGCB's official list.
  2. Confirm you are on the operator's own site or app.
  3. Expect age and location verification.
  4. Read bonus and withdrawal terms for Michigan.
  5. Use the responsible gaming tools.
