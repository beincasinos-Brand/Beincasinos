Transparency

Commercial disclosure

Paid listings and any future affiliate links will be disclosed clearly and kept separate from factual verification.

Planned revenue order

Our current plan is to build a useful audience and verified brand directory first. Paid listings may be considered only after the site has real, measurable audience data and a transparent media kit. Affiliate links are planned as a later, secondary revenue stream—not the basis for initial editorial decisions.

How paid listings will be labelled

If paid listings are introduced, each paid placement will be labelled clearly near the listing (for example, “Paid listing” or “Sponsored”). A fee must not be treated as evidence that a brand is licensed, safe, suitable, or better than another operator. Payment will not buy an undisclosed editorial rating or safety verdict.

How affiliate links may work later

If BeInCasinos later uses affiliate links, they will be labelled near the relevant link or recommendation. A commission is not proof that an operator is safe, licensed for your location, or suitable for you. Before adding links, we must verify the program’s terms, allowed geographies, required disclosures, tracking practices, and the legality of the offer for the intended audience.

Current commercial status

No paid listings, operator rankings, bonus offers, or affiliate links are currently being offered or endorsed on this page. Brand profiles will be published only after their key details are checked against credible sources. Commercial status and the last verification date will be made visible on relevant profiles.
