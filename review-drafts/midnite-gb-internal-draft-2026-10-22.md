## Summary
Midnite is a consumer-facing sports betting and casino brand at midnite.com. However, the UK Gambling Commission (UKGC) currently shows two licensees associated with the Midnite name/domain: Dribble Media Limited (account 42647) and Midnite Limited (account 101839). The current official site pages and terms name Midnite Limited for Great Britain, while the historical register association remains visible for Dribble Media Limited. This is a material operator-mapping issue that must be resolved and documented before publication.

**Current verdict: HOLD — operator transfer/association not fully reconciled.** No score is assigned.

## What can be said cautiously
The public records and current terms provide useful starting evidence for the current Great Britain operator and the brand's product scope. They do not resolve why the older licensee record still associates the brand/domain, nor do they establish a transfer date, customer-account treatment or the entity responsible for every legacy customer relationship.
