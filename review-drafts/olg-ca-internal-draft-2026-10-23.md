# OLG.ca — Ontario internal casino review draft
**Status:** INTERNAL ONLY — HOLD; NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION  
**Evidence date:** 23 October 2026  
**Method:** primary-source desk research; no hands-on testing, account creation, deposit, gameplay or withdrawal performed. No rating assigned.

## Summary
OLG.ca is the online gaming platform operated under the conduct and management of the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG), a provincial Crown corporation. iGaming Ontario's regulated-market directory states that OLG.ca is operated legally under OLG. The current OLG Player Agreement sets geographic and age eligibility requirements for pay-to-play products. This establishes a useful operator and market starting point, but it is not a quality verdict.

**Verdict: HOLD. Rating not assigned.**

## What primary sources establish
- iGaming Ontario's regulated-market directory (listing stated accurate as of 1 September 2026) identifies OLG.ca as operated under OLG: https://www.igamingontario.ca/en/player/regulated-igaming-market
- OLG's Player Agreement governs its online platform and defines pay-to-play games to include casino-style games such as roulette and slots: https://www.olg.ca/en/terms-and-condition.html
- The agreement states that a player must reside in Ontario and be physically located in Ontario each time they play. It sets 19+ eligibility for online casino/pay-to-play games; some lottery-only functions have different age thresholds. Do not flatten these different thresholds into a blanket “all OLG products 19+” statement.
- The agreement incorporates game-specific rules, applicable offer terms, a responsible-gambling/My PlayBreak policy and privacy/cookie policy by reference. Each incorporated document still needs to be captured and reviewed separately.

## Consumer review observations from desk research
OLG.ca should be evaluated as a province-specific regulated product rather than a Canada-wide casino. Eligibility and geolocation restrictions materially affect access. The Player Agreement is a central legal document, but the review cannot assess the practical clarity of individual game rules, promotional terms, deposit/withdrawal experience, or customer support without further evidence and testing.

No statement is made here about withdrawal speed, payout reliability, fairness of any specific game, support quality or overall safety.
