## What readers should check

- **Payments:** the policy lists cards, e-wallets, bank-transfer options and crypto assets, but availability and limits can vary by country and payment method. Recheck the live cashier/terms for the selected market.
- **Games and sportsbook:** the terms describe casino, live casino and sportsbook services; game availability is market-dependent. No game or transaction was independently tested.
- **Complaints and support:** the terms advertise live chat and email and refer to an independent dispute route where applicable. Confirm the exact provider and escalation conditions from the current complaints policy.
- **Safer gambling and privacy:** link to the current operator policies and explain the available tools without implying that they remove gambling risk.
