How to use this directory

Search by brand, market or listed domain. Use the filters to narrow the queue. “Verification pending” means the brand is not approved for a consumer recommendation. “Excluded lead” means it must not count toward the target unless the exclusion is formally resolved.

01 · Permission first

Match the exact brand, domain, legal operator and casino product to the relevant regulator’s current record.

02 · Evidence before claims

Record dated terms, bonus rules, withdrawal limits, fees, complaint routes and safer-play controls.

03 · Independent sign-off

Publish only after a second reviewer checks the evidence and editorial/legal approval is recorded.

04 · Image rights are separate

Images found on official sites are candidates, not automatically reusable assets. Each remains labelled rights pending until permission, exact brand identity and direct rendering are checked; otherwise the card falls back to editorial artwork.

05 · Evidence grades are not ratings

Grade A means a key claim has primary-source support; Grade B means primary evidence is incomplete; Grade C is a discovery lead. None is a casino quality score, safety guarantee or recommendation. Every card remains on hold until its checklist is cleared.
