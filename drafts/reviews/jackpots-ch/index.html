What current public evidence supports

The BeInCasinos market-research page records jackpots.ch as the online platform listed by the Swiss Federal Gaming Board for Casino Baden. That listing is a research starting point, not a completed product review or proof that every individual game is permitted.

  • The Swiss regulator's platform list should be treated as the primary starting point for identifying the licensed casino and platform relationship.
  • The regulator notes that online casinos require a licence extension and individual online games may require permits.
  • The review must separately inspect the contracting entity, current terms, game-specific authorisations where relevant, payment rules and safer-gambling tools.

Promotions and terms

Do not treat marketing headlines as complete terms. Capture the live offer and its linked rules on the publication date, including eligibility, wagering base, excluded games, expiry, maximum cashout and maximum-bet conditions. If official pages conflict, pause the claim until reconciled.

Payments, withdrawals and support

No first-hand deposit, withdrawal or support test is claimed here. Document the available cashier options and written withdrawal stages, verification requirements, limits, fees and dispute route. If a fact has not been checked, state that clearly rather than inferring customer experience.

Safer gambling

Verify current limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, age information and local help resources directly. A policy link alone does not establish that each tool is accessible or has been tested.

Testing disclosure

No independent account, gameplay, payment, withdrawal, support-response, mobile-performance or accessibility test is represented as completed in this draft. No numerical rating or performance claim is assigned.
